The '''2026 PDC Players Championship series''' is an ongoing series of 34 non-televised [[darts]] tournaments on the [[2026 PDC Pro Tour]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Darts 2026 schedule and dates: Premier League Darts, World Matchplay and more with Luke Littler in action |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12040/13428768/darts-2026-schedule-and-dates-premier-league-darts-world-matchplay-and-more-with-luke-littler-in-action |website=Sky Sports |access-date=9 February 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=Calendar |url=https://www.pdc.tv/calendar |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=9 February 2026}}</ref> The Players Championship Order of Merit, a ranking list that only includes the prize money won by each player in the Players Championship events, will determine the field of 64 participants for the [[2026 Players Championship Finals]] in November.

As of Players Championship 33, 22 [[nine-dart finish]]es have been hit during the series.

== Prize money ==
The total prize money for Players Championship events in 2026 increased to £150,000 per event from a previous total of £125,000 in 2025.<ref>{{cite web |title=Players Championships 2025 PC 01 Wigan |url=https://mastercaller.com/tournaments/players-championships/2025-pc-01-wigan |website=Mastercaller |access-date=9 February 2026}}</ref> The winner of each event receives £15,000.
