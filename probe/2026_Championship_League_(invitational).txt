The '''2026 Championship League Invitational''' (officially the '''2026 BetVictor Championship League Snooker Invitational''') was a professional non-[[Snooker world rankings|ranking]] [[snooker]] tournament that took place from 2 January to 11 February 2026 at the [[Leicester Arena]] in [[Leicester]], England. Part of the [[2025–26 snooker season]], it was the 20th edition of the invitational version of the tournament, which was first staged in [[2008 Championship League|2008]].

[[Mark Selby]] was the defending champion, having retained the title at the [[2025 Championship League (invitational)|2025 event]] by defeating [[Kyren Wilson]] 3{{nbnd}}0 in the final. He won the title for a third consecutive time by defeating [[Wu Yize]] 3{{nbnd}}1 in the final.

There were 173 [[century break]]s made during the tournament, including  five [[maximum break]]s, made respectively by [[Chris Wakelin]] in his Group 1 match against [[Pang Junxu]], [[Matthew Selt]] in his Group 4 match against [[Xiao Guodong]], Xiao in his Group 6 match against [[Oliver Lines]], [[Wu Yize]] in his Group 6 match against Lines, and [[Zhao Xintong]] in his Group 6 semi-final against [[Jak Jones]]. Wakelin, Selt, Wu, and Zhao all made their first maximums in professional competition, while Xiao made his third. They were the record{{nbh}}extending 17th, 18th, 19th, 20th, and 21st maximums of the season.

===Format===
A total of 25 players were initially invited to the event, with the matches played [[Behind closed doors (sport)|behind closed doors]] without an audience. Players earn prize money for every {{cuegloss|frame}} won, as well as being semi{{nbh}}finalists, runners{{nbh}}up, and winners of each group, with more money involved in the Winners' Group. Since [[2010 Championship League|2010]], all matches have been played as the [[Playoff format#Higher number of games|best of five]] frames.

The tournament was played in a [[Round-robin tournament|round{{nbh}}robin]] format, consisting of eight groups of seven players. The top four players in each group qualify for a play{{nbh}}off, with the winner entering the Winners' Group. The bottom two players in each group were eliminated, and the remaining four moved to the next group, where they were joined by three more players. This format was played for each group, from one to seven. In each group, the players were ranked by the number of matches won, then by most frames won, and then by least frames lost. If two players were tied by these criteria, the player who won the match between them was ranked higher in the table. The group play{{nbh}}off semi{{nbh}}finals were contested between the 1st and 4th place players in the table, and between the 2nd and 3rd place players in the table. The Winners' Group was played at the end, and its play{{nbh}}off winner became champion of the tournament.<ref name="CLS_inv_format">{{cite web |title=Championship League Snooker |url=https://championshipleaguesnooker.co.uk/invitational/ |website=championshipleaguesnooker.co.uk |access-date=13 February 2026 |archive-date=13 February 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260213115337/https://championshipleaguesnooker.co.uk/invitational/ |url-status=live}}</ref><ref name="CLS_info">{{cite web |title=2026 BetVictor Championship League Snooker invitational event returns: everything you need to know |url=https://championshipleaguesnooker.co.uk/2026-betvictor-championship-league-snooker-invitational-event-returns-everything-you-need-to-know/ |website=championshipleaguesnooker.co.uk |date=22 December 2025 |access-date=13 February 2026 |archive-date=2 January 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260102075838/https://championshipleaguesnooker.co.uk/2026-betvictor-championship-league-snooker-invitational-event-returns-everything-you-need-to-know/ |url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=BetVictor Championship League Snooker Invitational returns in January |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/december/22/betvictor-championship-league-snooker-invitational-returns-in-january/ |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |date=22 December 2025 |access-date=13 February 2025 |archive-date=7 January 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260107143226/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/december/22/betvictor-championship-league-snooker-invitational-returns-in-january/ |url-status=live}}</ref>
