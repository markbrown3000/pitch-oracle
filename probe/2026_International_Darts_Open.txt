The '''2026 International Darts Open''' (known for sponsorship reasons as the '''2026 Elten Safety Shoes International Darts Open''') was a professional [[darts]] tournament that took place at the [[Sachsen Arena|SACHSENarena]] in [[Riesa]], Germany, from 22 to 24 May 2026.<ref>{{cite web |title=Elten Safety Shoes International Darts Open 2026 |url=https://www.pdc-europe.tv/tournaments/et-2026-en/international-darts-open-2026/ |website=PDC Europe |access-date=1 March 2026}}</ref> It was the seventh of fifteen [[PDC European Tour]] events on the [[2026 PDC Pro Tour]]. It featured a field of 48 players and £230,000 in prize money, with £35,000 going to the winner.

[[Stephen Bunting]] was the defending champion, having defeated [[Nathan Aspinall]] 8–5 in the [[2025 International Darts Open|2025]] final.<ref name="BBCFinal">{{cite web |date=6 April 2025 |title=International Open darts: Stephen Bunting beats Nathan Aspinall to win first European Tour title |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/darts/articles/cx2v530kl45o |website=[[BBC Sport]] |access-date=6 April 2025}}</ref> However, he lost 6–2 to [[Rob Cross]] in the third round.

[[Ross Smith (darts player)|Ross Smith]] won the tournament, defeating [[Ryan Searle (darts player)|Ryan Searle]] 8–3 in the final. It was his first European Tour title, having finished as runner-up in his first four European Tour finals.

==Prize money==
As part of a mass boost in prize money for [[Professional Darts Corporation]] (PDC) events in 2026, the prize fund for all [[2026 PDC Pro Tour#European Tour#European Tour|2026 European Tour]] events rose to £230,000, of which the winner will receive £35,000.<ref>{{cite web |last=Phillips |first=Josh |date=31 March 2025 |title=Biggest prize money increase in PDC history confirmed |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/march/31/biggest-prize-money-increase-pdc-history-confirmed |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=1 March 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=International Darts Open 2026 |url=https://mastercaller.com/tournaments/international-darts-open/2026 |website=Mastercaller |access-date=1 March 2026}}</ref>

== Qualification and format ==
The top 16 players on the two-year [[PDC Order of Merit]] were [[Seeding (sports)|seeded]] and entered the tournament in the second round, while the next 16 highest-ranked players from the one-year [[PDC Pro Tour Order of Merit]] automatically qualified for the first round. The seedings were confirmed on 9 April.<ref>{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=9 April 2026 |title=2026 ET7-8 Seeds & Tour Card Holder qualifier entries confirmed |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/april/09/ET7-8-Seeds-Tour-Card-Holder-qualifier-entries-confirmed/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=6 May 2026}}</ref> The remaining 16 places went to players from four qualifying events – 10 from the Tour Card Holder Qualifier (held on 15 April),<ref>{{cite web |date=15 April 2026 |title=PDC ET07 Tour Card Holder Qualifier |url=https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdceutcq26et07/matches |website=DartConnect |access-date=15 May 2026}}</ref> four from the Host Nation Qualifier (held on 1 March),<ref>{{cite web |date=1 March 2026 |title=PDC ET07 Host Nation Qualifier |url=https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdc26et07hnq/matches |website=Dart Connect TV |access-date=1 March 2026}}</ref> one from the Nordic & Baltic Associate Member Qualifier (held on 24 April),<ref>{{cite web |date=24 April 2026 |title=PDCNB 2026 Tour - Finland - Weekend 03 |url=https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdcnbpro26e03/matches |website=DartConnect |access-date=15 May 2026}}</ref> and one from the East European Associate Member Qualifier (held on 12 April).<ref>{{cite web |date=12 April 2026 |title=PDC ET07 E. Europe Qualifier |url=https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdceeu26et07q/matches |website=DartConnect |access-date=15 May 2026}}</ref>

[[Luke Humphries]] and [[Michael Smith (darts player)|Michael Smith]] withdrew and were replaced by [[Rob Cross]] and [[Maik Kuivenhoven]]. In Humphries' place, [[Luke Woodhouse]] moved up to become the 16th seed.<ref name="Draw">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=21 May 2026 |title=2026 Elten Safety Shoes International Darts Open draw & schedule |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/may/21/2026-elten-safety-shoes-international-darts-open-draw-schedule/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=23 May 2026}}</ref> [[Gerwyn Price]] withdrew after the draw was made and was replaced by reserve player [[Christian Kist]], going straight in at the second-round stage.<ref name="PDCR1">{{cite web |last=Simpson |first=Will |date=22 May 2026 |title=Doets dazzles and Manby wins on debut at International Darts Open |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/may/22/Doets-dazzles-and-Manby-wins-on-debut-at-International-Darts-Open/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=23 May 2026}}</ref>

== Summary ==
=== First round ===
[[File:Ricardo Pietreczko 2025 (cropped).png|thumb|upright=0.90|[[Ricardo Pietreczko]] ''(pictured in 2025)'' was the only victorious German player in the first round. He defeated [[Maik Kuivenhoven]] 6–2.]]
The first round (best of 11 [[Leg (darts)|legs]]) was played on 22 May.<ref name="PDCR1"/> [[Charlie Manby]] was successful in his European Tour debut, defeating [[Niko Springer]] 6–3. On facing [[Ryan Searle (darts player)|Ryan Searle]] in the second round, he commented: "I think Ryan [Searle] and I are both top-quality players. I know I'm good enough as well, and I've got the belief in myself that I need to be here."<ref name="SkyR1">{{cite web |date=22 May 2026 |title=International Darts Open: Charlie Manby progresses past round one on European Tour debut |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13547080/international-darts-open-charlie-manby-progresses-past-round-one-on-european-tour-debut |website=Sky Sports |access-date=23 May 2026}}</ref> [[Kevin Doets]] achieved the first round's only [[three-dart average]] above the 100 mark, averaging 102.48 in a [[Whitewash (sport)|whitewash]] win over [[Tom Bissell (darts player)|Tom Bissell]].<ref name="EUR1">{{cite web |date=22 May 2026 |title=ELTEN Safety Shoes International Darts Open: International Darts Open: Pietreczko ends dry spell |url=https://www.pdc-europe.tv/news/elten-safety-shoes-international-darts-open-international-darts-open-pietreczko-ends-dry-spell/ |website=PDC Europe |access-date=23 May 2026}}</ref> "I didn't go all the way last time, so that's what I'm trying to do this weekend," said Doets, in reference to his runner-up finish at the [[2026 Austrian Darts Open|Austrian Darts Open]].<ref name="PDCR1"/> All four host nation qualifiers went out in the first round: Finn Behrens lost 6–1 to [[Connor Scutt]], Liam Maendl-Lawrance lost 6–3 to [[Dave Chisnall]], Paul Krohne lost 6–2 to [[William O'Connor (darts player)|William O'Connor]], and [[Michael Unterbuchner]] lost in a [[Glossary of darts#Decider|deciding leg]] to [[Cameron Menzies]].<ref name="EUR1"/> [[Ricardo Pietreczko]] was the only German player to advance to the second round, earning his first win in 11 matches amid a battle with [[dartitis]] by defeating [[Maik Kuivenhoven]] 6–2.<ref>{{cite web |last=Schamburg |first=Julius |date=22 May 2026 |title=Pikachu bricht den Bann |trans-title=Pikachu breaks the spell |url=https://www.sport1.de/news/darts-sport/2026/05/darts-pikachu-bricht-den-bann-dann-wird-es-emotional |website=Sport1 |access-date=23 May 2026 |language=de}}</ref> The [[2020 International Darts Open|2020]] champion [[Joe Cullen (darts player)|Joe Cullen]] recovered from 5–3 down to beat [[Chris Landman]] 6–5.<ref name="SkyR1"/>

[[Rob Cross]] aided his chances at qualifying for the [[2026 World Matchplay|World Matchplay]] by defeating [[Dirk van Duijvenbode]] in a deciding leg, with all 11 legs of the match being [[Glossary of darts#Hold|holds of throw]]. "I just kept my nose in front," stated Cross afterwards. It just shows I didn't need his throw, I needed mine. I was lucky enough to get away with it."<ref name="PDCR1"/> [[Karel Sedláček]] whitewashed Swedish qualifier [[Johan Engström]], while [[Kim Huybrechts]] missed opportunities to seal his own whitewash victory against [[Daryl Gurney]], eventually winning 6–2. [[Krzysztof Ratajski]] earned a 6–4 victory over [[Cristo Reyes]], who missed 19 darts at [[Glossary of darts#Double|double]]. [[Niels Zonneveld]], [[Madars Razma]], and [[Ryan Joyce]] were 6–2 winners against [[Keane Barry]], György Jehirszki, and [[Jeffrey Sparidaans]], respectively. [[Bradley Brooks]] achieved his first European Tour win of the year by beating [[Andrew Gilding]] 6–4.<ref name="SkyR1"/>

=== Second round ===
[[File:2026-03-26 Premier League Darts – Night 8 – Berlin 2026 by Sandro Halank–045.jpg|thumb|[[Michael van Gerwen]] ''(pictured in 2026)'' won his second-round match 6{{nbnd}}5 against [[Madars Razma]], who missed six [[Glossary of darts#Match dart|match darts]].]]
The second round (best of 11 legs) was played on 23 May.<ref name="PDCR2">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=23 May 2026 |title=Bunting opens title defence in style at International Darts Open |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/may/23/bunting-opens-title-defence-style-international-darts-open/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=24 May 2026}}</ref> The defending champion [[Stephen Bunting]] began his campaign with a 6–2 victory over Krzysztof Ratajski, averaging 102.54 with five [[Glossary of darts#Maximum|maximums]]. "I feel great, I feel fresh and I feel ready to win," Bunting affirmed. "I won in the [[2025 Premier League Darts|Premier League]] in [[Berlin]] last year and then came straight here and won this, so hopefully there's a lot of similarities."<ref name="SkyR2">{{cite web |date=23 May 2026 |title=Stephen Bunting, Gian van Veen and Michael van Gerwen all make winning starts at International Darts Open |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13547435/stephen-bunting-gian-van-veen-and-michael-van-gerwen-all-make-winning-starts-at-international-darts-open |website=Sky Sports |access-date=24 May 2026}}</ref> Top seed [[Gian van Veen]] won 6–3 against Connor Scutt, having lost his last three matches on the European Tour. "I think everyone who's watched me over the last few weeks knows it's been tough," commented Van Veen, who said the win on the day was needed.<ref name="PDCR2"/> [[Michael van Gerwen]] hit [[Glossary of darts#Checkout|checkouts]] of 164 and 127 as he came back from 3–0 to win a deciding leg against Madars Razma, who missed a total of six [[Glossary of darts#Match dart|match darts]]. Cameron Menzies and [[Wessel Nijman]] also produced comeback victories, overturning deficits of 4–0 and 5–3 in their respective wins over sixth seed [[Josh Rock]] and William O'Connor.<ref name="SkyR2"/>

Ricardo Pietreczko reached the last 16 of a European Tour event for the first time in a year by defeating seventh seed [[Danny Noppert]] 6–5. He was joined by compatriot [[Martin Schindler]], who survived a match dart as he beat Karel Sedláček by the same scoreline.<ref>{{cite web |date=23 May 2026 |title=ELTEN Safety Shoes International Darts Open: German duo storms into the Last 16 in Riesa |url=https://www.pdc-europe.tv/news/elten-safety-shoes-international-darts-open-german-duo-storms-into-the-last-16-in-riesa/ |website=PDC Europe |access-date=24 May 2026}}</ref> Kim Huybrechts eliminated ninth seed [[Chris Dobey]], averaging 104.66 in a 6–5 win. Rob Cross won six consecutive legs to beat thirteenth seed [[Mike De Decker]] 6–2, while Kevin Doets won a deciding leg against sixteenth seed [[Luke Woodhouse]] for his third victory over Woodhouse in 19 days. [[Christian Kist]], who replaced fifth seed [[Gerwyn Price]] in the tournament, was beaten 6–2 by Ryan Joyce. [[James Wade]] earned a whitewash win over Dave Chisnall, while [[Jermaine Wattimena]] came through a deciding leg against Bradley Brooks. [[Ross Smith (darts player)|Ross Smith]] and Ryan Searle were 6–3 winners over Joe Cullen and Charlie Manby, respectively, and [[Damon Heta]] defeated Niels Zonneveld 6–2.<ref name="PDCR2"/>

=== Final day ===
[[File:RossSmithEC25.png|thumb|upright=0.90|[[Ross Smith (darts player)|Ross Smith]] ''(pictured in 2025)'' won his first European Tour title in his fifth final.]]
The third round, quarter-finals, semi-finals and final were played on 24 May. The third round and quarter-finals were contested over the best of 11 legs, the semi-finals over the best of 13 legs, and the final over the best of 15 legs.<ref name="PDCFinal">{{cite web |last=Wagner |first=Connor |date=24 May 2026 |title=Super Smith wins maiden European Tour title in Riesa |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/may/24/2026-elten-safety-shoes-international-darts-open-finals-day-latest/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=25 May 2026}}</ref> The final day saw Ryan Searle and Ross Smith reach the final. Searle followed a 6–1 win over Kim Huybrechts by eliminating the top seed Gian van Veen in the quarter-finals, defeating the Dutchman 6–3. He was taken to a deciding leg in the semi-finals, as Rob Cross compiled checkouts of 126, 130, and 164, before a [[Glossary of darts#Maximum check|170 checkout]] levelled the score at 6–6. Searle found his own [[Glossary of darts#Ton|ton]]-plus finish, a 130 checkout on the [[Bullseye (target)|bullseye]], to prevail 7–6.<ref name="PDCFinal"/><ref name="DNewsFinal">{{cite web |last=Wood |first=Kieran |date=24 May 2026 |title=Ross Smith ends long European Tour wait with emotional first title in Riesa after Ryan Searle final |url=https://dartsnews.com/pdc/international-darts-open-2026-roundup-ross-smith-ends-long-european-tour-wait-with-emotional-first-title-in-riesa-after-ryan-searle-final |website=dartsnews.com |access-date=25 May 2026}}</ref> Smith defeated Ricardo Pietreczko 6–4 before claiming a deciding-leg victory over Michael van Gerwen in the quarter-finals. A 7–0 whitewash of Cameron Menzies, who reached his first European Tour semi-final of the year, secured Smith's place in the final.<ref name="SkyFinal">{{cite web |date=24 May 2026 |title=International Darts Open: Ross Smith beats Ryan Searle to maiden European Tour title |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12040/13547722/international-darts-open-ross-smith-beats-ryan-searle-to-maiden-european-tour-title |website=Sky Sports |access-date=25 May 2026}}</ref> Both finalists were looking to win their first European Tour title, with Smith losing in his four previous finals, whereas Searle lost his only previous final at the [[2024 Swiss Darts Trophy]].<ref name="EUFinal">{{cite web |date=24 May 2026 |title=ELTEN Safety Shoes International Darts Open: Ross Smith celebrates maiden European Tour title |url=https://www.pdc-europe.tv/news/elten-safety-shoes-international-darts-open-ross-smith-celebrates-maiden-european-tour-title/ |website=PDC Europe |access-date=25 May 2026}}</ref>

The English pair traded the opening two legs of the final, but Smith took a 3–1 lead by finding a [[Glossary of darts#Break|break of throw]] in the fourth leg. In the sixth leg, he set up a smaller finish from 161 with Searle left on 133; Searle was unable to convert, and Smith hit double 8 to maintain a two-leg advantage at 4–2. Searle registered another leg, but Smith went on to win the final 8–3, claiming five of the last six legs. Smith averaged 90.81 compared to Searle's 90.11.<ref name="DNewsFinal"/><ref name="EUFinal"/> Smith finished the tournament with the most maximums of any player, hitting 14.<ref>{{cite web |date=24 May 2026 |title=Riesa in Numbers – The 2026 ELTEN Safety Shoes International Darts Open |url=https://www.pdc-europe.tv/news/riesa-in-numbers-the-2026-elten-safety-shoes-international-darts-open/ |website=PDC Europe |access-date=25 May 2026}}</ref>

Smith won his ninth PDC ranking title and first European Tour title, having finished as runner-up at the [[2024 European Darts Open]], the [[2024 European Darts Grand Prix]], the [[2025 Austrian Darts Open]], and the [[2026 European Darts Grand Prix]]. He also became the seventh different winner on the European Tour in 2026.<ref name="SkyFinal"/><ref name="EUFinal"/> "I’m so happy to finally win one," said Smith after the final. On his final opponent, he commented: "Ryan will win loads of these, he’s Ryan Searle, he’s fantastic, he definitely let me off in the final there."<ref name="PDCFinal"/> Searle believed he "shot [his] bolt" against Rob Cross in the semi-finals, describing his performance in the final as "a little bit flat".<ref name="PDCFinal"/>
