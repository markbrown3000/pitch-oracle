{{short description|Series of darts tournament}}
The '''2026 World Series of Darts''' is a series of televised [[darts]] tournaments organised by the [[Professional Darts Corporation]]. Six [[World Series of Darts|World Series]] events will be held followed by one [[2026 World Series of Darts Finals|finals]] event, which like every year since [[2021 World Series of Darts|2021]] has taken place in [[Amsterdam]], Netherlands.

One new event took place for the first time in 2026, with the [[2026 Saudi Arabia Darts Masters|Saudi Arabia Darts Masters]] taking place at the Global Theater in [[Riyadh]], Saudi Arabia. However, the [[2025 Dutch Darts Masters|Dutch]] and [[2025 Poland Darts Masters|Poland Darts Masters]] were both removed from the series, with the latter being scrapped due to a new event, the [[2026 Poland Darts Open|Poland Darts Open]], being added to the [[PDC European Tour|European Tour]] schedule.<ref name="Schedule">{{cite web |last=Phillips |first=Josh |date=5 October 2025 |title=2026 World Series of Darts schedule confirmed |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/october/05/2026-world-series-darts-schedule-confirmed |website=Professional Darts Corporation}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |last= |first= |date=11 September 2025 |title=European Tour set for further expansion in 2026 with PDC calendar released |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/september/11/european-tour-set-further-expansion-2026-pdc-calendar-released |website=Professional Darts Corporation}}</ref>

== Prize money ==
The total prize fund for each of the six World Series events was unchanged from the previous year at £100,000 per event. The total prize fund for the [[2026 World Series of Darts Finals]] was increased from the previous year from £400,000 to £450,000.<ref>{{cite web |last=Phillips |first=Josh |date=31 March 2025 |title=Biggest prize money increase in PDC history confirmed |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/march/31/biggest-prize-money-increase-pdc-history-confirmed |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=7 January 2026}}</ref> The prize fund breakdowns are shown below:
