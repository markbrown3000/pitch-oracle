The '''2027 Tour Championship''' is an upcoming professional [[snooker]] tournament that will take place from 29 March to 4 April 2027 at the [[Manchester Central Convention Complex|Manchester Central]] in [[Manchester]], England. It will feature the top 12 players on the one-year [[Snooker world rankings|ranking list]], as it stands after the [[2027 World Open (snooker)|2027 World Open]]. The ninth consecutive edition of the [[Tour Championship (snooker)|Tour Championship]] since it was first staged in [[2019 Tour Championship|2019]], it will be the 17th and penultimate [[List of snooker ranking tournaments|ranking event]] of the [[2026–27 snooker season]], following the 2027 World Open and preceding the [[2027 World Snooker Championship]]. It will be the last of three events in the season's [[Players Series]], following the [[2027 World Grand Prix (snooker)|2027 World Grand Prix]] and the [[2027 Players Championship (snooker)|2027 Players Championship]].

[[Zhao Xintong]] is the defending champion, having defeated [[Judd Trump]] 10–3 in the [[2026 Tour Championship|2026 final]].

== Overview ==
[[File:Manchester Central Arena.jpg |thumb |upright=0.85 |alt=Photograph of Manchester Central Arena |The event will take place at the [[Manchester Central Convention Complex|Manchester Central]] in [[Manchester]], England.]]

First held in 2019, the [[Tour Championship (snooker)|Tour Championship]] is the only event on the professional tour other than the [[World Snooker Championship]] to feature multi-{{Cuegloss|session}} matches throughout the entire tournament.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Day |first=Michael |date=12 April 2024 |title=Tour Championship Snooker - Information and a Potted History |url=https://www.totallysnookered.com/sport/snooker/tournaments/tour-championship/tour-championship-snooker-information-and-a-potted-history-4585458 |access-date=9 February 2026 |website=Totally Snookered |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260416161132/https://www.totallysnookered.com/sport/snooker/tournaments/tour-championship/tour-championship-snooker-information-and-a-potted-history-4585458|archive-date=16 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>  [[Ronnie O'Sullivan]] won the [[2019 Tour Championship|inaugural edition]], staged in [[Llandudno]], Wales, with a 13–11 victory over [[Neil Robertson]] in the final.<ref>{{Cite news |date=24 March 2019 |title=Tour Championship: Ronnie O'Sullivan beats Neil Robertson to become world number one |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/47688509 |access-date=9 February 2026 |work=[[BBC Sport]] |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260416160222/https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/47688509|archive-date=16 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref> The field of players was originally the top 8 on the one-year [[snooker world ranking|ranking list]], but it was subsequently expanded to the top 12, beginning with the [[2024 Tour Championship|2024 edition]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://snookerhq.com/2024/03/31/tour-championship-2024-draw-preview-schedule-results-how-watch/|title=Tour Championship: 2024 draw, preview, schedule, results, how to watch|work=SnookerHQ|first=David|last=Caulfield|date=31 March 2024|access-date=29 April 2026|language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260429105012/https://snookerhq.com/2024/03/31/tour-championship-2024-draw-preview-schedule-results-how-watch/|archive-date=29 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

The 2027 edition of the tournament—its ninth consecutive staging since the inaugural edition in 2019—will take place from 29 March to 4 April at the [[Manchester Central Convention Complex|Manchester Central]] in [[Manchester]], England.<ref>{{cite web |title=Tour Championship 2027 |url=https://www.wst.tv/tickets/tour-championship-2027-tickets|work=[[World Snooker Tour]]|access-date=20 April 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=Sportsbet.io Tour Championship|url=https://www.wst.tv/tourchampionship/|work=[[World Snooker Tour]]|access-date=29 April 2026}}</ref> It will be the 17th and penultimate [[List of snooker ranking tournaments|ranking event]] of the [[2026–27 snooker season]], following the [[2027 World Open (snooker)|2027 World Open]] and preceding the [[2027 World Snooker Championship]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.snooker.org/res/index.asp?season=2026&template=2&tour=all|title=Calendar 2026/2027|website=snooker.org|access-date=29 April 2026|language=en}}</ref> It will be the last of three events in the season's [[Players Series]], following the [[2027 World Grand Prix (snooker)|2027 World Grand Prix]] and the [[2027 Players Championship (snooker)|2027 Players Championship]].{{cn|date=April 2026}}
