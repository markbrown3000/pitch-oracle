The '''2026 Tour Championship''' (officially the '''2026 Sportsbet.io Tour Championship''')<ref name="s.o_main"/> was a professional [[snooker]] tournament that took place from 30 March to 5 April 2026 at the [[Manchester Central Convention Complex|Manchester Central]] in [[Manchester]], England. It featured the top 12 players on the one-year ranking list, as it stood after the [[2026 World Open (snooker)|2026 World Open]]. The eighth consecutive edition of the [[Tour Championship (snooker)|Tour Championship]] since it was first staged in [[2019 Tour Championship|2019]], it was the 17th and penultimate [[List of snooker ranking tournaments|ranking event]] of the [[2025–26 snooker season]], following the 2026 World Open and preceding the [[2026 World Snooker Championship|2026 World Championship]]. It was the last of three events in the season's [[Players Series]], following the [[2026 World Grand Prix (snooker)|2026 World Grand Prix]] and the [[2026 Players Championship (snooker)|2026 Players Championship]]. The tournament was broadcast by [[5 (British TV channel)|5]] in the United Kingdom, by [[Eurosport]] in mainland Europe, by local channels in Asia and Africa, and by [[WST Play]] in all other territories. The winner received £150,000 from a total prize fund of £500,000.

[[John Higgins]] was the defending champion, having defeated [[Mark Selby]] 10–8 in the [[2025 Tour Championship|2025 final]], but he lost 1–10 to [[Zhao Xintong]] in the semi-finals. Zhao went on to defeat [[Judd Trump]] 10–3 in the final to win his sixth ranking title. Having already won the 2026 World Grand Prix and the 2026 Players Championship, Zhao became the first player to win all three Players Series events in the same season. The winner's prize made him the third player, after Trump and [[Ronnie O'Sullivan]], to surpass £1 million in prize money in a single season. He advanced to a career high of fourth in the [[Snooker world rankings|world rankings]] after the event. The tournament produced 24 [[century breaks]], of which the highest was a 140 by Trump in his quarter-final match against [[Shaun Murphy]].

=== Format ===
The event featured 12 participants. The top four seeds received [[Bye (sports)|bye]]s to the quarter-finals. Players seeded 5 through 8 faced players seeded 9 through 12 for the other four quarter-final places. All matches, including the final, were played as the best of 19 {{cuegloss|frame|frames}}, held over two sessions.<ref name="s.o_main">{{cite web |title=2026 Sportsbet.io Tour Championship |url=https://www.snooker.org/res/index.asp?event=2311 |access-date=8 February 2026 |website=snooker.org|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260417065713/https://www.snooker.org/res/index.asp?event=2311|archive-date=17 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

=== First round ===
[[File:Judd Trump 2026 (3).jpg|thumb|[[Judd Trump]] ''(pictured in 2026)'' defeated [[Mark Allen (snooker player)|Mark Allen]] 10–8 to win his first match at the [[Tour Championship (snooker)|Tour Championship]] since the [[2020 Tour Championship|2020 edition]]. Trump went on to reach the final of the event for the first time.]]

In the first round, players [[seeding (sports)|seeded]] 5 through 8 faced players seeded 9 through 12. The [[List of world number one snooker players|world number one]] and fifth seed [[Judd Trump]] faced the twelfth seed [[Mark Allen (snooker player)|Mark Allen]]. Despite making a highest {{Cuegloss|break}} of only 38 in the first four {{Cuegloss|frame|frames}}, Allen won all four on the {{Cuegloss|colours}}. Trump made breaks including 71, 104, and 88 as he also won four consecutive frames, tying the scores at 4{{En dash}}4 after the first {{Cuegloss|session}}.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |date=30 March 2026 |title=Trump fights back against Allen |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/march/30/Trump-Fights-Back-Against-Allen/ |access-date=30 March 2026 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260416161435/https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/march/30/Trump-Fights-Back-Against-Allen/|archive-date=16 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref> When play resumed, Allen won frame nine with a [[century break]] of 121, but Trump tied the scores at 5–5 and then moved ahead for the first time by winning frame 11 on a {{Cuegloss|re-spotted black}}. Allen tied the scores at 6–6 with another century of 121, but Trump won frame 13 with a century of 114 and then came from 42 points behind to take frame 14 with an 86 {{Cuegloss|clearance}}, moving two frames ahead at 8–6. Allen won the 15th frame, but Trump took the 16th with a 67 break to lead 9–7. In frame 17, Trump missed a {{Cuegloss|red ball|red}} while on a break of 35, and Allen made a frame-winning clearance of 79. Trump produced a century of 100 in frame 18 to complete a 10–8 win, his first victory at the [[Tour Championship (snooker)|Tour Championship]] since the [[2020 Tour Championship|2020 edition]]. "At 4–4 I was delighted, and tonight was a great standard," Trump said afterwards. "I have been consistent for the last five or six months, I have had a lot of semis and finals. I am happy with my form."<ref name=":1">{{Cite web |date=30 March 2026 |title=Trump ends six-year Tour drought |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/march/30/Trump-Ends-Six-Year-Tour-Drought-/ |access-date=30 March 2026 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260416161527/https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/march/30/Trump-Ends-Six-Year-Tour-Drought-/|archive-date=16 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Battison |first=Paul |date=30 March 2026 |title=Tour Championship: Trump beats Allen in Tour Championship opener |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cvg0pz478n0o |access-date=16 April 2026 |website=[[BBC Sport]] |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260416160256/https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cvg0pz478n0o|archive-date=16 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

The eighth seed [[Barry Hawkins]], recent winner of the [[2026 Welsh Open (snooker)|2026 Welsh Open]], faced the ninth seed [[Thepchaiya Un-Nooh]], recent winner of the [[2026 World Open (snooker)|2026 World Open]]. Un-Nooh led 3–2, but Hawkins then made breaks including 79 and 86 as he won three consecutive frames, ending the opening session with a 5–3 advantage.<ref name=":0" /> When play resumed for the second session, Un-Nooh won frame nine on the colours, but Hawkins took frame 10 with a century of 101. Un-Nooh won frame 11 after {{Cuegloss|fluke|fluking}} the last {{Cuegloss|yellow ball|yellow}} and also won the next two, moving 7–6 ahead. Un-Nooh missed a red in frame 14 while on a break of 50, and Hawkins tied the scores at 7–7 with a clearance of 72. Un-Nooh won frame 15 with a 66 break but missed a red in frame 16 while on a break of 48, and Hawkins tied the scores again at 8–8 with a 68 break. After winning a tactical battle on the last two reds to take frame 17, Hawkins made a 63 break in frame 18 and went on to win it on the colours, completing a 10–8 victory. "I nicked quite a few frames," Hawkins commented afterwards. "I didn't feel I played great, I was just battling away. It was getting twitchy at the end so it's a great win. I have had good runs in this event, especially as it's such a tough event just to win matches in, given the players who qualify."<ref name=":2">{{Cite web |date=31 March 2026 |title=Hawkins knocks out in-form Un-Nooh |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/march/31/Hawkins-Knocks-Out-In-Form-Un-Nooh/ |access-date=31 March 2026 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260417070004/https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/march/31/Hawkins-Knocks-Out-In-Form-Un-Nooh/|archive-date=17 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

[[File:Chris Wakelin 2026 (4).jpg|thumb|[[Chris Wakelin]] ''(pictured in 2026)'' won eight consecutive {{cuegloss|frames}} as he came from 2–6 behind to defeat [[Wu Yize]] 10–6.]]

The seventh seed [[Wu Yize]], who had entered the top 16 for the first time that season after winning the [[2025 International Championship]], faced the tenth seed [[Chris Wakelin]]. Wu produced breaks of 73, 74, 84, 75, and 81 as he moved into a 6–2 lead after the first session.<ref name=":1" /> In the second session, Wakelin made breaks including 84, 62, and 108 as he won all eight frames played, defeating Wu 10–6. "The way Wu played in the first session, I went to bed last night thinking I can't compete with that," Wakelin commented after the match. "I knew I had to come out tonight and find my own form and get something going. He missed a couple of balls in the first frame and I thought I could apply some pressure. Once I got back to 6–4, it was game on."<ref name=":3">{{Cite web |last=Battison |first=Paul |date=31 March 2026 |title=Tour Championship: Higgins holds off Williams fightback at Tour Championship |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cr5182pree9o |access-date=31 March 2026 |website=[[BBC Sport]] |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260416160313/https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cr5182pree9o|archive-date=16 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref><ref name=":4">{{Cite web |date=31 March 2026 |title=Higgins elated after 'special' win over Williams |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/march/31/Higgins-Elated-After--Special--Win-Over-Williams/ |access-date=31 March 2026 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260417070007/https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/march/31/Higgins-Elated-After--Special--Win-Over-Williams/|archive-date=17 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

The [[2024 Tour Championship|2024]] winner and sixth seed [[Mark Williams (snooker player)|Mark Williams]] played the defending champion and eleventh seed [[John Higgins]]. Williams made a century of 121 as he took a 2–0 lead, but Higgins won the remaining six frames of the session to lead 6–2.<ref name=":2" /> In the second session, Higgins made an 84 break to win frame nine, his seventh consecutive frame of the match, for a 7–2 lead. Williams took frame 10 after Higgins missed a {{cuegloss|pot}} on the last {{Cuegloss|green ball|green}}, made breaks of 74 and 82 to win frames 11 and 12, and also took frame 13 to reduce Higgins's lead to one at 7–6. Higgins produced a century of 110 to move 8–6 ahead, but Williams made breaks of 107 and 76 as he tied the scores at 8–8. Despite having lost six of the previous seven frames, Higgins won the last two frames for a 10–8 victory. It was the first time Higgins had beaten Williams in a multi-session match since the semi-finals of the [[2011 World Snooker Championship|2011 World Championship]]. "I am delighted to do to Mark [Williams] what he has done to me in the last few years," said Higgins afterwards. "It would have been a tough loss having been 7–2 in front."<ref name=":3" /><ref name=":4" />

=== Quarter-finals ===
In the quarter-finals, the first-round winners played the top four seeds. Trump faced the number four seed [[Shaun Murphy]], who won the opening two frames. Trump took frame three on the colours, made a {{Cuegloss|total clearance}} of 140 to win frame four, and then took frame five after {{Cuegloss|snooker|snookering}} Murphy on the last {{Cuegloss|blue ball|blue}}, moving 3–2 ahead. Murphy won the next two frames with breaks of 132 and 71, but Trump won the last of the session to tie the scores at 4–4.<ref name=":5">{{Cite web |date=1 April 2026 |title=Murphy and Trump level at 4-4 |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/april/01/Murphy-And-Trump-Level-At-4-4/ |access-date=1 April 2026 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260417070016/https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/april/01/Murphy-And-Trump-Level-At-4-4/|archive-date=17 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref> When play resumed, Trump took frame nine, but Murphy then won three in a row, making breaks including 69 and 127 to lead 7–5. Trump responded with breaks of 62 and 86 to tie the scores at 7–7. Murphy won frame 15, but Trump won the next two with breaks of 90 and 137 to move 9–8 ahead. Murphy made a 96 break in frame 18 to level at 9–9. In the {{cuegloss|deciding frame}}, Murphy lost position while on a break of 30 and missed an attempted {{Cuegloss|double}} on a red, allowing Trump to secure a 10–9 victory with a 59 break. It was the first time Trump had won a deciding frame that season, out of nine played. "Having not won a decider all season, it was tough," Trump commented. "In my most successful seasons, I have won nearly all of my deciding frames and that can give you a lifeline in tournaments."<ref name=":6">{{Cite web |date=1 April 2026 |title=Trump wins Murphy thriller to reach semis |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/april/01/Trump-Wins-Murphy-Thriller-To-Reach-Semis/ |access-date=1 April 2026 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260417070023/https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/april/01/Trump-Wins-Murphy-Thriller-To-Reach-Semis/|archive-date=17 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref><ref name=":7">{{Cite web |last=Millington |first=Adam |date=1 April 2026 |title=Tour Championship: Trump beats Murphy in final-frame decider to reach semis |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cwywv9l3p10o |access-date=1 April 2026 |website=[[BBC Sport]] |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260416160649/https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cwywv9l3p10o|archive-date=16 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

[[File:Neil Robertson 2025.jpg|thumb|The number one seed [[Neil Robertson]] ''(pictured in 2025)'' defeated [[Barry Hawkins]] 10–8 in the quarter-finals but lost 4–10 to [[Judd Trump]] in the semi-finals.]]

Hawkins played the number one seed [[Neil Robertson]], who had claimed back-to-back titles in [[2021 Tour Championship|2021]] and [[2022 Tour Championship|2022]]. The scores were tied at 2–2 and 3–3, but Robertson won frame seven after laying a snooker on the last brown and took frame eight with a 78 break to end the session 5–3 ahead.<ref name=":5" /><ref name=":7" /> In the second session, Hawkins won four consecutive frames to lead 7–5, but Robertson narrowed Hawkins' lead to one frame at 8–7. In frame 16, Hawkins was on a break of 55 when he missed a pot on the {{cuegloss|pink}}. Robertson recovered to win the frame after laying a snooker on the last red, tying the scores at 8–8, and went on to secure a 10–8 victory. "The frame to go 8–8 was massive," Robertson said afterwards. "I had a good tactical train of thought. I was thinking ahead and staying calm."<ref name=":8">{{Cite web |date=2 April 2026 |title=Robertson digs deep to beat Hawkins |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/april/02/Robertson-Digs-Deep-To-Beat-Hawkins/ |access-date=2 April 2026 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260417070020/https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/april/02/Robertson-Digs-Deep-To-Beat-Hawkins/|archive-date=17 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref><ref name=":9">{{Cite web |last=Sutcliffe |first=Steve |date=2 April 2026 |title=Higgins fights back to beat Selby and reach semis |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/c0mj84ypvjgo |access-date=2 April 2026 |website=[[BBC Sport]] |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260416160630/https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/c0mj84ypvjgo|archive-date=16 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref> Wakelin faced the reigning World Champion and number two seed [[Zhao Xintong]], winner of the season's two previous [[Players Series]] events, the [[2026 World Grand Prix (snooker)|2026 World Grand Prix]] and the [[2026 Players Championship]]. Zhao made centuries of 103 and 120 in the first session, ending it 5–3 ahead.<ref name=":6" /><ref name=":7" /> In the second session, Zhao won five of the six frames played, making three further centuries of 134, 108, and 101 as he completed a 10–4 victory, his first win at the Tour Championship.<ref name=":10">{{Cite web |date=2 April 2026 |title=Deja cue! Higgins beats Selby again |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/april/02/Deja-Cue--Higgins-Beats-Selby-Again/ |access-date=2 April 2026 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260417070033/https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/april/02/Deja-Cue--Higgins-Beats-Selby-Again/|archive-date=17 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref><ref name=":9" />

[[File:Mark Selby 2025 (2).jpg|thumb|The previous year's finalists, [[Mark Selby]] ''(pictured in 2025)'' and [[John Higgins]], met in the quarter-finals. As in the previous year's final, Selby won five consecutive {{cuegloss|frames}} to go 8–5 in front but Higgins won the next five for a 10–8 victory.]]

The defending champion Higgins won three consecutive frames as he took a 3–1 lead over the previous year's runner-up and number three seed [[Mark Selby]]. Selby recovered to tie the scores at 3–3, but Higgins won the last two frames of the session for a 5–3 advantage.<ref name=":8" /> In the second session, Selby took frame nine with a break of 127 and won the tenth after Higgins missed a pot on the second-last red along the {{Cuegloss|top cushion}}. Selby took the 11th frame when Higgins missed the blue after potting the last red, won the 12th after Higgins missed the penultimate red while playing the shot with the long {{Cuegloss|rest}}, and took the 13th with a century of 131 to lead 8–5. Higgins won the 14th frame on the colours and then made a 92 break to take the 15th. Selby led by 46 points in frame 16 when he missed a pot on the blue, and Higgins produced a clearance of 74 to tie the scores at 8–8. Higgins went on to make breaks of 78 and 72 as he secured a 10–8 victory. The match followed the same pattern as the previous year's final, in which Selby had also won five consecutive frames to lead 8–5, only to lose the last five frames. "At 8–5 I was just thinking back to last year's final and keeping positive thoughts," Higgins said afterwards. "The first four frames tonight, I wasn't really competing. Mark [Selby] gave me a couple of chances but I just felt my game wasn't there. But the game just spun around."<ref name=":10" /><ref name=":9" />

=== Semi-finals ===
[[File:John Higgins Masters 2026 1.jpg|thumb|The defending champion [[John Higgins]] ''(pictured in 2026)'' lost 1–10 to the reigning World Champion [[Zhao Xintong]]. It was Higgins's heaviest ever defeat in a best-of-19-frame match.]]
In the first semi-final, the world number one Trump played the number one seed Robertson. Trump made breaks of 87, 94, and 113 as he won the first three frames without Robertson scoring a point and led 4–0 at the mid-session interval. In frame five, Robertson was on a break of 52 when he missed the black off {{Cuegloss|black spot|its spot}}; Trump secured the frame and then won the sixth with breaks of 37 and 49 to go 6–0 ahead. Robertson took the seventh with a break of 61, but Trump made a 58 break to win frame eight, ending the first session with a 7–1 advantage.<ref>{{Cite web |date=3 April 2026 |title=Trump races ahead in Tour semi |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/april/03/Trump-Races-Ahead-in-Tour-Semi/ |access-date=3 April 2026 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260417070028/https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/april/03/Trump-Races-Ahead-in-Tour-Semi/|archive-date=17 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref> Robertson won the first two frames of the second session, but Trump took three of the next four to complete a 10–4 victory. "I have not played well in this event before so it's nice to come here this week to a great arena and put that right," Trump said afterwards. "I never expected it to be comfortable today but I got off to a really good start. After that I dropped my standard a bit but I had done all the damage." Robertson said that his play had been "garbage," adding: "When I get involved in safety I am just not good enough at this level to compete."<ref name=":11">{{Cite web |date=3 April 2026 |title=Trump hammers Robertson to reach Manchester final |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/april/03/trump-hammers-robertson-to-reach-manchester-final/ |access-date=3 April 2026 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260417070557/https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/april/03/trump-hammers-robertson-to-reach-manchester-final/|archive-date=17 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Sutcliffe |first=Steve |date=3 April 2026 |title=Tour Championship: Trump sweeps Robertson aside to reach Tour Championship final |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cgqkn2kzxqjo |access-date=3 April 2026 |website=[[BBC Sport]] |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260416160603/https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cgqkn2kzxqjo|archive-date=16 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

In the second semi-final, the defending champion Higgins played the reigning World Champion Zhao. It was Higgins's 93rd ranking semi-final, putting him three behind the record 96 played by [[Ronnie O'Sullivan]].<ref name=":10" /> Zhao made breaks including 69, 64, and 81 as he took a 4–0 lead at the mid-session interval. Zhao then produced further breaks of 74, 78, 62, and 104 as he completed a session [[whitewash (sport)|whitewash]] for an 8–0 lead.<ref>{{Cite web |date=4 April 2026 |title=Superb Zhao 8-0 up on Higgins |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/april/04/Superb-Zhao-8-0-Up-On-Higgins/ |access-date=4 April 2026 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en}}</ref> When play resumed, Zhao moved 9–0 ahead. While on a break of 41 in frame 10, Zhao missed the pink, and Higgins produced a 79 clearance to win his only frame of the match. Zhao completed a 10–1 victory with an 85 break in frame 11. It was Higgins's heaviest defeat in a best-of-19-frame match. "I played well and I am very happy to reach the final," said Zhao afterwards. "My {{cuegloss|cue|cueing}} was very good. I tried not to think too much; I just enjoyed the table and concentrated on the balls."<ref name=":12">{{Cite web |date=4 April 2026 |title=Zhao completes Higgins rout to set up Trump final |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/april/04/zhao-completes-higgins-rout-to-set-up-trump-final/ |access-date=4 April 2026 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260417070349/https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/april/04/zhao-completes-higgins-rout-to-set-up-trump-final/|archive-date=17 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Peter |first=Mike |date=4 April 2026 |title=Higgins thumped by Zhao in heaviest defeat of career |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cx24qr54vdwo |access-date=4 April 2026 |website=[[BBC Sport]] |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260416160407/https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cx24qr54vdwo|archive-date=16 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

=== Final ===
[[File:Zhao Xintong PHC 2016-2.jpg|thumb|[[Zhao Xintong]] ''(pictured in 2016)'' defeated [[Judd Trump]] 10–3, winning his sixth ranking title. He became the first player to win all three [[Players Series]] titles in the same season and advanced to a career high of fourth in the [[snooker world rankings|world rankings]].]]
The final took place on 5 April as the best of 19 frames, played over two sessions, between the reigning World Champion Zhao and the world number one Trump. It was the 53rd ranking final of Trump's career and the sixth of Zhao's. Both players reached the Tour Championship final for the first time. It was their sixth meeting in professional competition, Trump having won three of the previous five. It was the first time they had contested a final.<ref name=":11" /><ref name=":12" /><ref>{{Cite web |last=Day |first=Michael |date=5 April 2026 |title=2026 Tour Championship final preview: World champ v world number one in blockbuster |url=https://www.totallysnookered.com/sport/snooker/tournaments/tour-championship/2026-tour-championship-final-preview-world-champ-faces-world-number-one-in-manchester-blockbuster-6563817 |access-date=5 April 2026 |website=Totally Snookered |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260417070340/https://www.totallysnookered.com/sport/snooker/tournaments/tour-championship/2026-tour-championship-final-preview-world-champ-faces-world-number-one-in-manchester-blockbuster-6563817|archive-date=17 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

Zhao made a break of 64 in the opening frame to leave Trump {{cuegloss|snookers required|requiring snookers}}. Trump obtained the {{cuegloss|penalty points}} he needed, but Zhao won the frame after potting the last red and made a 68 break to win the second. Trump took the third with a 61 break and also won the fourth to tie the scores at 2–2. Zhao won frame five with breaks of 40 and 25 and then took frames six and seven with breaks of 91 and 98. Trump won frame eight to leave Zhao with a 5–3 advantage.<ref>{{Cite web |date=5 April 2026 |title=Zhao leads Trump in Manchester final |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/april/05/Zhao-Leads-Trump-In-Manchester-Final/ |access-date=5 April 2026 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260417070338/https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/april/05/Zhao-Leads-Trump-In-Manchester-Final/|archive-date=17 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref> When play resumed for the second session, Zhao won frame nine after doubling the last red and took frame 10 with breaks of 45 and 39. In frame 11, Zhao took the lead with a 63 break. Trump attempted a clearance but failed to gain position on the last red, and Zhao secured the frame to lead 8–3. Zhao went on to complete a 10–3 victory, winning his sixth ranking title and becoming the first player to win all three Players Series events—the [[World Grand Prix (snooker)|World Grand Prix]], [[Players Championship (snooker)|Players Championship]], and Tour Championship—in the same season. He became the third player, following Trump and O'Sullivan, to earn over £1 million in a single season, and he advanced to a career high of fourth place in the [[snooker world rankings|world rankings]].<ref name=":13">{{Cite web |date=5 April 2026 |title=Zhao beats Trump to complete Players Series slam |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/april/05/zhao-beats-trump-to-complete-players-series-slam/ |access-date=5 April 2026 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260417070334/https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/april/05/zhao-beats-trump-to-complete-players-series-slam/|archive-date=17 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Sutcliffe |first=Steve |date=5 April 2026 |title=Tour Championship: Zhao thrashes Trump to win Tour Championship |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cy41r7zwv9vo |access-date=5 April 2026 |website=[[BBC Sport]] |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260416160447/https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cy41r7zwv9vo|archive-date=16 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

Afterwards, Zhao said: "Judd [Trump] is my favourite player, I know he is very good. I am really happy just to play a final against him and I am lucky to win one. It's an amazing moment for my career. This win is very important for me, I didn't want to lose in the final. Next it's the [[2026 World Snooker Championship|World Championship]] and hopefully I can keep going." Trump commented: "I didn't perform today, for whatever reason. I'm just disappointed because the crowd spurred me on. Zhao has had an amazing tournament, he has been brilliant in these three [Players Series] events and won them all very convincingly."<ref name=":13" />
