The '''2025 [[PDC Pro Tour]]''' was a series of non-televised [[darts]] tournaments organised by the [[Professional Darts Corporation]] (PDC). [[PDC Pro Tour#Players Championships|Players Championships]] and [[PDC European Tour|European Tours]] are the events that make up the Pro Tour. The 2025 calendar consisted of 34 Players Championship events, 14 European Tour events, as well as 24 events for each of the PDC's [[PDC secondary tours|secondary tours]]—the [[2025 PDC Challenge Tour series|Challenge Tour]], [[2025 PDC Development Tour series|Development Tour]] and [[2025 PDC Women's Series|Women's Series]].<ref name="More ProTour">{{cite web |last=Allen |first=Dave |date=29 August 2024 |title=More darts than ever in 2025 as PDC calendar released |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2024/august/29/more-darts-ever-2025-pdc-calendar-released |access-date=29 August 2024 |publisher=PDC}}</ref><ref name=Sky2025Calendar>{{cite web |date=3 January 2025 |title=Darts 2025 schedule and dates: Premier League Darts, World Matchplay and more with Luke Littler among those in action |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13282459/darts-2025-schedule-and-dates-premier-league-darts-world-matchplay-and-more-with-luke-littler-among-those-in-action |publisher=[[Sky Sports]] |access-date=4 January 2025}}</ref><ref name=PlanetCalendar>{{cite web |last=Allen |first=Scott |date=6 January 2025 |title=2025 Darts Calendar: Every major televised tournament, dates, results and where to watch |url=https://www.planetsport.com/news/darts-2025-calendar-every-major-televised-tournament-dates-pdc-results-and-where-to-watch |website=Planet Sport |access-date=15 January 2025}}</ref><ref name=SLifeCalendar>{{cite web |date=22 January 2025 |title=PDC Darts 2025 season: Tournament calendar, fixtures and results |url=https://www.sportinglife.com/darts/news/pdc-darts-2025-season-tournament-calendar-fixtures-and-results/222906 |website=Sporting Life |access-date=23 January 2025}}</ref>

==PDC Tour Card==

128 players were granted Tour Cards, which enabled them to participate in all Players Championships events, the [[2025 UK Open|UK Open]], qualifiers for all European Tours, as well as the qualifiers for the [[2025 PDC World Masters|World Masters]], [[2025 World Series of Darts Finals|World Series Finals]], [[2025 Grand Slam of Darts|Grand Slam]] and the [[2026 PDC World Darts Championship|World Championship]].

The 128 '''2025 PDC Tour Cards''' were awarded to:<ref>{{cite web |date=14 January 2025 |title=The 128 PDC Tour Card holders in 2025: Who made it through qualifying school to earn their place alongside the world's elite? |url=https://www.sportinglife.com/darts/news/the-128-pdc-tour-card-holders-in-2025-who-made-it-through-qualifying-school-to-earn-their-place-alongside-the-worlds-elite/223087 |website=Sporting Life |access-date=15 January 2025}}</ref>

* (2) Two highest qualifiers from the [[2024 PDC Development Tour series|2024 Development Tour]] ([[Niko Springer]] and [[Sebastian Białecki]]).

====Q-School====
PDC Qualifying School (or Q-School) was split into two different events: '''UK Q-School''' for British and Irish players, held at [[Arena MK]] in [[Milton Keynes]], England;<ref>{{cite web |last=Wood |first=Kieran |date=6 January 2025 |title=List of participants confirmed for UK Q-School 2025 including Simon Whitlock, John Henderson, Mervyn King, Fallon Sherrock and Beau Greaves |url=https://dartsnews.com/pdc/list-of-participants-confirmed-for-uk-q-school-2025-including-simon-whitlock-john-henderson-mervyn-king-fallon-sherrock-and-beau-greaves |publisher=Darts News |access-date=12 January 2025}}</ref> and '''European Q-School''' for the rest of Europe, held at [[Wunderland Kalkar]] in [[Kalkar]], Germany.<ref>{{cite web |last=Wood |first=Kieran |date=5 January 2025 |title=These 498 players will compete for a PDC Tour Card at European Q-School 2025 |url=https://dartsnews.com/pdc/these-498-players-will-compete-for-a-pdc-tour-card-at-european-q-school-2025 |publisher=Darts News |access-date=4 January 2025}}</ref> Non-European players could choose which Q-School to compete in.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=24 September 2024 |title=2025 PDC Qualifying Schools entries now open |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2024/september/24/2025-pdc-qualifying-schools-entries-now-open |access-date=31 December 2024 |website=PDC}}</ref>
 
There are two stages in Q-School:<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=31 December 2024 |title=Entry deadline approaching for 2025 PDC Qualifying Schools |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2024/december/31/entry-deadline-approaching-2025-pdc-qualifying-schools |access-date=2 January 2025 |website=PDC}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |date=8 January 2025 |title=PDC Q School 2025: How Players Earn Their Spot on the Pro Tour |url=https://www.reddragondarts.com/blogs/news/pdc-q-school-2025-how-players-earn-their-spot-on-the-pro-tour |website=Red Dragon Darts |access-date=9 January 2025}}</ref>
* The '''First Stage''', played between 6–8 January,<ref>{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=6 January 2025 |title=Hopp, Brooks & McGuirk progress to Final Stage on Day One at PDC Q Schools |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/january/06/hopp-brooks-mcguirk-progress-final-stage-day-one-pdc-q-schools |publisher=[[Professional Darts Corporation]] |access-date=12 January 2025}}</ref> was entered by any darts player who registered to compete. The eight quarter-finalists from each of the three days qualified for the Final Stage alongside other players coming from a First Stage Order of Merit ranking to produce a full list of 128 players for each Final Stage. 
* The '''Final Stage''', held between 9–12 January, consisted of all players qualified from the First Stage plus the players exempted to the Final Stage; players who were exempted were those who lost their PDC Tour Cards after the [[2025 PDC World Darts Championship|2025 World Championship]], and those who finished from third to sixteenth in the [[2024 PDC Challenge Tour series|2024 Challenge Tour]] and [[2024 PDC Development Tour series|Development Tour]] Orders of Merit who did not have PDC Tour Cards yet.<ref name=Sky7Jan>{{cite web |date=6 January 2025 |title=PDC Tour Qualifying School: Fallon Sherrock, Noa-Lynn van Leuven both beaten early on day one of First Stage |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13284589/pdc-tour-qualifying-school-fallon-sherrock-noa-lynn-van-leuven-both-beaten-early-on-day-one-of-first-stage |website=[[Sky Sports]] |access-date=12 January 2025}}</ref>

The winner of each day's play in the Final Stage was given a PDC Tour Card.

==Players Championships==
{{Main|2025 PDC Players Championship series}}

The '''PDC Players Championships (PC)''' are a series of non-televised darts events that are part of the PDC Pro Tour. Starting in 2025, the number of events increased by four compared to previous years, bringing the total to 34 [[2025 PDC Players Championship series|Players Championship]] events per year.<ref name="More ProTour"/><ref name=Sky2025Calendar/><ref name=PlanetCalendar/><ref name=SLifeCalendar/>

The Players Championship Order of Merit is determined based on the total prize money won by each player in the Players Championship events throughout the year. The top 64 players on this ranking qualified for the [[2025 Players Championship Finals|Players Championship Finals]].

==European Tour==
{{further|PDC European Tour}}

The '''PDC European Tour (ET)''' is a series of darts events that are part of the PDC Pro Tour. In 2025, the total number of events expanded to fourteen, one more than the previous years.<ref>{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=28 August 2024 |title=PDC European Tour expanded to 14 events with 2025 dates confirmed |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2024/august/28/pdc-european-tour-expanded-14-events-2025-dates-confirmed |publisher=PDC |access-date=28 August 2024}}</ref>

In a change from the previous year, the top 16 on the two-year main [[PDC Order of Merit]] ranking were [[seeding (sports)|seeded]] for events, while the 16 qualifiers from the one-year Pro Tour Order of Merit ranking were unseeded.<ref>{{cite web |last=Shaw |first=Jamie |date=13 January 2025 |title=PDC confirm further controversial changes to European Tour criteria in 2025 |url=https://www.live-darts.com/pdc-darts-news/european-tour/pdc-confirm-further-changes-to-euro-tour-criteria/ |website=Live Darts |access-date=15 January 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |last=Gill |first=Samuel |date=13 January 2025 |title=PDC European Tour rules change again in controversial move seeming to protect big names |url=https://dartsnews.com/pdc/pdc-european-tour-rules-change-again-in-controversial-move-seeming-to-protect-big-names |website=Darts News |access-date=15 January 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250115135120/https://dartsnews.com/pdc/pdc-european-tour-rules-change-again-in-controversial-move-seeming-to-protect-big-names |archive-date=15 January 2025 |url-status=dead}}</ref>

The PDC European Tour Order of Merit is determined based on the total prize money won by each player in the European Tour events throughout the year. The top 32 players on this ranking qualified for the [[2025 European Championship (darts)|European Championship]].<ref>{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=28 August 2024 |title=PDC European Tour expanded to 14 events with 2025 dates confirmed |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2024/august/28/pdc-european-tour-expanded-14-events-2025-dates-confirmed |access-date=28 August 2024 |publisher=PDC}}</ref>

[[Nathan Aspinall]] finished the year as the number one player on the European Tour Order of Merit, with [[Martin Schindler]] second and [[Stephen Bunting]] third.<ref>{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=19 October 2025 |title=2025 Machineseeker European Championship field and draw confirmed |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/october/19/2025-machineseeker-european-championship-field-and-draw-confirmed |access-date=20 October 2025 |publisher=PDC}}</ref>
