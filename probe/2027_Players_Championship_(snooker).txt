The '''2027 Players Championship''' is an upcoming professional [[snooker]] tournament that will take place from 2 to 7 March 2027 at the Telford International Centre in [[Telford]], England. The top 16 players on the {{nowrap|one-year}} [[snooker world rankings|ranking list]], as it stands after the [[2027 World Grand Prix (snooker)|2027 World Grand Prix]], will qualify for the event. The 11th consecutive staging of the [[Players Championship (snooker)|Players Championship]] since it became a standalone tournament in [[2017 Players Championship (snooker)|2017]], it will be the 15th [[List of snooker ranking tournaments|ranking]] event of the [[2026–27 snooker season]], following the 2027 World Grand Prix and preceding the [[2027 World Open (snooker)|2027 World Open]]. It will be the second of three events in the [[Players Series]], following the 2027 World Grand Prix and preceding the [[2027 Tour Championship]].

[[Zhao Xintong]] is the defending champion, having defeated [[John Higgins]] 10–7 in the [[2026 Players Championship (snooker)|2026 final]].

==Overview==
The tournament originated as the final of the [[Players Tour Championship]], a former series of minor-ranking tournaments that was held in Europe and Asia from 2011 to 2016. In [[2017 Players Championship (snooker)|2017]], the event was rebranded as the [[Players Championship (snooker)|Players Championship]], a standalone tournament for the top 16 players on the one-year [[snooker world rankings|ranking list]]. The inaugural winner was [[Judd Trump]], who defeated [[Marco Fu]] 10–8 in the 2017 final.<ref>{{Cite web |date=13 March 2017 |title=Judd Trump triumphs at Players Championship |url=https://www.rte.ie/sport/snooker/2017/0313/859243-judd-trump/ |access-date=26 January 2026 |website=[[RTÉ Sport]] |language=en-GB |archive-date=5 April 2017 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170405070056/https://www.rte.ie/sport/snooker/2017/0313/859243-judd-trump/ |url-status=live }}</ref>
