The '''2026 Hungarian Darts Trophy''' was a professional [[darts]] tournament that took place at the [[MVM Dome]] in [[Budapest]], Hungary, from 28 to 30 August 2026.<ref>{{cite web |title=Hungarian Darts Trophy 2026 |url=https://www.pdc-europe.tv/tournaments/et-2026-en/hungarian-darts-trophy-2026/ |website=PDC Europe |access-date=2 July 2026}}</ref> It was the eleventh of fifteen [[PDC European Tour]] events on the [[2026 PDC Pro Tour]]. It featured a field of 48 players and £230,000 in prize money, with £35,000 going to the winner.

[[Beau Greaves]] became the first woman to compete on the European Tour, losing 6–5 to [[Rob Cross]] in the first round. [[Niko Springer]] was the defending champion, having defeated [[Danny Noppert]] 8–7 in the [[2025 Hungarian Darts Trophy|2025]] final.<ref name="BBC22">{{cite web |last=Fletcher |first=Alex |date=21 September 2025 |title=Springer beats Humphries on way to Hungarian title |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/darts/articles/cm2zzj488r3o |website=BBC Sport |access-date=2 July 2026}}</ref> However, he lost 6–4 to [[Ryan Searle (darts player)|Ryan Searle]] in the second round.

[[Ross Smith (darts player)|Ross Smith]] won his second European Tour title by defeating [[Gary Anderson (darts player)|Gary Anderson]] 8–2 in the final.

==Prize money==
As part of a mass boost in prize money for [[Professional Darts Corporation]] (PDC) events in 2026, the prize fund for all [[2026 PDC Pro Tour#European Tour#European Tour|2026 European Tour]] events rose to £230,000, of which the winner will receive £35,000.<ref>{{cite web |last=Phillips |first=Josh |date=31 March 2025 |title=Biggest prize money increase in PDC history confirmed |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/march/31/biggest-prize-money-increase-pdc-history-confirmed |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=1 March 2026}}</ref>

== Qualification and format ==
The top 16 players on the two-year [[PDC Order of Merit]] were [[Seeding (sports)|seeded]] and entered the tournament in the second round, while the next 16 highest-ranked players from the one-year [[PDC Pro Tour Order of Merit]] automatically qualified for the first round. The seedings were confirmed on 2 July.<ref>{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=2 July 2026 |title=2026 ET11-12 Seeds & Tour Card Holder qualifier entries confirmed |url=https://pdc.tv/news/2026/july/02/2026-et11-12-seeds-tour-card-holder-qualifier-entries-confirmed/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=2 July 2026}}</ref> The remaining 16 places went to players from four qualifying events – 10 from the Tour Card Holder Qualifier (held on 8 July),<ref>{{cite web |date=8 July 2026 |title=PDC ET11 Tour Card Holder Qualifier |url=https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdceutcq26et11/matches |website=DartConnect |access-date=2 July 2026}}</ref> four from the Host Nation Qualifier (held on 25 July),<ref>{{cite web |date=25 July 2026 |title=PDC ET11 Host Nation Qualifier |url=https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdc26et11hnq/matches |website=DartConnect |access-date=2 July 2026}}</ref> one from the Nordic & Baltic Associate Member Qualifier (held on 15 May),<ref>{{cite web |date=24 April 2026 |title=PDCNB 2026 Tour - Sweden - Weekend 04 |url=https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdcnbpro26e04/matches |website=DartConnect |access-date=2 July 2026}}</ref> and one from the East European Associate Member Qualifier (held on 4 July).<ref>{{cite web |date=4 July 2026 |title=PDC ET11 E. Europe Qualifier |url=https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdceeu26et11q/matches |website=DartConnect |access-date=2 July 2026}}</ref>

[[Beau Greaves]] became the first woman to qualify for a European Tour event by claiming one of the ten spots in the Tour Card Holder Qualifier.<ref>{{cite web |date=9 July 2026 |title=Greaves first woman to reach European Tour event |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/darts/articles/c862x00927po |website=BBC Sport |access-date=16 July 2026}}</ref>

[[Gerwyn Price]] withdrew and was replaced by [[Callan Rydz]]. [[Luke Woodhouse]] moved up to become the 16th seed. Fifth seed [[James Wade]] withdrew after the draw was made and was replaced by [[Madars Razma]].<ref name="Draw">{{cite web |last=Gorton|first=Josh |date=27 August 2026 |title=2026 Hungarian Darts Trophy draw & schedule confirmed |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/august/27/2026-hungarian-darts-trophy-draw-schedule-confirmed/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=27 August 2026}}</ref>

==Summary==
===First round===
[[File:2025-04-03 Premier League Darts Berlin 2025 by Sandro Halank–088.jpg|thumb|The [[2018 PDC World Darts Championship|2018]] world champion [[Rob Cross]] ''(pictured in 2025)'' won his first-round match 6–5 against [[Beau Greaves]], the first woman to compete on the European Tour.]]
The first round (best of 11 [[Leg (darts)|legs]]) was played on 28 August.<ref name="PDC_R1">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=28 August 2026 |title=Cross denies Greaves debut glory at Hungarian Darts Trophy |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/august/28/cross-denies-greaves-debut-glory-hungarian-darts-trophy/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=29 August 2026}}</ref> Debutant [[Beau Greaves]] became the first woman to compete on the European Tour, being drawn against the [[2018 PDC World Darts Championship|2018]] world champion [[Rob Cross]]. Cross hit a [[Glossary of darts#Maximum check|170 checkout]] in the opening leg before the pair went to a [[Glossary of darts#Decider|deciding leg]], where two errant darts from Greaves while attempting a 70 finish allowed Cross to win 6–5. Cross and Greaves ended the match with [[three-dart average]]s of 101.90 and 104.88, respectively. "She [Greaves] has been phenomenal on the [[2026 PDC Pro Tour|Pro Tour]], and I think when she learns her trade on the big stage, she's going to do more," said Cross about his opponent.<ref name="Sky_R1">{{cite web |date=29 August 2026 |title=Hungarian Darts Trophy: Beau Greaves just loses out to Rob Cross in last-leg decider on European Tour debut |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13578173/hungarian-darts-trophy-beau-greaves-just-loses-out-to-rob-cross-in-last-leg-decider-on-european-tour-debut |website=Sky Sports |access-date=29 August 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |last=Telfer |first=Alastair |date=28 August 2026 |title=Greaves loses thriller to Cross on European debut |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/darts/articles/czjz78lwjzzo |website=BBC Sport |access-date=29 August 2026}}</ref> Two of the four Hungarian qualifiers advanced to the second round, with debutant [[László Kádár]] beating [[Daryl Gurney]] and [[Nándor Major]] defeating [[Niels Zonneveld]], both by a 6–5 scoreline. [[Andrew Gilding]] earned a [[Whitewash (sport)|whitewash]] win over Hungarian debutant Milán Biró, while Biró's compatriot András Borbély lost 6–3 to [[2023 Hungarian Darts Trophy|2023]] champion [[Dave Chisnall]].<ref>{{cite web |last=Wiszt |first=Péter |date=29 August 2026 |title=Az egyetlen nő minden férfit lekörözött Budapesten, semmit nem ért vele |url=https://magyarnemzet.hu/sport/2026/08/beau-greaves-hungarian-darts-trophy-major-nandor-kadar |website=Magyar Nemzet |access-date=29 August 2026 |language=hu}}</ref> The [[2022 Hungarian Darts Trophy|2022]] champion [[Joe Cullen (darts player)|Joe Cullen]] joined fellow former champion Chisnall in the second round by defeating [[Adam Leek]] 6–3.<ref name="Sky_R1"/>

Defending champion [[Niko Springer]] started his title defence with a 6–1 victory over [[Maik Kuivenhoven]]. "I had a big smile because I was so happy to be here again, and I cannot wait to play tomorrow," commented Springer afterwards.<ref name="PDC_R1"/> Finnish qualifier Jonas Masalin capitalised on 31 missed [[Glossary of darts#Double|doubles]] from opponent [[Kevin Doets]] to earn a 6–4 upset win. [[Dirk van Duijvenbode]] defeated Czech qualifier David Písek 6–1 to set up a second-round meeting with top seed [[Luke Humphries]]. [[Callan Rydz]] missed two [[Glossary of darts#Match dart|match darts]] in a 6–5 loss to [[2026 European Darts Open|European Darts Open]] champion [[Krzysztof Ratajski]], while [[James Hurrell]] earned his first win on the European Tour by defeating [[Damon Heta]] by the same scoreline. Irish duo [[Keane Barry]] and [[William O'Connor (darts player)|William O'Connor]] achieved respective 6–2 wins against [[Martin Schindler]] and Samuel Price. [[Tom Sykes (darts player)|Tom Sykes]] averaged 101.20 as he beat [[Cameron Menzies]] 6–1. The [[2024 European Championship (darts)|2024 European Championship]] winner [[Ritchie Edhouse]] won six straight legs to defeat [[Connor Scutt]] 6–1, while [[Ryan Joyce]] beat [[Cristo Reyes]] 6–3.<ref name="PDC_R1"/>

===Second round===
[[File:2026-03-26 Premier League Darts – Night 8 – Berlin 2026 by Sandro Halank–059.jpg|thumb|Three-time world champion [[Michael van Gerwen]] ''(pictured in 2026)'', who won the [[2024 Hungarian Darts Trophy|2024 edition]], came back from 3–1 down to defeat [[James Hurrell]] 6–4 in his opening match.]]
The second round (best of 11 legs) was played on 29 August.<ref name="PDC_R2">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=29 August 2026 |title=Van Duijvenbode dumps out Humphries to reach Finals Day in Budapest |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/august/29/van-duijvenbode-dumps-out-humphries-reach-finals-day-budapest/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=29 August 2026}}</ref> Top seed and world number two Luke Humphries, who made his first European Tour appearance since winning the [[2026 Belgian Darts Open]], was defeated in his opening match by Dirk van Duijvenbode, who landed a 140 [[Glossary of darts#Checkout|checkout]] on his way to a 6–3 victory.<ref name="BBC_R2">{{cite web |last=Telfer |first=Alastair |date=29 August 2026 |title=Humphries suffers early Hungarian Darts Trophy exit |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/darts/articles/cwyzd117xv4o |website=BBC Sport |access-date=29 August 2026}}</ref><ref name="Sky_R2">{{cite web |last=Hodge |first=Stuart |date=29 August 2026 |title=Hungarian Darts Trophy: Luke Humphries suffers shock defeat to Dirk van Duijvenbode as Michael van Gerwen delivers comeback win |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13578830/hungarian-darts-trophy-luke-humphries-suffers-shock-defeat-to-dirk-van-duijvenbode-as-michael-van-gerwen-delivers-comeback-win |website=Sky Sports |access-date=29 August 2026}}</ref> Ending a nine-match losing streak against the former world champion, Van Duijvenbode expressed his happiness with life after "a lot of struggles" and called Humphries a "phenomenal player".<ref name="Sky_R2"/> The [[2024 Hungarian Darts Trophy]] champion and three-time world champion [[Michael van Gerwen]] overturned a 3–1 deficit to beat James Hurrell 6–4.<ref name="BBC_R2"/> Hungary's László Kádár and Nándor Major both exited the tournament in the second round, losing their respective matches 6–3 to [[Jonny Clayton]] and 6–0 to [[Gian van Veen]].<ref>{{cite web |date=29 August 2026 |title=Hungarian Darts Trophy - Elmaradt az újabb bravúr, Kádár és Major is kiesett szombaton |trans-title=Hungarian Darts Trophy - Another feat failed, Kádár and Major both eliminated on Saturday |url=https://www.eurosport.hu/darts/hungarian-darts-trophy-kadar-major-kiesett_sto23332400/story.shtml |website=Eurosport |access-date=29 August 2026 |language=hu}}</ref> Reigning champion Niko Springer was also eliminated, losing 6–4 to [[Ryan Searle (darts player)|Ryan Searle]].<ref name="PDC_R2"/>

Taken to his second last-leg decider of the tournament, Rob Cross converted a 130 checkout to complete a 6–5 win against sixteenth seed [[Luke Woodhouse]], who had missed darts at double to win the match. Two-time world champion [[Gary Anderson (darts player)|Gary Anderson]] registered his first win on the European Tour in 2026, averaging 105.27 as he beat Krzysztof Ratajski 6–2. [[Ross Smith (darts player)|Ross Smith]] and Dave Chisnall were also 6–2 winners, averaging over 100 to defeat William O'Connor and twelfth seed [[Wessel Nijman]], respectively. [[Chris Dobey]] came back from 4–0 down to beat Tom Sykes in a deciding leg, while [[Josh Rock]] achieved a whitewash victory over Joe Cullen. Reserve player [[Madars Razma]] hit a 158 checkout on the [[Bullseye (target)|bullseye]] before losing 6–2 to Keane Barry, who reached the last 16 of a European Tour event for the first time since 2023. [[Nathan Aspinall]], [[Danny Noppert]] and [[Jermaine Wattimena]] earned respective 6–4 wins against Ryan Joyce, Ritchie Edhouse, and Andrew Gilding, while [[Stephen Bunting]] defeated Jonas Masalin 6–1.<ref name="PDC_R2"/><ref name="Sky_R2"/>

===Final day===
[[File:Ross Smith 2024 World Matchplay.png|thumb|[[Ross Smith (darts player)|Ross Smith]] ''(pictured in 2024)'' won his second European Tour title of 2026, defeating [[Gary Anderson (darts player)|Gary Anderson]] 8–2 in the final.]]
The third round, quarter-finals, semi-finals and final were played on 30 August. The third round and quarter-finals were contested over the best of 11 legs, the semi-finals over the best of 13 legs, and the final over the best of 15 legs.<ref name="PDC_Final">{{cite web |last=Simpson |first=Will |date=30 August 2026 |title=Smith routs Anderson to win Hungarian Darts Trophy |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/august/30/Smith-routs-Anderson-to-win-Hungarian-Darts-Trophy/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=30 August 2026}}</ref> The final day saw Ross Smith and Gary Anderson reach the final. Smith began the day by defeating fourth seed Jonny Clayton 6–4, following that win by beating Dave Chisnall 6–2 and Danny Noppert 7–3.<ref name="Sky_Final">{{cite web |last=Hodge |first=Stuart |date=30 August 2026 |title=Hungarian Darts Trophy: Ross Smith defeats Gary Anderson as Scot's fairy-tale run ends in final |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13579117/hungarian-darts-trophy-ross-smith-defeats-gary-anderson-as-scots-fairy-tale-run-ends-in-final |website=Sky Sports |access-date=31 August 2026}}</ref> Meanwhile, Gary Anderson contested three consecutive last-leg deciders to reach the final. He secured 6–5 wins against Stephen Bunting and Gian van Veen, before surviving five match darts from Nathan Aspinall to prevail 7–6 in the semi-finals.<ref name="24hu">{{cite web |date=30 August 2026 |title=A közönségkedvenc is fejet hajtott, új győztese van a Hungarian Darts Trophynak |trans-title=The crowd favourite has also bowed his head, the Hungarian Darts Trophy has a new winner |url=https://24.hu/sport/2026/08/30/hungarian-darts-trophy-ross-smith-gary-anderson/ |website=24.hu |access-date=31 August 2026 |language=hu |agency=Magyar Távirati Iroda}}</ref> Chris Dobey also hit a 170 finish to complete a 6–3 win against Josh Rock in the third round.<ref name="Sky_Final"/> Smith looked to win his second European Tour title in his third final of the year, while Anderson played in his first European Tour final of 2026 in only his third appearance all year.<ref name="PDC_Final"/>

Smith went 2–1 in front during the early proceedings of the final, landing checkouts of 124 and 103.<ref name="PDC_Final"/> He extended this lead to 6–1 to take a five-leg advantage.<ref name="24hu"/> Anderson put a halt to Smith's run in the following leg, but the Englishman claimed the next two to secure an 8–2 win, completing the victory with an 11-dart [[Glossary of darts#Break|break of throw]].<ref name="PDC_Final"/><ref>{{cite web |last=Thomson |first=Milly |date=30 August 2026 |title=The first time was so nice Ross Smith had to do it twice, seals 2026 European Tour double with Gary Anderson thrashing |url=https://dartsnews.com/pdc/hungarian-darts-trophy-round-up-the-first-time-was-so-nice-ross-smith-had-to-do-it-twice-seals-2026-european-tour-double-with-gary-anderson-thrashing |website=dartsnews.com |access-date=31 August 2026}}</ref> Smith finished the match with a three-dart average of 101.01 and hit 73 per cent of his darts at double.<ref name="Sky_Final"/>

Smith won his second European Tour title, adding to his first at the [[2026 International Darts Open|International Darts Open]] earlier in the year. Smith rose to 15th in the [[PDC World Rankings]], having started the tournament as the world number 17, and overtook Wessel Nijman to occupy the top spot in the 2026 European Tour rankings.<ref name="PDC_Final"/><ref name="24hu"/> Smith said that he felt "a bit in disbelief" with the win, which came after two losses on the [[2026 World Series of Darts|World Series]]. Celebrating with his son afterwards, he expressed his love for the European Tour: "To travel the world and see everything and take my little lad with me, it’s an absolute privilege."<ref name="PDC_Final"/><ref name="24hu"/> Speaking in defeat, Anderson believed his run to the final was "sheer luck" and "a gift from the gods", also highlighting Smith's "brilliant" performances throughout the day.<ref name="PDC_Final"/>
