{{Short description|Darts tournament series}}
The '''2025 PDC Players Championship series''' was a series of 34 non-televised [[darts]] tournaments on the [[2025 PDC Pro Tour]].<ref name="More ProTour">{{cite web |last=Allen |first=Dave |date=29 August 2024 |title=More darts than ever in 2025 as PDC calendar released |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2024/August/29/more-darts-ever-2025-pdc-calendar-released |access-date=29 August 2024 |publisher=PDC}}{{dead link|date=February 2026|bot=medic}}{{cbignore|bot=medic}}</ref><ref name=Sky2025Calendar>{{cite web |date=3 January 2025 |title=Darts 2025 schedule and dates: Premier League Darts, World Matchplay and more with Luke Littler among those in action |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13282459/darts-2025-schedule-and-dates-premier-league-darts-world-matchplay-and-more-with-luke-littler-among-those-in-action |publisher=[[Sky Sports]] |access-date=4 January 2025}}</ref><ref name=PlanetCalendar>{{cite web |last=Allen |first=Scott |date=6 January 2025 |title=2025 Darts Calendar: Every major televised tournament, dates, results and where to watch |url=https://www.planetsport.com/news/darts-2025-calendar-every-major-televised-tournament-dates-pdc-results-and-where-to-watch |website=Planet Sport |access-date=15 January 2025}}</ref><ref name=SLifeCalendar>{{cite web |date=22 January 2025 |title=PDC Darts 2025 season: Tournament calendar, fixtures and results |url=https://www.sportinglife.com/darts/news/pdc-darts-2025-season-tournament-calendar-fixtures-and-results/222906 |website=Sporting Life |access-date=23 January 2025}}</ref> The Players Championship Order of Merit, a ranking list that only includes the prize money won by each player in the Players Championship events, determined the field of 64 participants for the [[2025 Players Championship Finals]] in November.

The number of events increased by four compared to the four previous years, bringing the total to 34 Players Championship events.

A total of 33 [[nine-dart finish]]es were hit during the series.

==Prize money==
The prize money remained the same as the previous year, with a prize fund for each event of £125,000; £15,000 is given to each event‘s winner.
