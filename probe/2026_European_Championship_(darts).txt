The '''2026 European Championship''' (known for sponsorship reasons as the '''2026 Machineseeker European Championship''') was a professional [[darts]] tournament held at the [[Westfalenhallen]] in [[Dortmund]], Germany, from 22 to 25 October 2026. It was the 19th staging of the [[European Championship (darts)|European Championship]] by the [[Professional Darts Corporation]] (PDC). The total prize fund was £750,000, with the winner receiving £150,000.

The tournament featured the top 32 players based on ranking money earned in the 15 [[PDC European Tour|European Tour]] events that were held as part of the [[2026 PDC Pro Tour]]. [[Gian van Veen]] was the defending champion, having defeated [[Luke Humphries]] 11–10 in the [[2025 European Championship (darts)|2025 final]], but he lost 10–6 to [[Luke Woodhouse]] in the second round. Woodhouse also hit a [[nine-dart finish]] in the fourth leg of his first round match against [[Dirk van Duijvenbode]]. 

[[Kevin Doets]] and [[Niels Zonneveld]] made their debuts at the tournament.

[[Ross Smith (darts player)|Ross Smith]] won the tournament, his third major title and second European Championship, by defeating [[Martin Schindler]] 11–8 in the final. Schindler also became the first German player to reach the final of a PDC major.

==Qualification==
The [[2026 PDC Pro Tour#European Tour|2026 European Tour]] was held as part of the [[2026 PDC Pro Tour]] and consisted of 15 events. Following the final event of the season, the [[2026 Dutch Darts Championship|Dutch Darts Championship]], the top 32 players on the European Tour Order of Merit qualified for the European Championship. [[Ross Smith (darts player)|Ross Smith]] entered the tournament as the top [[seeding (sports)|seed]] after winning two titles during the season: the [[2026 International Darts Open|International Darts Open]] and the [[2026 Hungarian Darts Trophy|Hungarian Darts Trophy]].

12th seed & 2025 runner-up [[Luke Humphries]] withdrew from the tournament after the draw with illness. 33rd ranked [[Kim Huybrechts]] replaced him directly in the draw.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Simpson |first1=Will |title=Humphries withdraws from Machineseeker European Championship |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/october/22/humphries-withdraws-from-machineseeker-european-championship/ |website=www.pdc.tv |access-date=22 October 2026}}</ref>
