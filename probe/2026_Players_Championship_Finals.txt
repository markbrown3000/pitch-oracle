The '''2026 Players Championship Finals''' (known for sponsorship reasons as the '''2026 Ladbrokes Players Championship Finals''') is a professional [[darts]] tournament that is scheduled to take place at the [[Butlin's Minehead|Butlin's Resort]] in [[Minehead]], England, from 27 to 29 November 2026. It will be the 19th staging of the [[Players Championship Finals]] by the [[Professional Darts Corporation]] (PDC). The total prize fund will be an increased total of £750,000, with the winner receiving £130,000.

Sponsored by [[Ladbrokes]], the tournament will be the last PDC event before the [[2027 PDC World Darts Championship]]. It will feature the top 64 players based on ranking money earned in the 34 [[2026 PDC Players Championship series|Players Championship events]] that were held as part of the [[2026 PDC Pro Tour]].

[[Luke Littler]] was the reigning champion, having defeated [[Nathan Aspinall]] 11–8 in the [[2025 Players Championship Finals|2025 final]]. However, Littler failed to qualify for the tournament and will therefore not defend his title.

== Overview ==
=== Background ===
The 2026 [[Players Championship Finals]] will take place from 27 to 29 November 2026 at the [[Butlin's Minehead|Butlin's Resort]] in [[Minehead]], England.<ref name="MC26">{{cite web |title=Players Championship Finals 2026 |url=https://mastercaller.com/tournaments/players-championship-finals/2026 |website=Mastercaller |access-date=1 November 2026}}</ref> British gambling company [[Ladbrokes]] continued its sponsorship of the event after becoming title sponsor in 2024.<ref>{{cite web |last=Donaldson |first=Alex |date=13 February 2024 |title=PDC announces pair of event sponsorships with Ladbrokes |url=https://www.sportcal.com/sponsorship/pdc-announces-pair-of-event-sponsorships-with-ladbrokes/ |website=Sportcal |access-date=1 November 2026}}</ref> [[Luke Littler]] won the [[2025 Players Championship Finals|2025 final]] by defeating [[Nathan Aspinall]] 11–8 to win the title for the first time.<ref>{{cite web |last=Rajan |first=Adwaidh |date=23 November 2025 |title=Littler 'ticks off' first Players Championship win |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/darts/articles/cx2d0dv418lo |website=BBC Sport |access-date=24 November 2025}}</ref>

=== Format ===
The 64-player field will be [[Seeding (sport)|seeded]] based on the Players Championship Order of Merit, a ranking comprising prize money won in Players Championship events during the year. All matches will be in [[Leg (darts)|leg]] play format, with the number of legs required to win increasing as the tournament progresses.

*'''First round and second round:''' Best of 11 legs
*'''Third round and quarter-finals:''' Best of 19 legs
*'''Semi-finals and final:''' Best of 21 legs

== Qualification ==
The [[2026 PDC Players Championship series|2026 Players Championship series]] is being held as part of the [[2026 PDC Pro Tour]] and consists of 34 events. Following the final event of the season, the top 64 players on the Players Championship Order of Merit will qualify for the tournament.

Reigning champion [[Luke Littler]] failed to finish inside the top 64 of the Players Championship Order of Merit after withdrawing from the final Players Championship events of the season, meaning he will not defend his title.<ref>{{cite web |date=9 October 2026 |title=Luke Littler: World champion can still qualify for Players Championship Finals – but will he play, and what else can he win? |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/13596082/luke-littler-world-champion-can-still-qualify-for-players-championship-finals-but-will-he-play-and-what-else-can-he-win |website=Sky Sports |access-date=1 November 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |last=Wood |first=Kieran |date=31 October 2026 |title=Entries Players Championship 33 & 34: Luke Littler absent from double-header as Gary Anderson, Josh Rock and Nathan Aspinall fight for last chance Minehead qualification |url=https://dartsnews.com/pdc/entires-players-championship-33-34-luke-littler-absent-from-double-header-as-gary-anderson-josh-rock-and-nathan-aspinall-fight-for-last-chance-minehead-qualification |website=dartsnews.com |access-date=1 November 2026}}</ref>

There was no draw held as all players were put in a fixed bracket by their seeding positions. Numbers to the left of a player's name show the seedings for the 64 players in the tournament. Figures to the right of a player's name state their [[three-dart average]] in a match. Players in bold denote match winners.
