The '''2025 PDC World Masters''' (known for sponsorship reasons as the '''2025 Winmau World Masters''') was a professional [[darts]] tournament that took place at the [[Arena MK]] in [[Milton Keynes]], England, from 30 January to 2 February 2025. The preliminary rounds of the tournament were held at the Marshall Arena on 29 January 2025. It was the 13th [[PDC World Masters|Masters]] to be organised by the [[Professional Darts Corporation]] and the first under the World Masters rebrand. It was the first time the tournament was ranked and was using [[Set (darts)|set]] play.

[[Stephen Bunting]] was the defending champion after beating [[Michael van Gerwen]] 11–7 in the [[2024 Masters (darts)|2024 final]]. However, he lost 4–3 to [[Danny Noppert]] in the quarter-finals.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Mills |first1=Stephen |title=Luke Littler out of World Masters Darts after 4-2 defeat to Jonny Clayton, as Stephen Bunting also exits |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12040/13301874/luke-littler-out-of-world-masters-darts-after-4-2-defeat-to-jonny-clayton-as-stephen-bunting-also-exits |website=[[Sky Sports]] |access-date=2 February 2025 |date=2 February 2025}}</ref> [[Luke Humphries]] won the tournament, defeating [[Jonny Clayton]] 6–5 in the final.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Keogh |first1=Frank |last2=Higham |first2=Paul |title=Humphries survives Clayton comeback to win epic Masters |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/darts/articles/cr46kv0ggl9o |website=[[BBC Sport]] |access-date=2 February 2025 |date=2 February 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |author1=Pa Sport Staff |title=Luke Humphries beats Jonny Clayton in last-leg decider to win World Masters |url=https://www.independent.co.uk/sport/luke-humphries-luke-littler-australian-michael-van-gerwen-b2690883.html |website=[[The Independent]] |access-date=2 February 2025 |date=2 February 2025}}</ref>

[[Dimitri Van den Bergh]] hit a [[nine-dart finish]] during his second round victory over [[Michael van Gerwen]].<ref>{{cite web |last=Wellens |first=Megan  |date=2 February 2025 |title=World Masters darts: Luke Littler whitewashes James Wade to book quarter-final spot as Dimitri Van den Bergh hits nine-darter in win over Michael van Gerwen |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13301165/world-masters-darts-luke-littler-whitewashes-james-wade-to-book-quarter-final-spot-as-dimitri-van-den-bergh-hits-nine-darter-in-win-over-michael-van-gerwen |website=[[Sky Sports]] |access-date=2 February 2025}}</ref>

==Overview==

=== Background ===
The inaugural edition of the [[PDC World Masters|Masters]], the [[2013 Masters (darts)|2013 Masters]], was held by the [[Professional Darts Corporation]] (PDC) from 1–3 November 2013 at the [[Royal Highland Centre]] in [[Edinburgh]], Scotland and was broadcast live on [[ITV4]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=14 June 2013 |title=PDC & ITV Sign New Four-Event Deal |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2013/06/14/pdc-itv-sign-new-four-event-deal |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250129132814/https://www.pdc.tv/news/2013/06/14/pdc-itv-sign-new-four-event-deal |url-status=dead |archive-date=29 January 2025 |access-date=10 January 2025 |website=[[Professional Darts Corporation]]}}</ref> [[Phil Taylor (darts player)|Phil Taylor]] won the first final, defeating [[Adrian Lewis]] 10–1.<ref>{{Cite web |date=4 November 2013 |title=Masters: Phil Taylor thrashes Adrian Lewis in all-Stoke final |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/darts/24804052 |access-date=10 January 2025 |website=[[BBC Sport]]}}</ref> Since 2015, the tournament has been held in [[Milton Keynes]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=2 February 2015 |title=Michael van Gerwen beats Van Barneveld to win first Masters title |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/darts/31092634 |access-date=10 January 2025 |website=[[BBC Sport]]}}</ref> The tournament initially featured the top 16 players in the world before increasing to the top 24 in 2021.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Allen |first=Dave |date=5 January 2021 |title=Ladbrokes Masters expands to 24 players in 2021 |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2021/01/05/ladbrokes-masters-expands-24-players-2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210105152529/https://www.pdc.tv/news/2021/01/05/ladbrokes-masters-expands-24-players-2021 |url-status=dead |archive-date=5 January 2021 |access-date=10 January 2025 |website=[[Professional Darts Corporation]]}}</ref>

In August 2024, it was announced that for the 2025 event, the field would expand to 32 players and it would be the first edition where the tournament was ranked.<ref>{{cite web |last=Allen |first=Dave |date=29 August 2024 |title=The Masters expands to 32-player ranked event in 2025 |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2024/august/29/masters-expands-32-player-ranked-event-2025 |access-date=31 December 2024 |website=[[Professional Darts Corporation]]}}</ref> However, this preceded a bigger announcement that was made on 28 October. The PDC announced a complete revamp of the competition, rebranding it from the Masters to the World Masters (sponsored by [[Winmau]]), emulating the [[World Masters (darts)|World Masters]] which was first held in 1975 and organised by the [[British Darts Organisation]] and later the [[World Darts Federation]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Phillips |first=Josh |date=28 October 2024 |title=Winmau World Masters to return as PDC event undergoes radical rebrand |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2024/october/28/winmau-world-masters-return-pdc-event-undergoes-radical-rebrand |access-date=31 December 2024 |website=PDC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Shaw |first=Jamie |date=28 October 2024 |title=Winmau World Masters to return in 2025 as PDC rebrand historic tournament |url=https://www.live-darts.com/pdc-darts-news/the-masters/winmau-world-masters-to-return-in-2025/ |access-date=10 January 2025 |website=Live Darts}}</ref> The revamp saw a change in format from [[Leg (darts)|leg]] play to classic World Masters [[Set (darts)|set]] play (best of 3 legs in a set), and saw preliminary rounds introduced for players outside of the top 24 and players from the PDC's affiliated tours to determine who would join the top 24 in the main competition.

The 2025 event was held from 30 January to 2 February at the [[Marshall Arena]] in [[Milton Keynes]], England.<ref name="Sky2025Calendar">{{cite web |date=3 January 2025 |title=Darts 2025 schedule and dates: Premier League Darts, World Matchplay and more with Luke Littler among those in action |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13282459/darts-2025-schedule-and-dates-premier-league-darts-world-matchplay-and-more-with-luke-littler-among-those-in-action |access-date=5 January 2025 |publisher=[[Sky Sports]]}}</ref> The defending champion was [[Stephen Bunting]], who won his first PDC televised title by defeating [[Michael van Gerwen]] 11–7 in the [[2024 Masters (darts)|2024 final]].<ref name=SkyDraw>{{cite web |date=21 January 2025 |title=Winmau World Masters darts: Schedule, draw, results, format as Luke Littler, Luke Humphries headline 2025 edition |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13293835/winmau-world-masters-darts-schedule-draw-results-format-as-luke-littler-luke-humphries-headline-2025-edition |access-date=22 January 2025 |publisher=[[Sky Sports]]}}</ref><ref name="BuntingMastersBBC">{{cite web |date=5 February 2024 |title=Cazoo Masters: Stephen Bunting beats Michael van Gerwen to win maiden televised PDC title |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/darts/68202432 |access-date=4 January 2025 |website=[[BBC Sport]]}}</ref>

===Format===
All matches were played as straight in (player begins scoring with their first throw no matter what section is hit), double out (a segment on the outer ring or the [[Bullseye (target)|bullseye]]), requiring the players to score 501 points to win a [[Leg (darts)|leg]], finishing on a double. The matches were played in [[Set (darts)|set]] format, with a minimum of two sets required to win a match. All sets were played to the best of three legs.<ref name="SetsFormat">{{cite web |last=Gill |first=Samuel |date=25 December 2024 |title=Format confirmed for 2025 Winmau World Masters including much-loved sets returning |url=https://dartsnews.com/pdc/format-confirmed-for-2025-winmau-world-masters-including-much-loved-sets-returning |access-date=31 December 2024 |publisher=Darts News}}</ref><ref name=SkyDraw/>

==Preliminary rounds==
[[File:Marshall Arena Milton Keynes 6 July 2020 (cropped).jpg|right|thumb|The preliminary rounds and main tournament were held at the [[Marshall Arena]] in [[Milton Keynes]], England.]]
The preliminary rounds began with a group stage, with one seeded player per group (the players ranked 57–88 were seeded). The winner of each group progressed to the last 64 where they faced players ranked 25–56. The eight winners of the last 16 matches qualified for the main tournament.<ref name=EntriesConfirmed>{{cite web |last=Allen |first=Dave |date=27 January 2025 |title=Entries confirmed for 2025 Winmau World Masters Preliminary Rounds |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/january/27/entries-confirmed-2025-winmau-world-masters-preliminary-rounds |website=[[Professional Darts Corporation]] |access-date=27 January 2025}}</ref>

[[Raymond van Barneveld]] (ranked world number thirty-four) did not enter the preliminary rounds. [[Dylan Slevin]] therefore received a bye to the last 64.
