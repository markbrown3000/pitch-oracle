The '''2026 Wuhan Open''' (officially the '''2026 Optics Valley of China Wuhan Open''') was a professional [[snooker]] tournament that took place from 23 to 29 August 2026 at the [[Optics Valley of China|Optics Valley Gymnasium]], [[Huazhong University of Science and Technology]] in [[Wuhan]], China. The qualifiers took place from 15 to 18 June at the [[Leicester Arena]] in [[Leicester]], England. The fourth consecutive edition of the tournament since it was first staged in [[2023 Wuhan Open (snooker)|2023]], it was the third [[List of snooker ranking tournaments|ranking event]] of the [[2026–27 snooker season]], following the [[2026 China Open (snooker)|2026 China Open]] and preceding the [[2026 British Open]].

[[Xiao Guodong]] was the two-time defending champion, having defeated [[Gary Wilson (snooker player)|Gary Wilson]] 10–9 in the [[2025 Wuhan Open (snooker)|2025 final]], and beating [[Si Jiahui]] 10–7 in the [[2024 Wuhan Open (snooker)|2024 final]], but he was beaten 2–6 by [[Zhao Xintong]] in the semi-finals.

[[Chang Bingyu]] won the tournament, beating Zhao 10–7 in the final.

== Overview ==
The tournament was the fourth consecutive edition of the [[Wuhan Open (snooker)|Wuhan Open]], which was first held in [[2023 Wuhan Open (snooker)|2023]].<ref name="WST_main">{{cite web |url=https://www.wst.tv/wuhanopen/ |title=Wuhan Open |access-date=26 May 2026 |language=en |work=[[World Snooker Tour]] |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260526075106/https://www.wst.tv/wuhanopen/ |archive-date=26 May 2026 |url-status=live}}</ref> The inaugural champion was [[Judd Trump]], who won the 2023 event with a 10–7 victory over [[Ali Carter]].<ref>{{cite news |date=15 October 2023 |title=Wuhan Open: Judd Trump beat Ali Carter to seal back-to-back titles |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/67117784 |access-date=21 August 2025 |work=[[BBC Sport]] |language=en-GB |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260416141837/https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/67117784 |archive-date=16 April 2026 |url-status=live}}</ref> The defending champion was [[Xiao Guodong]], who defended his [[2024 Wuhan Open (snooker)|2024 title]] by defeating [[Gary Wilson (snooker player)|Gary Wilson]] 10–9 in the [[2025 Wuhan Open (snooker)|2025 final]],<ref>{{cite web |date=12 October 2024 |title=Wuhan Open: Xiao wins Wuhan Open to end 17-year title wait |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/c4gr71zrymvo |access-date=16 April 2026 |website=[[BBC Sport]] |language=en-GB |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260416141920/https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/c4gr71zrymvo |archive-date=16 April 2026 |url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=Xiao defends Wuhan crown |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/august/30/xiao-defends-wuhan-crown/ |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |date=30 August 2025 |access-date=30 August 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260416152712/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/august/30/xiao-defends-wuhan-crown/ |archive-date=16 April 2026 |url-status=live}}</ref> but he was beaten 2–6 by [[Zhao Xintong]] in the semi-finals.<ref name="WST_semis" />

The main stage of the event took place from 23 to 29 August 2026 in [[Wuhan]], China.<ref name="s.o_M">{{cite web |url=https://www.snooker.org/res/index.asp?event=2757 |title=Wuhan Open 2026 |website=snooker.org |access-date=26 May 2026 |language=en}}</ref> Qualifiers were held from 15 to 18 June at the [[Leicester Arena]] in [[Leicester]], England.<ref name="s.o_Q">{{cite web |url=https://www.snooker.org/res/index.asp?event=2758 |title=Wuhan Open Qualifiers 2026 |website=snooker.org |access-date=26 May 2026 |language=en}}</ref> The tournament was the third [[List of snooker ranking tournaments|ranking event]] of the [[2026–27 snooker season]], following the [[2026 China Open (snooker)|2026 China Open]] and preceding the [[2026 British Open]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.snooker.org/res/index.asp?season=2026&template=2&tour=all |title=Calendar 2026/2027 |website=snooker.org |access-date=26 May 2026 |language=en}}</ref>

There were 95 [[century break]]s made in the tournament, with 59 made at the qualifying stage,<ref name="si_cent_Q" /> and 36 at the main stage.<ref name="si_cent_M" /> The highest {{cuegloss|break}} was a 145, made by [[Artemijs Žižins]] during qualification, and the highest break at the main stage was a 140 by the eventual winner [[Chang Bingyu]].<ref name="WST_Chang_final" />

=== Format ===
In a change to previous editions, the flat-draw format – which saw everyone starting in the last 128, with some matches held over to the final venue – has been replaced with multiple rounds of qualifying in a tiered format similar to formats used in the [[Home Nations Series]] and the [[German Masters (snooker)|German Masters]]. Two rounds of qualifying were played between players ranked outside the top 32 of the world ranking. Four matches involving Chinese [[Wild card (sports)|wildcards]] (Chen Qi'en, Guan Zhen, Tang Hewen, and Wang Xinzhong) in the second round of qualifying were held over to the main venue in [[Wuhan]]. The third round (the last 64) was split into two halves: matches involving the top 16 [[Seeding (sports)|seeds]], as determined from the start of season ranking list, were held over to the final venue, with players seeded 17–32 playing their match as part of the qualifiers in [[Leicester]].<ref>{{cite web |title=China formats this season explained |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/june/03/china-formats-this-season-explained/ |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |date=3 June 2026 |access-date=17 June 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=Star names happy as big format change for Chinese events on the World Snooker Tour |first=Michael |last=Day |url=https://www.totallysnookered.com/sport/snooker/tournaments/star-names-happy-as-big-format-change-for-chinese-events-on-the-world-snooker-tour-8648904 |date=30 May 2026 |access-date=16 June 2026 |website=Totally Snookered}}</ref> All matches were played as the best of 9 {{cuegloss|frame|frames}} until the semi-finals, which were the best of 11 frames. The final was a best of 19 frames match, played over two {{cuegloss|session|sessions}}.<ref name="s.o_Q" />

=== Semi-finals ===
The semi-finals were played on 28 August as the best of 11 frames.<ref name="s.o_M" />

[[File:Zhao Xintong PHC 2016-2.jpg|left|thumb|upright=0.85|alt=Zhao Xintong playing a shot.|[[Zhao Xintong]] (''pictured in 2016'') beat defending champion [[Xiao Guodong]] in the semi-finals.]]
Chang beat O'Sullivan 6–3. After the match Chang said: "Every time I play against Ronnie {{bracket|O'Sullivan}}, it's a pleasure. He probably wasn't in the best form today, and I managed to take some of the opportunities that came my way. I think my {{cuegloss|safety}} play was probably better right from the start today, and I could clearly feel that my safety game was much better than it had been before." O'Sullivan said: "The disease came back big time today. If I'm honest I don't think I will ever get over it. I will see out the next 18 months and see how we go. I don't want to throw the towel in just yet. I've committed to playing until then. I want to give myself a chance. I'd hate to finish my career on performances like that."<ref>{{cite web |title=Chang fires past the Rocket |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/august/28/chang-fires-past-the-rocket/ |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |date=28 August 2026 |access-date=28 August 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |title=I'd hate to finish career like this – O'Sullivan |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cp93zj22v3zo |work=[[BBC Sport]] |date=28 August 2026 |access-date=28 August 2026}}</ref>

Zhao beat defending champion Xiao 6–2, making a 124 break in the eighth frame. After the match Zhao said: "To be honest, I had quite a bit of luck today. Neither Xiao {{bracket|Guodong}} nor I played particularly well. I adjusted quicker than he did, so I was able to take my chances and win the match. It was just one match, and I don't think winning it proves anything. Since I was ten years old, becoming world number one was my target. It is really hard, but I've done it. I don't want it just to be a few days, I want it to be longer. I need to learn from the likes of Mark Selby, Judd Trump and Ronnie O'Sullivan." Xiao said:"There are no regrets. I think the way Zhao {{bracket|Xintong}} is playing now is at world number one level. I did my best, but there was still nothing I could do"<ref name="WST_semis">{{cite web |title=Zhao ends Xiao's Wuhan reign |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/august/28/zhao-ends-xiao-s-wuhan-reign/ |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |date=28 August 2026 |access-date=28 August 2026}}</ref>

=== Final ===
The final was played on 29 August as the best of 19 frames, played over two {{cuegloss|session|sessions}}.<ref name="s.o_M" />

At the end of the afternoon session, Chang led Zhao 5–4.<ref>{{cite web |title=Chang leads Wuhan final |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/august/29/chang-leads-wuhan-final/ |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |date=29 August 2026 |access-date=29 August 2026}}</ref> Chang went on to win his first ranking title 10–7.<ref name="WST_final_match">{{cite web |title=Match: Zhao Xintong v Chang Bingyu |url=https://www.wst.tv/match-centre/1be98485-ce19-4aec-8c84-37e2930e5707 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |date=29 August 2026 |access-date=29 August 2026 |archive-date=29 August 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260829160750/https://www.wst.tv/match-centre/1be98485-ce19-4aec-8c84-37e2930e5707 |url-status=live}}</ref> After the match Chang said: "Before the match, I honestly didn't think I could beat him {{bracket|Zhao Xintong}}. He is the new world number one after all, so I just wanted to learn from him. I've watched a lot of his matches over the past few years and learned a great deal from him. So I went into the final with the same attitude of learning." Zhao said: "Although I'm a little disappointed with the result today, I think for me personally it’s just one match. I can't be in finals forever or win every time. Of course, I didn't play particularly well today, so I think it was quite normal that Chang {{bracket|Bingyu}} was able to win the match. His game, and that of many of the younger Chinese players, has already reached a very high level."<ref>{{cite web |title=LIVE BLOG {{!}} 2026 Wuhan Open Snooker Final – Zhao Xintong v Chang Bingyu |url=https://www.totallysnookered.com/sport/snooker/tournaments/wuhan-open/live-blog-2026-wuhan-open-snooker-final-zhao-xintong-v-chang-bingyu-8948955 |website=totallysnookered.com |first=Michael |last=Day |date=29 August 2026 |access-date=29 August 2026}}</ref><ref name="WST_Chang_final">{{cite web |title=Chang lands maiden crown in Wuhan |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/august/29/chang-lands-maiden-crown-in-wuhan/ |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |date=29 August 2026 |access-date=29 August 2026}}</ref>
