The '''2026 European Darts Grand Prix''' (known for sponsorship reasons as the '''2026 Elten Safety Shoes European Darts Grand Prix''') was a professional [[darts]] tournament that took place at the [[Glaspalast Sindelfingen|Glaspalast]] in [[Sindelfingen]], Germany, from 17 to 19 April 2026.<ref>{{cite web |title=Elten Safety Shoes European Darts Grand Prix 2026 |url=https://pdc-europe.tv/tournaments/et-2026-en/european-darts-grand-prix-2026/ |website=PDC Europe |access-date=1 March 2026}}</ref> It was the fifth of fifteen [[PDC European Tour]] events on the [[2026 PDC Pro Tour]]. It featured a field of 48 players and £230,000 in prize money, with £35,000 going to the winner.

[[Gary Anderson (darts player)|Gary Anderson]] was the defending champion, having defeated [[Andrew Gilding]] 8–0 in the [[2025 European Darts Grand Prix|2025 final]].<ref>{{cite web |date=4 May 2025 |title=Anderson thrashes Gilding to retain European Grand Prix title |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/darts/articles/clyw5d8478po |website=BBC Sport |access-date=1 March 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |last=Mills |first=Stephen |date=5 May 2025 |title=European Darts Grand Prix: Gary Anderson defends title with whitewash victory over Andrew Gilding in Sindelfingen |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13361391/european-darts-grand-prix-gary-anderson-defends-title-with-whitewash-victory-over-andrew-gilding-in-sindelfingen |website=Sky Sports |access-date=1 March 2026}}</ref> However, he lost 6–3 to [[Niko Springer]] in the second round.

[[Gerwyn Price]] won the tournament by defeating [[Ross Smith (darts player)|Ross Smith]] 8–6 in the final, after [[Martin Schindler]] missed a [[Glossary of darts#Match dart|match dart]] to beat him in the quarter-finals. He became the second player to win ten European Tour titles after [[Michael van Gerwen]].

== Prize money ==
As part of a mass boost in prize money for [[Professional Darts Corporation]] (PDC) events in 2026, the prize fund for all [[2026 PDC Pro Tour#European Tour#European Tour|2026 European Tour]] events rose to £230,000, of which the winner will receive £35,000.<ref>{{cite web |last=Phillips |first=Josh |date=31 March 2025 |title=Biggest prize money increase in PDC history confirmed |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/march/31/biggest-prize-money-increase-pdc-history-confirmed |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=1 March 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=European Darts Grand Prix 2026 |url=https://mastercaller.com/tournaments/european-darts-grand-prix/2026 |website=Mastercaller |access-date=1 March 2026}}</ref>

== Qualification and format ==
The top 16 players on the two-year [[PDC Order of Merit]] were [[Seeding (sports)|seeded]] and entered the tournament in the second round, while the next 16 highest-ranked players from the one-year [[PDC Pro Tour Order of Merit]] automatically qualified for the first round. The seedings were confirmed on 27 March.<ref>{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=27 March 2026 |title=2026 ET5-6 Seeds & Tour Card Holder qualifier entries confirmed |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/march/27/2026-et5-6-seeds---tour-card-holder-qualifier-entries-confirmed/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=17 April 2026}}</ref> The remaining 16 places went to players from four qualifying events – 10 from the Tour Card Holder Qualifier (held on 1 April),<ref>{{cite web |date=1 April 2026 |title=PDC ET05 Tour Card Holder Qualifier|url= https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdceutcq26et05/matches|website=Dart Connect TV |access-date=10 April 2026}}</ref> four from the Host Nation Qualifier (held on 28 February),<ref>{{cite web |date=28 February 2026 |title=PDC ET05 Host Nation Qualifier |url=https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdc26et05hnq/matches |website=Dart Connect TV |access-date=28 February 2026}}</ref> one from the Nordic & Baltic Associate Member Qualifier (held on 29 March),<ref>{{cite web |date=29 March 2026 |title=PDCNB 2026 Tour - Latvia 2 - Weekend 02 |url=https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdcnbpro26e02/matches|website=Dart Connect TV |access-date=10 April 2026}}</ref> and one from the East European Associate Member Qualifier (held on 11 April).<ref>{{cite web |date=11 April 2026 |title=PDC ET05 E. Europe Qualifier |url=https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdceeu26et05q/matches|website=Dart Connect TV |access-date=11 April 2026}}</ref>

[[Ryan Joyce]] withdrew and was replaced by [[Ian White (darts player)|Ian White]].<ref name="Draw">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=16 April 2026 |title=2026 Elten Safety Shoes European Darts Grand Prix draw & schedule |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/april/16/2026-elten-safety-shoes-european-darts-grand-prix-draw---schedule/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=16 April 2026}}</ref>

== Summary ==
=== First round ===
[[File:2025-04-03 Premier League Darts Berlin 2025 by Sandro Halank–069.jpg|thumb|[[Rob Cross]] ''(pictured in 2025)'' was one of three former world champions to go out in the first round.]]
The first round (best of 11 [[Leg (darts)|legs]]) was played on 17 April.<ref name="PDC1">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=18 April 2026 |title=Magnificent Menzies stars on Day One in Sindelfingen |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/april/17/Magnificent-Menzies-stars-day-one-Sindelfingen/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=18 April 2026}}</ref> [[Cameron Menzies]] registered a [[three-dart average]] of 104.50 to defeat five-time world champion [[Raymond van Barneveld]] 6–3. "I felt tremendous there. I wish I could play like that all the time," commented Menzies, who hailed Van Barneveld as a hero of his growing up.<ref name="Sky1">{{cite web |date=17 April 2026 |title=European Darts Grand Prix: Cameron Menzies puts in sensational display against Raymond van Barneveld as Dave Chisnall, Joe Cullen also progress |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13533041/european-darts-grand-prix-cameron-menzies-puts-in-sensational-display-against-raymond-van-barneveld-as-dave-chisnall-joe-cullen-also-progress |website=Sky Sports |access-date=18 April 2026}}</ref> Two other former world champions were eliminated in the first round: [[Peter Wright (darts player)|Peter Wright]], the winner of the [[2017 European Darts Grand Prix]], lost 6–3 to [[Jeffrey de Zwaan]], while [[2023 European Darts Grand Prix|2023]] champion [[Rob Cross]] was beaten 6–2 by [[Mickey Mansell]]. Host nation qualifier [[Michael Unterbuchner]] earned his second European Tour victory by defeating [[William O'Connor (darts player)|William O'Connor]] in a [[Glossary of darts#Decider|deciding leg]]; the other three host nation qualifiers—Michael Hurtz, Paul Krohne and Robin Masino—lost to [[Krzysztof Ratajski]], [[Andrew Gilding]], and [[Niko Springer]], respectively.<ref name="PDC1"/><ref name="EU1">{{cite web |date=17 April 2026 |title=ELTEN Safety Shoes European Darts Grand Prix: Unterbuchner shines in opening session |url=https://www.pdc-europe.tv/news/elten-safety-shoes-european-darts-grand-prix-unterbuchner-shines-in-opening-session/ |website=PDC Europe |access-date=18 April 2026}}</ref> 

Latvian qualifier Valters Melderis became the 600th player to compete on the European Tour as he beat [[Karel Sedláček]] 6–5; his compatriot [[Madars Razma]] lost 6–4 to [[Damon Heta]]. [[Wessel Nijman]], who won [[2026 PDC Players Championship series#Players Championship 10|Players Championship 10]] earlier in the week, defeated [[2019 European Darts Grand Prix|2023]] champion [[Ian White (darts player)|Ian White]] 6–2. [[Oskar Lukasiak]] missed nine [[Glossary of darts#Match dart|match darts]] before eventually sealing a 6–5 win over [[Daryl Gurney]]. [[Keane Barry]] landed [[Glossary of darts#Checkout|checkouts]] of 124 and 130 on his way to defeating [[Niels Zonneveld]] 6–3.<ref name="Sky1"/><ref name="EU1"/>

=== Second round ===
[[File:2022-06-13 Play-offs (2022 Premier League Darts) by Sandro Halank–010.jpg|thumb|[[Joe Cullen (darts player)|Joe Cullen]] ''(pictured in 2022)'' defeated the top seed [[Gian van Veen]] 6–3 in the second round.]]
The second round (best of 11 legs) was played on 18 April.<ref name="PDC2">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=18 April 2026 |title=Springer dumps out Anderson on Day Two of European Darts Grand Prix |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/april/18/Springer-dumps-out-Anderson-Day-Two-European-Darts-Grand-Prix/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=19 April 2026}}</ref> Two-time defending champion [[Gary Anderson (darts player)|Gary Anderson]] was eliminated in a 6–3 defeat to Niko Springer. Progressing to a third-round meeting with [[Danny Noppert]] after the Dutchman beat Oskar Lukasiak 6–5, Springer recounted the pair's final at the [[2025 Hungarian Darts Trophy]], calling Noppert a "fantastic player" who he would have to "play the same level" against the following day.<ref name="PDC2"/> [[Martin Schindler]] joined compatriot Springer in the third round by winning a deciding leg against Damon Heta.<ref name="EU2"/> Top seed [[Gian van Veen]] was beaten 6–3 by [[Joe Cullen (darts player)|Joe Cullen]], who reached the last 16 of a European Tour event for the first time in a year.<ref name="Sky2">{{cite web |date=19 April 2026 |title=European Darts Grand Prix: Michael van Gerwen mocks opponent Michael Unterbuchner after win |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13533383/european-darts-grand-prix-michael-van-gerwen-mocks-opponent-michael-unterbuchner-after-win |website=Sky Sports |access-date=19 April 2026}}</ref> The [[2016 European Darts Grand Prix|2016]] and [[2018 European Darts Grand Prix|2018]] champion [[Michael van Gerwen]] was the only former winner of the tournament to advance to the third round, defeating Michael Unterbuchner 6–2.<ref name="EU2">{{cite web |date=18 April 2026 |title=ELTEN Safety Shoes European Darts Grand Prix: The King of Sindelfingen has been dethroned |url=https://www.pdc-europe.tv/news/elten-safety-shoes-european-darts-grand-prix-the-king-of-sindelfingen-has-been-dethroned/ |website=PDC Europe |access-date=19 April 2026}}</ref> Following the match, Van Gerwen was critical of his opponent, stating: "He's never beaten me in his life and he will never beat me in his life. He's not good enough. Even my B-game and C-game beats him but who cares?" He added: "The day I'm going to worry myself about Unterbuchner, that's the day I'm going to quit playing darts."<ref name="Sky2"/>

Two other seeded players were eliminated in the second round alongside Van Veen and Anderson: Wessel Nijman beat tenth seed [[Ryan Searle (darts player)|Ryan Searle]] 6–2 with an average of 104.12, and Krzysztof Ratajski defeated sixteenth seed [[Mike De Decker]] 6–4. [[Nathan Aspinall]], who won the [[2026 German Darts Grand Prix]], began his campaign by beating Jeffrey de Zwaan 6–1, a win that included a [[Glossary of darts#Maximum check|170 checkout]]. Andrew Gilding, the runner-up [[2025 European Darts Grand Prix|the previous year]], lost 6–2 to [[Chris Dobey]]. [[Jonny Clayton]] averaged 106.78 on his way to defeating Valters Melderis 6–1, while [[James Wade]] averaged 104.15 to claim a 6–3 win over [[Kim Huybrechts]], marking the end of a five-match losing streak for Wade on the European Tour. [[2026 Premier League Darts|Premier League]] players [[Gerwyn Price]], [[Josh Rock]], and [[Stephen Bunting]] defeated [[Dave Chisnall]], Keane Barry, and Mickey Mansell, respectively. [[Ross Smith (darts player)|Ross Smith]] defeated Cameron Menzies 6–3, while [[Jermaine Wattimena]] beat [[Kevin Doets]] by the same scoreline.<ref name="PDC2"/><ref name="EU2"/><ref name="Sky2"/>

=== Final day ===
[[File:2026-03-26 Premier League Darts – Night 8 – Berlin 2026 by Sandro Halank–112.jpg|thumb|[[Gerwyn Price]] ''(pictured in 2026)'' won his tenth European Tour title, becoming the second player to achieve the feat.]]
The third round, quarter-finals, semi-finals and final were played on 19 April. The third round and quarter-finals were contested over the best of 11 legs, the semi-finals over the best of 13 legs, and the final over the best of 15 legs.<ref name="PDCFinal">{{cite web |last=Simpson |first=Will |date=19 April 2026 |title=Peerless Price claims tenth European Tour title in Sindelfingen |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/april/19/Peerless-Price-claims-tenth-European-Tour-title-in-Sindelfingen/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=19 April 2026}}</ref> The final day saw Ross Smith and Gerwyn Price reach the final. Smith began the day by defeating Josh Rock in a deciding leg, and advanced to the title decider following a 6–4 win against Nathan Aspinall and a 7–5 victory over Krzysztof Ratajski. Meanwhile, Price defeated Chris Dobey 6–4 before winning a deciding leg against Martin Schindler, who missed a match dart at [[Glossary of darts#Double|double]] 5 to eliminate the Welshman.<ref name="SkyFinal"/><ref>{{cite web |date=19 April 2026 |title=Price schlägt Schindler und holt Titel |trans-title=Price beats Schindler and wins title |url=https://www.sport1.de/news/darts-sport/2026/04/schindler-verliert-krimi-gegen-price |website=Sport1 |access-date=19 April 2026 |language=de}}</ref> In the semi-finals, Price defeated Wessel Nijman 7–4 in a rematch of the [[2026 European Darts Trophy|European Darts Trophy]] final.<ref name="PDCFinal"/> Despite the loss, Nijman's run to the semi-finals resulted in him entering the top 16 of the [[PDC World Rankings]] for the first time.<ref>{{cite web |last=Walsh |first=Alex |date=19 April 2026 |title=Wessel Nijman reaches major career milestone at 2026 European Darts Grand Prix |url=https://www.sportingnews.com/ca/darts/news/wessel-nijman-career-milestone-2026-european-darts-grand-prix/cb52a8d8692f59ce43ffcaa3 |website=The Sporting News |access-date=19 April 2026}}</ref> Smith looked to win his first European Tour title in his fourth final, while Price, competing in his second European Tour final in two appearances in 2026, aimed to win his tenth title.<ref name="SkyFinal">{{cite web |last=Wellens |first=Megan |date=19 April 2026 |title=European Darts Grand Prix: Gerwyn Price picks up record-making 10th European title with 8-6 win over Ross Smith |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13533781/european-darts-grand-prix-gerwyn-price-picks-up-record-making-10th-european-title-with-8-6-win-over-ross-smith |website=Sky Sports |access-date=19 April 2026}}</ref>

Price started the final by taking a 3–0 lead, but a run of four consecutive legs from Smith that included two [[Glossary of darts#Break|breaks of throw]] saw the Englishman go in front. Price responded with a 130 checkout to equalise at 4–4.<ref>{{cite web |last=Michael |first=Lucas |date=19 April 2026 |title=Gerwyn Price secures 10th Euro Tour title against Ross Smith in closely fought final |url=https://dartsnews.com/pdc/live-european-darts-grand-prix-sunday-evening-van-gerwen-price-aspinall-all-in-the-hunt-for-glory-in-sindelfingen |website=dartsnews.com |access-date=20 April 2026}}</ref> Momentum shifted in Price's favour when Smith missed five darts at double to go 6–5 ahead, allowing Price to reclaim the lead with a break.<ref name="PDCFinal"/> Price kept control of the match from there and hit double 12 to complete an 8–6 victory, finishing the match with a three-dart average of 95.70.<ref name="SkyFinal"/><ref name="Results"/>

Price won his tenth European Tour title, making him the second player to reach that mark after Michael van Gerwen, who was on 38 titles at the time of Price's victory.<ref>{{cite web |date=19 April 2026 |title=ELTEN Safety Shoes European Darts Grand Prix: Price makes history in the Glaspalast |url=https://www.pdc-europe.tv/news/elten-safety-shoes-european-darts-grand-prix-price-makes-history-in-the-glaspalast/ |website=PDC Europe |access-date=20 April 2026}}</ref> "We [Price and Smith] have both had great tournaments and I was just struggling to get over the winning line," admitted Price after the match.<ref name="SkyFinal"/> On his title milestone, he joked: "I've been in the PDC for 12 years now, so less than one a year on average isn't very good."<ref name="PDCFinal"/> Speaking in defeat, Smith said: "It's fine margins. You're playing the best players in the world—if you don't hit your doubles, you're going to get punished." He concluded: "It is what it is. I'll crack on with the next one."<ref name="PDCFinal"/>
