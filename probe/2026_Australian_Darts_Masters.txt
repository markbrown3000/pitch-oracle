The '''2026 Australian Darts Masters''' (known for sponsorship reasons as the '''2026 KenoGO Australian Darts Masters''') is a professional [[darts]] tournament that took place from 21 to 22 August 2026 at the [[WIN Entertainment Centre]] in [[Wollongong]], Australia. It was the third staging of the tournament by the [[Professional Darts Corporation]] (PDC), and was the sixth and final event in the [[2026 World Series of Darts]] before the [[2026 World Series of Darts Finals|World Series Finals]].<ref>{{cite web |last=Phillips |first=Josh |date=5 October 2025 |title=2026 World Series of Darts schedule confirmed |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/october/05/2026-world-series-darts-schedule-confirmed |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=1 July 2026}}</ref> The total prize fund was £100,000, with the winner receiving £30,000.

The tournament featured 16 players, consisting of eight PDC representatives and eight [[Oceanian]] representatives. [[Luke Littler]] was the reigning champion, having defeated [[Mike De Decker]] 8–4 in the [[2025 Australian Darts Masters|2025 final]]. However, Littler did not enter the tournament and therefore did defend his title.

[[James Wade]] won his first [[World Series of Darts|World Series]] title by defeating Brody Klinge 8–5 in the final. Klinge became the first Oceanian qualifier to reach a World Series final since [[Damon Heta]] at the [[2019 Brisbane Darts Masters]].

=== Format ===
Eight elite PDC representatives were drawn to play eight [[Oceanian]] representatives in the first round on Friday 21 August; the quarter-finals, semi-finals and final took place on Saturday 22 August.<ref name="Draw"/>

*'''First round and quarter-finals:''' Best of eleven legs
*'''Semi-finals:''' Best of thirteen legs
*'''Final:''' Best of fifteen legs

== Summary ==
=== First round ===
[[File:Team Wales Darts WC 2025.png|thumb|upright=1.1|Welsh compatriots [[Jonny Clayton]] and [[Gerwyn Price]] ''(Clayton left, Price right, pictured in 2025)'' both reached the semi-finals.]]
The first round (best of 11 [[Leg (darts)|legs]]) was played on 21 August.<ref name="PDC_D1">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=21 August 2026 |title=Klinge stuns Smith to set up Van Veen clash in Wollongong |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/august/21/Klinge-stuns-Smith-set-up-Van-Veen-clash-Wollongong/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=21 August 2026}}</ref> Seven of the eight PDC representatives were victorious in the opening round. Fourth seed [[James Wade]] registered a [[three-dart average]] of 112.73, the second-highest average achieved at an Australian World Series event, on his way to a [[Whitewash (sport)|whitewash]] win over [[Tim Pusey]], finishing the match with a 164 [[Glossary of darts#Checkout|checkout]]. "That felt amazing. Everything just fell into my hands there," said Wade afterwards, who added that "everyone is stuffed" if he could replicate his performance the following day.<ref name="Sky_D1">{{cite web |date=21 August 2026 |title=Australian Darts Masters: James Wade dominates tournament opener as Ross Smith stunned by Brody Klinge in Wollongong |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13575441/australian-darts-masters-james-wade-dominates-tournament-opener-as-ross-smith-stunned-by-brody-klinge-in-wollongong |website=Sky Sports |access-date=21 August 2026}}</ref> Oceanian representative Brody Klinge defied a 102.88 average from [[Ross Smith (darts player)|Ross Smith]] to defeat the PDC representative in a [[Glossary of darts#Decider|deciding leg]], with the Australian completing the win with an 11-dart [[Glossary of darts#Break|break of throw]] after Smith missed two [[Glossary of darts#Match dart|match darts]]. Klinge commented that it was "the adrenaline rush of my life" and that he was "on a free rein now" without the pressure of getting past the first round.<ref name="PDC_D1"/>

[[Adam Leek]] took a 3–2 lead against [[Josh Rock]], but Rock won the next four legs to win 6–3.<ref name="NL">{{cite web |last=McNabb |first=Johnny |date=21 August 2026 |title=Australian Darts Masters: Josh Rock hits two ton-plus checkouts to reach quarter-final stage |url=https://www.newsletter.co.uk/sport/other-sport/australian-darts-masters-josh-rock-hits-two-ton-plus-checkouts-to-reach-quarter-final-stage-8935109 |website=News Letter |access-date=21 August 2026}}</ref> Top seed [[Gerwyn Price]], the [[2024 Australian Darts Masters|2024]] champion, converted back-to-back checkouts of 124 and 108 to beat [[Simon Whitlock]] 6–2. Australian number one [[Damon Heta]] averaged 102.39 as he beat Joe Comito 6–2. Third seed [[Jonny Clayton]], who won the [[2026 New Zealand Darts Masters|New Zealand Darts Masters]] the previous week, defeated [[Ben Robb]] 6–3 to begin his pursuit for back-to-back titles. Second seed [[Gian van Veen]] and [[Stephen Bunting]] were also 6–3 victors, earning respective wins over [[Raymond Smith (darts player)|Raymond Smith]] and [[Mal Cuming]].<ref name="Sky_D1"/><ref name="NL"/>

=== Quarter-finals, semi-finals and final ===
[[File:2022-06-13 Play-offs (2022 Premier League Darts) by Sandro Halank–068.jpg|thumb|[[James Wade]] ''(pictured in 2022)'' won his first World Series title by defeating Oceanian representative Brody Klinge 8–5 in the final.]]
The quarter-finals (best of 11 legs), semi-finals (best of 13 legs) and final (best of 15 legs) were played on 22 August.<ref name="PDC_Final">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=22 August 2026 |title=Wonderful Wade wins maiden World Series title in Wollongong |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/august/22/wonderful-Wade-wins-maiden-world-series-title-wollongong/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=22 August 2026}}</ref> Gerwyn Price won five consecutive legs to defeat Josh Rock 6–2 with a three-dart average of 101.34 in the opening quarter-final. James Wade hit a 164 checkout as he overturned a 5–2 deficit to beat Stephen Bunting in a deciding leg. Brody Klinge produced another upset win to progress to the semi-finals, eliminating world number two Gian van Veen in a 6–4 victory. In the last quarter-final match, Jonny Clayton defeated Damon Heta 6–3. In the semi-finals, Wade averaged 101.27 to beat top seed Price 7–4, while Klinge converted a [[Glossary of darts#Maximum check|170 checkout]] to complete a 7–5 win over Clayton, ending the Welshman's hopes of winning back-to-back titles.<ref name="PDC_Final"/><ref name="DNews_Final">{{cite web |last=Wood |first=Kieran |date=22 August 2026 |title=James Wade denies 104+ average from Aussie qualifier Brody Klinge to win long-awaited first-ever World Series title |url=https://dartsnews.com/pdc/australian-darts-masters-2026-roundup-james-wade-denies-104-average-from-aussie-qualifier-brody-klinge-to-win-long-awaited-first-ever-world-series-title |website=dartsnews.com |access-date=22 August 2026}}</ref> In just his second World Series appearance, Klinge became the first Oceanian qualifier to reach a World Series final since Heta at the [[2019 Brisbane Darts Masters]].<ref name="Klinge">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=22 August 2026 |title=Klinge vows to 'take opportunity' following Australian Darts Masters breakthrough |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/august/22/klinge-vows-to--take-opportunity-following-australian-darts-masters-breakthrough/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=23 August 2026}}</ref> Wade and Klinge both aimed to win their first World Series title.<ref name="PDC_Final"/>

Wade found an 11-dart break of throw to establish an early 2–0 lead in the final.<ref name="PDC_Final"/> The pair, both averaging 107, entered the interval with Wade 4–2 in front.<ref name="DNews_Final"/><ref name="Sky_Final">{{cite web |last=Hodge |first=Stuart |date=22 August 2026 |title=Australian Darts Masters: James Wade claims first-ever World Series of Darts title with victory over home favourite Brody Klinge |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13575787/australian-darts-masters-james-wade-claims-first-ever-world-series-of-darts-title-with-victory-over-home-favourite-brody-klinge |website=Sky Sports |access-date=22 August 2026}}</ref> He extended this lead to 6–2 to complete a run of four consecutive legs won. Klinge halted his opponent's momentum and staged a potential comeback, winning the next three legs to reduce the gap to 6–5, including a 140 checkout for 6–4. However, Wade won the next two to secure an 8–5 victory. Wade finished the match with a three-dart average of 101.62, while Klinge averaged 104.56 in defeat.<ref name="Sky_Final"/><ref name="BBC_Final">{{cite web |last=Nelson |first=Craig |date=22 August 2026 |title=Wade wins first World Series title in Australia |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/darts/articles/c4gwg9nnx36o |website=BBC Sport |access-date=22 August 2026}}</ref>

Wade won his first World Series title and his first televised title since the [[2021 UK Open]], 13 years after participating in the first World Series event at the [[2013 Dubai Duty Free Darts Masters|2013 Dubai Darts Masters]].<ref name="Sky_Final"/><ref name="BBC_Final"/> He also won the [[2018 World Series of Darts Finals|2018 World Series Finals]].<ref>{{cite web |date=7 November 2018 |title=James Wade continues return to form with World Series of Darts Finals title |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/11545434/james-wade-continues-return-to-form-with-world-series-of-darts-finals-title |website=Sky Sports |access-date=22 August 2026}}</ref> In his post-match interview, Wade admitted that he thought he would get a "nice easy win" against the regional qualifier, but Klinge's performance meant he had to work hard for the victory. "I will be really, really disappointed if this man [Klinge] doesn't pursue what he is doing," added Wade, before joking about the reaction he got from the partisan crowd in [[Wollongong]].<ref name="BBC_Final"/> "The crowd have been behind me the whole time and I've really enjoyed the whole experience. I cannot believe I got here," said Klinge, who vowed to keep playing and "see what happens".<ref name="Klinge"/>
