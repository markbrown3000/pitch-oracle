The '''2026 World Series of Darts Finals''' was a professional [[darts]] tournament that took place at the [[AFAS Live]] in [[Amsterdam]], Netherlands, from 17 to 20 September 2026. It was the 12th staging of the [[World Series of Darts Finals]] by the [[Professional Darts Corporation]] (PDC). The total prize fund was an increased total of £450,000, with the winner receiving £100,000.

The tournament served as the culmination to the [[2026 World Series of Darts]], a collection of six unranked events held by the PDC in six different countries. It featured 32 players, comprising the top 24 players from the World Series rankings, the top four non-qualified players from the [[PDC World Rankings]], and four [[List of players with a 2026 PDC Tour Card|Tour Card holder]] qualifiers.

[[Michael van Gerwen]] was the defending champion, having defeated [[Luke Littler]] 11–7 in the [[2025 World Series of Darts Finals|2025 final]], but he lost 6–5 to [[Daryl Gurney]] in the first round. [[Ross Smith (darts player)|Ross Smith]] won the tournament and his first major title since 2022, defeating [[Gerwyn Price]] 11–5 in the final.

=== Format ===
The top eight players in the [[2026 World Series of Darts]] rankings were [[Seeding (sport)|seeded]] in the first round.<ref name="SkyDraw"/> All matches were in [[Leg (darts)|leg]] play format, with the number of legs required to win increasing as the tournament progresses:<ref name="24Pre">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=12 September 2024 |title=2024 Jack's World Series of Darts Finals preview |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2024/september/12/2024-jacks-world-series-darts-finals-preview |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=12 August 2026}}</ref>

*'''First round:''' Best of 11 legs
*'''Second round & Quarter-finals:''' Best of 19 legs
*'''Semi-finals and final:''' Best of 21 legs

== Qualification ==
The [[2026 World Series of Darts]] consisted of six events:<ref>{{cite web |last=Phillips |first=Josh |date=5 October 2025 |title=2026 World Series of Darts schedule confirmed |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/october/05/2026-world-series-darts-schedule-confirmed |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=10 August 2026}}</ref>

*[[2026 Saudi Arabia Darts Masters]]
*[[2026 Nordic Darts Masters]]
*[[2026 US Darts Masters]]
*[[2026 New Zealand Darts Masters]]
*[[2026 Australian Darts Masters]]

The top 24 players in the 2026 World Series rankings, based on points earned across the six events, qualified for the tournament. They were joined by the top four players in the [[PDC World Rankings]] who had not already qualified. [[Gary Anderson (darts player)|Gary Anderson]], who had qualified through this route, declined his invitation and was replaced by [[Jermaine Wattimena]]. The remaining places went to four [[List of players with a 2026 PDC Tour Card|PDC Tour Card holders]] from a qualifying event held in [[Leicester]] on 24 August.<ref name="Field">{{cite web |last=Simpson |first=Will |date=25 August 2026 |title=Van Gerwen and Littler headline 2026 World Series of Darts Finals field |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/august/25/Van-Gerwen-and-Littler-headline-2026-World-Series-of-Darts-Finals-field/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=25 August 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |last=Simpson |first=Will |date=24 August 2026 |title=Cross spearheads 2026 World Series Finals qualifiers |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/august/24/Cross-spearheads-2026-World-Series-Finals-qualifiers/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=24 August 2026}}</ref> On 4 September, the PDC announced that [[Man Lok Leung]] had withdrawn from the tournament due to illness. He was replaced by [[Dirk van Duijvenbode]].<ref>{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=4 September 2026 |title=Van Duijvenbode replaces Leung in 2026 World Series of Darts Finals |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/september/04/van-duijvenbode-replaces-leung-in-2026-world-series-of-darts-finals/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=4 September 2026}}</ref> On 9 September, the PDC announced that [[Lourence Ilagan]] and [[Alexis Toylo]] had withdrawn from the tournament due to visa issues. They were replaced by [[Daryl Gurney]] and [[Maik Kuivenhoven]].<ref>{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=9 September 2026 |title= Gurney & Kuivenhoven replace Ilagan & Toylo in World Series Finals field |url= https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/september/09/gurney-kuivenhoven-replace-ilagan-toylo-in-world-series-finals-field/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=9 September 2026}}</ref>

The following players qualified for the tournament:<ref>{{cite web |date=25 August 2026 |title=World Series of Darts Finals: Luke Littler and Michael van Gerwen to headline £450k event as full 32-player field confirmed |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13576932/world-series-of-darts-finals-luke-littler-and-michael-van-gerwen-to-headline-lb450k-event-as-full-32-player-field-confirmed |website=Sky Sports |access-date=25 August 2026}}</ref>
