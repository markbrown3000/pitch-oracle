The '''2026 US Darts Masters''' (known for sponsorship reasons as the '''2026 bet365 US Darts Masters''') was a professional [[darts]] tournament that took place from 25 to 26 June 2026 at the [[Infosys Theater at Madison Square Garden]] in [[New York City]], United States. It was the eighth staging of the tournament by the [[Professional Darts Corporation]] (PDC), and was the fourth event in the [[2026 World Series of Darts]].<ref>{{cite web |last=Phillips |first=Josh |date=5 October 2025 |title=2026 World Series of Darts schedule confirmed |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/october/05/2026-world-series-darts-schedule-confirmed |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=6 May 2026}}</ref> The total prize fund was £100,000, with the winner receiving £30,000. The [[2026 in PDC#North American Championship|North American Championship]] also took place on the afternoon of 26 June exclusively for the North American representatives, which was won by [[Adam Sevada]].<ref>{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=26 June 2026 |title=Superb Sevada storms to bet365 North American Championship title |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/june/26/superb-sevada-storms-north-american-championship-title/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=27 June 2026}}</ref>

Sponsored by [[bet365]], the tournament featured 16 players, consisting of eight PDC representatives and eight North American representatives. [[Luke Humphries]] was the defending champion, having defeated [[Nathan Aspinall]] 8–6 in the [[2025 US Darts Masters|2025 final]].<ref>{{cite web |last=Hudson |first=Elizabeth |date=29 June 2025 |title=Humphries beats Aspinall in US Darts Masters final |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/darts/articles/cp825zlw2y1o |website=BBC Sport |access-date=6 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |date=29 June 2025 |title=Luke Humphries: World No 1 defeats Luke Littler's conqueror Nathan Aspinall to win US Darts Masters in New York |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13389975/luke-humphries-world-no-1-defeats-luke-littlers-conqueror-nathan-aspinall-to-win-us-darts-masters-in-new-york |website=Sky Sports |access-date=6 May 2026}}</ref> Humphries reached his second straight final, where he defeated [[Luke Littler]] 8–7 to win his third [[World Series of Darts|World Series]] title and become the first player to retain the US Darts Masters, having survived two [[Glossary of darts#Match dart|match darts]] from [[Gian van Veen]] in the semi-finals.

In the United States, the tournament was broadcast on [[ESPN+]] and [[ESPN2]], as part of a deal between the PDC and [[ESPN]] that was announced on 25 June 2026.<ref>{{cite news |title=ESPN to broadcast PDC events in USA |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/june/25/espn-to-broadcast-/ |access-date=25 June 2026 |work=PDC.tv |publisher=[[Professional Darts Corporation]] |date=25 June 2026}}</ref> It aired on [[ITV3]] in the United Kingdom with delayed coverage. == Summary ==
=== First round ===
[[File:2026-03-26 Premier League Darts – Night 8 – Berlin 2026 by Sandro Halank–025.jpg|thumb|[[Josh Rock]] ''(pictured in 2026)'' was the sole PDC representative to be eliminated in the first round, losing 6–4 to Canada's [[Jim Long (darts player)|Jim Long]].]]
The first round (best of 11 [[Leg (darts)|legs]]) was played on 25 June.<ref name="PDC_D1">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=25 June 2026 |title=Long stuns Rock on Day One at US Darts Masters |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/june/25/2026-bet365-us-darts-masters---day-one-latest/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=26 June 2026}}</ref> Seven of the eight PDC representatives were victorious in the first round. [[Jim Long (darts player)|Jim Long]] emerged as the only North American player to progress to the quarter-finals, defeating US Darts Masters debutant [[Josh Rock]] 6–4. "I've played Josh [Rock] three times on the tour so we're familiar with each other, and I was fortunate he missed a couple of doubles at the end there," said the Canadian after the match.<ref>{{cite web |last=McNabb |first=Johnny |date=26 June 2026 |title=US Darts Masters: Josh Rock stunned by Jim Long on opening day in New York |url=https://www.newsletter.co.uk/sport/other-sport/us-darts-masters-josh-rock-stunned-by-jim-long-on-opening-day-in-new-york-8763731 |website=News Letter |access-date=26 June 2026}}</ref> Reigning world champion and top seed [[Luke Littler]] overcame a 4–3 deficit to claim a 6–4 victory over [[David Cameron (darts player)|David Cameron]]. Defending champion [[Luke Humphries]] began his campaign by defeating [[Leonard Gates]] 6–1. Speaking afterwards, Humphries hailed the US Darts Masters as a "really prestigious event" in "one of the best venues in the world".<ref>{{cite web |date=26 June 2026 |title=US Darts Masters: Luke Littler, Luke Humphries safely through to quarters as Josh Rock crashes out in New York |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13557900/us-darts-masters-luke-littler-luke-humphries-safely-through-to-quarters-as-josh-rock-crashes-out-in-new-york |website=Sky Sports |access-date=26 June 2026}}</ref>

Big-stage newcomers Fred Krueger and Brayden Hall were beaten in their opening matches, losing 6–1 to [[Gian van Veen]] and 6–4 to [[Gerwyn Price]], respectively. [[James Wade]] made a successful return to the World Series, hitting a 144 [[Glossary of darts#Checkout|checkout]] before defeating [[Adam Sevada]] 6–3. [[Stephen Bunting]] and [[Jonny Clayton]] were 6–2 winners over [[Alex Spellman]] and [[Gary Mawson]]; Bunting earned his first win at the event, while Clayton posted a [[three-dart average]] of 98.54 in victory.<ref name="PDC_D1"/><ref>{{cite web |last=Gill |first=Samuel |date=26 June 2026 |title=Luke Littler, Gerwyn Price tested in New York as Jim Long stuns Josh Rock |url=https://dartsnews.com/pdc/us-darts-masters-day-one-round-up-luke-littler-gerwyn-price-tested-in-new-york-as-jim-long-stuns-josh-rock |website=dartsnews.com |access-date=26 June 2026}}</ref>

=== Quarter-finals, semi-finals and final ===
[[File:2026-03-26 Premier League Darts – Night 8 – Berlin 2026 by Sandro Halank–043.jpg|thumb|[[Luke Humphries]] ''(pictured in 2026)'' won his third World Series title and became the first player to retain the US Darts Masters.]]
The quarter-finals (best of 11 legs), semi-finals (best of 13 legs) and final (best of 15 legs) were played on 26 June.<ref name="PDC_Final">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=27 June 2026 |title=Humphries defies Littler to win back-to-back US Darts Masters titles |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/june/27/Humphries-defies-Littler-back-to-back-US-Masters-titles/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=27 June 2026}}</ref> Luke Littler opened the quarter-finals with a 6–1 win over Jim Long, ending the North American representation in the tournament. Gerwyn Price averaged 103.93 but was beaten 6–3 by James Wade. Stephen Bunting landed a 164 checkout, the highest of the tournament, before losing 6–3 to Luke Humphries. Gian van Veen averaged 106.45 on his way to beating Jonny Clayton by the same scoreline. Wade successfully pinned all of his attempts at [[Glossary of darts#Double|double]] but only registered four legs as Littler defeated him 7–4 in the first semi-final. In the other semi-final, Van Veen missed two [[Glossary of darts#Match dart|match darts]] as Humphries came back from 6–3 down to win 7–6 and advance.<ref name="Sky_Final">{{cite web |date=27 June 2026 |title=US Darts Masters: Luke Humphries defends New York title after beating Luke Littler 8-7 in thrilling final at Madison Square Gardens |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13558202/us-darts-masters-luke-humphries-defends-new-york-title-after-beating-luke-littler-8-7-in-thrilling-final-at-madison-square-gardens |website=Sky Sports |access-date=27 June 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |date=27 June 2026 |title=U.S. Darts Masters: Humphries verteidigt seinen Titel |trans-title=U.S. Darts Masters: Humphries defends his title |url=https://www.dartn.de/news/u-s-darts-masters-humphries-verteidigt-seinen-titel/ |website=dartn.de |access-date=27 June 2026 |language=de}}</ref> This set up a final between the world's top two, Littler and Humphries, who teamed up to win the [[2026 PDC World Cup of Darts|World Cup of Darts]] for [[England at the PDC World Cup|England]] earlier in the month. Littler contested his first US Darts Masters final, while Humphries looked to win the tournament for the second year in a row.<ref name="PDC_Final"/>

Humphries began the final by taking a commanding 4–1 lead, but he missed darts to extend his advantage to four legs, allowing Littler to reduce the deficit to 4–2. The reigning world champion completed a run of four consecutive legs to go 5–4 in front, before Humphries converted a 72 checkout to level the match. From there, the pair went to a deciding leg which Humphries won in 11 darts to seal an 8–7 victory. Humphries finished the match with an average of 104.05, while Littler landed nine [[Glossary of darts#Maximum|maximums]] as he averaged just under 100.<ref name="PDC_Final"/><ref name="BBC_Final">{{cite web |last=Sanghera |first=Mandeep |date=27 June 2026 |title=Humphries beats Littler to win US title in New York |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/darts/articles/cx245892gpmo |website=BBC Sport |access-date=27 June 2026}}</ref>

Humphries won his third World Series title and became the first player to retain the US Darts Masters.<ref name="Sky_Final"/> "It's great to go back-to-back. I'm always out to set records and do things that not many players have done," said Humphries afterwards, adding that the match was "just another brilliant game in the saga of Luke v Luke".<ref name="BBC_Final"/> Littler described the event as an "unbelievable experience" and acknowledged the English fans in the crowd, who were in attendance before going to the [[England national football team|England national team]]'s match against [[Panama national football team|Panama]] at the [[2026 FIFA World Cup|FIFA World Cup]] the following day.<ref name="Sky_Final"/><ref>{{cite web |last=MacInnes |first=Paul |date=27 June 2026 |title=England enjoy New York minute but fans check out to watch darts instead |url=https://www.theguardian.com/football/2026/jun/26/england-fans-choose-darts-over-joining-world-cup-big-fan-energy |website=The Guardian |access-date=1 July 2026}}</ref>
