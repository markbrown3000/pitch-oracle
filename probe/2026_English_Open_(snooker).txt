The '''2026 English Open''' (officially the '''2026 BetVictor English Open''') was a professional [[snooker]] tournament that took place from 7 to 13 September 2026 at the Brentwood Centre in [[Brentwood, Essex|Brentwood]], England. Qualifying took place on 25 and 26 July at the [[Leicester Arena]] in [[Leicester]], England. The 11th consecutive edition of the tournament since it was first staged in [[2016 English Open (snooker)|2016]], it was the fifth [[List of snooker ranking tournaments|ranking event]] of the [[2026–27 snooker season]], following the [[2026 British Open]] and preceding the [[2026 Shenzhen Open]]. It was the first of four tournaments in the season's [[Home Nations Series]], preceding the [[2026 Northern Ireland Open]], the [[2026 Scottish Open (snooker)|2026 Scottish Open]], and the [[2027 Welsh Open (snooker)|2027 Welsh Open]].

[[Mark Allen (snooker player)|Mark Allen]] won the [[2025 English Open (snooker)|2025 edition]], defeating [[Zhou Yuelong]] 9–8 in the final. However, he withdrew from the 2026 edition for personal reasons.

[[Ali Carter]] won the tournament, beating [[Mark Williams (snooker player)|Mark Williams]] 9–6 in the final.

=== Format ===
The tournament used a tiered format first implemented for the [[Home Nations Series]] in the [[2024–25 snooker season]]. In round one, players seeded 65–96 faced those [[seeding (sports)|seeded]] 97 and below, including selected amateurs. In round two, the winners from round one faced players seeded 33–64. The winners from round two faced the top 32 seeds.<ref>{{cite web |date=5 April 2024 |title=Tiered format for Home Nations and German Masters in 2024/25 |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2024/april/05/tiered-format-for-home-nations-and-german-masters-in-2024-25/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240405185554/https://www.wst.tv/news/2024/april/05/tiered-format-for-home-nations-and-german-masters-in-2024-25/ |archive-date=5 April 2024 |access-date=5 April 2024 |work=[[World Snooker Tour]]}}</ref> All matches were played as the best of 7 {{cuegloss|frame|frames}} until the quarter{{nbh}}finals, which were the best of 11. The semi{{nbh}}finals were the best of 11, and the final was a best{{nbh}}of{{nbh}}17-frame match played over two {{cuegloss|session|sessions}}.<ref name="s.o_M" />

=== Semi-finals ===
The semi-finals were played on 12 September as the best of 11 frames.<ref name="s.o_M" />

[[File:Ali Carter PHC 2015-1.jpg|left|upright=0.85|thumb|alt=picture of Ali Carter|[[Ali Carter]] (''pictured in 2015'') beat [[Ding Junhui]] 6–2.]]
[[File:MarkWilliams2025Masters.jpg|upright=0.85|thumb|alt=picture of Mark Williams|[[Mark Williams (snooker player)|Mark Williams]] (''pictured in 2025'') beat [[Shaun Murphy]] 6–5.]]

In the afternoon session Carter beat Ding 6–2. After the match Carter said: "I felt I was just hanging on in the first four frames, but to get out 2–2 was great as I was always on the back foot. I went out after the interval and played no miss snooker so it was amazing. To get in the top 16 at my ripe old age of 47 wouldn't be too bad would it? There is a lot to play for tomorrow, but I'm going to be in there fighting. Arguably Shaun {{bracket|Murphy}} is one of the best players in the world and Mark Williams is an all-time great. It is going to be really tough but I'm going to savour it. It is brilliant to be here. It goes without saying that it is all about the winning, but it has been a good week to get to another final. I'm still competing with the best players in the world and it is a feather in my cap."<ref>{{cite web |title=Captain set for Essex final |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/september/12/captain-set-for-essex-final/ |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |date=12 September 2026 |access-date=12 September 2026}}</ref>

In the evening session Williams defeated Murphy 6–5. After the match Williams said: "I'm not sure how I managed to win. I looked at the scoreboard, saw 5–1 and thought I was out. I stuck in there and made a few good {{cuegloss|clearance|clearances}}. I got it back to 5–4 and he {{bracket|Murphy}} started to miss a few. It was a great clearance to go 5–5 and an unbelievable break in the {{cuegloss|deciding frame|decider}}. He missed an easy black at 5–2 and I just thought at least if I could clear up I wasn't giving it to him. Those matches coming from 5–1 down probably only happen once in a thousand. I just had to stick in and that was the one time it turned round for me. It has to be up there with my best comebacks. I was 5–1 down and 60 odd behind on two occasions. I'm over the moon to have a crack at the final now."<ref>{{cite web |title=Williams fightback floors Murphy |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/september/12/williams-fightback-floors-murphy/ |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |date=12 September 2026 |access-date=13 September 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |title=Williams to face Carter after semi-final fightback |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/ckgw191xkrlo |work=[[BBC Sport]] |first=Ben |last=Collins |date=13 September 2026 |access-date=13 September 2026}}</ref>

=== Final ===
[[File:Ali Carter 2026 English Open.jpg|thumb|upright=0.5|alt=Ali Carter holding the Steve Davis trophy|[[Ali Carter]] with the Steve Davis trophy]]
The final was played on 13 September as the best of 17 frames, played over two sessions.<ref name="s.o_M" />

At the end of the afternoon session, Williams led Carter 5–3.<ref>{{cite web |title=Williams leads Carter in English Open final |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/september/13/williams-leads-carter-in-english-open-final/ |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |date=13 September 2026 |access-date=13 September 2026}}</ref> However, Carter went on to win the match, and the tournament, 9–6.<ref name="WST_Carter_Williams">{{cite web |title=Match: Ali Carter v Mark Williams |url=https://www.wst.tv/match-centre/0f8ee6d8-4f8f-47ce-aaaa-8ca40e6c5ca1 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |date=13 September 2026 |access-date=14 September 2026 |archive-date=14 September 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260914082200/https://www.wst.tv/match-centre/0f8ee6d8-4f8f-47ce-aaaa-8ca40e6c5ca1 |url-status=live}}</ref> After the match Carter said: "It doesn't feel real at the moment. It hasn't really sunk in, I've not won as many titles as someone like Mark Williams, but it is great. The boys know I can play, that is for sure. In all my ranking events I've never beat one of the all{{nbh}}time greats in the final, to do that today makes it extra special. Not being detrimental to the other players I've beat it has been lower ranked players than me. To play Mark Williams and win in front of my home crowd is a dream come true. I was flying my aeroplane last night. I was getting in it, running the check list, starting it up and going out flying for half an hour. I was getting away from it all. That was nectar for me." Williams said: "I tried my best. I played alright in patches. I didn't play too well tonight, but Ali {{bracket|Carter}} put pressure on me and I didn't handle it very well. He deserved to win. It was a great game and I enjoyed it. I tried my best. The crowd has been unbelievable all week. The conditions have been good, tough at times, but good. Well done and good luck to Ali."<ref name="WST_FinalResult">{{cite web |title=Captain soars to English Open crown |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/september/13/captain-soars-to-english-open-crown/ |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |date=13 September 2026 |access-date=14 September 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |title=Carter beats Williams to claim first English Open |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cly50jq45yeo |work=[[BBC Sport]] |first=Alastair |last=Telfer |date=13 September 2026 |access-date=14 September 2026}}</ref>
