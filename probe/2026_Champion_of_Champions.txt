The '''2026 Champion of Champions''' (officially the '''2026 Sportsbet.io Champion of Champions''') is a professional non-ranking [[snooker]] tournament that is taking place from 9 to 15 November 2026 at the [[Leicester Arena]] in [[Leicester]], England. It features 16 participants, all of whom won significant tournaments since the [[2025 Champion of Champions|previous year's event]]. Part of the [[2026–27 snooker season]], it is the 16th edition of the [[Champion of Champions (snooker)|Champion of Champions]] since the tournament was first held in [[1978 Champion of Champions|1978]] and the 14th consecutive edition since it was revived in [[2013 Champion of Champions|2013]]. The winner will receive £150,000 from a total prize fund of £440,000.

[[Mark Selby]] is the defending champion, having defeated [[Judd Trump]] 10–5 in the 2025 final.

== Overview ==
The [[Champion of Champions (snooker)|Champion of Champions]] is a professional non-ranking [[snooker]] tournament that was first held in [[1978 Champion of Champions|1978]], when it was contested by four players at the [[Wembley Conference Centre]] in London.<ref>{{cite news |date=2 November 1978 |title=Snooker – World champion in semi-final faces Fagan |newspaper=[[The Times]] |page=13}}</ref><ref name="s.o_1978">{{cite web |date=24 November 2023 |title=Daily Mirror Champion of Champions (1978) |url=https://www.snooker.org/res/index.asp?event=1653 |access-date=16 November 2024 |website=snooker.org |archive-date=25 December 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20241225170221/https://www.snooker.org/res/index.asp?event=1653 |url-status=live}}</ref> The inaugural winner was [[Ray Reardon]], who defeated [[Alex Higgins]] 11–9 in the final.<ref name="s.o_1978" /> After its second staging in [[1980 Champion of Champions|1980]], the tournament was not held again until [[2013 Champion of Champions|2013]], since which time it has been staged annually.<ref name="CoC_history">{{cite web |title=History |url=https://championofchampionssnooker.co.uk/history/ |access-date=8 November 2025 |website=championofchampionssnooker.co.uk |archive-date=8 November 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20251108010614/https://championofchampionssnooker.co.uk/history/ |url-status=live}}</ref> As of 2026, the most successful player in its history is [[Ronnie O'Sullivan]], who has won the title four times and been runner-up twice.<ref name="TS_info">{{cite web |last=Day |first=Michael |date=12 April 2024 |title=Champion of Champions Snooker: Information and a potted history |url=https://www.totallysnookered.com/sport/snooker/tournaments/champion-of-champions/champion-of-champions-snooker-information-and-a-potted-history-4585417 |access-date=8 November 2025 |website=totallysnookered.com}}</ref>

The 2026 edition of the tournament—the 16th staging overall and the 14th consecutively since its revival in 2013—is taking place from 9 to 15 November 2026 at the [[Leicester Arena]] in [[Leicester]], England.<ref name="CoC_history" /><ref name="TS_info" /><ref name="s.o_main">{{cite web |title=Champion of Champions 2026 |url=https://www.snooker.org/res/index.asp?event=2723 |website=snooker.org |access-date=24 April 2026}}</ref> Organised by [[Matchroom Sport]], the tournament is sponsored by sports betting company Sportsbet.io.<ref name="CoC_sponsor">{{cite web |url=https://championofchampionssnooker.co.uk/sportsbet-io-marks-return-as-title-sponsor-of-the-2026-champion-of-champions/ |title=Sportsbet.io marks return as title sponsor of the 2026 Champion of Champions |website=championofchampionssnooker.co.uk |date=5 May 2026 |access-date=13 July 2026}}</ref> [[Mark Selby]] is the defending champion, having defeated [[Judd Trump]] 10–5 in the [[2025 Champion of Champions|2025 final]].<ref>{{cite web |date=16 November 2025 |title=Selby beats Trump to become Champion of Champions for first time |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/november/16/Selby-Beats-Trump-To-Become-Champion-Of-Champions-For-First-Time/ |access-date=16 November 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260417072049/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/november/16/Selby-Beats-Trump-To-Become-Champion-Of-Champions-For-First-Time/ |archive-date=17 April 2026 |url-status=live}}</ref>
