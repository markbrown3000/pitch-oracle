The '''2026 World Grand Prix''' (known for sponsorship reasons as the '''2026 BOYLE Sports World Grand Prix''') was a professional [[darts]] tournament that took place at the [[Mattioli Arena]] in [[Leicester]], England, from 28 September to 4 October 2026. It was the 29th staging of the [[World Grand Prix (darts)|World Grand Prix]] by the [[Professional Darts Corporation]] (PDC). The total prize fund was an increased total of £750,000, with the winner receiving £150,000.

Sponsored by [[BoyleSports]], the tournament was played in the World Grand Prix's traditional "[[Glossary of darts#Double in|double in, double out]]" format. It featured 32 players, comprising the top 16 players in the [[PDC World Rankings]] and the top 16 players from the one-year [[PDC Pro Tour]] rankings who had not yet qualified. [[Luke Littler]] was the defending champion, having defeated [[Luke Humphries]] 6–1 in the [[2025 World Grand Prix (darts)|2025 final]], but he lost 2–1 to [[Luke Woodhouse]] in the first round.

Humphries won the tournament for the second time in his fourth consecutive final, defeating [[Gerwyn Price]] 6–4 after going 0–3 down in sets. Price broke the record for the highest average in a World Grand Prix set, averaging 132.62 in the first set of the final.

=== Format ===
The tournament featured 32 players; the top 16 players in the [[PDC World Rankings]] were [[Seeding (sport)|seeded]] and were drawn against the 16 qualifiers from the [[PDC Pro Tour]] rankings in the first round. All matches were in set play format, with each set being played to the best of five legs (first to three). As per the "double in, double out" format, players had to begin a leg by hitting a double or the bullseye and do the same to win a leg.
