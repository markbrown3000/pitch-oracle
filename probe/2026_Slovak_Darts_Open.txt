The '''2026 Slovak Darts Open''' (known for sponsorship reasons as the '''2026 Niké Slovak Darts Open''') was a professional [[darts]] tournament that took place at the [[Incheba]] in [[Bratislava]], Slovakia, from 19 to 21 June 2026.<ref name="SDO">{{cite web |title=Slovak Darts Open 2026 |url=https://www.pdc-europe.tv/tournaments/et-2026-en/slovak-darts-open-2026/ |website=PDC Europe |access-date=20 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=Šipkárska elita na podujatí Niké Slovak Darts Open 2026 |url=https://www.incheba.sk/eventy/sipkarska-elita-na-podujati-nike-slovak-darts-open-2026/ |trans-title=Darts elite at the Niké Slovak Darts Open 2026 event |website=Incheba Expo Bratislava |access-date=20 June 2026 |language=sk}}</ref> It was the ninth of fifteen [[PDC European Tour]] events on the [[2026 PDC Pro Tour]], and the first PDC event to be held in Slovakia.<ref>{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=11 September 2025 |title=European Tour set for further expansion in 2026 with PDC calendar released |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/september/11/european-tour-set-further-expansion-2026-pdc-calendar-released/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=2 June 2026}}</ref> It featured a field of 48 players and £230,000 in prize money, with £35,000 going to the winner.

[[Wessel Nijman]] won his second European Tour title, defeating [[Rob Cross]] 8–3 in the final to become the tournament's inaugural champion.

== Prize money ==
As part of a mass boost in prize money for [[Professional Darts Corporation]] (PDC) events in 2026, the prize fund for all [[2026 PDC Pro Tour#European Tour#European Tour|2026 European Tour]] events rose to £230,000, of which the winner will receive £35,000.<ref>{{cite web |last=Phillips |first=Josh |date=31 March 2025 |title=Biggest prize money increase in PDC history confirmed |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/march/31/biggest-prize-money-increase-pdc-history-confirmed |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=6 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=Slovak Darts Open 2026 |url=https://mastercaller.com/tournaments/slovak-darts-open/2026 |website=Mastercaller |access-date=21 May 2026}}</ref>

== Qualification and format ==
The top 16 players on the two-year [[PDC Order of Merit]] were [[Seeding (sports)|seeded]] and entered the tournament in the second round, while the next 16 highest-ranked players from the one-year [[PDC Pro Tour Order of Merit]] automatically qualified for the first round. The seedings were confirmed on 13 May.<ref>{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=9 April 2026 |title=2026 ET9-10 Seeds & Tour Card Holder qualifier entries confirmed |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/may/15/et9-10-seeds-tour-card-holder-qualifiers-entries/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=20 May 2026}}</ref> The remaining 16 places went to players from four qualifying events – 10 from the Tour Card Holder Qualifier (held on 20 May),<ref>{{cite web |date=20 May 2026 |title=PDC ET09 Tour Card Holder Qualifier |url=https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdceutcq26et09/matches |website=DartConnect |access-date=20 May 2026}}</ref> four from the Host Nation Qualifier (held on 18 June),<ref>{{cite web |date=18 June 2026 |title=PDC ET09 Host Nation Qualifier |url=https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdc26et09hnq/matches |website=DartConnect |access-date=18 June 2026}}</ref> one from the Nordic & Baltic Associate Member Qualifier (held on 24 April),<ref>{{cite web |date=24 April 2026 |title=PDCNB 2026 Tour - Finland - Weekend 03 |url=https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdcnbpro26e03/matches |website=DartConnect |access-date=6 May 2026}}</ref> and one from the East European Associate Member Qualifier (held on 11 April).<ref>{{cite web |date=11 April 2026 |title=PDC ET09 E. Europe Qualifier |url=https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdceeu26et09q/matches |website=DartConnect |access-date=20 May 2026}}</ref>

Before the Tour Card Holder Qualifier, [[James Wade]] withdrew and was replaced by [[Dave Chisnall]]. Before the draw for the tournament, [[Gary Anderson (darts player)|Gary Anderson]] withdrew and was replaced by [[Madars Razma]]. [[Luke Woodhouse]] and [[Damon Heta]] moved up to become the 15th and 16th seed, respectively.<ref name="SDO"/><ref name="Draw">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=18 June 2026 |title=2026 Slovak Darts Open draw & schedule confirmed |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/june/18/2026-slovak-darts-open-draw-schedule-confirmed/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=18 June 2026}}</ref>

== Summary ==
=== First round ===
[[File:Benjamin Pratnemer (horizontal).png|thumb|[[Benjamin Pratnemer]] ''(pictured in 2022)'' defeated [[Justin Hood (darts player)|Justin Hood]] 6–5 in the first round, having trailed his opponent 4–1.]]
The first round (best of 11 [[Leg (darts)|legs]]) was played on 19 June.<ref name="PDC_D1">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=19 June 2026 |title=Super Sykes steals the show at Slovak Darts Open |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/june/19/super-sykes-steals-show-slovak-darts-open/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=20 June 2026}}</ref> The [[2025 Hungarian Darts Trophy]] champion [[Niko Springer]] faced European Tour debutant [[Tom Sykes (darts player)|Tom Sykes]], who recorded a [[three-dart average]] of 103.64 and hit a 148 [[Glossary of darts#Checkout|checkout]] on his way to defeating Springer 6–2. Two other players earned their first win on the European Tour, as [[Jim Long (darts player)|Jim Long]] and [[Tyler Thorpe]] defeated [[Daryl Gurney]] and [[Karel Sedláček]], respectively. "I love the attention, the crowd, all the noise. It's completely different to anything," commented Thorpe, who averaged 103.30 during his win.<ref name="PDC_D1"/> The four Slovak qualifiers were eliminated in the first round: Ján Sliacky, the first Slovak player to compete on the European Tour, lost 6–1 to [[Niels Zonneveld]]; Adrián Dudek landed a 150 checkout but was beaten in a [[Glossary of darts#Decider|deciding leg]] by [[Dave Chisnall]]; Juraj Holub lost 6–3 to [[2018 PDC World Darts Championship|2018]] world champion [[Rob Cross]]; and Gabriel Váraljay suffered a 6–1 defeat to [[Cameron Menzies]].<ref name="PDCE_D1">{{cite web |date=19 June 2026 |title=Slovak Darts Open: Bratislava celebrates European Tour debut |url=https://www.pdc-europe.tv/news/slovak-darts-open-bratislava-celebrates-european-tour-debut/ |website=PDC Europe |access-date=20 June 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |last=Kováčik |first=Miroslav |date=19 June 2026 |title=Slovák spôsobil ošiaľ, bol blízko senzácie. Ukázali sme, že nie sme do počtu, vraví |trans-title=The Slovak caused a stir, was close to a sensation. We showed that we are not in the minority, he says |url=https://sportnet.sme.sk/spravy/sipky-vysledky-dnes-sumar-1-cast-piatok-slovak-darts-open-2026/ |website=Sportnet |access-date=20 June 2026 |language=sk}}</ref> [[Madars Razma]] converted a 154 finish on his way to beating [[Dirk van Duijvenbode]] 6–4, while [[Kim Huybrechts]] hit five [[Glossary of darts#Maximum|maximums]] as he defeated [[Keane Barry]] by the same scoreline.<ref name="PDC_D1"/>

Hungarian qualifier Péter Kelemen, another European Tour debutant, lost 6–3 to [[Joe Cullen (darts player)|Joe Cullen]]. [[Kevin Doets]] averaged 102.49 in his own 6–3 victory over [[Owen Bates]]. [[William O'Connor (darts player)|William O'Connor]] and [[Ryan Joyce]] were also 6–3 winners, earning respective victories against Swedish players [[Jeffrey de Graaf]] and [[Johan Engström]]. [[Andrew Gilding]] secured a 6–4 win over [[Mervyn King (darts player)|Mervyn King]], who made his first European Tour appearance in over two years. [[Benjamin Pratnemer]] initially trailed [[Justin Hood (darts player)|Justin Hood]] 4–1, but won the next four legs to take a 5–4 lead; they went to a deciding leg, where Pratnemer won the match 6–5. [[Krzysztof Ratajski]] beat [[Cristo Reyes]] 6–2 in the round's final match.<ref name="PDCE_D1"/><ref>{{cite web |last=Michael |first=Lucas |date=19 June 2026 |title=Chisnall survives seismic scare against Slovakian qualifier as Doets, Gilding and Joyce all advance |url=https://dartsnews.com/pdc/live-slovak-darts-open-friday-afternoon-chisnall-gilding-doets-headline-inaugural-session-in-bratislava |website=dartsnews.com |access-date=20 June 2026}}</ref>

=== Second round ===
[[File:Mike de Decker 2024.png|thumb|upright=0.9|[[Mike De Decker]] ''(pictured in 2024)'' ended an eight-match losing streak on the European Tour with a 6–2 victory over [[William O'Connor (darts player)|William O'Connor]].]]
The second round (best of 11 legs) was played on 20 June.<ref name="PDC_D2">{{cite web |last=Wagner |first=Connor |date=20 June 2026 |title=Menzies and De Decker shine in Bratislava with statement victories |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/june/20/2026-slovak-darts-open---day-two-latest/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=21 June 2026}}</ref> Cameron Menzies registered a three-dart average of 106 to defeat fourth seed and [[2021 PDC World Darts Championship|2021]] world champion [[Gerwyn Price]] 6–2. Menzies admitted to being nervous in his post-match interview but said that hitting [[Glossary of darts#Treble|trebles]] with his first dart in hand helped him win. He also expressed his love for the host city [[Bratislava]] and added that he hoped to come back.<ref name="PDC_D2"/> [[Mike De Decker]] earned his first win on the European Tour since the first event of the season, the [[2026 Poland Darts Open|Poland Darts Open]], by beating William O'Connor 6–2 with an average of 107.14.<ref name="PDCE_D2">{{cite web |title=Slovak Darts Open: De Decker beendet Durststrecke |trans-title=Slovak Darts Open: De Decker ends dry spell |url=https://www.pdc-europe.tv/de/aktuell/slovak-darts-open-de-decker-beendet-durststrecke/ |website=PDC Europe |access-date=21 June 2026 |language=de |date=20 June 2026}}</ref> Top seed [[Gian van Veen]] was eliminated in a 6–3 defeat to Rob Cross. Three-time world champion [[Michael van Gerwen]] led 4–1 against Krzysztof Ratajski but missed a total of seven [[Glossary of darts#Match dart|match darts]], eventually hitting his eighth to complete a 6–5 win. Similarly, Niels Zonneveld staged a comeback from 5–2 down against [[Stephen Bunting]], who missed seven match darts of his own before prevailing by the same scoreline. In two other last-leg deciders, [[Ross Smith (darts player)|Ross Smith]] overturned a 4–2 deficit to defeat Joe Cullen, while Ryan Joyce eliminated third seed [[Jonny Clayton]], who hit a [[Glossary of darts#Maximum check|170 checkout]] during the match.<ref name="DNews_D2">{{cite web |last=Wood |first=Kieran |date=20 June 2026 |title=Cameron Menzies destroys Gerwyn Price with 106 average as Rob Cross dumps out top seed Van Veen and Michael van Gerwen survives |url=https://dartsnews.com/pdc/slovak-darts-open-2026-roundup-cameron-menzies-destroys-gerwyn-price-with-106-average-as-rob-cross-dumps-out-top-seed-van-veen-and-michael-van-gerwen-survives |website=dartsnews.com |access-date=21 June 2026}}</ref>

Tom Sykes continues his run, defeating thirteenth seed [[Martin Schindler]] in a deciding leg. "I never give up, I never will, I'll always be there or thereabouts, so we'll carry onto tomorrow and see what happens," commented Sykes afterwards.<ref name="PDC_D2"/> Four Dutch players were victorious during the round's afternoon session, joining compatriot Van Gerwen in the third round: Kevin Doets, [[Wessel Nijman]], and [[Danny Noppert]] earned respective 6–2 wins against sixteenth seed [[Damon Heta]], Benjamin Pratnemer, and Madars Razma, while [[Jermaine Wattimena]] defeated [[Dave Chisnall]] 6–3.<ref name="PDCE_D2"/><ref>{{cite web |last=Kováčik |first=Miroslav |date=20 June 2026 |title=Večer priniesol zlyhania favoritov. Angličan básnil o Dúbravkovi, pre neho je legendou |trans-title=The evening brought failures for the favourites. The Englishman waxed poetic about Dúbravka, for him he is a legend |url=https://sportnet.sme.sk/spravy/sipky-vysledky-dnes-sumar-1-cast-sobota-slovak-darts-open-2026/ |website=Sportnet |access-date=21 June 2026 |language=sk}}</ref> Fifteenth seed and recent [[2026 Baltic Sea Darts Open|Baltic Sea Darts Open]] champion [[Luke Woodhouse]] was beaten 6–4 by Andrew Gilding. [[Ryan Searle (darts player)|Ryan Searle]] and [[Nathan Aspinall]] set up a tie against each other in the third round with their wins over Kim Huybrechts and Jim Long, while [[Chris Dobey]] ended the run of Tyler Thorpe with a 6–4 victory.<ref name="PDC_D2"/>

=== Final day ===
[[File:Wessel Nijman 2025 World Matchplay.png|thumb|upright=0.9|[[Wessel Nijman]] ''(pictured in 2025)'' won the inaugural Slovak Darts Open, his second European Tour title.]]
The third round, quarter-finals, semi-finals and final were played on 21 June. The third round and quarter-finals were contested over the best of 11 legs, the semi-finals over the best of 13 legs, and the final over the best of 15 legs.<ref name="PDC_Final">{{cite web |last=Simpson |first=Will |date=21 June 2026 |title=Unassailable Nijman bags inaugural Slovak Darts Open title |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/june/21/Unassailable-Nijman-bags-inaugural-Slovak-Darts-Open-title/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=22 June 2026}}</ref> The final day saw Rob Cross and Wessel Nijman reach the final. Cross began the day by winning a deciding leg against Kevin Doets, followed by a 6–2 victory over Nathan Aspinall in the quarter-finals, marking his first win against his English compatriot in 10 matches.<ref name="PDC_Final"/> Cross advanced to the final with a 7–2 win against Tom Sykes, who became the eighth player to reach the semi-finals of their maiden European Tour event.<ref name="EU_Final">{{cite web |date=21 June 2026 |title=Slovak Darts Open: Nijman crowned inaugural champion in Bratislava |url=https://www.pdc-europe.tv/news/slovak-darts-open-nijman-crowned-inaugural-champion-in-bratislava/ |website=PDC Europe |access-date=22 June 2026}}</ref> Nijman's run started with a 6–1 win over Stephen Bunting and a 6–3 win over Mike De Decker, the latter featuring a 170 checkout and 104.50 average from the victorious Dutchman. He initially led 5–2 against Ross Smith in the semi-finals, but Smith recovered to take the match to a deciding leg, where Nijman converted a 161 checkout to complete a 7–6 victory.<ref name="DNews_Final">{{cite web |last=Wood |first=Kieran |date=21 June 2026 |title=Wessel Nijman crowned first-ever champion in Bratislava after 103 average sinks Rob Cross in final |url=https://dartsnews.com/pdc/slovak-darts-open-2026-roundup-wessel-nijman-crowned-first-ever-champion-in-bratislava-after-103-average-sinks-rob-cross-in-final |website=dartsnews.com |access-date=22 June 2026}}</ref> Nijman and Cross entered the tournament as the respective winners of [[2026 PDC Players Championship series#Players Championship 21|Players Championship 21]] and [[2026 PDC Players Championship series#Players Championship 22|Players Championship 22]] earlier in the week. Cross was aiming to win his third European Tour title in his first final in over two years, while Nijman was looking to win his second European Tour title of the year.<ref name="PDC_Final"/>

Nijman converted a 104 checkout to win the opening leg of the final. Cross found the match's first [[Glossary of darts#Break|break of throw]] to go 2–1 ahead, but Nijman broke back and completed a run of four consecutive legs to establish a 5–2 lead, including a 116 finish. Cross was able to win the next leg to cut the gap to 5–3. Nijman avoided dropping another leg, breaking his opponent's throw in 12 darts to claim the penultimate leg before hitting a 74 checkout in the next to secure an 8–3 victory. Nijman ended the match with a three-dart average of 103.80, compared to Cross' 102.57.<ref name="PDC_Final"/><ref name="EU_Final"/><ref name="DNews_Final"/>

Nijman became the inaugural Slovak Darts Open champion, winning his second European Tour title after his first at the [[2026 European Darts Trophy]] in March.<ref>{{cite web |last=Kováčik |first=Miroslav |date=21 June 2026 |title=Urobil hlúposť, trest ho zmenil. Holanďan oslavuje titul, zdolal aj miláčika Slovákov |trans-title=He did something stupid, the penalty changed him. The Dutchman celebrates the title, he also defeated the darling of the Slovaks |url=https://sportnet.sme.sk/spravy/sipky-vysledky-dnes-sumar-finale-nedela-slovak-darts-open-2026/ |website=Sportnet |access-date=22 June 2026 |language=sk}}</ref> It was his eighth ranking title of 2026 and his 11th PDC ranking title overall.<ref name="EU_Final"/><ref name="SkyResults">{{cite web |date=21 June 2026 |title=Slovak Darts Open: Results as the European Tour visited Bratislava with Wessel Nijman sealing glory |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13555305/slovak-darts-open-schedule-results-as-the-european-tour-visits-bratislava-with-michael-van-gerwen-gerwyn-price-and-stephen-bunting-in-action |website=Sky Sports |access-date=22 June 2026}}</ref> He became the fourth player to claim at least eight [[PDC Pro Tour|Pro Tour]] titles in a single season, after [[Phil Taylor (darts player)|Phil Taylor]], Michael van Gerwen, and [[Peter Wright (darts player)|Peter Wright]]. He also became the first multiple-time champion of the [[2026 PDC Pro Tour#European Tour|2026 European Tour]] season.<ref name="SkyResults"/> His title win placed him at the top of the 2026 European Tour rankings and saw him rise to a new career-high ranking of world number 14 in the [[PDC World Rankings]].<ref name="PDC_Final"/><ref>{{cite web |last=Simpson |first=Will |date=22 June 2026 |title=PDC Werner Rankings Ladder update after Slovak Darts Open |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/june/22/pdc-werner-rankings-ladder-update-after-slovak-darts-open/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=24 June 2026}}</ref> "Sometimes you have those days where you feel this can be a winning day because you feel so confident and today was a day like that," said Nijman after the match.<ref name="EU_Final"/> He added that despite the absences of players like [[Luke Littler]] and [[Luke Humphries]], the tournament still showcased the high level of PDC darts.<ref name="PDC_Final"/> Cross commended his opponent's performance and showed his appreciation for the Bratislava crowd. Discussing his form, he said: "I wouldn't say I'm at my peak at the minute, but I'm starting to get there. I won a title in the week, another final today, and this was good practice for the [[2026 World Matchplay|World Matchplay]]."<ref name="PDC_Final"/>
