The '''2025 UK Championship''' (officially the '''2025 Victorian Plumbing UK Championship''')<ref>{{cite web |date=29 October 2025 |title=Victorian Plumbing returns as title partner of snooker's UK Championship |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/october/28/victorian-plumbing-returns-as-title-partner-of-snooker-s-uk-championship-/ |access-date=29 October 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260422081849/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/october/28/victorian-plumbing-returns-as-title-partner-of-snooker-s-uk-championship-/|archive-date=22 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref> was a professional [[snooker]] tournament that took place from 29&nbsp;November to 7&nbsp;December 2025 at the [[York Barbican]] in [[York]], England. The 49th consecutive edition of the [[UK Championship]] since it was first staged in [[1977 UK Championship|1977]], it was the ninth [[List of snooker ranking tournaments|ranking event]] of the [[2025–26 snooker season]], following the [[2025 International Championship]] and preceding the [[2025 Snooker Shoot Out]]. It was the first of the season's three [[Triple Crown (snooker)|Triple Crown]] events, preceding the [[2026 Masters (snooker)|2026 Masters]] and the [[2026 World Snooker Championship]]. The tournament was broadcast by the [[BBC]] and [[TNT Sports]] domestically, by [[Eurosport]] in mainland Europe, by local channels in China and elsewhere in Asia, and by [[WST Play]] in all other territories. The winner received £250,000 from a total prize fund of £1,205,000.

The top 16 players in the [[snooker world rankings]] were [[Seeding (sports)|seeded]] through to the main stage. An additional 128 players competed in a four‑round qualifying tournament from 22 to 27 November at the Robin Park Leisure Centre in [[Wigan]], England, with higher ranked players given [[Bye (sports)|byes]] to the later rounds. The 16 successful qualifiers advanced to the main stage in York, where they were drawn at random against the 16 seeds. The last 32 featured 12 players from China, a record at any Triple Crown tournament.

[[Judd Trump]] was the defending champion, having defeated [[Barry Hawkins]] 10–8 in the [[2024 UK Championship|2024 final]]. [[Mark Selby]] defeated Trump 10–8 in the final to win his third UK Championship title, following his previous wins in [[2012 UK Championship|2012]] and [[2016 UK Championship|2016]]. It was Selby's 10th Triple Crown title and 25th ranking title, and was his first victory at a Triple Crown tournament since the [[2021 World Snooker Championship]] four and a half years earlier. He became the fourth player in professional snooker history to win 10 or more Triple Crown titles, following [[Steve Davis]], [[Stephen Hendry]], and [[Ronnie O'Sullivan]].

The event produced 118 [[Century break|century breaks]], 83 during the qualifiers in Wigan and 35 at the main stage in York. [[Liam Pullen]] and [[Chang Bingyu]] both made the maiden [[maximum break]]s of their professional careers, in the first and third rounds of qualifying respectively. These were the 15th and 16th maximums of the season, breaking the record of 15 set in the [[2024–25 snooker season|2024–25 season]]. The highest break at the main stage was a 138 by Hawkins in his quarter-final match against Selby.

==== Second qualification round ====
The second qualification round was played on 23 and 24 November. [[Michael Holt (snooker player)|Michael Holt]], a quarter-finalist at the [[2024 UK Championship|previous year's event]], trailed 12-time World Women's Champion [[Reanne Evans]] 1–3 at the mid-{{cuegloss|session}} interval. He made a 131 {{cuegloss|break}} in frame five and tied the scores at 3–3, but Evans restored her two-frame advantage at 5–3. [[File:Judd Trump at Snooker German Masters (Martin Rulsch) 2014-02-01 18 (cropped).jpg|thumb|The defending champion [[Judd Trump]] ''(pictured in 2014)'' defeated [[Neil Robertson]] to reach his fifth [[UK Championship]] final.]]
The semi-finals were played on 6 December.<ref name="TS_draw_schedule" /> For the first time since the [[2009 UK Championship|2009 event]], all four semi-finalists were former champions.<ref>{{Cite tweet |user=davehendon |number=1997086091166310587 |title=First UK Championship since 2009 that all four semi-finalists are previous winners of the title|access-date=22 April 2026}}</ref> In the first semi-final, the defending champion Trump faced the fourth seed Robertson. Trump made breaks of 65 and 82 as he took a 2–1 lead, but Robertson made a century of 114 to tie the scores at 2–2 at the mid-session interval. Robertson moved ahead by winning the 47-minute fifth frame with a {{Cuegloss|cross double}} on the last black, but Trump then made breaks of 72, 53, 68, and 53 as he won four frames in a row for a 6–3 victory. "I still felt positive despite losing the fifth frame," Trump said afterwards. "A lot of the time this season, I'd have put my head down a little bit. I was able to get in early next frame, rattle off a quick frame and I was back in and amongst it." Commenting on his recent defeats in the finals of the 2025 Northern Ireland Open and [[2025 Champion of Champions]], Trump added: "I have been in a few finals recently and it is never nice to be on the losing end. I need to use that as motivation and have the determination to come out on the right end."<ref name="BBC_25-12-06_01">{{Cite web |last=Sutcliffe |first=Steve |date=6 December 2025 |title=Selby beats Murphy to set up UK final with Trump|url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/c39pdllkemxo |access-date=6 December 2025 |website=[[BBC Sport]] |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260422080715/https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/c39pdllkemxo|archive-date=22 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref><ref name="WST_Trump_into_final">{{cite web |date=6 December 2025 |title=Trump swerves the Thunder to make final |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/december/06/trump-swerves-the-thunder-to-make-final/ |access-date=6 December 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260422083724/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/december/06/trump-swerves-the-thunder-to-make-final/|archive-date=22 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Day |first=Michael |date=6 December 2025 |title=World number one & defending champion Judd Trump into the 2025 UK Championship final |url=https://www.totallysnookered.com/sport/snooker/tournaments/uk-championship/world-number-one-and-defending-champion-judd-trump-into-the-2025-uk-championship-final-5432368 |access-date=6 December 2025 |website=Totally Snookered |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260422083720/https://www.totallysnookered.com/sport/snooker/tournaments/uk-championship/world-number-one-and-defending-champion-judd-trump-into-the-2025-uk-championship-final-5432368|archive-date=22 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

In the second semi-final, the 10th seed Murphy faced the 11th seed Selby. A 73 break gave Selby the first frame, but Murphy tied the scores with a century of 131 in frame two. Selby took the third frame after Murphy missed a red with the rest and also won frames four and five with breaks of 73 and 105 to lead 4–1. Murphy won frames six and seven, reducing Selby's lead to one at 4–3, but Selby took frame eight with breaks of 72 and 55 and then secured a 6–3 victory with a century of 127 in frame nine. "I felt good from start to finish," Selby said afterwards, having missed just 12 pots in the match. "I was a little bit edgy at the start but that is understandable. It was a massive game for us both to try and get into a Triple Crown final. At the interval I felt relaxed and thought I was playing decent stuff." Victory for Selby meant that he had won 20 of the 35 professional matches between the two players.<ref name="BBC_25-12-06_01" /><ref name="WST_Selby_into_final">{{cite web |date=6 December 2025 |title=Selby storms to York final |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/december/06/selby-into-york-final/ |access-date=6 December 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260422083715/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/december/06/selby-into-york-final/|archive-date=22 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

====Final====
[[File:Mark Selby 2020.jpg|thumb|[[Mark Selby]] ''(pictured in 2020)'' defeated [[Judd Trump]] 10–8 in the final to win his third [[UK Championship]] title, 10th [[Triple Crown (snooker)|Triple Crown]] title, and 25th [[List of snooker ranking tournaments|ranking]] title.]]
The final was played on 7 December, as the best of 19 frames played over two sessions, between the defending champion and world number one Judd Trump and the world number 11 Mark Selby.<ref name="TS_draw_schedule" /> Trump was competing in the fifth UK Championship final of his career, having previously won the title in [[2011 UK Championship|2011]] and 2024 and been runner-up in [[2014 UK Championship|2014]] and 2020. Selby was contesting his fourth final, having won the title in [[2012 UK Championship|2012]] and 2016 and been runner-up in [[2013 UK Championship|2013]].<ref name="WST_Trump_into_final" /><ref name="WST_Selby_into_final" /> Trump and Selby had played each other three weeks earlier in the 2025 Champion of Champions final, which Selby won 10–5.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Rostance |first=Tom |date=16 November 2025 |title=Selby wins Champion of Champions for first time |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cp8yg5j35lgo |access-date=22 April 2026|website=[[BBC Sport]] |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260422083353/https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cp8yg5j35lgo|archive-date=22 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref> The match was their third meeting in a Triple Crown tournament, following the quarter-finals of the [[2019 Masters (snooker)|2019 Masters]] and the [[2023 UK Championship]]; it was the first time they had faced each other in a Triple Crown final.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Day |first=Michael |date=7 December 2025 |title=Judd Trump v Mark Selby: Preview of Sunday's 2025 UK Snooker Championship final |url=https://www.totallysnookered.com/sport/snooker/tournaments/uk-championship/judd-trump-v-mark-selby-preview-of-sundays-2025-uk-snooker-championship-final-stats-head-to-head-5432567 |access-date=7 December 2025 |website=Totally Snookered |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260422085622/https://www.totallysnookered.com/sport/snooker/tournaments/uk-championship/judd-trump-v-mark-selby-preview-of-sundays-2025-uk-snooker-championship-final-stats-head-to-head-5432567|archive-date=22 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

Selby came from behind to win the first frame with a 77 clearance. He won the second frame after a {{cuegloss|safety}} battle on the last yellow and then made a 97 break to win frame three. Trump led by 68 points in frame four when he missed a red, and Selby took the frame with a 70 clearance. Selby made a century of 105 in the next frame to move 5–0 ahead. Only two players, Higgins in [[2010 UK Championship|2010]] and Allen in 2022, had ever overcome a five-frame deficit in a best-of-19-frame UK Championship final. Trump took frames six and seven with breaks of 74 and 75. In frame eight, Trump required two snookers, as he trailed by 50 points with 43 remaining. Selby left a free ball after failing to escape from a snooker, and Trump {{cuegloss|nominate|nominated}} a green over a {{cuegloss|baulk pocket}}; however, he left the cue ball in the {{Cuegloss|jaws}} after potting it, and then fouled by failing to hit the {{Cuegloss|object ball}} while playing a {{Cuegloss|roll-up}}. Trump {{Cuegloss|concession|conceded}} the frame, and the first session ended with Selby leading 6–2.<ref name="Selbyholds">{{Cite web |last=Sutcliffe |first=Steve |date=7 December 2025 |title=Selby holds off spirited Trump to win third UK title |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cly160wnn1yo |access-date=22 April 2026|website=[[BBC Sport]] |language=en-GB |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260422080320/https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cly160wnn1yo|archive-date=22 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref><ref name="BBC_25-12-07_01">{{Cite web |last=Beardmore |first=Michael |date=7 December 2025 |title=Relive UK Championship final as Selby edges past Trump |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/live/c62d9n0lnxxt |access-date=7 December 2025 |website=[[BBC Sport]] |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260422080435/https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/live/c62d9n0lnxxt|archive-date=22 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |date=7 December 2025 |title=Selby in charge of UK Championship final |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/december/07/selby-in-charge-of-uk-championship-final/ |access-date=7 December 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260422081900/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/december/07/selby-in-charge-of-uk-championship-final/|archive-date=22 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

When play resumed for the second session, Selby won frame nine, again moving five clear at 7–2. Trump took frame ten, helped by a 62 break. In the 38-minute 11th frame, referee [[Olivier Marteel]] awarded Selby a free ball, only to reverse his decision after Trump objected and Selby agreed with his opponent. Trump went on to win the frame. In frame 12, Trump produced his first century of the match, a 109, winning a third consecutive frame and reducing Selby's lead to 7–5. Selby took frame 13 with a break of 91, but Trump won the next with another century of 125. Selby twice missed the black off {{Cuegloss|black spot|its spot}} in frame 15, which Trump won with a 55 break to narrow Selby's lead to one at 8–7. Selby made a 77 break in frame 16 to move one from victory at 9–7. Trump won frame 17 after Selby missed a red. Selby went on to secure a 10–8 victory with a 69 break, winning his third UK Championship title and 25th ranking title. It was his tenth Triple Crown title, surpassing John Higgins's total of nine and making Selby the fourth player in professional snooker history to win ten or more Triple Crown titles, following [[Steve Davis]], [[Stephen Hendry]], and O'Sullivan. It was Selby's first victory at a Triple Crown tournament since winning the [[2021 World Snooker Championship]] four and a half years previously. He advanced from eleventh to sixth place in the world rankings after the tournament.<ref name="BBC_25-12-07_01" /><ref>{{Cite web |last=Day |first=Michael |date=7 December 2025 |title=Mark Selby holds on to complete hat-trick of UK Snooker Championship crowns in York |url=https://www.totallysnookered.com/sport/snooker/tournaments/uk-championship/mark-selby-holds-on-to-complete-hat-trick-of-uk-snooker-championship-crowns-in-york-5432938 |access-date=7 December 2025 |website=Totally Snookered |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260422081856/https://www.totallysnookered.com/sport/snooker/tournaments/uk-championship/mark-selby-holds-on-to-complete-hat-trick-of-uk-snooker-championship-crowns-in-york-5432938|archive-date=22 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref><ref name="Selbyholds"/><ref name="WST_Selby_win">{{cite web |date=7 December 2025 |title=Selby captures UK crown in York |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/december/07/selby-capture-uk-crown-in-york/ |access-date=7 December 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260422081854/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/december/07/selby-capture-uk-crown-in-york/|archive-date=22 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

"I missed a dolly [i.e., easy] red when I was in to win 10–7 and I was sitting in my chair thinking that if I lose 10–9 that will haunt me forever," Selby commented after the final. "It would have been worse than the [[Willie Thorne]] blue! [A shot missed by Thorne in the [[1985 UK Championship]] final.] To make the break in the last, it is probably one of the best breaks of my career." Trump's loss was his third defeat in a UK Championship final and meant that he had failed to win an event in a calendar year for the first time since 2013. "I had a lot of chances this evening," Trump said afterwards. "It is my own fault and I missed too many easy balls. It isn't easy to get over the line in these big events and I was in a similar situation last year. I hoped that I would find my best but it didn't happen."<ref name="WST_Selby_win" />
