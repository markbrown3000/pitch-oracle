The '''2025 PDC World Cup of Darts''' (known for sponsorship reasons as the '''2025 [[BetVictor]] World Cup of Darts''') was a professional [[darts]] tournament, held from 12 to 15 June 2025 at the [[Eissporthalle Frankfurt|Eissporthalle]] in [[Frankfurt]], Germany. It was the fifteenth edition of the [[PDC World Cup of Darts]] to be staged by the [[Professional Darts Corporation]]. The tournament featured 40 national teams; the top four nations based on lowest combined [[PDC Order of Merit]] ranking entered in the second round, while the remaining 36 teams started in the group stage. The total prize fund was £450,000, with the winning team receiving £80,000.

[[2013 PDC World Cup of Darts|2013]] finalists [[List of PDC World Cup of Darts teams#Belgium|Belgium]] were eliminated in the group stage, marking the nation's earliest exit at the tournament. [[List of PDC World Cup of Darts teams#Argentina|Argentina]] were the only nation to make their first appearance, qualifying from their group on debut.

[[List of PDC World Cup of Darts teams#England|England]], represented by [[Luke Humphries]] and [[Luke Littler]], were the defending champions after Humphries and [[Michael Smith (darts player)|Michael Smith]] defeated [[List of PDC World Cup of Darts teams#Austria|Austria]] 10–6 in the [[2024 PDC World Cup of Darts|2024 final]]. However, they lost to [[List of PDC World Cup of Darts teams#Germany|Germany]] 8–4 in the second round.

[[List of PDC World Cup of Darts teams#Northern Ireland|Northern Ireland]], represented by [[Josh Rock]] and [[Daryl Gurney]], won their first ever World Cup title by defeating [[List of PDC World Cup of Darts teams#Wales|Wales]] 10–9 in the final.

==Overview==
===Background===
The 2025 [[PDC World Cup of Darts]] was the fifteenth edition of the tournament to be staged by the [[Professional Darts Corporation]] since the first edition in [[2010 PDC World Cup of Darts|2010]]. It was introduced as part of PDC chairman [[Barry Hearn]]'s plans to expand professional darts, which he initiated after his offer to acquire the [[British Darts Organisation]] was rejected.<ref>{{cite web |date=16 February 2010 |title=PDC launch World Cup |url=https://www.skysports.com/more-sports/news/12288/5954069/pdc-launch-world-cup |website=Sky Sports |access-date=5 June 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210913130307/https://www.skysports.com/more-sports/news/12288/5954069/pdc-launch-world-cup |archive-date=13 September 2021 |url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |date=31 January 2010 |title=BDO counties reject takeover bid by Barry Hearn's PDC |url=https://news.bbc.co.uk/sport2/hi/other_sports/darts/8490421.stm |website=BBC Sport |access-date=5 June 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20231120143003/http://news.bbc.co.uk/sport2/hi/other_sports/darts/8490421.stm |archive-date=20 November 2023 |url-status=live}}</ref> The first edition was held at the Rainton Meadows Arena in [[Houghton-le-Spring]], England, and was won by the [[List of PDC World Cup of Darts teams#Netherlands|Netherlands]] ([[Raymond van Barneveld]] and [[Co Stompé]]).<ref>{{cite web |last=Ashdown |first=Richard |date=14 June 2023 |title=World Cup of Darts - The Power of Pairs |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2023/06/14/world-cup-darts-power-pairs |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230614122102/https://www.pdc.tv/news/2023/06/14/world-cup-darts-power-pairs |url-status=dead |archive-date=June 14, 2023 |website=PDC |access-date=5 June 2025}}</ref> The tournament has since been exclusively held in Germany, with the exception of [[2020 PDC World Cup of Darts|2020]] which was held in Austria. 

The 2025 edition was held from 12 to 15 June 2025 at the [[Eissporthalle Frankfurt|Eissporthalle]] in [[Frankfurt]] for the fourth consecutive year.<ref>{{cite web |date=4 June 2025 |title=World Cup of Darts: Luke Littler and Luke Humphries to lead Team England at Frankfurt's Eissporthalle from June 12-15 |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13379121/world-cup-of-darts-luke-littler-and-luke-humphries-to-lead-team-england-at-frankfurts-eissporthalle-from-june-12-15 |website=Sky Sports |access-date=5 June 2025}}</ref> [[Gibraltar]]-based online gambling company [[BetVictor]] continued its sponsorship of the event after becoming title sponsor the previous year.<ref>{{cite web |date=3 May 2024 |title=BetVictor confirmed as World Cup of Darts title sponsor |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2024/may/03/betvictor-confirmed-world-cup-darts-title-sponsor |website=PDC |access-date=5 June 2025}}</ref>

[[List of PDC World Cup of Darts teams#England|England]] were the defending champions after [[Luke Humphries]] and [[Michael Smith (darts player)|Michael Smith]] defeated the [[List of PDC World Cup of Darts teams#Austria|Austrian]] pair of [[Mensur Suljović]] and [[Rowby-John Rodriguez]] 10–6 in the [[2024 PDC World Cup of Darts|2024 final]], marking England's first triumph since [[2016 PDC World Cup of Darts|2016]].<ref>{{cite web |date=30 June 2024 |title=Humphries stars as England win World Cup of Darts |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/darts/articles/c3gvl924gg1o |website=BBC Sport |access-date=12 May 2025}}</ref> For the 2025 edition, Humphries teamed with reigning world champion and World Cup debutant [[Luke Littler]]. Tournament sponsor BetVictor unveiled England as pre-tournament favourites at [[odds]] of 4/11.<ref>{{cite web |last=Allen |first=Dave |date=4 June 2025 |title=England odds-on for BetVictor World Cup glory |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/june/04/england-odds-betvictor-world-cup-glory |website=PDC |access-date=5 June 2025}}</ref>

===Format===
The 40-team format remained. The top four [[Seeding (sport)|seeded]] teams received a [[Bye (sports)|bye]] to the second round, while the other 36 competed in a group stage of twelve groups of three, with one qualifying from each group.

In this format, all rounds are a single match played in doubles format:<ref name="BBCPre">{{cite web |last=Colman |first=Jonty |date=10 June 2025 |title=How does the World Cup of Darts work? |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/darts/articles/cn4qdxenpw2o |website=BBC Sport |access-date=12 June 2025}}</ref>

*'''Group stage:''' Best of seven legs
*'''Second round, quarter and semi-finals:''' Best of fifteen legs
*'''Final:''' Best of nineteen legs

==Summary==
===Groups===
In each of the three group stage sessions, one match was played from each group. The loser of the first match played in the second match, while the winner of the first match played in the third match, ensuring there were no [[Dead rubber|dead rubbers]]. In each session, the matches were played in the following group order: G, J, K, D, F, I, E, B, A, C, H, L.<ref name="GroupSchedule">{{cite web |date=12 June 2025 |title=World Cup of Darts draw, fixtures, schedule, teams and pairings: Luke Littler and Luke Humphries to lead Team England |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13382458/world-cup-of-darts-draw-fixtures-schedule-teams-and-pairings-luke-littler-and-luke-humphries-to-lead-team-england |website=Sky Sports |access-date=12 June 2025}}</ref> The first session of group stage matches took place on 12 June. The Netherlands, represented by [[Danny Noppert]] and debutant [[Gian van Veen]], began their campaign with a 4–0 [[Whitewash (sport)|whitewash]] win over Italy. Host nation Germany won 4–2 against Portugal, while [[2024 PDC World Cup of Darts|2024]] finalists Austria defeated Spain 4–1.<ref name="PDCDay1">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=12 June 2025 |title=Netherlands dominate on Day One at BetVictor World Cup of Darts |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/june/12/netherlands-dominate-day-one-betvictor-world-cup-darts |website=PDC |access-date=12 June 2025}}</ref> North America ended the session without a win, as the United States and Canada were beaten by Hong Kong and Malaysia respectively.<ref name="SkyDay1">{{cite web  |date=12 June 2025 |title=World Cup of Darts: Netherlands, Germany, Republic of Ireland among first-night winners in group stage |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13382793/world-cup-of-darts-netherlands-germany-republic-of-ireland-among-first-night-winners-in-group-stage |website=Sky Sports |access-date=12 June 2025}}</ref> Japan's 4–3 win over Croatia was the third of a trio of Asian victories, with the pairing of Ryusei Azemoto and Tomoya Goto coming back from 3–1 down and surviving [[Glossary of darts#match dart|match darts]] to triumph in a deciding leg. Sweden, the Czech Republic, Ireland, Poland, Belgium and New Zealand were also victorious in their opening group stage matches.<ref name="PDCDay1"/>

In the second session of group stage matches (13 June), the Philippines, Singapore, Denmark, Australia and Argentina won their first matches, setting up winner-take-all matches for session three in their respective groups.<ref name="DNSession2">{{cite web |last=Clucas |first=Jack |date=13 June 2025 |title=Paul Lim rolls back the years as Singapore and Argentina impress at the World Cup of Darts|url=https://dartsnews.com/pdc/paul-lim-rolls-back-the-years-as-singapore-and-argentina-impress-at-the-world-cup-of-darts |website=Darts News |access-date=13 June 2025}}</ref> Argentina's win was their first victory at the World Cup. As a result of their losses, Canada and Finland became the first seeds to be eliminated, losing both games alongside unseeded teams Latvia, Portugal and Spain. Lithuania and Norway were also eliminated by leg difference despite Lithuania winning a match and Norway still having to play Poland in the final session.<ref name="DNSession2"/> Italy, Gibraltar, South Africa, the United States, Chinese Taipei and Croatia also won their first matches.<ref name="PDCDay2">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=13 June 2025 |title=Philippines dump out Belgium on Day Two in Frankfurt |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/june/13/philippines-dump-out-belgium-day-two-frankfurt |website=PDC |access-date=13 June 2025}}</ref>

[[File:Team Netherlands Darts WC 2025.png|thumb|The [[List of PDC World Cup of Darts teams#Netherlands|Netherlands]], represented by [[Danny Noppert]] and [[Gian van Veen]], won Group A without losing a leg.]]
The final group stage session (13 June) saw five more seeds eliminated, with the Netherlands, Germany and the Czech Republic being the only seeds to qualify by winning both their games. The Netherlands won Group A without losing a leg as they won 4–0 against Hungary.<ref>{{cite web |date=13 June 2025 |title=Nederland zonder problemen naar tweede ronde World Cup of Darts |trans-title=The Netherlands advances to the second round of the World Cup of Darts without any problems |url=https://www.telegraaf.nl/sport/darts/nederland-zonder-problemen-naar-tweede-ronde-world-cup-of-darts/71874794.html |website=De Telegraaf |access-date=13 June 2025 |language=nl}}</ref> Germany defeated Singapore (71-year-old [[Paul Lim]] and his partner Phuay Wei Tan) to top Group C; they were drawn to play tournament favourites England in the second round.<ref>{{cite web |date=13 June 2025 |title=Deutsches Duo im Achtelfinale - Nun gegen England |trans-title=German duo in the round of 16 - now against England |url=https://www.zeit.de/news/2025-06/13/sieg-im-generationen-duell-deutsches-duo-im-achtelfinale |website=Zeit Online |access-date=13 June 2025 |language=de}}</ref> The Czech Republic beat India 4–2 to seal their qualification. Sweden and Ireland were the only other seeds to qualify.<ref name="DNSession3">{{cite web |last=Thomson |first=Milly |date=13 June 2025 |title=Belgian blow 3-0 lead to crash out of World Cup of Darts on dramatic night as Netherlands, Australia and Germany power through |url=https://dartsnews.com/pdc/belgian-blow-3-0-lead-to-crash-out-of-world-cup-of-darts-on-dramatic-night-as-netherlands-australia-and-germany-power-through |website=Darts News |access-date=13 June 2025}}</ref> After Gibraltar beat China 4–2 and China surprisingly beat Ireland by the same scoreline, Ireland progressed from Group D based on legs won [[Glossary of darts#Break|against the throw]].<ref name="SkyDay2">{{cite web |date=13 June 2025 |title=World Cup of Darts: Philippines shock Belgium as England pair Luke Littler and Luke Humphries draw Germany |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13383367/world-cup-of-darts-philippines-shock-belgium-as-england-pair-luke-littler-and-luke-humphries-draw-germany |website=Sky Sports |access-date=13 June 2025}}</ref> In Group G, France suffered a 4–3 loss to Lithuania but were 4–3 winners against Sweden, with Sweden advancing to the knockout stage based on leg difference. After winning their opening match 4–2 against Latvia earlier in the day, the Philippines qualified from Group B through a 4–3 upset victory over Belgium. The pairing of [[Lourence Ilagan]] and [[Paolo Nebrida]] came back from 3–0 down and survived seven missed match darts from [[Mike De Decker]] and [[Dimitri Van den Bergh]]. It marked the earliest exit for Belgium in the tournament's history. Ilagan commented, "It's not easy to play against two of the best players in the PDC, but we just tried to play our own game and we trusted each other."<ref name="SkyDay2"/> The 2024 finalists Austria were also eliminated, losing 4–1 to group winners and former champions Australia ([[Damon Heta]] and [[Simon Whitlock]]). Hong Kong and Switzerland won their matches to progress, while Norway's defeat of Poland allowed South Africa to qualify from Group E. Malaysia qualified for the second round for the first time, defeating Denmark, while Argentina – the edition's only debuting nation – beat New Zealand in the final match to qualify for the knockout stage.<ref name="DNSession3"/><ref name="SkyDay2"/>

===Second round===
[[File:Team Germany Darts WC 2025.png|thumb|[[Ricardo Pietreczko]] and [[Martin Schindler]] of [[List of PDC World Cup of Darts teams#Germany|Germany]] eliminated defending champions [[List of PDC World Cup of Darts teams#England|England]] in the second round.]]
The second round took place on 14 June. In the afternoon session, fourth seeds Northern Ireland ([[Josh Rock]] and [[Daryl Gurney]]) began their run by defeating South Africa 8–2, while Ireland beat Switzerland 8–3, setting up a clash between the two winners in the quarter-finals.<ref>{{cite web |last=McNabb |first=Johnny |date=14 June 2025 |title=Northern Ireland set up quarter-final clash against the Republic of Ireland at the World Cup of Darts |url=https://www.newsletter.co.uk/sport/other-sport/northern-ireland-set-up-quarter-final-clash-against-the-republic-of-ireland-at-the-world-cup-of-darts-5177269 |website=News Letter |access-date=14 June 2025}}</ref> In the session's other contests, Hong Kong won 8–4 against Sweden and the Czech Republic defeated Malaysia 8–3.<ref>{{cite web |date=14 June 2025 |title=Republic of Ireland to face Northern Ireland in World Cup of Darts quarter-finals, England beaten by Germany |url=https://www.rte.ie/sport/darts/2025/0614/1518474-ireland-to-face-north-in-world-cup-of-darts-1-4-finals/ |website=RTÉ |access-date=14 June 2025 |agency=PA}}</ref> In the evening session, defending champions England ([[Luke Humphries]] and [[Luke Littler]]) were beaten by Germany in a shock defeat. With the score level at 2–2, Germany won five consecutive legs to take an unassailable 7–2 lead as the German crowd loudly booed the English team. [[Ricardo Pietreczko]] secured the 8–4 victory with a 62 [[Glossary of darts#Checkout|checkout]]. [[Martin Schindler]] stated in Germany's post-match interview, "We are delighted to go through. German darts is in good shape right now and we will continue to push forward."<ref>{{cite web |date=14 June 2025 |title=England knocked out of World Cup of Darts by Germany |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/darts/articles/cn05d0e80nwo |website=BBC Sport |access-date=14 June 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |last=Rathborn |first=Jack |date=14 June 2025 |title=Luke Littler and Luke Humphries booed before stunning World Cup of Darts upset |url=https://www.independent.co.uk/sport/darts/luke-humphries-luke-littler-world-cup-darts-england-germany-b2770210.html |website=The Independent |access-date=14 June 2025}}</ref> The Netherlands continued to whitewash their opposition as they defeated former champions Scotland ([[Gary Anderson (darts player)|Gary Anderson]] and [[Peter Wright (darts player)|Peter Wright]]) 8–0 to extend their leg-winning streak to 16. Gian van Veen commented, "Before tonight I had never beaten Peter or Gary. They are two legends of the game, so to do the double and beat them both is amazing!"<ref name="PDCR2">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=14 June 2025 |title=Inspired Germany dump out England at BetVictor World Cup of Darts |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/june/14/inspired-germany-dump-out-england-betvictor-world-cup-darts |website=PDC |access-date=14 June 2025}}</ref> Two-time champions Wales ([[Jonny Clayton]] and [[Gerwyn Price]]) advanced by beating the Philippines 8–2 and Australia also went through, defeating Argentina 8–1.<ref name="PDCR2"/>

===Quarter-finals, semi-finals and final===
The quarter-finals, semi-finals and final all took place on 15 June. Northern Ireland progressed to the semi-finals by overturning a 5–4 deficit to defeat Ireland 8–5.<ref>{{cite web |date=15 June 2025 |title=Ireland lose derby World Cup quarter-final to the North |url=https://www.rte.ie/sport/darts/2025/0615/1518580-ireland-lose-derby-world-cup-quarter-final-to-the-north/ |website=RTÉ |access-date=15 June 2025}}</ref> In an interview afterwards, Daryl Gurney described the match as "a great advert for Irish darts".<ref name="BBCF">{{cite web |last1=Keogh |first1=Francis |last2=Cartwright |first2=Phil |date=15 June 2025 |title=Northern Ireland lift World Cup with epic last-leg win over Wales |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/darts/articles/c79e9qe8j8lo |website=BBC Sport |access-date=15 June 2025}}</ref> Damon Heta missed a match dart to seal an 8–6 victory for Australia over Germany, allowing Germany to come back and win the match in a deciding leg. Wales recorded a [[three-dart average]] of 99.66 to beat Hong Kong 8–4. The Netherlands lost their first legs of the tournament to the Czech Republic but still emerged as 8–2 victors.<ref name="BBCF"/>

In the semi-finals, Northern Ireland were dominant in an 8–1 triumph against Germany, while Wales came back from 4–2 down to beat the Netherlands 8–5, setting up a final between fourth seeds Northern Ireland and second seeds Wales. Northern Ireland were taking part in their first World Cup final, while Wales were looking to win their third title.<ref name="BBCF"/>

[[File:Team NI Winner's Press Conference 2025.png|thumb|[[Northern Ireland at the PDC World Cup|Northern Ireland]]'s [[Daryl Gurney]] and [[Josh Rock]] were crowned champions after defeating [[Wales at the PDC World Cup|Wales]] 10–9.]]
In the final (best of 19 legs), Wales established a 7–5 lead to go three legs away from victory, but Northern Ireland claimed the next four to go 9–7 ahead. With the score at 9–8, Northern Ireland missed four match darts and Wales took the leg to level the match at 9–9 – a last-leg shootout to determine the champion. In the deciding leg, Northern Ireland secured the title by winning in 11 darts,<ref name="SkyF">{{cite web |date=15 June 2025 |title=World Cup of Darts: Josh Rock and Daryl Gurney inspire Northern Ireland to glory over Wales |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13384084/world-cup-of-darts-josh-rock-and-daryl-gurney-inspire-northern-ireland-to-glory-over-wales |website=Sky Sports |access-date=15 June 2025}}</ref> with Daryl Gurney hitting the match-winning double eight.<ref name="BBCF"/> Northern Ireland claimed their first World Cup title and became the sixth nation to win the tournament, as well as the first new nation to triumph since Australia in [[2022 PDC World Cup of Darts|2022]].<ref>{{cite web |last=Phillips |first=Josh |date=15 June 2025 |title=Northern Ireland claim historic first World Cup of Darts title |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/june/15/northern-ireland-claim-historic-first-world-cup-darts-title |website=PDC |access-date=15 June 2025}}</ref> Gurney expressed his pride in winning the tournament alongside Josh Rock, claiming: "I put this in higher regard than I did winning an individual event," before adding that he thought he would have a sore head the next morning. Rock also conveyed his delight by saying, "It's a privilege to represent your country. To make history and to win a World Cup for Northern Ireland is unreal. There will always be a star on our shirt from now on. I'm so proud of that." Gurney expressed his gratitude to his Welsh opponents, who applauded the Northern Irish team post-match.<ref>{{cite web |last=Bailie |first=Keith |date=15 June 2025 |title=Daryl Gurney and Josh Rock win the World Cup of Darts for NI: 'I'll have a sore head in the morning' |url=https://www.belfasttelegraph.co.uk/sport/daryl-gurney-and-josh-rock-win-the-world-cup-of-darts-for-ni-ill-have-a-sore-head-in-the-morning/a562708981.html |website=Belfast Telegraph |access-date=15 June 2025}}</ref> Jonny Clayton congratulated the winners and declared his admiration for Welsh teammate Gerwyn Price: "Gezzy is a class act, I'm a proud man to have a teammate like him." Price talked highly of Northern Ireland's performance before saying, "At 9–9 we gave ourselves a chance but they were a bit better than us and they deserve it."<ref name="SkyF"/>

==Group stage==
The group stage draw was announced on 4 June.<ref name="GroupDraw">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=4 June 2025 |title=2025 BetVictor World Cup of Darts group stage draw confirmed |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/june/04/2025-betvictor-world-cup-darts-group-stage-draw-confirmed |website=PDC |access-date=4 June 2025}}</ref> Bracketed numbers in the tables show the seedings for the top twelve teams in the group stage. The figures to the right of a nation's name state their three-dart average in a match. Nations in bold denote match winners.<ref>{{cite web |title=Groupstage of PDC World Cup 2025 |url=https://mastercaller.com/tournaments/pdc-world-cup/2025/groupstage |website=Mastercaller |access-date=12 June 2025}}</ref>

''All group matches were best of seven legs'' <br /> ''After three games, the team that finished top in each group qualified for the knockout stage'' <br /> ''If teams were tied on points after all the matches were completed, the ties were broken based on leg difference, then legs for, then legs against, then legs won against the throw. If they remained tied, then a team's overall [[three-dart average]] was taken into account.''

NB: P = Played; W = Won; L = Lost; LF = Legs for; LA = Legs against; +/- = Leg difference; Pts = Points

The figures to the right of a nation's name state their three-dart average in a match.
