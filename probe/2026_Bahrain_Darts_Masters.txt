The '''2026 Bahrain Darts Masters''' was a professional [[darts]] tournament that was held at Exhibition World Bahrain in [[Sakhir]], Bahrain, from 15 to 16 January 2026. It was the fourth staging of the tournament by the [[Professional Darts Corporation]] (PDC) since the [[2023 Bahrain Darts Masters|inaugural edition]] in 2023, and was the first event in the [[2026 World Series of Darts]].<ref>{{cite web |last=Phillips |first=Josh |date=5 October 2025 |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/october/05/2026-world-series-darts-schedule-confirmed |title=2026 World Series of Darts schedule confirmed |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=4 December 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=Bahrain Darts Masters |url=https://www.bahraingp.com/blog/events/bahrain-darts-masters/ |access-date=7 January 2026 |work=Bahrain GP}}</ref> The winner received £30,000 from a total prize fund of £100,000.

The tournament featured 16 players (eight PDC representatives and eight Asian representatives). [[Stephen Bunting]] was the defending champion, having defeated [[Gerwyn Price]] 8–4 in the [[2025 Bahrain Darts Masters|2025 final]].<ref>{{cite web |last=Wellens |first=Megan |date=18 January 2025 |title=Bahrain Darts Masters: Stephen Bunting beats Gerwyn Price to be crowned champion as Luke Littler exits in quarter-finals |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13290818/bahrain-darts-masters-stephen-bunting-beats-gerwyn-price-to-be-crowned-champion-as-luke-littler-exits-in-quarter-finals |access-date=7 January 2026 |publisher=Sky Sports}}</ref> However, he lost 6–4 to [[Michael van Gerwen]] in the quarter-finals. Van Gerwen went on to win the tournament for the first time, his 17th [[World Series of Darts|World Series]] title, by defeating [[Gian van Veen]] 8–6 in the final.

Eight elite PDC representatives were drawn to play eight Asian representatives in the first round on Thursday 15 January; the quarter-finals, semi-finals and final took place on Friday 16 January.<ref name="Draw">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=14 January 2026 |title=Littler to face Lim at 2026 Bahrain Darts Masters |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/january/14/littler-face-lim-2026-bahrain-darts-masters |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=14 January 2026}}</ref>

*'''Semi-finals:''' Best of thirteen legs
*'''Final:''' Best of fifteen legs

===Broadcasts===
The tournament was broadcast on [[ITV4]] and [[ITVX]] in the United Kingdom. It was the first televised tournament on [[ITV4]] of the new multi-year broadcast deal with the PDC. It was also available for subscribers outside of Germany, Austria and Switzerland on the PDC's streaming service, PDCTV.<ref name="PDCPreview">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=14 January 2026 |title=2026 Bahrain Darts Masters preview |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/january/14/2026-bahrain-darts-masters-preview |access-date=15 January 2026}}</ref><ref name="ITVCoverage">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=15 January 2026 |title=New-look line-up unveiled for ITV's 2026 darts coverage |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/january/15/new-look-line-unveiled-itvs-2026-darts-coverage |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=15 January 2026}}</ref>

==Summary==
===First round===
[[File:2025-04-03 Premier League Darts Berlin 2025 by Sandro Halank–073.jpg|thumb|[[Luke Humphries]] ''(pictured in 2025)'' defeated Bahraini qualifier Abdulla Saeed 6–0 for the third consecutive year.]]
The first round took place on 15 January, where all eight PDC representatives won their opening match.<ref name="PDCDay1">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=15 January 2026 |title=Littler powers past Lim with blistering Bahrain display |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/january/15/littler-powers-past-lim-blistering-bahrain-display |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=15 January 2026}}</ref> Competing in his first match since winning the [[2026 PDC World Darts Championship|2026 World Championship]], 18-year-old [[Luke Littler]] faced 71-year-old [[Paul Lim]]. Littler hit six of eight attempts at [[Glossary of darts#Double|double]] and converted a [[Glossary of darts#Maximum check|170 checkout]] on his way to a 6–1 win, recording a [[three-dart average]] of 106.50.<ref name="SkyDay1">{{cite web |last=Stafford |first=Ali |date=15 January 2026 |title=Bahrain Darts Masters: Luke Littler thrashes Paul Lim in first match since World Darts Championship success as Luke Humphries also progresses |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12040/13494687/bahrain-darts-masters-luke-littler-thrashes-paul-lim-in-first-match-since-world-darts-championship-success-as-luke-humphries-also-progresses |website=Sky Sports |access-date=15 January 2026}}</ref> "Paul [Lim]'s doing well. He's been doing well for many years," said Littler, who also dismissed the possibility of him playing darts once he reaches Lim's age.<ref>{{cite web |last=Veal |first=Jonathan |date=15 January 2026 |title=Luke Littler makes short work of veteran Paul Lim in Bahrain Masters |url=https://www.independent.co.uk/sport/luke-littler-middle-east-stephen-bunting-bahrain-michael-van-gerwen-b2901518.html |website=The Independent |access-date=15 January 2026}}</ref> [[Gerwyn Price]], the [[2025 Bahrain Darts Masters|2025 Bahrain Masters]] runner-up, defeated Motomu Sakai 6–4 to set up a tie against Littler in the next round.<ref name="PDCDay1"/> [[Luke Humphries]] faced Abdulla Saeed for the third consecutive year, only allowing Saeed one dart at double as he claimed another [[Whitewash (sport)|whitewash]] victory against the Bahraini qualifier. "I want to come here and start the season a little bit better than I usually do, and I've tried my best to keep in the flow with my practice this year," stated Humphries after the match.<ref name="SkyDay1"/>

The defending champion [[Stephen Bunting]] beat Ryusei Azemoto 6–2, while [[Michael van Gerwen]] overturned a 4–3 deficit to defeat [[2025 in PDC#Asian Tour|2025 Asian Tour]] winner Alexis Toylo 6–4.<ref name="PDCDay1"/> In a rematch of their meeting at the 2026 World Championship, [[Nathan Aspinall]] claimed a 6–4 win over [[Lourence Ilagan]], the reigning [[2025 in PDC#Asian Championship|Asian champion]]. Making his third appearance at the event, Bahrain's Basem Mahmood won a [[Leg (darts)|leg]] for the first time before losing 6–1 to [[Danny Noppert]]. World Championship runner-up [[Gian van Veen]] defeated [[Man Lok Leung]] 6–2.<ref name="SkyDay1"/>

===Quarter-finals, semi-finals and final===
[[File:2022-06-13 Play-offs (2022 Premier League Darts) by Sandro Halank–092.jpg|thumb|[[Michael Van Gerwen]] won his 17th [[World Series of Darts|World Series]] title, his first in Bahrain.]]
The quarter-finals, semi-finals and final took place on 16 January.<ref name="PDCDay2">{{cite web |last=Simpson |first=Will |date=16 January 2026 |title=Van Gerwen blazes to maiden Bahrain Darts Masters title |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/january/16/van-gerwen-blazes-maiden-bahrain-darts-masters-title |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=16 January 2026}}</ref> Gerwyn Price defeated Luke Littler 6–2, eliminating the reigning world champion in the quarter-finals for the second straight year. The loss marked the end of Littler's 21-match winning streak, with his last defeat being at [[2025 PDC Players Championship series#Players Championship 34|Players Championship 34]] on 30 October 2025.<ref>{{cite web |last=Abraham |first=Timothy |date=16 January 2026 |title=Price beats Littler to end 21-match winning streak |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/darts/articles/c5yvjddj74vo |website=BBC Sport |access-date=16 January 2026}}</ref> Stephen Bunting's title defence ended as he lost 6–4 to Michael van Gerwen. Nathan Aspinall trailed Luke Humphries 3–1 but won the next five legs to claim a 6–3 victory. The last quarter-final match between Gian van Veen and Danny Noppert saw a brief halt in the action due to a power outage; Van Veen went on to beat his Dutch compatriot 6–1.<ref>{{cite web |last=Walker |first=Mark |date=16 January 2026 |title=Michael van Gerwen gets better of Gian van Veen to win Bahrain Masters |url=https://www.independent.ie/sport/other-sports/darts/michael-van-gerwen-gets-better-of-gian-van-veen-to-win-bahrain-masters/a2016032410.html |website=Irish Independent |access-date=16 January 2026 |agency=Press Association}}</ref> In the semi-finals, Van Gerwen defeated Price 7–2 and Van Veen earned a 7–4 win against Aspinall, setting up a meeting between the two Dutchmen in the final. Van Gerwen contested his 26th [[World Series of Darts|World Series]] final in his 50th event, while Van Veen reached his first World Series final in his third event.<ref name="PDCDay2"/>

Van Gerwen started the final with an early [[Glossary of darts#Break|break of throw]], capitalising on a missed double from Van Veen to take a 1–0 lead with a 130 [[Glossary of darts#Checkout|checkout]] on the [[Bullseye (target)|bullseye]].<ref name="SkyDay2">{{cite web |last=Mirza |first=Raz |date=16 January 2026 |title=Bahrain Darts Masters: Michael van Gerwen wins title against Gian van Veen as Luke Littler's winning streak is ended |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12040/13495058/bahrain-darts-masters-michael-van-gerwen-wins-title-against-gian-van-veen-as-luke-littlers-winning-streak-is-ended |website=Sky Sports |access-date=16 January 2026}}</ref> He found his second [[Glossary of darts#Ton|ton]]-plus finish of the match, a 113 checkout, to extend his advantage to 3–0.<ref name="SkyBlog">{{cite web |date=16 January 2026 |title=Bahrain Darts Masters: Scores, results, commentary with Luke Littler, Luke Humphries, Michael van Gerwen in action |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/live-blog/12040/13494617/bahrain-darts-masters-live-scores-results-commentary-with-luke-littler-luke-humphries-michael-van-gerwen-in-action |website=Sky Sports |access-date=16 January 2026}}</ref> Van Veen found a break of his own and a 111 checkout to win his first two legs of the contest, but Van Gerwen then raced into a 6–2 lead.<ref name="SkyDay2"/> Although Van Veen claimed the next three legs consecutively to reduce the deficit to 6–5, Van Gerwen converted finishes of 103 and 66 to win the match 8–6.<ref name="PDCDay2"/> Van Gerwen won the Bahrain Masters for the first time, his 17th World Series title in total and his first since January 2024.<ref name="PDCDay2"/> "It's always special because I've never won here [in Bahrain] but everywhere I go I always want to win," said Van Gerwen after the victory. Speaking about his opponent, he stated: "What Gian [van Veen] has been producing lately, with the World Championship final and now straight away this tournament, we are going to see a lot more of him ... he is a great talent but I feel old now—I'm only 36."<ref name="SkyDay2"/><ref name="SkyBlog"/> Bahrain debutant Van Veen said that he had a "fantastic tournament", adding: "It's good to make two finals in the last couple of weeks, but it's two times runner-up. It's something to work on."<ref name="PDCDay2"/>
