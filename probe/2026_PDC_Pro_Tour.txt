The '''2026 PDC Pro Tour''' is a series of non-televised [[darts]] tournaments organised by the [[Professional Darts Corporation]] (PDC). [[PDC Pro Tour#Players Championships|Players Championships]] and [[PDC European Tour|European Tours]] are the events that make up the [[PDC Pro Tour|Pro Tour]]. The 2026 calendar consists of 34 Players Championship events, 15 European Tour events, as well as 24 events for each of the PDC's [[PDC secondary tours|secondary tours]]—the [[2026 PDC Challenge Tour|Challenge Tour]], [[2026 PDC Development Tour|Development Tour]] and [[2026 PDC Women's Series|Women's Series]].<ref>{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=11 September 2025 |title=European Tour set for further expansion in 2026 with PDC calendar released |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/september/11/european-tour-set-further-expansion-2026-pdc-calendar-released |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=12 October 2025}}</ref>

==PDC Tour Card==
128 players are granted [[PDC Tour Card]]s, which enables them to participate in all Players Championship events, the [[UK Open]], and qualifiers for all European Tours and select televised events.

===Tour Cards===
{{main|List of players with a 2026 PDC Tour Card}}
The 2026 PDC Tour Cards were awarded to:
* (64) The top 64 players from the [[PDC Order of Merit]] after the [[2026 PDC World Darts Championship|2026 World Championship]]
* (28) Qualifiers from [[2025 PDC Pro Tour#Q-School|2025 Q-School]] not ranked in the top 64 of the [[PDC Order of Merit]] following the World Championship
* (2) Two highest qualifiers from the [[2024 PDC Challenge Tour series|2024 Challenge Tour]] ([[Wesley Plaisier]] and [[Christian Kist]])
* (1) The highest qualifier from the [[2024 PDC Development Tour series|2024 Development Tour]] ([[Sebastian Białecki]])
** [[Niko Springer]], who also earned a Tour Card through the 2024 Development Tour, entered the top 64 in his first year on tour. Therefore, one extra Tour Card was awarded at 2026 Q-School.
* (2) Two highest qualifiers from the [[2025 PDC Challenge Tour series|2025 Challenge Tour]] ([[Stefan Bellmont]] and [[Darius Labanauskas]])
* (2) Two highest qualifiers from the [[2025 PDC Development Tour series|2025 Development Tour]] ([[Beau Greaves]] and [[Owen Bates]])
* (16) The daily finalists from the 2026 Q-Schools.

Afterwards, the playing field was complemented by the highest qualified players from the Q-School Orders of Merit until the maximum number of 128 Tour Card players had been reached.

===Q-School===
PDC Qualifying School (or Q-School) is split into two different events: '''UK Q-School''' for British and Irish players, held at the [[Arena MK]] in [[Milton Keynes]], England; and '''European Q-School''' for the rest of Europe, held at [[Wunderland Kalkar]] in [[Kalkar]], Germany. Non-European players can choose which Q-School to compete in.<ref name="DNQS">{{cite web |last=Gill |first=Samuel |date=2 November 2025 |title=When is PDC UK and European Q-School? How many Tour Cards are available? Key Dates, Costs, Locations, Format and How to Enter |url=https://dartsnews.com/faq/explanation-pdc-q-school-2025-what-do-you-have-to-do-to-win-a-pdc-tour-card-and-what-can-you-do-with-it |website=Darts News |access-date=13 November 2025}}</ref>
 
There are two stages in Q-School:<ref>{{cite web |title=2026 PDC Qualifying School - PDPA Information |url=https://pdpa.co.uk/qs-info-induction/ |website=PDPA |access-date=8 January 2026}}</ref>
* The First Stage, played between 5–7 January, was entered by any darts player who registered to compete. The players who reached the last 16 of each of the three days qualified for the Final Stage alongside other players coming from a First Stage Order of Merit ranking, producing a full list of 128 players for each Final Stage. 
* The Final Stage, held between 8–11 January, consisted of all players qualified from the First Stage plus the players exempted to the Final Stage; players who were exempted were those who lost their PDC Tour Cards after the [[2026 PDC World Darts Championship|2026 World Championship]], and those who finished from third to sixteenth in the [[2025 PDC Challenge Tour series|2025 Challenge Tour]] and [[2025 PDC Development Tour series|Development Tour]] Orders of Merit who did not have PDC Tour Cards yet. In a change from prior years, players from lower in the First Stage Order of Merit came in after the first day to replace Tour Card winners, with the intent of consistently having a 128-player field at both Q-Schools.

The final two players of each day's play in the Final Stage were given a PDC Tour Card. The remaining Tour Cards were then awarded to players in qualifying positions on the respective UK and European Q-School Orders of Merit.<ref>{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=7 January 2026 |title=Henderson headlines automatic Final Stage qualifiers at 2026 Q Schools |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/january/07/henderson-headlines-automatic-final-stage-qualifiers-2026-q-schools |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=8 January 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |date=8 January 2026 |title=PDC Qualifying School: Three-time world champion John Part through to final stage alongside John Henderson |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12040/13491605/pdc-qualifying-school-three-time-world-champion-john-part-through-to-final-stage-alongside-john-henderson |website=Sky Sports |access-date=11 January 2026}}</ref> A total of 29 PDC Tour Cards were distributed—13 at UK Q-School and 16 at European Q-School.<ref name="DNQS" />

==Players Championships==
{{Main|2026 PDC Players Championship series}}

The '''PDC Players Championships (PC)''' are a series of non-televised darts events that are part of the PDC Pro Tour. The number of events was the same as the previous year, with a total of 34 [[2026 PDC Players Championship series|Players Championship]] events.

The Players Championship Order of Merit is determined based on the total prize money won by each player in the Players Championship events throughout the year. The top 64 players on this ranking will qualify for the [[2026 Players Championship Finals|Players Championship Finals]].

==European Tour==
{{further|PDC European Tour}}
The '''PDC European Tour (ET)''' is a series of darts events that are part of the PDC Pro Tour. In 2026, the total number of events expanded to fifteen, one more than the previous year. European Tour events in Poland and Slovakia will be held for the first time.<ref>{{cite web |date=17 December 2025 |title=Darts 2026 schedule and dates: Premier League Darts, World Matchplay and more with Luke Littler in action |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/37278/13428768/darts-2026-schedule-and-dates-premier-league-darts-world-matchplay-and-more-with-luke-littler-in-action |website=Sky Sports |access-date=1 January 2026}}</ref>

The PDC European Tour Order of Merit is determined based on the total prize money won by each player in the European Tour events throughout the year. The top 32 players on this ranking will qualify for the [[2026 European Championship (darts)|European Championship]].
