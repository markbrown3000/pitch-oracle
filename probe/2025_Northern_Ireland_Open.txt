The '''2025 Northern Ireland Open''' (officially the '''2025 BetVictor''' '''Northern Ireland Open''') was a professional [[snooker]] tournament that took place from 19 to 26 October 2025 at the [[Waterfront Hall]] in [[Belfast]], Northern Ireland. Qualifiers took place from 4 to 7 September at the [[Leicester Arena]] in [[Leicester]], England. The 10th consecutive edition of the tournament since it was first staged in [[2016 Northern Ireland Open|2016]], it was the seventh [[Snooker world rankings|ranking event]] of the [[2025–26 snooker season]], following the [[2025 Xi'an Grand Prix]] and preceding the [[2025 International Championship]]. It was the second of four tournaments in the season's [[Home Nations Series]], following the [[2025 English Open (snooker)|2025 English Open]] and preceding the [[2025 Scottish Open (snooker)|2025 Scottish Open]] and the [[2026 Welsh Open (snooker)|2026 Welsh Open]]. The tournament was broadcast by [[TNT Sports]] in the United Kingdom and Ireland, by [[Eurosport]] in mainland Europe, by local channels in China and elsewhere in Asia, and by [[WST Play]] in all other territories. The winner received £100,000 from a total prize fund of £550,400.

[[Kyren Wilson]] was the defending champion, having defeated [[Judd Trump]] 9–3 in the [[2024 Northern Ireland Open|2024 final]], but he lost 3–5 to [[Jack Lisowski]] in the quarter-finals. The final was contested between Lisowski and Trump, who reached the 50th ranking final of his career; it took place before 1,435 spectators, a record audience at a UK snooker event outside of the [[Masters (snooker)|Masters]]. Lisowski defeated Trump 9–8 to win the first ranking title of his 15-year professional career, having previously lost six ranking finals. He dedicated the title to his parents, following the death of his father earlier that year. The event produced 64 [[Century break|century breaks]], 26 during the qualifiers in Leicester and 38 during the main stage in Belfast, of which the highest was a 143 by [[Liam Pullen]] in his second qualifying round match against [[Long Zehuang]].

=== Format ===
The tournament used a tiered format first implemented for the [[Home Nations Series]] in the [[2024–25 snooker season]]. In the first qualifying round, players [[seeding (sports)|seeded]] 65–96 faced those seeded 97 and under, including selected amateurs. In the second qualifying round, the 32 winners from the first qualifying round faced players seeded 33–64. At the last-64 stage, the 32 winners from the second qualifying round faced the top 32 seeds.<ref>{{cite web |date=5 April 2024 |title=Tiered format for Home Nations and German Masters in 2024/25 |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2024/april/05/tiered-format-for-home-nations-and-german-masters-in-2024-25/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240405185554/https://www.wst.tv/news/2024/april/05/tiered-format-for-home-nations-and-german-masters-in-2024-25/ |archive-date=5 April 2024 |access-date=5 April 2024 |work=[[World Snooker Tour]]}}</ref> All matches were played as the best of seven {{cuegloss|frame|frames}} until the quarter{{nbh}}finals, which were the best of nine. The semi{{nbh}}finals were the best of 11, and the final was a best{{nbh}}of{{nbh}}17 frame match played over two {{cuegloss|session|sessions}}.<ref name="bbcall">{{Cite web |last=Petrie |first=Richard |date=18 October 2025 |title=Northern Ireland Open 2025 - all you need to know|url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/c04q9yrr390o |access-date=22 April 2026|website=[[BBC Sport]] |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260422071852/https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/c04q9yrr390o|archive-date=22 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

[[Mark Williams (snooker player)|Mark Williams]] withdrew from the main stage of the tournament. [[Marco Fu]] also withdrew due to injury and [[Neil Robertson]] withdrew for health reasons. Their respective opponents, [[Long Zehuang]], [[Tom Ford]], and [[David Grace (snooker player)|David Grace]], received byes to the last 32.<ref name="Williams_withdraw">{{cite web |date=14 October 2025 |title=Williams pulls out of BetVictor Northern Ireland Open |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/october/14/williams-pulls-out-of-betvictor-northern-ireland-open/ |access-date=15 October 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]]|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260422074130/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/october/14/williams-pulls-out-of-betvictor-northern-ireland-open/|archive-date=22 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref><ref name="Robertson_withdraw">{{cite web |date=18 October 2025 |title=Robertson pulls out of BetVictor Northern Ireland Open |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/october/18/robertson-pulls-out-of-betvictor-northern-ireland-open/ |access-date=18 October 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]]|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260422074126/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/october/18/robertson-pulls-out-of-betvictor-northern-ireland-open/|archive-date=22 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

I got some momentum after that and my game was a bit more attacking."<ref name=":8" /><ref>{{cite web |date=25 October 2025 |title=2025 Northern Ireland Open: Judd Trump v Mark Allen |url=https://www.wst.tv/match-centre/79f3bc75-f5bd-4296-b2cd-66be256e896d |access-date=25 October 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260422074854/https://www.wst.tv/match-centre/79f3bc75-f5bd-4296-b2cd-66be256e896d|archive-date=22 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref><ref name=":9">{{cite web |date=25 October 2025 |title=Trump to face Lisowski in 50th ranking final |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/october/25/Trump-To-Face-Lisowski-In-50th-Ranking-Final/ |access-date=25 October 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260422074851/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/october/25/Trump-To-Face-Lisowski-In-50th-Ranking-Final/|archive-date=22 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

=== Final ===
[[File:Jack Lisowski PHC 2016.jpg|thumb|[[Jack Lisowski]] ''(pictured in 2016)'' defeated [[Judd Trump]] 9–8 to win the first ranking title of his professional career.]]
The final was played as the best of 17 frames, held over two sessions, between the world number one Judd Trump and the world number 29 Jack Lisowski. Trump played in his sixth Northern Ireland Open final, having previously won four titles and been runner-up once, while Lisowski competed in the final of the tournament for the first time. It was Trump's fiftieth ranking final and Lisowski's seventh. The players had contested three previous ranking finals, at the [[2020 World Grand Prix (2020–21 season)|2020 World Grand Prix]], the [[2021 German Masters]], and the [[2021 Gibraltar Open]], all of which had been won by Trump.<ref name=":8" /><ref name=":9" /> Trump had won their eight previous professional matches.<ref name=":10" /> The final was played before 1,435 spectators, a record audience for any UK snooker event outside of the [[Masters (snooker)|Masters]].<ref name=":11">{{Cite web |last=Gray |first=Andy |date=27 October 2025 |title='An emotional wreck' - Lisowski ends 15-year wait |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cy7enep1y6go |access-date=27 October 2025 |website=[[BBC Sport]] |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260422071941/https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cy7enep1y6go|archive-date=22 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref> 

Lisowski won the 39-minute opening frame after a safety battle on the colours, but Trump won the second with a 97 break and also took the 48-minute third frame after Lisowski missed the last blue. Lisowski came from 53 points behind to win frame four, tying the scores at 2–2 at the mid-session interval. Trump won frame five after {{Cuegloss|double|doubling}} a red to a centre pocket and making a frame-winning break of 36, but Lisowski responded with his seventh century of the tournament, a 112, tying the scores again at 3–3. Lisowski was on a break of 49 in frame seven when he called a {{Cuegloss|push shot}} foul on himself, and Trump made a frame-winning clearance of 75. In the eighth frame, Trump ran out of position on a break of 48, and Lisowski won the frame with a 62 clearance. The first session ended level at 4–4.<ref name=":12" /><ref>{{cite web |date=26 October 2025 |title=All square in Belfast final |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/october/26/All-Square-In-Belfast-Final/ |access-date=26 October 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en-GB |archive-date=1 November 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20251101021735/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/october/26/All-Square-In-Belfast-Final/ |url-status=live }}</ref>

When play resumed for the second session, Trump won the ninth frame, but Lisowski won the tenth after Trump missed the last red. Trump won frame 11 with an 85 break, but Lisowski responded with an 84 to tie the scores at 6–6 at the mid-session interval. Trump took frame 13 after Lisowski missed a pot on the black, but Lisowski made a half-century of 51 in the 14th to tie the scores again at 7–7. In the 15th frame, Lisowski missed a {{Cuegloss|frame ball|frame-ball}} black while on a break of 60 but later secured the frame by potting the last red, taking the lead for the first time since 1–0. However, Trump made two breaks of 38 in the 16th to tie the scores at 8–8. In the deciding frame, Lisowski made a 27 break before missing a red. Both players missed opportunities, but Lisowski eventually clinched a 9–8 victory with a match-winning break of 46.<ref name=":12">{{cite web |date=26 October 2025 |title=2025 Northern Ireland Open: Jack Lisowski v Judd Trump |url=https://www.wst.tv/match-centre/b104a79d-364d-4b37-8f20-f764b7533267 |access-date=26 October 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260422074848/https://www.wst.tv/match-centre/b104a79d-364d-4b37-8f20-f764b7533267|archive-date=22 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref><ref name=":10" /> 

It was the first ranking title of Lisowski's 15-year professional career, following six previous defeats in ranking finals. The players, best friends since the age of 14, embraced after the match. Lisowski, whose father had passed away in March, dedicated the title to his parents. "It's surreal. I can't believe it," he said afterwards. "I'm exhausted and I'm an emotional wreck. I felt like I was going to pass out. It's bizarre, it doesn't feel real and like it's a dream." He added: "Since my dad passed away I feel like it is more serious now. Before, it was a bit of a game, a bit of fun. Now I feel like I have to win for him." He advanced from 29th to 24th in the world rankings after the event.<ref>{{cite web |date=26 October 2025 |title=Johnstone's Paint world rankings update: Jack's back! |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/october/26/Johnstone-s-Paint-World-Rankings-Update--Jack-s-Back-/ |access-date=28 October 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260422073338/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/october/26/Johnstone-s-Paint-World-Rankings-Update--Jack-s-Back-/|archive-date=22 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref> After finishing as runner-up for a second consecutive year, Trump commented: "I am so pleased for [Lisowski]. I am probably a lot happier than I would have been if I had won. I gave it my absolute all. I missed a few shots, but [Lisowski] fully deserved to win. It's nice for him to shut everyone up because he is always being called out [for not having won a ranking title], but no one can say that again."<ref name=":10">{{cite web |date=26 October 2025 |title=Lisowski wins maiden title in epic Belfast final |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/october/26/Lisowski-Wins-Maiden-Title-In-Epic-Belfast-Final/ |access-date=26 October 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en-GB |archive-date=11 December 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20251211102349/https://www.wst.tv//news/2025/october/26/Lisowski-Wins-Maiden-Title-In-Epic-Belfast-Final/ |url-status=live }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=McCabe |first=Jana |date=26 October 2025 |title=Lisowski beats Trump to win first title at NI Open |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/c4gp8rxly5jo |access-date=26 October 2025 |website=[[BBC Sport]] |language=en-GB |archive-date=2 November 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20251102040542/https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/c4gp8rxly5jo |url-status=live }}</ref><ref name=":11" />
