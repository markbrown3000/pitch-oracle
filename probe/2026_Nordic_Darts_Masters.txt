The '''2026 Nordic Darts Masters''' (known for sponsorship reasons as the '''2026 Mr Vegas Nordic Darts Masters''') was a professional [[darts]] tournament that took place from 5 to 6 June 2026 at the [[Forum Copenhagen]] in [[Copenhagen]], Denmark. It was the sixth staging of the tournament by the [[Professional Darts Corporation]] (PDC), and was the third event in the [[2026 World Series of Darts]].<ref>{{cite web |last=Phillips |first=Josh |date=5 October 2025 |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/october/05/2026-world-series-darts-schedule-confirmed |title=2026 World Series of Darts schedule confirmed |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=25 April 2026}}</ref> The total prize fund was £100,000, with the winner receiving £30,000.

The tournament featured 16 players, consisting of eight PDC representatives, and eight [[Nordic countries|Nordic]] and [[Baltic states|Baltic]] representatives. [[Stephen Bunting]] was the defending champion, having defeated [[Rob Cross]] 8–4 in the [[2025 Nordic Darts Masters|2025 final]].<ref>{{cite web |date=7 June 2025 |title=Bunting beats Cross to win Nordic Masters |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/darts/articles/cvgvqxxvpkzo |website=BBC Sport |access-date=25 April 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |date=7 June 2025 |title=Nordic Darts Masters: Stephen Bunting wins second World Series title of 2025 as Luke Littler and Luke Humphries lose in quarter-finals |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13380472/nordic-darts-masters-stephen-bunting-wins-second-world-series-title-of-2025-as-luke-littler-and-luke-humphries-lose-in-quarter-finals |website=Sky Sports |access-date=25 April 2026}}</ref> However, he lost 6–5 to [[Viktor Tingström]] in the first round.

[[Michael van Gerwen]] won the tournament, his 18th [[World Series of Darts|World Series]] title, by defeating [[Luke Humphries]] 8–7 in the final. Tingström and Humphries missed [[Glossary of darts#Match dart|match darts]] to defeat Van Gerwen in the quarter-finals and final, respectively.

=== Format ===
Eight elite PDC representatives were drawn to play eight [[Nordic countries|Nordic]] and [[Baltic states|Baltic]] representatives in the first round on Friday 5 June; the quarter-finals, semi-finals and final took place on Saturday 6 June.<ref name="Draw">{{cite web |last=Simpson |first=Will |date=1 June 2026 |title=Bunting begins 2026 Nordic Darts Masters defence against Tingstrom |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/june/01/bunting-begins-2026-nordic-darts-masters-defence-against-tingstrom/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=1 June 2026}}</ref>

*'''First round and quarter-finals:''' Best of eleven legs
*'''Semi-finals:''' Best of thirteen legs
*'''Final:''' Best of fifteen legs

=== Broadcasts ===
The tournament was broadcast on [[ITV4]] in the United Kingdom, [[Viaplay]] in [[Scandinavia]], the Netherlands and Iceland, and [[TV3 Group (Baltics)|TV3]] in the Baltic states. Other broadcasters included [[DAZN]] in Germany, Austria and Switzerland; [[Canal+ (Polish TV provider)|Canal+]] in Poland; [[Fox Sports (Australia)|Fox Sports]] in Australia; [[Sky Sport (New Zealand)|Sky Sport]] in New Zealand; [[VTM (TV channel)|VTM]] in Belgium; [[FanDuel TV]] in the United States and Brazil; [[AMC Networks International Central and Northern Europe|AMC Network]] in Hungary; Zonasport in Croatia; [[Arena Sport]] in Serbia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Montenegro, North Macedonia and Kosovo; [[Setanta Sports Eurasia|Setanta]] in Ukraine and the [[Commonwealth of Independent States]]; and [[Nova Sport (Czech Republic and Slovakia)|Nova]] in the Czech Republic and Slovakia. It was also available for subscribers outside of the United Kingdom, Germany, Austria and Switzerland on the PDC's streaming service, PDCTV.<ref>{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=4 June 2026 |title=Where to watch the 2026 Mr Vegas Nordic Darts Masters |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/june/04/where-to-watch-the-2026-mr-vegas-nordic-darts-masters/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=4 June 2026}}</ref>

==Summary==
===First round===
[[File:2026-03-26 Premier League Darts – Night 8 – Berlin 2026 by Sandro Halank–084.jpg|thumb|The defending champion [[Stephen Bunting]] ''(pictured in 2026)'' was eliminated by Sweden's Viktor Tingström in the first round.]]
The first round was played on 5 June. The defending champion [[Stephen Bunting]] was eliminated in his opening match, losing 6–5 to Sweden's [[Viktor Tingström]] after missing seven [[Glossary of darts#Match dart|match darts]]. "I've had some good memories in darts, but I don't think anything can beat this at the moment," said Tingström, who became the third Nordic and Baltic representative to win a match in the event's history. "I just came here trying to enjoy it. I don't put too much pressure on myself. I don't have any pressure on me."<ref name="Sky_R1">{{cite web |date=5 June 2026 |title=Nordic Darts Masters: Stephen Bunting beaten by Viktor Tingstrom as Luke Littler, Luke Humphries, Michael van Gerwen progress |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13551177/nordic-darts-masters-stephen-bunting-beaten-by-viktor-tingstrom-as-luke-littler-luke-humphries-michael-van-gerwen-progress |website=Sky Sports |access-date=5 June 2026}}</ref> [[Gerwyn Price]] also survived a match dart as he recorded a [[three-dart average]] of 104.03 in his 6–5 win against [[Darius Labanauskas]], a match that featured six [[Glossary of darts#Ton|ton]]-plus [[Glossary of darts#Checkout|checkouts]].<ref name="PDC_R1">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=5 June 2026 |title=Tingstrom stuns Bunting as Humphries delivers in Denmark |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/june/05/tingstrom-stuns-bunting-humphries-delivers-denmark/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=5 June 2026}}</ref> Reigning world champion [[Luke Littler]] made his first appearance since winning his second [[2026 Premier League Darts|Premier League]] title and claimed a 6–1 victory over [[Cor Dekker]].<ref>{{cite web |date=5 June 2026 |title=Luke Littler starts Nordic Masters in style but reigning champion Stephen Bunting suffers shock defeat |url=https://www.rte.ie/sport/darts/2026/0605/1577046-littler-starts-nordic-masters-in-style-bunting-shocked/ |website=RTÉ Sport |access-date=5 June 2026 |agency=Press Association}}</ref> [[Gian van Veen]] landed a 167 checkout to complete a 6–3 win over [[Daniel Larsson (darts player)|Daniel Larsson]].<ref name="PDC_R1"/>

[[Luke Humphries]] registered the highest three-dart average of the round, averaging 109.92 on his way to defeating [[Jeffrey de Graaf]] 6–3. Speaking after the match, Humphries stated that he had been feeling comfortable in his throw for the last four to five weeks, declaring that he can "play this level a lot more often".<ref name="Sky_R1"/> [[Jonny Clayton]] earned a [[Whitewash (sport)|whitewash]] win over Sweden's [[Andreas Harrysson]]. [[James Wade]] defeated [[Madars Razma]] 6–1 in his first World Series appearance since 2022, while [[Michael van Gerwen]] won 6{{nbnd}}2 against [[Oskar Lukasiak]].<ref name="PDC_R1"/>

===Quarter-finals, semi-finals and final===
[[File:2026-03-26 Premier League Darts – Night 8 – Berlin 2026 by Sandro Halank–046.jpg|thumb|[[Michael van Gerwen]] ''(pictured in 2026)'' won his second World Series title of 2026 and his 18th overall.]]
The quarter-finals, semi-finals and final were played on 6 June. Viktor Tingström threatened another upset victory as he established a 5–3 lead over Michael van Gerwen in the first quarter-final. However, the Dutchman survived a match dart from Tingström and won the match in a [[Glossary of darts#Decider|deciding leg]]. Jonny Clayton earned a 6–3 win against Welsh compatriot Gerwyn Price, while Luke Littler defeated James Wade 6–1. In the last quarter-final, Luke Humphries and Gian van Veen also went to a deciding leg, where Humphries prevailed 6–5.<ref name="RTE_Final">{{cite web |date=6 June 2026 |title=Michael van Gerwen overcomes Luke Humphries to win Nordic Masters |url=https://www.rte.ie/sport/darts/2026/0606/1577142-van-gerwen-overcomes-humphries-to-win-nordic-masters/ |website=RTÉ Sport |access-date=6 June 2026}}</ref> Van Gerwen averaged 101.55 in a 7–4 victory over Clayton in the first semi-final. In the second semi-final, Littler and Humphries faced off in a rematch of the Premier League final nine days earlier. The pair were level at 4–4 when Humphries found a [[Glossary of darts#Break|break of throw]] with a 132 checkout, hitting two [[Bullseye (target)|bullseyes]] and [[Glossary of darts#Double|double]] 16 to take the lead. Humphries ultimately claimed a 7–5 win to set up a final with Van Gerwen, finishing the match with an average of 105.82.<ref name="PDC_Final">{{cite web |last=Simpson |first=Will |date=6 June 2026 |title=Van Gerwen claims second 2026 World Series title with Nordic Darts Masters triumph |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/june/06/Van-Gerwen-claims-second-World-Series-title-of-2026-with-Nordic-Darts-Masters-triumph/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=6 June 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |last=Parsons |first=Mike |date=6 June 2026 |title=Luke Littler beaten by Luke Humphries, Nordic Darts Masters |url=https://www.warringtonguardian.co.uk/sport/latest_sport/26172576.luke-littler-beaten-luke-humphries-nordic-darts-masters/ |website=Warrington Guardian |access-date=6 June 2026}}</ref> Top seed and world number four Van Gerwen was aiming to win his second World Series title of 2026 in his third consecutive final, while world number two Humphries was looking to win his third World Series title overall.<ref name="PDC_Final"/><ref name="Sky_Blog">{{cite web |date=6 June 2026 |title=Nordic Darts Masters LIVE! Latest scores, results, with Luke Littler, Luke Humphries, Gerwyn Price in action |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/live-blog/12288/13551106/nordic-darts-masters-live-latest-scores-results-with-luke-littler-luke-humphries-gerwyn-price-in-action |website=Sky Sports |access-date=6 June 2026}}</ref>

Humphries began the final by taking a 3–1 lead, but Van Gerwen claimed the next four legs to go 5–3 in front.<ref name="BBC_Final">{{cite web |last=Collins |first=Ben |date=6 June 2026 |title=Van Gerwen beats Humphries 8-7 to win Nordic Masters |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/darts/articles/cjeg2yn99g4o |website=BBC Sport |access-date=6 June 2026}}</ref> Humphries restored parity, winning successive legs to level at 5–5.<ref name="PDC_Final"/> The pair traded breaks of throw before taking the match to a last-leg decider, where Humphries missed the bullseye for a 127 checkout to win the title, allowing Van Gerwen to convert a 72 finish on double 20 to triumph 8–7.<ref name="RTE_Final"/><ref name="Sky_Blog"/> Van Gerwen recorded a three-dart average of 98.90 and hit 57 per cent of his attempts at double, while Humphries averaged 98.83.<ref name="PDC_Final"/><ref name="Results"/>

Van Gerwen won his 18th World Series title and his second of 2026, having claimed his first at the [[2026 Bahrain Darts Masters|Bahrain Darts Masters]] in January. He also became the first two-time winner of the Nordic Darts Masters.<ref name="BBC_Final"/> The three-time world champion called the final a "really tough game" and claimed that Humphries let him get away with it when he missed an opportunity to go 4–1 ahead. On winning the title, he said: "It is always nice to have a win behind your name again. That gives confidence and a good feeling because my performances early on in the rounds were not good."<ref name="Sky_Final">{{cite web |date=6 June 2026 |title=Nordic Darts Masters: Michael van Gerwen wins title as Luke Humphries fumes at whistling in Copenhagen |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13551465/nordic-darts-masters-michael-van-gerwen-wins-title-as-luke-humphries-fumes-at-whistling-in-copenhagen |website=Sky Sports |access-date=7 June 2026}}</ref> Speaking in defeat, Humphries expressed disappointment in the [[Copenhagen]] crowd who could be heard booing and whistling throughout the match, stating: "I'm up here to win titles. I just want to give everyone entertainment. You feel disappointed and it's hard work in the end."<ref name="Sky_Final"/> He later discussed the upcoming [[2026 PDC World Cup of Darts|World Cup of Darts]]: "Hopefully in the World Cup I can gain some silverware and win a tournament—it would be nice for me, that's what I need."<ref name="PDC_Final"/>
