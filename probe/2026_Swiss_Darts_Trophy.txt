The '''2026 Swiss Darts Trophy''' was a professional [[darts]] tournament that took place at the [[St. Jakobshalle]] in [[Basel]], Switzerland, from 9 to 11 October 2026.<ref>{{cite web |title=Swiss Darts Trophy 2026 |url=https://www.pdc-europe.tv/tournaments/et-2026-en/swiss-darts-trophy-2026/ |website=PDC Europe |access-date=7 August 2026}}</ref> It was the fourteenth of fifteen [[PDC European Tour]] events on the [[2026 PDC Pro Tour]]. It featured a field of 48 players and £230,000 in prize money, with £35,000 going to the winner.

[[Stephen Bunting]] was the defending champion after defeating [[Luke Woodhouse]] 8–3 in the [[2025 Swiss Darts Trophy|2025 final]]. However, he lost 6–4 to [[Jeffrey de Graaf]] in the second round.

[[Jonny Clayton]] won his fourth European Tour title by defeating [[Luke Humphries]] 8–4 in the final.<ref>{{cite web |date=11 October 2026 |title=Clayton beats Humphries to claim Swiss title |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/darts/articles/cmly02qe7nz0o |website=BBC Sport |access-date=12 October 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |last=Wellens |first=Meg |date=12 October 2026 |title=Swiss Darts Trophy 2026: Jonny Clayton defeats Luke Humphries to secure fourth Euro Tour title |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/13597879/swiss-darts-trophy-2026-jonny-clayton-defeats-luke-humphries-to-secure-fourth-euro-tour-title |website=Sky Sports |access-date=12 October 2026}}</ref>

==Prize money==
As part of a mass boost in prize money for [[Professional Darts Corporation]] (PDC) events in 2026, the prize fund for all [[2026 PDC Pro Tour#European Tour#European Tour|2026 European Tour]] events rose to £230,000, of which the winner received £35,000.<ref>{{cite web |last=Phillips |first=Josh |date=31 March 2025 |title=Biggest prize money increase in PDC history confirmed |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/march/31/biggest-prize-money-increase-pdc-history-confirmed |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=1 March 2026}}</ref>

== Qualification and format ==
The top 16 players on the two-year [[PDC Order of Merit]] were [[Seeding (sports)|seeded]] and entered the tournament in the second round, while the next 16 highest-ranked players from the one-year [[PDC Pro Tour Order of Merit]] automatically qualified for the first round. The seedings were confirmed on 23 July.<ref>{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=26 July 2026 |title=2026 ET13-14 Seeds & Tour Card Holder qualifier entries confirmed |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/july/26/2026-et13-14-seeds---tour-card-holder-qualifier-entries-confirmed/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=27 July 2026}}</ref> The remaining 16 places went to players from four qualifying events – 10 from the Tour Card Holder Qualifier (held on 30 July),<ref>{{cite web |date=30 July 2026 |title=PDC ET14 Tour Card Holder Qualifier |url=https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdceutcq26et14/matches |website=DartConnect |access-date=7 August 2026}}</ref> four from the Host Nation Qualifier (held on 8 October),<ref>{{cite web |date=8 October 2026 |title=PDC ET14 Host Nation Qualifier |url=https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdc26et14hnq/matches |website=DartConnect |access-date=8 October 2026}}</ref> one from the Nordic & Baltic Associate Member Qualifier (held on 7 August),<ref>{{cite web |date=7 August 2026 |title=PDCNB 2026 Tour - Norway - Weekend 05 |url=https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdcnbpro26e05/matches |website=DartConnect |access-date=7 August 2026}}</ref> and one from the East European Associate Member Qualifier (held on 8 August).<ref>{{cite web |date=8 August 2026 |title=PDC ET14 E. Europe Qualifier |url=https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdceeu26et14q/matches |website=DartConnect |access-date=8 August 2026}}</ref>

Before the Tour Card Holder Qualifier, [[Luke Littler]] withdrew and was replaced by [[Dave Chisnall]], with [[Jermaine Wattimena]] becoming the 16th seed.

[[Gerwyn Price]], [[Gary Anderson (darts player)|Gary Anderson]], [[Nathan Aspinall]] and [[Ryan Joyce]] withdrew and were replaced by [[Karel Sedláček]], [[Alan Soutar]], [[Rob Owen (darts player)|Rob Owen]] and [[Nick Kenny (darts player)|Nick Kenny]]. [[Luke Woodhouse]], [[Martin Schindler]] and [[Krzysztof Ratajski]] moved up to become the 14th, 15th and 16th seeds respectively.<ref name="Draw">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=8 October 2026 |title=2026 Swiss Darts Trophy draw & schedule confirmed|url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/october/08/2026-swiss-darts-trophy-draw-schedule-confirmed/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=8 October 2026}}</ref>
