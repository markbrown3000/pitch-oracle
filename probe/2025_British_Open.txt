The '''2025 British Open''' (officially the '''2025 Unibet British Open''')<ref name=":1" /> was a professional [[snooker]] tournament that took place from 22 to 28 September 2025 at the [[Cheltenham Racecourse|Centaur]] in [[Cheltenham]], England. Qualifying took place from 25 to 28 June at the [[Leicester Arena]] in [[Leicester]]. The fifth consecutive edition of the tournament since it was revived in [[2021 British Open|2021]], it was the fifth [[List of snooker ranking tournaments|ranking event]] of the [[2025–26 snooker season]], following the [[2025 English Open (snooker)|2025 English Open]] and preceding the [[2025 Xi'an Grand Prix]]. It was broadcast by [[ITV (TV network)|ITV]] in the United Kingdom and Ireland, by local channels in China and elsewhere in Asia, and by [[WST Play]] in mainland Europe and all other territories. The winner received £100,000 from a total prize fund of £502,000.

[[Mark Selby]] was the defending champion, having defeated [[John Higgins]] 10–5 in the [[2024 British Open|2024 final]], but he lost 1–6 to [[Shaun Murphy]] in the semi-finals. The final was contested between Murphy and [[Anthony McGill]], who reached his first ranking final in eight years. Murphy defeated McGill 10–7 to win his first British Open title and the 13th ranking title of his career. The event produced 74 [[century break]]s, 11 during the qualifiers in Leicester and 63 at the main venue in Cheltenham, of which the highest was a 144 by [[Gary Wilson (snooker player)|Gary Wilson]] in his second-round match against McGill.

==Overview==
[[File:2025 British Open (70982).jpg|thumb|The Centaur at [[Cheltenham Racecourse]] ''(pictured)'' hosted the event.]]
The tournament began in [[1980 British Gold Cup|1980]] as the non-ranking British Gold Cup, won by [[Alex Higgins]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=2010-07-25 |title=Alex 'Hurricane' Higgins |url=https://www.thetimes.com/travel/destinations/uk-travel/northern-ireland-travel/alex-hurricane-higgins-nh367gk2cj8 |access-date=2025-09-01 |website=[[The Times]] |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418133006/https://www.thetimes.com/travel/destinations/uk-travel/northern-ireland-travel/alex-hurricane-higgins-nh367gk2cj8|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref> Held as the non-ranking Yamaha Organs Trophy in 1981 and the non-ranking Yamaha International Masters from 1982 to 1984, it was renamed the [[British Open (snooker)|British Open]] in [[1985 British Open|1985]], when it also gained ranking status.<ref name="cajt.pwp.blueyonder.co.uk">{{cite web |last=Turner |first=Chris |title=British Open Including British Gold Cup, Yamaha Organs Trophy and Yamaha International Masters |url=http://www.cajt.pwp.blueyonder.co.uk/brit.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120216155129/http://www.cajt.pwp.blueyonder.co.uk/brit.html |archive-date=16 February 2012 |access-date=1 September 2025 |work=cajt.pwp.blueyonder.co.uk |publisher=Chris Turner's Snooker Archive}}</ref> Staged 21 times as a ranking event from 1985 until its discontinuation after the [[2004 British Open|2004 edition]], the tournament was revived as a ranking event in [[2021 British Open|2021]]. In [[2022 British Open|2022]], the tournament trophy was named the [[Clive Everton]] Trophy to honour the longtime commentator and snooker journalist.<ref name="CE_trophy">{{cite web |url=https://wst.tv/british-open-trophy-named-after-clive-everton/ |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220921121610/https://wst.tv/british-open-trophy-named-after-clive-everton/ |url-status=dead |archive-date=21 September 2022 |title=British Open trophy named after Clive Everton |work=[[World Snooker Tour]] |date=21 September 2022 |access-date=1 October 2023}}</ref>

===Format===
There was no [[seed (sports)|seed]]ing system and the draw was randomised for every round.<ref name="WST_main">{{cite web |title=British Open |url=https://www.wst.tv/unibetbritishopen/ |access-date=25 June 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250613014358/https://www.wst.tv/unibetbritishopen/ |archive-date=13 June 2025 |publisher=[[World Snooker Tour]]}}</ref> All matches up to the quarter{{nbh}}finals were played as the best of 7 {{cuegloss|frame|frames}}. The quarter{{nbh}}finals were the best of 9 frames, the semi{{nbh}}finals were the best of 11 frames, and the final was the best of 19 frames, played over two {{cuegloss|session|sessions}}.<ref name="s.o_main">{{cite web |title=British Open 2025 |url=https://www.snooker.org/res/index.asp?event=2182 |access-date=25 June 2025 |website=snooker.org|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418133909/https://www.snooker.org/res/index.asp?event=2182|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

==Summary==

=== Round one (qualifiers) ===
[[File:Bai Yulu 2024.jpg|thumb|Two female players reached the last 64, the reigning World Women's Champion [[Bai Yulu]] ''(pictured in 2024)'' and 12-time World Women's Champion [[Reanne Evans]].]]
All matches before the quarter-final stage were played as the best of seven {{Cuegloss|frame|frames}}.<ref name="s.o_main" /> At the qualifiers held in Leicester, [[Marco Fu]] lost the first three frames against [[Stephen Maguire]] but made {{cuegloss|breaks}} of 140, 81, and 104 to tie the scores at 3–3 and then won the {{Cuegloss|deciding frame}}. [[Jackson Page]] trailed [[Elliot Slessor]] 1–2 but won three consecutive frames for a 4–2 victory, while [[Haris Tahir]] beat [[Ken Doherty]] 4–3, making an 81 break in the decider. Amateur player Ryan Davies, who received a top-up place in the event via the [[2025 Q School#Q School Order of Merit|Q School Order of Merit]], defeated [[Jimmy Robertson (snooker player)|Jimmy Robertson]] on the last {{Cuegloss|black ball|black}} of the deciding frame. [[Jack Lisowski]] made a 72 break in the decider to beat [[Liam Highfield]], while [[Liam Davies (snooker player)|Liam Davies]] defeated [[Jimmy White]] 4–2.<ref>{{Cite web |date=25 June 2025 |title=Fab Fu continues fast start |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/june/25/fab-fu-continues-fast-start/ |access-date=21 September 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418132614/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/june/25/fab-fu-continues-fast-start/|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref> [[Michał Szubarczyk]], aged 14, the youngest player ever to turn professional, took a 3–2 lead over Umut Dikme, another top-up amateur player from the Q School Order of Merit. Dikme came from behind to win frame six on the {{Cuegloss|colours}} and then made a 55 break to win the decider. [[Stuart Bingham]] made breaks of 102, 56, and 75 as he beat the [[2022 British Open|2022]] champion [[Ryan Day (snooker player)|Ryan Day]], and [[Ben Mertens]] recovered from 1–3 behind against [[Liam Pullen]] to win the match in a deciding frame. [[Chang Bingyu]] made breaks of 118, 93, 64, and 70 as he beat [[Kreishh Gurbaxani]] 4–2.<ref>{{Cite web |date=26 June 2025 |title=Dikme denies Szubarczyk first win |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/june/26/Dikme-Denies-Szubarczyk-First-Win/ |access-date=21 September 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418133930/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/june/26/Dikme-Denies-Szubarczyk-First-Win/|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

Two female players advanced to the last 64, the reigning [[World Women's Snooker Championship|World Women's Champion]] [[Bai Yulu]] and the 12-time World Women's Champion [[Reanne Evans]]. Bai defeated [[Artemijs Žižins]] 4–2 after he attempted a [[maximum break]] in the second frame of the match but missed the 14th black. Evans secured a 4–1 victory over 16-year-old [[Lan Yuhao]], who was playing his first season on the [[World Snooker Tour|professional tour]]. [[Robbie McGuigan]] defeated [[Lyu Haotian]] 4–2, while [[Stan Moody]] made breaks of 55, 66, and 97 as he beat [[Zhou Yuelong]] by the same score. Returning to the tour after an extended absence due to a neck injury, [[Sam Craigie]] recorded his first professional victory in 16 months with a 4–1 win over [[Yao Pengcheng]]. New tour player [[Leone Crowley]] recorded his first professional victory with a [[Whitewash (sport)|whitewash]] win over [[Hatem Yassen]], while [[Matthew Selt]] whitewashed [[David Gilbert (snooker player)|David Gilbert]] and [[Lei Peifan]] whitewashed [[Mateusz Baranowski]]. From 46 {{cuegloss|points}} behind in the decider, [[Long Zehuang]] made a {{Cuegloss|clearance}} of 57 to defeat [[Thepchaiya Un-Nooh]], while [[Robert Milkins]] advanced with a 4–2 victory over [[Fan Zhengyi]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=27 June 2025 |title=Bai books Cheltenham spot |url=https://www.wst.tv//news/2025/june/27/bai-books-cheltenham-spot/ |access-date=21 September 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418132407/https://www.wst.tv//news/2025/june/27/bai-books-cheltenham-spot/|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=28 June 2025 |title=Evans continues perfect start to season |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/june/28/evans-continues-perfect-start-to-season/ |access-date=21 September 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418132313/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/june/28/evans-continues-perfect-start-to-season/|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

At the held-over qualifiers played in [[Cheltenham]], the defending champion [[Mark Selby]] defeated [[David Grace (snooker player)|David Grace]] 4–1, and the previous year's runner-up [[John Higgins]] came from 1–3 behind to beat [[Mark Davis (snooker player)|Mark Davis]] in a deciding frame. [[Judd Trump]], the [[List of world number one snooker players|world number one]], made two [[century break|centuries]] of 103 and 101 as he whitewashed [[Aaron Hill (snooker player)|Aaron Hill]] in 53 minutes. "I have felt good this season, the results just haven't quite come," said Trump, who had not won a title in 2025. "Games like the one tonight give you a boost. I have scored well in most matches, my opponents have just {{cuegloss|pot|potted}} some brilliant balls at the winning line and not given me a chance." The reigning World Champion [[Zhao Xintong]] defeated amateur player [[Ashley Hugill]] in a deciding frame, and [[Neil Robertson]] whitewashed amateur player Alfie Davies. [[Kyren Wilson]] made a 141 break as he beat [[Chris Wakelin]] 4–1, and [[Mark Williams (snooker player)|Mark Williams]] made a 127 in the last frame of his 4–2 victory over [[Si Jiahui]]. [[Shaun Murphy]] advanced by beating [[Ross Muir]] 4–1, while [[Mark Allen (snooker player)|Mark Allen]], recent winner of the [[2025 English Open (snooker)|2025 English Open]], defeated [[Jiang Jun]] by the same score.<ref>{{Cite web |date=22 September 2025 |title=Unibet British Open day one |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/september/22/unibet-british-open-day-one/ |access-date=24 September 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418132309/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/september/22/unibet-british-open-day-one/|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-09-23 |title=Allen beats Jun to progress in British Open|url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cj6xnl6dllwo |access-date=2026-04-18 |website=BBC Sport |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418130400/https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cj6xnl6dllwo|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

=== Round two (last 64) ===
[[File:2025 British Open (48546).jpg|thumb|The interior of the arena at the Centaur in [[Cheltenham]], pictured during the event.]]
The world number 55 [[Stan Moody]], recently a quarter-finalist at the [[2025 Wuhan Open (snooker)|2025 Wuhan Open]], made a century of 132 as he moved into a 3–1 lead over Kyren Wilson, the [[2024 World Snooker Championship|2024 World Champion]]. Wilson made an 82 break in the fifth frame, but Moody secured a 4–2 victory by winning frame six. Calling it his "best win on tour," Moody said afterwards: "Beating Ding [at the 2025 Wuhan Open] was very good but this tops it, on [[ITV (TV network)|ITV]] in front of a big crowd in Cheltenham. I tried to stay as calm as I could towards the end and managed to get over the line." Higgins trailed Lisowski 0–2 and 2–3 but won the sixth frame on the colours and then took the deciding frame with a century of 132. Following his second consecutive victory in a decider, Higgins said: "I am so lucky to still be in the tournament. I really need to improve. I know I have not put the effort into practice and you get what you deserve. I should have gone out in the first round. But the last frame today did give me some confidence." Bai recovered to tie the scores at 3–3 as she attempted to become the first woman to defeat a top-16 opponent in a ranking tournament. However, Zhang won the decider to advance. Mertens whitewashed Evans, the other female player in the last 64. McGuigan defeated [[He Guoqiang (snooker player)|He Guoqiang]], winning the deciding frame on the last black. [[Anthony McGill]] eliminated [[Gary Wilson (snooker player)|Gary Wilson]] 4–1, although Wilson made the tournament's highest break of 144 in the frame he won. Robertson defeated [[Xu Si]] by the same score. Allen advanced by beating [[Bulcsú Révész]], also by a 4–1 scoreline, while [[Iulian Boiko]] defeated Craigie 4–2.<ref name=":2" /><ref name="Mannrecovers"/>

Trump lost the first frame against Crowley but won four in a row for a 4–1 victory. Murphy defeated [[Scott Donaldson]] by the same score. === Round four (last 16) ===
[[File:Judd Trump at Snooker German Masters (Martin Rulsch) 2014-02-01 18 (cropped).jpg|thumb|The world number one [[Judd Trump]] ''(pictured in 2014)'' lost in the last 16 to [[Shaun Murphy]].]]
The world number 88 Heathcote defeated Davies 4–2 to reach the third ranking quarter-final of his career. After the match, Heathcote spoke about his mentoring relationship with former professional player [[Anthony Hamilton (snooker player)|Anthony Hamilton]], who had retired at the end of the [[2024–25 snooker season|2024–25 season]] after 34 years on tour. "I started practising with him because I felt I was always losing to those type of experienced players so I wanted to learn something from playing with him. I knew it was going to be torture, but I wanted to learn how to beat him," said Heathcote. Mann defeated Hawkins by the same score, reaching the quarter-finals of a full ranking event for the second time, having previously done so at the [[2021 Northern Ireland Open]]. Higgins beat Mertens 4–1.<ref name=":3" /><ref name=":4" />

"In a way it made my job easy today because I knew I needed to play at my best." Trump's defeat meant that he had failed to reach a semi-final thus far that season. Selby went on to win the frame following a {{Cuegloss|safety|safety battle}} on the last red. === Semi-finals ===
[[File:2025 British Open (snooker) (74525).jpg|thumb|[[Anthony McGill]] ''(pictured at the event)'' defeated [[John Higgins]] to reach his first ranking final in eight years.]]
The semi-finals were played as the best of 11 frames.<ref name="s.o_main" /> Playing Higgins in the first semi-final, McGill won the 30-minute opening frame when Higgins went {{Cuegloss|in-off}} after potting the last {{Cuegloss|yellow ball|yellow}}. Higgins took the second after a safety battle on the last {{Cuegloss|brown ball|brown}} and also won the third with a 62 break. In frame four, Higgins missed a red, and McGill made a 99 break to tie the scores at 2–2 at the mid-session interval. Higgins made a 111 break to win frame five, but McGill then won four consecutive frames with breaks of 50, 57, 104, and 93 to secure a 6–3 victory. "It's fantastic win for me because [Higgins] is a legend," said McGill afterwards. "This will be the biggest final of my career. I have had a lot of good wins at the [[Crucible Theatre|Crucible]] but this would definitely top all of them. I wasn't nervous today, I was really enjoying the occasion." Higgins commented: "[McGill] hit the ball superbly well, like the way we all know he can. A big turning point was the fourth frame when I missed an unforgiveable red with the {{Cuegloss|rest}} with the balls at my mercy. If I had gone 3–1 up, I would have been in control."<ref>{{Cite web |date=27 September 2025 |title=2025 British Open: Anthony McGill v John Higgins |url=https://www.wst.tv/match-centre/99423d47-9a53-4978-a47a-0df5db140a4b |access-date=27 September 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418134343/https://www.wst.tv/match-centre/99423d47-9a53-4978-a47a-0df5db140a4b|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=27 September 2025 |title=McGill stuns Higgins to reach final |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/september/27/McGill-Stuns-Higgins-To-Reach-Final/ |access-date=27 September 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418132849/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/september/27/McGill-Stuns-Higgins-To-Reach-Final/|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref><ref name=":9">{{Cite web |date=27 September 2025 |title=McGill and Murphy reach British Open final |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cq5j7wl0exyo |access-date=27 September 2025 |website=[[BBC Sport]] |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418130707/https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cq5j7wl0exyo|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

Facing Selby in the second semi-final, Murphy won both of the first two frames after safety battles on the last green and took the third with a century of 116. Selby won the fourth with a century of 123 to leave Murphy leading 3–1 at the mid-session interval. In frame five, Selby missed a red while on a break of 54 and subsequently made a safety error on the last red that let Murphy extend his lead to 4–1. Selby {{Cuegloss|snookers required|required three snookers}} in frame six, as he trailed by 60 points with three reds remaining. He secured the required {{Cuegloss|penalty points}} but missed a pot on the last yellow, and Murphy went on to win the frame. Murphy then made a 93 break in frame seven to complete a 6–1 victory. "If you are going to beat [Selby] you have to take the game to him and score when you get chances," said Murphy afterwards. "I'm reluctant to say I mixed it with him in the safety department because I had to ride my luck at times. But overall I'm delighted with the performance." Selby commented: "[Murphy] played great from start to finish. There were a few fifty-fifty frames I could have won which could have made a difference. But I wasn't good enough and [Murphy] was very strong."<ref name=":9" /><ref name=":8">{{Cite web |date=27 September 2025 |title=Red hot Murphy crushes Selby |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/september/27/Red-Hot-Murphy-Crushes-Selby/ |access-date=27 September 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418134340/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/september/27/Red-Hot-Murphy-Crushes-Selby/|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

=== Final ===
[[File:2025 British Open (86551).jpg|thumb|[[Shaun Murphy]] ''(pictured at the event)'' defeated [[Anthony McGill]] 10–7 in the final to win his first [[British Open (snooker)|British Open]] title and the 13th ranking title of his career.]]
The final was played as the best of 19 frames, held over two {{cuegloss|sessions}}, between the world number 16 Murphy and the world number 57 McGill. Murphy was contesting the 27th ranking final of his career, while McGill was playing in his fourth ranking final and his first in eight years.<ref name="s.o_main" /><ref name=":8" /><ref name=":9" /> It was the first two-session ranking final of McGill's career, as his finals at the [[2016 Indian Open|2016]] and [[2017 Indian Open]] had been the best of nine frames and the [[2017 Snooker Shoot Out|2017 Snooker Shoot-Out]] final was played over one frame.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Day |first=Michael |date=27 September 2025 |title=Reenergised Anthony McGill has 'barely practiced', prioritising gym over baize |url=https://www.totallysnookered.com/sport/snooker/tournaments/british-open/reenergised-british-open-finalist-anthony-mcgill-has-barely-practised-prioritising-the-gym-over-the-baize-5336642 |access-date=28 September 2025 |website=Totally Snookered |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418131325/https://www.totallysnookered.com/sport/snooker/tournaments/british-open/reenergised-british-open-finalist-anthony-mcgill-has-barely-practised-prioritising-the-gym-over-the-baize-5336642|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref> Murphy had won eight of the last ten professional meetings between the two players. McGill won the first two frames of the final with breaks of 68 and 78. Murphy made a century of 101 to take the third, but McGill responded with a century of 109 in the fourth to lead 3–1 at the mid-session interval. Murphy made a century of 132 to win frame five. In frame six, Murphy led by 22 points when he missed a pot on the last red, and McGill cleared to restore a two-frame lead at 4–2. However, Murphy won the last two frames of the session with breaks of 78 and 112 to tie the scores at 4–4.<ref name=":10">{{Cite web |date=28 September 2025 |title=2025 British Open: Anthony McGill v Shaun Murphy |url=https://www.wst.tv/match-centre/5473a114-d88f-469e-b2d2-b5d454fef741 |access-date=28 September 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418131321/https://www.wst.tv/match-centre/5473a114-d88f-469e-b2d2-b5d454fef741|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref><ref name=":11">{{Cite web |date=28 September 2025 |title=Murphy and McGill all square in Cheltenham final |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/september/28/Murphy-And-McGill-All-Square-In-Cheltenham-Final/ |access-date=28 September 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418131317/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/september/28/Murphy-And-McGill-All-Square-In-Cheltenham-Final/|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

When play resumed for the second session, Murphy won the ninth frame with a 78 break to lead for the first time at 5–4. In frame 10, Murphy missed a pot on the black, and McGill made an 81 break to tie the scores at 5–5. Murphy again missed the black in frame 11 while leading by 37 points, and McGill took the frame with a 77 break. Murphy won the 12th frame after McGill missed a {{Cuegloss|long pot}} with four reds remaining, tying the scores at 6–6. McGill took frame 13 with a 73 break to move 7–6 ahead, but Murphy produced a break of 76 in the 14th to draw level again at 7–7. Murphy won the next two frames with breaks of 72 and 66 to lead 9–7. In frame 17, Murphy won a safety exchange on the last red and made a match-winning clearance to secure a 10–7 victory. In all, the match produced four century breaks and ten more breaks over 60.<ref name=":10" /><ref name=":12" /><ref name=":13" />

The win gave Murphy his first [[British Open (snooker)|British Open]] title, the 13th ranking title of his professional career, and his first victory at a ranking event since the [[2023 Championship League (ranking)|2023 Championship League]] 26 months earlier. He advanced from 16th to 12th place in the [[Snooker world rankings|world rankings]] after the tournament, while McGill as runner-up moved up from 57th to 45th.<ref>{{Cite web |date=28 September 2025 |title=Johnstone's Paint world rankings update: Murphy jumps four places |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/september/28/Johnstone-s-Paint-World-Rankings-Update--Murphy-Jumps-Four-Places/ |access-date=1 October 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418131309/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/september/28/Johnstone-s-Paint-World-Rankings-Update--Murphy-Jumps-Four-Places/|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref> "When you haven't been in a ranking final for a long time, I was grateful for the opportunity and so pleased that my game was there today when I needed it," Murphy said afterwards. [Murphy] is a quality player."<ref name=":12">{{Cite web |date=28 September 2025 |title=Murphy is best of British |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/september/28/Murphy-Is-Best-Of-British/ |access-date=28 September 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418131313/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/september/28/Murphy-Is-Best-Of-British/|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref><ref name=":13">{{Cite web |last=Ramsdale |first=Ben |date=28 September 2025 |title=Murphy beats McGill to win British Open for first time |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cy85l2e2j5xo |access-date=18 April 2026 |website=[[BBC Sport]] |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418130646/https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cy85l2e2j5xo|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>
