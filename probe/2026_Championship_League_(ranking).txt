The '''2026 Championship League''' (officially the '''2026 BetVictor Championship League Snooker''') was a professional ranking [[snooker]] tournament that took place from 22{{nbsp}}June to 15{{nbsp}}July 2026 at the [[Leicester Arena]] in [[Leicester]], England.<ref>{{cite web |title=Calendar 2026/2027 |url=https://www.snooker.org/res/index.asp?template=2&season=2026 |access-date=14 May 2026 |website=www.snooker.org}}</ref> It was the seventh staging of the ranking edition of the tournament, which was first held in [[2020 Championship League (ranking)|2020]]. Organised by [[Matchroom Sport]], it was the first [[List of snooker ranking tournaments|ranking event]] of the [[2026–27 snooker season]], preceding the [[2026 China Open (snooker)|2026 China Open]]. The winner received £33,000 from a total prize fund of £328,000.

[[Stephen Maguire]] was the defending champion, having defeated [[Joe O'Connor (snooker player)|Joe O'Connor]] 3–1 in the final of the [[2025 Championship League (ranking)|previous ranking edition]],<ref>{{cite web |date=23 July 2025 |title=Snooker: Stephen Maguire wins 2025 Championship League |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/c5yl13pz0xgo |access-date=24 July 2025 |website=[[BBC Sport]]}}</ref> but he was eliminated in Stage two [[#Group C|Group C]].

[[Jak Jones]] won the tournament, defeating [[David Gilbert (snooker player)|David Gilbert]] 3–2 in the final.<ref>{{cite web |title=Jak Jones claims first career ranking title at 2026 BetVictor Championship League |url=https://championshipleaguesnooker.co.uk/jak-jones-claims-first-career-ranking-title-at-2026-betvictor-championship-league/ |website=championshipleaguesnooker.co.uk |date=16 July 2026 |access-date=16 July 2026}}</ref>

There were 53 [[century break]]s made during Stage one, including a [[Maximum break|maximum]] by [[Si Jiahui]] in [[#Group 6|Group 6]]. There were 21 centuries made during Stage two, and 10 during Stage three.

== Format ==
The competition began with 32 rounds of group matches with each group consisting of four players. Two groups were played to a finish every day using a two{{nbh}}[[Billiard table|table]] setup in the arena. The groups were contested using a [[Round-robin tournament|round{{nbh}}robin]] format, with six matches played in each group. All matches in group play were played as best{{nbh}}of{{nbh}}four {{cuegloss|frame|frames}}, with three points awarded for a win and one point for a draw. Group positions were determined by points scored, frame difference and then head{{nbh}}to{{nbh}}head results between players who were tied. Places that were still tied were then determined by the highest {{cuegloss|break}} made in the group. If the highest break was also tied, the next highest break made by the players was used.<ref name="CLS_ranking">{{cite web |title=Championship League Snooker |url=https://championshipleaguesnooker.co.uk/ranking/ |access-date=14 May 2026 |work=championshipleaguesnooker.co.uk}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |date=11 June 2024 |title=BetVictor Championship League explainer |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2024/june/11/betvictor-championship-league-explainer/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240611204205/https://www.wst.tv/news/2024/june/11/betvictor-championship-league-explainer/ |archive-date=11 June 2024 |access-date=11 June 2024 |work=[[World Snooker Tour]]}}</ref>

The 32 players that topped the group tables qualified for the group winners' stage, consisting of eight groups of four players. The eight winners from the group winners' stage qualified for the two final groups, with the final taking place later on the same day.<ref name="CLS_ranking" /> The winner received the Championship League title and a place in the [[2026 Champion of Champions]].<ref name="CLS_info">{{cite web |title=BetVictor Championship League Snooker ranking event 2026 returns this summer, 22 June – 15 July |url=https://championshipleaguesnooker.co.uk/betvictor-championship-league-snooker-ranking-event-2026-returns-this-summer-22-june-15-july/ |date=10 June 2026 |access-date=12 June 2026 |work=championshipleaguesnooker.co.uk}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=BetVictor Championship League 2026 draw |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/june/15/betvictor-championship-league-2026-draw/ |date=15 June 2026 |access-date=16 June 2026 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]]}}</ref>
