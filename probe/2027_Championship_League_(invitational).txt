The '''2027 Championship League Invitational''' (officially the '''2027 BetVictor Championship League Snooker Invitational''') is an upcoming professional non-[[Snooker world rankings|ranking]] [[snooker]] tournament that will take place from 21{{nbsp}}December{{nbsp}}2026 to 23{{nbsp}}January{{nbsp}}2027 at the [[Leicester Arena]] in [[Leicester]], England. Part of the [[2026–27 snooker season]], it will be the 21st edition of the invitational version of the tournament, which was first staged in [[2008 Championship League|2008]].

[[Mark Selby]] is the defending champion, having retained the title for a third consecutive time at the [[2026 Championship League (invitational)|2026 event]] by defeating [[Wu Yize]] 3–1 in the final.

== Overview ==
The tournament will take place from 21{{nbsp}}December{{nbsp}}2026 to 23{{nbsp}}January{{nbsp}}2027 at the [[Leicester Arena]] in [[Leicester]], England.<ref>{{cite web |title=Tournaments |url=https://www.wst.tv/tournaments |access-date=17 July 2026 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]]}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=Calendar 2026/2027 |url=https://www.snooker.org/res/index.asp?season=2026&template=2 |access-date=17 July 2026 |website=snooker.org}}</ref> [[Mark Selby]] is the defending champion, having defeated [[Wu Yize]] 3–1 in the final of the [[2026 Championship League (invitational)|2026 event]], retaining the title for a third consecutive time.<ref>{{cite web |title=Selby retains league title |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/february/11/selby-retains-league-title-/ |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |date=11 February 2026 |access-date=11 February 2026}}</ref>

=== Format ===
A total of 25 players are initially invited to the event, with the matches played [[Behind closed doors (sport)|behind closed doors]] without an audience. Players earn prize money for every {{cuegloss|frame}} won, as well as being semi{{nbh}}finalists, runners{{nbh}}up, and winners of each group, with more money involved in the Winners' Group. Since [[2010 Championship League|2010]] all matches have been played as the [[Playoff format#Higher number of games|best of five]] frames.

The tournament is played in a [[Round-robin tournament|round{{nbh}}robin]] format, consisting of eight groups of seven players. The top four players in each group qualify for a play{{nbh}}off, with the winner entering the Winners' Group. The bottom two players in each group are eliminated, and the remaining four moved to the next group, where they are joined by three more players. This format is played for each group, from one to seven. In each group, the players are ranked by the number of matches won, then by most frames won, and then by least frames lost. If two players are tied by these criteria, the player who wins the match between them is ranked higher in the table. The group play{{nbh}}off semi{{nbh}}finals are contested between the 1st and 4th place players in the table, and between the 2nd and 3rd place players in the table. The Winners' Group is played at the end, and its play{{nbh}}off winner becomes champion of the tournament.<ref name="CLS_inv_format">{{cite web |title=Championship League Snooker |url=https://championshipleaguesnooker.co.uk/invitational/ |website=championshipleaguesnooker.co.uk |access-date=21 May 2026}}</ref><ref name="CLS_info">{{cite web |title=2026 BetVictor Championship League Snooker invitational event returns: everything you need to know |url=https://championshipleaguesnooker.co.uk/2026-betvictor-championship-league-snooker-invitational-event-returns-everything-you-need-to-know/ |website=championshipleaguesnooker.co.uk |date=22 December 2025 |access-date=13 February 2026 |archive-date=2 January 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260102075838/https://championshipleaguesnooker.co.uk/2026-betvictor-championship-league-snooker-invitational-event-returns-everything-you-need-to-know/ |url-status=live}}</ref>
