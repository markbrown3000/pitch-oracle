The '''2026 European Darts Open''' (known for sponsorship reasons as the '''2026 Elten Safety Shoes European Darts Open''') was a professional [[darts]] tournament that took place at the [[Ostermann-Arena]] in [[Leverkusen]], Germany, from 10 to 12 July 2026.<ref>{{cite web |title=Elten Safety Shoes European Darts Open 2026 |url=https://www.pdc-europe.tv/tournaments/et-2026-en/european-darts-open-2026/ |website=PDC Europe |access-date=1 March 2026}}</ref> It was the tenth of fifteen [[PDC European Tour]] events on the [[2026 PDC Pro Tour]]. It featured a field of 48 players and £230,000 in prize money, with £35,000 going to the winner.

[[Nathan Aspinall]] was the defending champion, having defeated [[Damon Heta]] 8–6 in the [[2025 European Darts Open|2025]] final,<ref>{{cite web |date=1 June 2025 |title=European Darts Open: Nathan Aspinall beats Damon Heta in final to claim second Euro Tour title of 2025 |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13377992/european-darts-open-nathan-aspinall-beats-damon-heta-in-final-to-claim-second-euro-tour-title-of-2025 |website=Sky Sports |access-date=3 June 2026}}</ref> but he lost 6–4 to [[Krzysztof Ratajski]] in the quarter-finals.

Ratajski won his third European Tour title by defeating [[Jermaine Wattimena]] 8–6 in the final.

==Prize money==
As part of a mass boost in prize money for [[Professional Darts Corporation]] (PDC) events in 2026, the prize fund for all [[2026 PDC Pro Tour#European Tour#European Tour|2026 European Tour]] events rose to £230,000, of which the winner will receive £35,000.<ref>{{cite web |last=Phillips |first=Josh |date=31 March 2025 |title=Biggest prize money increase in PDC history confirmed |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/march/31/biggest-prize-money-increase-pdc-history-confirmed |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=1 March 2026}}</ref>

== Qualification and format ==
The top 16 players on the two-year [[PDC Order of Merit]] were [[Seeding (sports)|seeded]] and entered the tournament in the second round, while the next 16 highest-ranked players from the one-year [[PDC Pro Tour Order of Merit]] automatically qualified for the first round. The seedings were confirmed on 13 May.<ref>{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=9 April 2026 |title=2026 ET9-10 Seeds & Tour Card Holder qualifier entries confirmed |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/may/15/et9-10-seeds-tour-card-holder-qualifiers-entries/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=20 May 2026}}</ref> The remaining 16 places went to players from four qualifying events – 10 from the Tour Card Holder Qualifier (held on 20 May),<ref>{{cite web |date=20 May 2026 |title=PDC ET10 Tour Card Holder Qualifier |url=https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdceutcq26et10/matches |website=DartConnect |access-date=20 May 2026}}</ref> four from the Host Nation Qualifier (held on 17 May),<ref>{{cite web |date=17 May 2026 |title=PDC ET10 Host Nation Qualifier |url=https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdc26et10hnq/matches |website=DartConnect |access-date=2 June 2026}}</ref> one from the Nordic & Baltic Associate Member Qualifier (held on 15 May),<ref>{{cite web |date=24 April 2026 |title=PDCNB 2026 Tour - Sweden - Weekend 04 |url=https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdcnbpro26e04/matches |website=DartConnect |access-date=2 June 2026}}</ref> and one from the East European Associate Member Qualifier (held on 4 July).

[[Gary Anderson (darts player)|Gary Anderson]], [[Ryan Searle (darts player)|Ryan Searle]], and [[Gerwyn Price]] withdrew and were replaced by [[Robert Owen (darts player)|Robert Owen]], [[Max Hopp]], and [[Dominik Grüllich]]. [[Luke Woodhouse]], [[Damon Heta]], and [[Krzysztof Ratajski]] moved up to become the 14th, 15th, and 16th seeds, respectively.<ref name="Draw">{{cite web |last=Simpson |first=Will |date=9 July 2026 |title=2026 European Darts Open draw & schedule confirmed |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/july/09/2026-european-darts-open-draw---schedule-confirmed/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=9 July 2026}}</ref>

== Summary ==
=== First round ===
[[File:Max Hopp 5-6 William O'Connor - Max Hopp - 2019249222623 2019-09-06 PDC European Darts Matchplay - 1209 - B70I6226.jpg|thumb|[[Max Hopp]] ''(pictured in 2019)'' defeated [[2018 PDC World Darts Championship|2018]] world champion [[Rob Cross]] in the first round.]]
The first round (best of 11 [[Leg (darts)|legs]]) was played on 10 July.<ref name="PDC_D1">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=10 July 2026 |title=Schmidt stuns Menzies as Springer lights up Leverkusen |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/july/10/schmidt-stuns-menzies-springer-lights-up-leverkusen/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=11 July 2026}}</ref> Host nation qualifier Jan Schmidt produced a comeback from 5–1 down to defeat [[Cameron Menzies]] in a [[Glossary of darts#Decider|deciding leg]]. "I really came here believing that I’m capable of winning on the European Tour, and I took this belief on to the stage," Schmidt said afterwards.<ref name="PDC_D1"/> Fellow German qualifier [[Dragutin Horvat]] achieved his first win on the European Tour since 2023 by beating [[Mario Vandenbogaerde]] 6–2. The two other host nation qualifiers, Jaimy Van de Weerd and [[Daniel Klose]], lost their respective matches to [[Dirk van Duijvenbode]] and [[Niels Zonneveld]].<ref name="PDC_D1"/> Aside from the host nation qualifiers, two other German players were victorious in the first round; [[Niko Springer]] registered a [[three-dart average]] of 105.21 in a 6–2 win over [[Darius Labanauskas]], and [[Max Hopp]] earned a 6–3 victory against [[2018 PDC World Darts Championship|2018]] world champion [[Rob Cross]].<ref>{{cite web |last=Jeffries |first=Rob |date=10 July 2026 |title=Jan Schmidt’s stunning comeback headlines dramatic opening day at European Darts Open |url=https://dartsplanet.tv/jan-schmidt-comeback-european-darts-open-opening-day/ |website=Darts Planet TV |access-date=11 July 2026}}</ref> [[Sebastian Białecki]] and [[Karel Sedláček]] both survived four [[Glossary of darts#Match dart|match darts]] from their opponents, winning deciding legs against [[William O'Connor (darts player)|William O'Connor]] and European Tour debutant [[Rhys Griffin]], respectively.<ref>{{cite web |last=Michael |first=Lucas |date=10 July 2026 |title=Menzies squanders 5-1 lead as Gilding also stunned in thrilling last-leg deciders |url=https://dartsnews.com/pdc/live-european-darts-open-friday-afternoon-menzies-gilding-joyce-van-duijvenbode-kick-off-the-action |website=dartsnews.com |access-date=11 July 2026}}</ref><ref name="DNews_1Eve">{{cite web |last=Wood |first=Kieran |date=10 July 2026 |title=Niko Springer fires 105 average warning to Luke Littler in Leverkusen as Max Hopp stuns Rob Cross |url=https://dartsnews.com/pdc/european-darts-open-2026-roundup-niko-springer-fires-105-average-warning-to-luke-littler-in-leverkusen-as-max-hopp-stuns-rob-cross |website=dartsnews.com |access-date=11 July 2026}}</ref>

[[Joe Cullen (darts player)|Joe Cullen]] took a 5–1 lead before eventually winning 6–4 against [[Charlie Manby]], who converted four [[Glossary of darts#Ton|ton]]-plus [[Glossary of darts#Checkout|checkouts]] in defeat. [[Kevin Doets]] overturned a 3–1 deficit to defeat [[Dominik Grüllich]] 6–4. [[Robert Owen (darts player)|Robert Owen]] missed [[Glossary of darts#Double|double]] 12 for a [[nine-dart finish]] in his match against [[Andrew Gilding]], but the Welshman recovered to claim a 6–5 victory, hitting eight [[Glossary of darts#Maximum|maximums]] in the process. [[Mickey Mansell]] defeated Czech qualifier Michal Šmejda 6–1, while [[Ryan Joyce]] beat Finnish qualifier Mio Varila by the same scoreline. Samuel Price, another European Tour debutant, was beaten 6–1 by [[Kim Huybrechts]]. [[Maik Kuivenhoven]] was a 6–3 winner against [[Daryl Gurney]], while [[Bradley Brooks]] defeated [[Lukas Wenig]] 6–2.<ref name="PDC_D1"/><ref name="DNews_1Eve"/>

=== Second round ===
[[File:2025-04-03 Premier League Darts Berlin 2025 by Sandro Halank–038.jpg|thumb|Defending champion [[Nathan Aspinall]] ''(pictured in 2025)'' reached the quarter-finals, where he was eliminated by [[Krzysztof Ratajski]].]]
The second round (best of 11 legs) was played on 11 July.<ref name="PDC_D2">{{cite web |last=Wagner |first=Connor |date=11 July 2026 |title=Aspinall and Schmidt steal the show in Leverkusen with resounding victories |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/july/11/2026-elten-safety-shoes-european-darts-open---day-two-latest/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=12 July 2026}}</ref> Defending champion [[Nathan Aspinall]] was victorious in his opening match, defeating Mickey Mansell 6–1 with an average of 107.23. In his post-match interview, Aspinall stated that Mansell played well but the thought of watching the [[England national football team|England]] match later in the night made him feel relaxed.<ref name="Sky_D2">{{cite web |date=11 July 2026 |title=European Darts Open: Nathan Aspinall kickstarts title defence with win as Stephen Bunting knocked out |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12040/13562585/european-darts-open-nathan-aspinall-kickstarts-title-defence-with-win-as-stephen-bunting-knocked-out |website=Sky Sports |access-date=12 July 2026}}</ref> Jan Schmidt continued his run with another upset victory, beating thirteenth seed [[Mike De Decker]] in his second last-leg decider.<ref name="Sport1_D2">{{cite web |last=Hinzberg |first=Lars |date=11 July 2026 |title=Deutsche Darts-Sensation geht weiter |trans-title=German darts sensation continues |url=https://www.sport1.de/news/darts-sport/2026/07/deutsche-darts-sensation-geht-weiter |website=Sport1 |access-date=12 July 2026 |language=de}}</ref> Top seed [[Gian van Veen]] won 6–3 against Niko Springer in a match where both players averaged over 100. Three-time world champion [[Michael van Gerwen]] defeated Joe Cullen 6–2, while sixth seed [[Stephen Bunting]] lost 6–4 to Ryan Joyce. Poland's Krzysztof Ratajski and Sebastian Białecki both advanced to the third round with victories against Max Hopp and fifth seed [[Danny Noppert]], respectively.<ref name="PDC_D2"/>

Dragutin Horvat was unable to join fellow host nation qualifier Schmidt in the last 16 as he lost 6–0 to [[Luke Woodhouse]].<ref name="Sport1_D2"/> Dutch compatriots [[Wessel Nijman]] and [[Jermaine Wattimena]] also achieved [[Whitewash (sport)|whitewashes]] in their respective wins against Bradley Brooks and Kim Huybrechts, with Nijman hitting a [[Glossary of darts#Maximum check|170 checkout]] during his match.<ref name="DPTV_D2">{{cite web |last=Jeffries |first=Rob |date=12 July 2026 |title=Aspinall impresses as Schmidt’s European Darts Open dream run continues |url=https://dartsplanet.tv/aspinall-impresses-schmidt-dream-run-european-darts-open/ |website=Darts Planet TV |access-date=12 July 2026}}</ref> Kevin Doets eliminated German number one and twelfth seed [[Martin Schindler]] in a 6–3 defeat, leaving Schmidt as the only German player left in the tournament.<ref name="DPTV_D2"/><ref>{{cite web |last=Sagner |first=Niklas |date=11 July 2026 |title=Deutsches Trio ausgeschieden |trans-title=German trio eliminated |url=https://www.sport1.de/news/darts-sport/2026/07/european-darts-open-deutsches-trio-um-martin-schindler-ausgeschieden |website=Sport1 |access-date=12 July 2026 |language=de}}</ref> [[Ross Smith (darts player)|Ross Smith]] was a 6–1 winner against Niels Zonneveld, while [[Jonny Clayton]] defeated Karel Sedláček by the same scoreline. Dirk van Duijvenbode beat seventh seed [[Chris Dobey]] 6–2, [[James Wade]] defeated Maik Kuivenhoven 6–4, and [[Damon Heta]] earned his own 6–4 win against Robert Owen.<ref name="Sky_D2"/>

=== Final day ===
[[File:Michael van Gerwen 6-5 Krzysztof Ratajski - Krzysztof Ratajski - 2019251192205 2019-09-08 PDC European Darts Matchplay - 0065 - B70I7302.jpg|thumb|[[Krzysztof Ratajski]] ''(pictured in 2019)'' won his third European Tour title in his third final.]]
The third round, quarter-finals, semi-finals and final were played on 12 July. The third round and quarter-finals were contested over the best of 11 legs, the semi-finals over the best of 13 legs, and the final over the best of 15 legs.<ref name="PDC_Final">{{cite web |last=Simpson |first=Will |date=12 July 2026 |title=Ratajski seals Euro Tour hat-trick with European Darts Open triumph |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/july/12/Ratajski-seals-Euro-Tour-hat-trick-with-European-Darts-Open-triumph/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=13 July 2026}}</ref> The final day saw Krzysztof Ratajski and Jermaine Wattimena reach the final. Ratajski began his day by eliminating top seed Gian van Veen, defeating the Dutchman 6–3. He followed that win by beating defending champion Nathan Aspinall 6–4 in the quarter-finals.<ref name="PDC_Final"/> In the European Tour's first all-Polish semi-final, he defeated first-time semi-finalist Sebastian Białecki 7–5 to advance to the final, hitting a 170 checkout en route.<ref>{{cite web |last=Miller |first=Tymon |date=13 July 2026 |title='Polish Eagle' soars again: Ratajski’s comeback win at European Darts Open |url=https://tvpworld.com/94316537/krzysztof-ratajski-beats-jemaine-wattimena-to-win-european-darts-open |website=TVP World |access-date=13 July 2026}}</ref><ref name="EU_Final">{{cite web |date=13 July 2026 |title=ELTEN Safety Shoes European Darts Open: Ratajski triumphs in Leverkusen |url=https://www.pdc-europe.tv/news/elten-safety-shoes-european-darts-open-ratajski-triumphs-in-leverkusen/ |website=PDC Europe |access-date=13 July 2026}}</ref><ref name="DNews_Final">{{cite web |last=Wood |first=Kieran |date=12 July 2026 |title=Krzysztof Ratajski storms back from 6-4 down to deny Jermaine Wattimena and win third Euro Tour title |url=https://dartsnews.com/pdc/european-darts-open-2026-roundup-krzysztof-ratajski-storms-back-from-6-4-down-to-deny-jermaine-wattimena-and-win-third-euro-tour-title |website=dartsnews.com |access-date=13 July 2026}}</ref> Wattimena defeated Ryan Joyce 6–1 before a 6–5 victory over Luke Woodhouse sent him through to his first European Tour semi-final. He proceeded to go one step further, beating Damon Heta 7–3 to progress to the title decider. Ratajski was aiming to maintain his perfect record in European Tour finals, while Wattimena was looking to win his first European Tour title.<ref name="PDC_Final"/>

Ratajski got off to a good start in the final, taking an early 4–1 lead. Wattimena responded by winning the next five legs, converting an 84 checkout to level the match and claiming the next leg to extend his advantage to 6–4.<ref name="EU_Final"/><ref name="Sky_Final">{{cite web |date=12 July 2026 |title=European Darts Open: Krzysztof Ratajski beats Jermaine Wattimena to lift title ahead of World Matchplay |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13562885/european-darts-open-krzysztof-ratajski-beats-jermaine-wattimena-to-lift-title-ahead-of-world-matchplay |website=Sky Sports |access-date=13 July 2026}}</ref> Missed doubles from Wattimena allowed Ratajski to level at 6–6. He hit a 68 finish to go 7–6 ahead, then completed an 8–6 victory by pinning double 10 in the 14th leg.<ref name="PDC_Final"/> Ratajski finished the final with a three-dart average of 92.43, while Wattimena averaged 86.73.<ref name="DNews_Final"/>

Ratajski won his third European Tour title, adding to his victories at the [[2019 Gibraltar Darts Trophy]] and the [[2023 German Darts Open]].<ref name="EU_Final"/> It was also his first ranking title of 2026 and his 12th overall.<ref name="Sky_Final"/><ref name="Rank">{{cite web |last=Simpson |first=Will |date=13 July 2026 |title=PDC Werner Rankings Ladder Update after European Darts Open |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/july/13/pdc-werner-rankings-ladder-update-after-european-darts-open/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=13 July 2026}}</ref> He earned the £35,000 top prize, which saw him return to the top 20 in the [[PDC World Rankings]] for the first time in over three years.<ref name="Rank"/> Ratajski stated that the win was not expected, claiming that he "would have been happy with the last eight or last four", and added that he hoped to play well at the upcoming [[2026 World Matchplay|World Matchplay]].<ref name="Sky_Final"/> Despite the defeat, Wattimena described the final sessions as a "magic day" and said that it was a fair result as both players missed chances during the match.<ref name="Sky_Final"/>
