The '''2026 Flanders Darts Trophy''' (known for sponsorship reasons as the '''2026 Blåkläder Flanders Darts Trophy''') was a professional [[darts]] tournament that took place at the Antwerp Expo in [[Antwerp]], Belgium, from 11 to 13 September 2026.<ref>{{cite web |title=Flanders Darts Trophy 2026 |url=https://www.pdc-europe.tv/tournaments/et-2026-en/flanders-darts-trophy-2026/ |website=PDC Europe |access-date=7 August 2026}}</ref> It was the thirteenth of fifteenth [[PDC European Tour]] events on the [[2026 PDC Pro Tour]]. It featured a field of 48 players and £230,000 in prize money, with £35,000 going to the winner. [[Luke Littler]] was the defending champion, having defeated [[Josh Rock]] 8–7 in the [[2025 Flanders Darts Trophy|2025 final]].<ref>{{cite web |date=31 August 2025 |title=Littler wins Flanders Darts title after Rock thriller |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/darts/articles/crm4gdd13xdo |website=BBC Sport |access-date=31 August 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |date=31 August 2025 |title=Luke Littler beats Josh Rock to add Antwerp crown to title haul |url=https://www.rte.ie/sport/darts/2025/0831/1531120-littler-beats-rock-to-add-antwerp-crown-to-title-haul/ |website=RTÉ |access-date=31 August 2025}}</ref> However, Littler withdrew from the tournament, and therefore did not defend his title.

[[James Wade]] won his third European Tour title and first since 2016, defeating [[Ross Smith (darts player)|Ross Smith]] 8–4 in the final.

==Prize money==
As part of a mass boost in prize money for [[Professional Darts Corporation]] (PDC) events in 2026, the prize fund for all [[2026 PDC Pro Tour#European Tour#European Tour|2026 European Tour]] events rose to £230,000, of which the winner received £35,000.<ref>{{cite web |last=Phillips |first=Josh |date=31 March 2025 |title=Biggest prize money increase in PDC history confirmed |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/march/31/biggest-prize-money-increase-pdc-history-confirmed |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=1 March 2026}}</ref><ref name="DNPrizeMoney">{{cite web |last=Gill |first=Samuel |date=13 September 2026 |title=Flanders Darts Trophy 2026 Prize Money: How much did James Wade and Ross Smith earn in Antwerp? |url=https://dartsnews.com/pdc/flanders-darts-trophy-2026-prize-money-how-much-did-james-wade-and-ross-smith-earn-in-antwerp |website=Darts News |access-date=21 September 2026}}</ref>

== Qualification and format ==
The top 16 players on the two-year [[PDC Order of Merit]] were [[Seeding (sports)|seeded]] and entered the tournament in the second round, while the next 16 highest-ranked players from the one-year [[PDC Pro Tour Order of Merit]] automatically qualified for the first round. The seedings were confirmed on 23 July.<ref>{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=26 July 2026 |title=2026 ET13-14 Seeds & Tour Card Holder qualifier entries confirmed |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/july/26/2026-et13-14-seeds---tour-card-holder-qualifier-entries-confirmed/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=27 July 2026}}</ref> The remaining 16 places will go to players from four qualifying events – 10 from the Tour Card Holder Qualifier (held on 30 July),<ref>{{cite web |date=30 July 2026 |title=PDC ET13 Tour Card Holder Qualifier |url=https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdceutcq26et13/matches |website=DartConnect |access-date=30 July 2026}}</ref> four from the Host Nation Qualifier (held on 10 September),<ref>{{cite web |date=10 September 2026 |title=PDC ET13 Host Nation Qualifier |url=https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdc26et13hnq/matches |website=DartConnect |access-date=10 September 2026}}</ref> one from the Nordic & Baltic Associate Member Qualifier (held on 7 August),<ref>{{cite web |date=7 August 2026 |title=PDCNB 2026 Tour - Norway - Weekend 05 |url=https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdcnbpro26e05/matches |website=DartConnect |access-date=7 August 2026}}</ref> and one from the East European Associate Member Qualifier (held on 8 August).<ref>{{cite web |date=8 August 2026 |title=PDC ET13 E. Europe Qualifier |url=https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdceeu26et13q/matches |website=DartConnect |access-date=8 August 2026}}</ref>

[[Luke Littler]], [[Luke Humphries]], [[Jonny Clayton]] and [[Gary Anderson (darts player)|Gary Anderson]] withdrew and were replaced by [[Gabriel Clemens]], [[James Hurrell]], [[Kim Huybrechts]] and [[Brendan Dolan]]. [[Jermaine Wattimena]], [[Luke Woodhouse]], [[Martin Schindler]] and [[Krzysztof Ratajski]] moved up to become the 13th, 14th, 15th and 16th seeds respectively.<ref name="PDPA">{{cite web |date=11 September 2026 |title=Flanders Darts Trophy 2026 |url=https://pdpa.co.uk/event/flanders-darts-trophy-2026/ |website=Professional Darts Players Association |access-date=21 September 2026}}</ref><ref name="Draw">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=10 September 2026 |title=2026 Blåkläder Flanders Darts Trophy draw & schedule confirmed |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/september/10/2026-flanders-darts-trophy-draw-schedule-confirmed/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=10 September 2026}}</ref>
