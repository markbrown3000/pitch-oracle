The '''2026 Belgian Darts Open''' (known for sponsorship reasons as the '''2026 Lecot Belgian Darts Open''') was a professional [[darts]] tournament that took place at the Oktoberhallen in [[Wieze]], Belgium, from 20 to 22 March 2026.<ref>{{cite web |title=Lecot Belgian Darts Open 2026 |url=https://pdc-europe.tv/tournaments/et-2026-en/belgian-darts-open-2026/ |website=PDC Europe |access-date=12 February 2026}}</ref> It was the third of fifteen [[PDC European Tour]] events on the [[2026 PDC Pro Tour]]. It featured a field of 48 players and £230,000 in prize money, with £35,000 going to the winner.

[[Luke Littler]] was the defending champion, having defeated [[Mike De Decker]] 8–5 in the [[2025 Belgian Darts Open|2025 final]].<ref name="IND">{{cite web |date=10 March 2025 |title=Luke Littler continues winning streak with defence of Belgian Open title |url=https://www.independent.co.uk/sport/luke-littler-premier-league-brighton-dave-chisnall-james-wade-b2711872.html |website=The Independent |access-date=8 February 2026 |agency=PA Sport}}</ref><ref name="SKYF">{{cite web |date=10 March 2025 |title=Belgian Darts Open: Luke Littler beats Mike De Decker 8-5 to retain European Tour title |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13325213/belgian-darts-open-luke-littler-beats-mike-de-decker-8-5-to-retain-european-tour-title |website=Sky Sports |access-date=8 February 2026}}</ref><ref name="BBCF">{{cite web |last=Collins |first=Ben |date=9 March 2025 |title=Littler beats De Decker to defend Belgian title |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/darts/articles/c39v7rvxxnno |website=BBC Sport |access-date=8 February 2026}}</ref> However, he lost 6–5 to [[Niels Zonneveld]] in the third round, marking the end of Littler's 12-match unbeaten streak at the tournament.

[[Luke Humphries]] won the tournament, his ninth European Tour title, by defeating [[Jonny Clayton]] 8–6 in the final.

==Prize money==
As part of a mass boost in prize money for [[Professional Darts Corporation]] (PDC) events in 2026, the prize fund for all [[2026 PDC Pro Tour#European Tour#European Tour|2026 European Tour]] events rose to £230,000, of which the winner receives £35,000.<ref>{{cite web |last=Phillips |first=Josh |date=31 March 2025 |title=Biggest prize money increase in PDC history confirmed |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/march/31/biggest-prize-money-increase-pdc-history-confirmed |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=16 February 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=Belgian Darts Open 2026 |url=https://mastercaller.com/tournaments/belgian-darts-open/2026 |website=Mastercaller |access-date=16 February 2026}}</ref>

==Qualification and format==
The top 16 players on the two-year [[PDC Order of Merit]] were [[Seeding (sports)|seeded]] and entered the tournament in the second round, while the next 16 highest-ranked players from the one-year [[PDC Pro Tour Order of Merit]] automatically qualified for the first round. The seedings were confirmed on 12 February.<ref name="Entries">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=12 February 2026 |title=2026 ET3-4 Seeds & Tour Card Holder qualifier entries confirmed |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/february/12/2026-et3-4-seeds---tour-card-holder-qualifier-entries-confirmed/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=12 February 2026}}</ref><ref name="Race">{{cite web |title=Race to ET3 and ET4 2026 |url=https://www.dartsrankings.com/et4 |website=dartsrankings.com |access-date=12 February 2026}}</ref> The remaining 16 places went to players from four qualifying events – 10 from the Tour Card Holder Qualifier (held on 18 February),<ref>{{cite web |date=18 February 2026 |title=PDC ET03 Tour Card Holder Qualifier |url=https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdceutcq26et03/matches |website=DartConnect |access-date=12 February 2026}}</ref> four from the Host Nation Qualifier (held on 19 March),<ref name="HNQ">{{cite web |date=19 March 2026 |title=PDC ET03 Host Nation Qualifier |url=https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdc26et03hnq/matches |website=DartConnect |access-date=12 February 2026}}</ref> one from the Nordic & Baltic Associate Member Qualifier (held on 7 February),<ref>{{cite web |date=7 February 2026 |title=PDCNB 2026 Tour - Latvia - Weekend 01 |url=https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdcnbpro26e01/matches |website=DartConnect |access-date=12 February 2026}}</ref> and one from the East European Associate Member Qualifier (held on 25 January).<ref>{{cite web |date=25 January 2026 |title=PDC ET03 E. Europe Qualifier |url=https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdceeu26et03q/matches |website=DartConnect |access-date=12 February 2026}}</ref>

[[Gian van Veen]], [[Gary Anderson (darts player)|Gary Anderson]] and [[Gerwyn Price]] withdrew and were replaced by [[Ricky Evans (darts player)|Ricky Evans]], [[Mickey Mansell]] and [[Ian White (darts player)|Ian White]]. [[Damon Heta]], [[Jermaine Wattimena]] and [[Mike De Decker]] moved up to become the 14th, 15th and 16th seeds respectively.<ref name="Draw">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=19 March 2026 |title=2026 Lecot Belgian Darts Open draw & schedule |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/march/19/2026-lecot-belgian-darts-open-draw-schedule/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=20 March 2026}}</ref>

==Summary==
===First round===
[[File:Robert Thornton 5-6 Kim Huybrechts - Kim Huybrechts - 2019249212936 2019-09-06 PDC European Darts Matchplay - 0947 - B70I5964.jpg|thumb|Belgian qualifiers [[Kim Huybrechts]] ''(pictured in 2019)'' and [[Andy Baetens]] were victorious in the first round. They reached the third round and the quarter-finals, respectively.]]
The first round (best of 11 [[Leg (darts)|legs]]) was played on 20 March.<ref name="PDCDay1">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=20 March 2026 |title=Huybrechts & Baetens deliver on Day One at Belgian Darts Open |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/march/20/Huybrechts-Baetens-deliver-Belgian-Darts-Open/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=21 March 2026}}</ref> Belgium's [[Kim Huybrechts]], a two-time European Tour winner, was victorious in his opening match, defeating [[Niko Springer]] 6–3 with a [[three-dart average]] of 101.76. "The feeling you get when you play in front of your home crowd and you win—this is amazing," said Huybrechts after the match.<ref name="PDCDay1"/> Five-time world champion [[Raymond van Barneveld]] was beaten 6–1 by [[Boris Krčmar]], while [[2023 PDC World Darts Championship|2023]] world champion [[Michael Smith (darts player)|Michael Smith]] lost 6–3 to [[Mickey Mansell]], who hit a 161 [[Glossary of darts#Checkout|checkout]].<ref>{{cite web |date=20 March 2026 |title=Belgian Darts Open: Former world champions Michael Smith and Raymond van Barneveld eliminated in first round |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13522430/belgian-darts-open-former-world-champions-michael-smith-and-raymond-van-barneveld-eliminated-in-first-round |website=Sky Sports |access-date=21 March 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |date=20 March 2026 |title=Mickey Mansell knocks former world champion Michael Smith out of Belgian Darts Open |url=https://www.rte.ie/sport/darts/2026/0320/1564472-mansell-knocks-smith-out-of-belgian-darts-open/ |website=RTÉ Sport |access-date=21 March 2026 |agency=Press Association}}</ref> Belgium's [[Andy Baetens]] went to a [[Glossary of darts#Decider|deciding leg]] against [[Joe Cullen (darts player)|Joe Cullen]], where both players missed [[Glossary of darts#Match dart|match darts]] before Baetens won the match on [[Glossary of darts#Double|double]] 8.<ref name="News1">{{cite web |last=Wood |first=Kieran |date=20 March 2026 |title=Belgian Darts Open 2026 Roundup - Wessel Nijman dumped out as Baetens stuns Cullen and Krcmar batters Van Barneveld in Friday night drama |url=https://dartsnews.com/pdc/belgian-darts-open-2026-roundup-wessel-nijman-dumped-out-as-baetens-stuns-cullen-and-krcmar-batters-van-barneveld-in-friday-night-drama |website=dartsnews.com |access-date=21 March 2026}}</ref> The other two host nation qualifiers were eliminated: [[Ryan Meikle]] came back from 4–1 behind to defeat François Schweyen 6–4, and [[Luke Woodhouse]] converted a 161 checkout on his way to winning 6–2 against European Tour debutant Pascal Devroey.<ref name="PDCEurope1">{{cite web |date=20 March 2026 |title=Belgian Darts Open: Huybrechts und Baetens sorgen für Ekstase in Wieze |trans-title=Belgian Darts Open: Huybrechts and Baetens cause ecstasy in Wieze |url=https://www.pdc-europe.tv/de/aktuell/belgian-darts-open-huybrechts-und-baetens-sorgen-fuer-ekstase-in-wieze/ |website=PDC Europe |access-date=21 March 2026 |language=de}}</ref> [[Wessel Nijman]], who won the [[2026 European Darts Trophy|European Darts Trophy]] the previous week, was beaten 6–3 by [[Sebastian Białecki]].<ref name="News1"/>

Finland's [[Jani Haavisto]] returned to the European Tour for the first time in 11 years and 11 months, losing 6–4 to [[Niels Zonneveld]].<ref name="PDCEurope1"/> [[Cristo Reyes]] hit a 160 checkout to complete a 6–4 win over [[Krzysztof Ratajski]]. Eight-time European Tour winner [[Dave Chisnall]] won five consecutive legs to defeat [[Thibault Tricole]] 6–2. [[William O'Connor (darts player)|William O'Connor]] and [[Cameron Menzies]] earned 6–1 victories over European Tour debutants [[Tyler Thorpe]] and [[Sietse Lap]], respectively. [[Daryl Gurney]] was also a 6–1 winner against [[Ian White (darts player)|Ian White]].<ref name="PDCDay1"/>

===Second round===
[[File:2025-04-03 Premier League Darts Berlin 2025 by Sandro Halank–130.jpg|thumb|The defending champion [[Luke Littler]] ''(pictured in 2025)'' won his opening match, but was eliminated by [[Niels Zonneveld]] in the third round.]]
The second round (best of 11 legs) was played on 21 March.<ref name="PDCDay2">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=21 March 2026 |title=Brilliant Littler dazzles in Wieze as Belgian duo progress |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/march/21/Brilliant-Littler-dazzles-Wieze-Belgian-duo-progress/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=22 March 2026}}</ref> World champion [[Luke Littler]] won his opening match 6–2 against Boris Krčmar, averaging 104.70 in the process. "My first European Tour event was here. I want to win it again, and performances like that will put me up there," commented Littler afterwards.<ref name="SkyDay2">{{cite web |date=21 March 2026 |title=Belgian Darts Open: Luke Littler averages 104 in opening win as James Wade and Josh Rock suffer early exits |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13522895/belgian-darts-open-luke-littler-averages-104-in-opening-win-as-james-wade-and-josh-rock-suffer-early-exits |website=Sky Sports |access-date=22 March 2026}}</ref> Belgian duo Kim Huybrechts and Andy Baetens progressed to the third round with respective 6–4 victories over [[2026 Premier League Darts|Premier League]] players [[Josh Rock]] and [[Stephen Bunting]]; their compatriot [[Mike De Decker]], the 16th seed, squandered a 5–3 lead and lost 6–5 to [[Niels Zonneveld]], who set up a third-round meeting with Littler.<ref>{{cite web |date=21 March 2026 |title='Deze mensen zijn gek. Fantastisch om hier te spelen': Kim Huybrechts en Andy Baetens kwalificeren zich voor derde ronde, Mike De Decker ligt eruit |trans-title='These people are crazy. Fantastic to play here': Kim Huybrechts and Andy Baetens qualify for third round, Mike De Decker is out |url=https://www.hln.be/darts/deze-mensen-zijn-gek-fantastisch-om-hier-te-spelen-kim-huybrechts-en-andy-baetens-kwalificeren-zich-voor-derde-ronde-mike-de-decker-ligt-eruit~ad1d225e/?slug_rd=1 |website=HLN |access-date=22 March 2026 |language=nl}}</ref> Dirk van Duijvenbode attempted to overturn a 4–0 deficit against world number two [[Luke Humphries]], missing darts to send the match to a deciding leg and allowing Humphries to take a 6–4 win. Third and fourth seeds [[Michael van Gerwen]] and [[Jonny Clayton]] were also victorious, securing wins over Mickey Mansell and Sebastian Białecki.<ref name="SkyDay2"/>

Sixth seed [[James Wade]] lost 6–2 to Cristo Reyes, eleventh seed [[Nathan Aspinall]] was beaten 6–3 by [[Ryan Joyce]], and Daryl Gurney defeated fourteenth seed [[Damon Heta]] 6–3. [[Danny Noppert]] averaged 106.17 on his way to beating Luke Woodhouse 6–3. [[Ryan Searle (darts player)|Ryan Searle]] converted a 122 checkout to win a deciding leg against [[Ricky Evans (darts player)|Ricky Evans]]. [[Jermaine Wattimena]], [[Chris Dobey]], and [[Martin Schindler]] all advanced with 6–3 victories, while [[Ross Smith (darts player)|Ross Smith]] was a 6–4 victor over Cameron Menzies.<ref name="PDCDay2"/>

===Final day===
[[File:2025-04-03 Premier League Darts Berlin 2025 by Sandro Halank–080.jpg|thumb|[[Luke Humphries]] ''(pictured in 2025)'' won his ninth European Tour title.]]
The third round, quarter-finals, semi-finals and final were played on 22 March. The third round and quarter-finals were contested over the best of 11 legs, the semi-finals over the best of 13 legs, and the final over the best of 15 legs.<ref name="PDCFinal">{{cite web |last=Wagner |first=Connor |date=22 March 2026 |title=Heroic Humphries edges past Clayton to clinch Belgian Darts Open |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/march/22/2026-lecot-belgian-darts-open---finals-day-latest/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=22 March 2026}}</ref> The final day saw Jonny Clayton and Luke Humphries reach the final. In the third round, where six of the eight matches went to a deciding leg,<ref name="EUFinal">{{cite web |date=22 March 2026 |title=Lecot Belgian Darts Open: Humphries erstmals Champion in Belgien |trans-title=Lecot Belgian Darts Open: Humphries becomes champion in Belgium for the first time |url=https://www.pdc-europe.tv/de/aktuell/lecot-belgian-darts-open-humphries-erstmals-champion-in-belgien/ |website=PDC Europe |access-date=23 March 2026 |language=de}}</ref> Luke Littler suffered a 6–5 loss to Niels Zonneveld, who won the decider with a 147 checkout. This marked Littler's first defeat in a ranking event since October 2025, and his first-ever defeat at the Belgian Darts Open after a streak of 12 matches unbeaten, including title wins in [[2024 Belgian Darts Open|2024]] and [[2025 Belgian Darts Open|2025]].<ref name="BBCFinal">{{cite web |date=22 March 2026 |title=Humphries wins Belgian Open then makes 'second best' joke |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/darts/articles/c78ldpxv5dpo |website=BBC Sport |access-date=23 March 2026}}</ref> "It's such a relief. I showed some really good nerve there. I'm so happy," commented Zonneveld after the win.<ref name="SkyFinal">{{cite web |last1=Mirza |first1=Raz |last2=Mills |first2=Stephen |date=22 March 2026 |title=Luke Humphries wins Belgian Darts Open as Luke Littler's hopes of Wieze hat-trick ends in shock defeat |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13523090/luke-humphries-wins-belgian-darts-open-as-luke-littlers-hopes-of-wieze-hat-trick-ends-in-shock-defeat |website=Sky Sports |access-date=23 March 2026}}</ref> Clayton followed a 6–5 victory over Ross Smith by beating first-time quarter-finalist Andy Baetens 6–1. In the semi-finals, Zonneveld missed four match darts to eliminate Clayton, who won 7–6 to advance to the final. Humphries secured his place in the final by defeating Jermaine Wattimena, Chris Dobey and Michael van Gerwen.<ref name="PDCFinal"/><ref name="EUFinal"/> Clayton and Humphries had contested the night six final of the [[2026 Premier League Darts|2026 Premier League]] earlier in the month, where Clayton won 6–1.<ref>{{cite web |last=Higham |first=Paul |date=12 March 2026 |title=Clayton overcomes gout to crush Humphries in final |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/darts/articles/cze0z3rp91do |website=BBC Sport |access-date=13 March 2026}}</ref>

Clayton claimed the opening leg of the final, but the Welshman soon found himself behind as Humphries won the next six legs to lead 6–1, converting checkouts of 142 and 130 during his run. Clayton then staged a comeback, winning four of the next five legs, including a 126 checkout on the [[Bullseye (target)|bullseye]]. He trailed 7–6, one leg away from a decider, but Humphries completed the next leg in 13 darts to secure an 8–6 victory.<ref name="BBCFinal"/> He finished the match with a three-dart average of 97.37.<ref name="Results"/>

Humphries won the tournament, his ninth European Tour title and first in Belgium. The victory extended his run of winning a European Tour title every year since 2022, and placed him in joint-second on the all-time list of European Tour winners alongside [[Gerwyn Price]] and [[Peter Wright (darts player)|Peter Wright]], and behind Van Gerwen.<ref name="SkyFinal"/><ref>{{cite web |last=Michael |first=Lucas |date=22 March 2026 |title=Belgian Darts Open 2026 Round-up - Luke Humphries holds off charging Clayton to clinch the title in Wieze |url=https://dartsnews.com/pdc/live-belgian-darts-open-2026-humphries-van-gerwen-clayton-among-the-names-eyeing-up-silverware-in-wieze |website=dartsnews.com |access-date=23 March 2026}}</ref> Speaking after the match, Humphries said he "felt the nerves" when attempting to close out the match, saying: "I did [feel the nerves] in the end there because Jonny [Clayton] is one of the most underrated darts players in the world. He's always there and you can't shake him off."<ref name="SkyFinal"/> "I'm just dedicated and working so hard to push every darts player and be the best version of myself," added the world number two, before joking that he was going to "keep trying to be the second-best player in the world".<ref>{{cite web |date=22 March 2026 |title=Luke Humphries wins in Belgium as Luke Littler’s hat-trick bid ends early |url=https://www.independent.co.uk/sport/luke-humphries-luke-littler-belgium-b2943503.html |website=The Independent |access-date=23 March 2026 |agency=Press Association}}</ref> Speaking in defeat, Clayton praised his opponent, calling Humphries "a great asset to darts". He remarked: "When you've got to face the likes of Luke [Humphries] week in, week out, it's difficult, but I'm still a part of it, so I'm proud of myself."<ref name="PDCFinal"/>
