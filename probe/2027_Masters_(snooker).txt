The '''2027 Masters''' (officially the '''2027 Johnstone's Paint Masters''') is an upcoming professional non-ranking [[snooker]] tournament that will take place from 10 to 17 January 2027 at [[Alexandra Palace]] in London, England. The second [[Triple Crown (snooker)|Triple Crown]] event of the [[2026–27 snooker season]], following the [[2026 UK Championship]] and preceding the [[2027 World Snooker Championship|2027 World Championship]], the tournament will be the 53rd edition of the [[Masters (snooker)|Masters]], which was first held in [[1975 Masters (snooker)|1975]].

[[Kyren Wilson]] will be the defending champion, having defeated [[John Higgins]] 10–6 in the [[2026 Masters (snooker)|2026 final]].

The 2027 edition of the tournament—the 53rd consecutive staging since its inaugural edition in 1975—will take place from 10 to 17 January at Alexandra Palace in London, England.<ref>{{cite web |title=Masters 2027|url=https://www.snooker.org/res/index.asp?event=2560|website=snooker.org|access-date=23 April 2026}}</ref><ref name="wst_tickets">{{cite web|url=https://www.wst.tv/tickets/masters-tickets-2027/|title=Johnstone's Paint Masters 2027|website=[[World Snooker Tour]]|access-date=23 April 2026}}</ref> It will be the second [[Triple Crown (snooker)|Triple Crown]] event of the [[2026–27 snooker season]], following the [[2026 UK Championship]] and preceding the [[2027 World Snooker Championship|2027 World Championship]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Calendar 2026/2027|url=https://www.snooker.org/res/index.asp?template=2&season=2026|website=snooker.org|access-date=23 April 2026}}</ref> It will be sponsored by [[Johnstone's Paint]].<ref name="wst_tickets"/> [[Kyren Wilson]] is the defending champion, having defeated [[John Higgins]] 10–6 in the 2026 final to win his first Masters title.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Sutcliffe |first=Steve |date=18 January 2026 |title=Wilson defeats Higgins to claim first Masters title |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cm2jlvmz1xdo |access-date=23 April 2026 |website=[[BBC Sport]] |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260423074105/https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cm2jlvmz1xdo|archive-date=23 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>
