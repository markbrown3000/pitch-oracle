The '''2026 British Open''' (officially the '''2026 Unibet British Open''') was a professional [[snooker]] tournament that took place from 31 August to 6 September 2026 at the [[Cheltenham Racecourse|Centaur]] in [[Cheltenham]], England. Qualifying took place on 23 and 24 July at the [[Leicester Arena]] in [[Leicester]], England. The sixth consecutive edition of the tournament since it was revived in [[2021 British Open|2021]], it was the fourth [[List of snooker ranking tournaments|ranking event]] of the [[2026–27 snooker season]], following the [[2026 Wuhan Open (snooker)|2026 Wuhan Open]] and preceding the [[2026 English Open (snooker)|2026 English Open]]. The winner received £100,000 from a total prize fund of £502,000. 

[[Shaun Murphy]] was the defending champion, having defeated [[Anthony McGill]] 10–7 in the [[2025 British Open|2025 final]], but he was beaten by [[David Gilbert (snooker player)|David Gilbert]] in the last 64.

[[Jak Jones]] won the tournament, beating [[Mark Selby]] 10–6 in the final.

==Overview==
[[File:2025 British Open (70982).jpg|upright=0.85|thumb|left|The Centaur at [[Cheltenham Racecourse]] ''(pictured in 2025)'' hosted the event.]]

The tournament began in [[1980 British Gold Cup|1980]] as the non-ranking British Gold Cup, won by [[Alex Higgins]].<ref>{{cite web |date=25 July 2010 |title=Alex 'Hurricane' Higgins |url=https://www.thetimes.com/travel/destinations/uk-travel/northern-ireland-travel/alex-hurricane-higgins-nh367gk2cj8 |access-date=1 September 2025 |website=[[The Times]] |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418133006/https://www.thetimes.com/travel/destinations/uk-travel/northern-ireland-travel/alex-hurricane-higgins-nh367gk2cj8 |archive-date=18 April 2026 |url-status=live}}</ref> Held as the non-ranking Yamaha Organs Trophy in 1981 and the non-ranking Yamaha International Masters from 1982 to 1984, it was renamed the [[British Open (snooker)|British Open]] in [[1985 British Open|1985]], when it also gained ranking status.<ref>{{cite web |last=Turner |first=Chris |title=British Open including British Gold Cup, Yamaha Organs trophy and Yamaha International Masters |url=http://www.cajt.pwp.blueyonder.co.uk/brit.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120216155129/http://www.cajt.pwp.blueyonder.co.uk/brit.html |archive-date=16 February 2012 |access-date=1 September 2025 |work=cajt.pwp.blueyonder.co.uk |url-status=dead}}</ref> Staged 21 times as a ranking event from 1985 until its discontinuation after the [[2004 British Open|2004 edition]], the tournament was revived as a ranking event in [[2021 British Open|2021]]. In [[2022 British Open|2022]], the tournament trophy was named the [[Clive Everton]] Trophy to honour the longtime commentator and snooker journalist.<ref name="CE_trophy">{{cite web |url=https://wst.tv/british-open-trophy-named-after-clive-everton/ |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220921121610/https://wst.tv/british-open-trophy-named-after-clive-everton/ |url-status=dead |archive-date=21 September 2022 |title=British Open trophy named after Clive Everton |work=[[World Snooker Tour]] |date=21 September 2022 |access-date=1 October 2023}}</ref>

Sponsored by [[Unibet]],<ref>{{cite web |date=4 June 2026 |title=Unibet returns as title partner for snooker's historic British Open |url=https://www.wst.tv//news/2026/june/04/unibet-returns-as-title-partner-for-snooker-s-historic-british-open-/ |access-date=19 August 2026 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en}}</ref> the 2026 edition of the tournament was held from 31 August to 6 September at the [[Cheltenham Racecourse|Centaur]] in [[Cheltenham]], England. Qualifying took place on 23 and 24 July at the [[Leicester Arena]] in [[Leicester]], England.<ref name="s.o_Q">{{cite web |url=https://www.snooker.org/res/index.asp?event=2767 |title=British Open Qualifiers 2026 |website=snooker.org |access-date=23 July 2026}}</ref> The tournament was the fourth [[List of snooker ranking tournaments|ranking event]] of the [[2026–27 snooker season]], following the [[2026 Wuhan Open (snooker)|2026 Wuhan Open]] and preceding the [[2026 English Open (snooker)|2026 English Open]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.snooker.org/res/index.asp?season=2026&template=2&tour=all |title=Calendar 2026/2027 |website=snooker.org |access-date=27 April 2026}}</ref> [[Shaun Murphy]] was the defending champion, having defeated [[Anthony McGill]] 10–7 in the [[2025 British Open|2025 final]],<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.snooker.org/res/index.asp?event=2182 |title=Unibet British Open 2025 |website=snooker.org |access-date=27 April 2026}}</ref> but he was beaten 4–2 by [[David Gilbert (snooker player)|David Gilbert]] in the last 64.<ref name="BBC_DefChampOut">{{cite news |title=Defending champion Murphy exits British Open |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/c93en2gq7dgo |work=[[BBC Sport]] |first=Timothy |last=Abraham |date=2 September 2026 |access-date=3 September 2026}}</ref>

[[Jak Jones]] won the event, beating [[Mark Selby]] 10–6 in the final.<ref name="WST_FinalResult" />

There were 72 [[century break]]s made during the tournament, with 23 during qualifying,<ref name="si_cent_Q" /> and 49 at the main stage.<ref name="si_cent_M" /> The highest {{cuegloss|break}} was a [[Maximum break|147]] made by [[Liam Highfield]]<ref name="Max 245" />

=== Format ===
There was no [[Seeding (sports)|seeding]] system and the draw was randomised for every round.<ref name="SHQ_preview">{{cite web |title=British Open: 2026 draw, preview, prize money, how to watch |url=https://snookerhq.com/2026/08/31/british-open-2026-draw-preview-prize-money-how-to-watch/ |website=snookerhq.com |first=David |last=Caulfield |date=31 August 2026 |access-date=31 August 2026}}</ref><ref name="WST_main" /> Matches were played as the best of 7 {{cuegloss|frame|frames}} up to the quarter-finals, which were the best of 9 frames. The semi-finals were the best of 11 frames, and the final was the best of 19 frames, played over two {{cuegloss|session|sessions}}.<ref>{{cite web |date=4 August 2026 |title=Win tickets to watch the sport's biggest names in action as the World Snooker Tour returns to Cheltenham |url=https://www.soglos.com/competition/entertainment/win-tickets-to-watch-the-sports-biggest-names-in-action-as-the-world-snooker-tour-returns-to-cheltenham/25971/ |access-date=19 August 2026 |website=www.soglos.com |language=en}}</ref>

== Summary ==
=== Qualifying ===
[[Liam Highfield]] made the first [[maximum break]] of his professional career in the first {{cuegloss|frame}} of his 4–1 victory over [[Stephen Maguire]]. It was the 245th official maximum.<ref name="Max 245">{{cite web |title=Highfield hits maximum in Unibet British Open qualifying |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/july/24/unibet-british-open-qualifying-day-two/ |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |date=24 July 2026 |access-date=25 July 2026 |archive-date=25 July 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260725111238/https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/july/24/unibet-british-open-qualifying-day-two/ |url-status=live}}</ref>

[[Mark Allen (snooker player)|Mark Allen]] and [[Ronnie O'Sullivan]] both withdrew from the tournament, and were replaced by amateur players Patrick Whelan and [[Mark Joyce]] respectively.<ref name="WST_Allen_wd">{{cite web |title=Allen withdraws from British Open and English Open |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/august/26/allen-withdraws-from-british-open-and-english-open/ |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |date=26 August 2026 |access-date=26 August 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |title=Allen to miss British and English Opens for personal reasons |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/c36l1z13z17o |work=[[BBC Sport]] |first=Richard |last=Petrie |date=26 August 2026 |access-date=27 August 2026}}</ref><ref name="WST_ROS_wd">{{cite web |title=O’Sullivan withdraws from British Open |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/august/28/o-sullivan-withdraws-from-british-open/ |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |date=28 August 2026 |access-date=28 August 2026}}</ref>

[[Judd Trump]] recovered from 0–2 down to take the next four {{cuegloss|frame|frames}}, beating [[Dylan Emery]] 4–2. Defending champion [[Shaun Murphy]] [[Whitewash (sport)#Snooker|whitewashed]] [[Reanne Evans]], and [[David Gilbert (snooker player)|David Gilbert]] beat [[Mark Williams (snooker player)|Mark Williams]], also by a whitewash.<ref name="WST_day01">{{cite web |title=Unibet British Open day one |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/august/31/unibet-british-open-day-one/ |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |date=31 August 2026 |access-date=1 September 2026}}</ref> [[Wu Yize]] defeated [[Liu Hongyu (snooker player)|Liu Hongyu]] 4–3, and [[Kyren Wilson]] beat [[Florian Nüßle]] 4–1.<ref>{{cite news |title=Wu to face Wilson in British Open second round |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/c5yde5d99q4o |work=[[BBC Sport]] |first=Jack |last=Skelton |date=1 September 2026 |access-date=1 September 2026}}</ref>

=== Round two (last 64) ===
[[Jack Lisowski]] defeated [[Mitchell Mann]], by 4–3 on the final {{cuegloss|black ball|black}}, [[Liam Davies (snooker player)|Liam Davies]] beat [[Barry Hawkins]] 4–2, and Wilson defeated Wu 4–1.<ref name="WST_day02">{{cite web |title=Unibet British Open day two |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/september/01/unibet-british-open-day-two/ |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |date=1 September 2026 |access-date=2 September 2026}}</ref> [[Mark Selby]] defeated [[Lei Peifan]] 4–3, Gilbert beat defending champion Murphy 4–2,<ref name="BBC_DefChampOut" /> Trump beat [[Ricky Walden]], also by 4–2, [[John Higgins]] defeated [[Noppon Saengkham]] 4–3, and [[Zhao Xintong]] beat [[Daniel Wells (snooker player)|Daniel Wells]] 4–2.<ref name="WST_day03">{{cite web |title=Unibet British Open day three |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/september/02/unibet-british-open-day-three/ |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |date=2 September 2026 |access-date=3 September 2026}}</ref>

=== Round three (last 32) ===
[[Iulian Boiko]] beat [[Ding Junhui]] 4–1, Lisowski recovered from 1–3 down to beat [[Ishpreet Singh Chadha]] 4–3, Selby defeated [[Jackson Page]] 4–3, [[Jak Jones]] beat [[Chang Bingyu]] 4–1, and [[Artemijs Žižins]] beat [[He Guoqiang (snooker player)|He Guoqiang]], also by 4–1.<ref name="WST_day03" />

=== Round four (last 16) ===
[[Stan Moody]] defeated Higgins 4–2, Lisowski beat [[Lyu Haotian]] 4–1, and Selby beat Boiko 4–2.<ref name="WST_day04">{{cite web |title=Unibet British Open day four |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/september/03/unibet-british-open-day-four/ |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |date=3 September 2026 |access-date=4 September 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |title=Teenager Moody knocks Higgins out of British Open |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cj9xev2dkmmo |work=[[BBC Sport]] |first=Mike |last=Peter |date=3 September 2026 |access-date=4 September 2026}}</ref> Trump whitewashed Gilbert, Jones beat Žižins 4–2, [[Aaron Hill (snooker player)|Aaron Hill]] whitewashed [[Fan Zhengyi]], and Highfield beat [[Long Zehuang]] 4–1.<ref name="WST_day04" />

=== Round five (quarter-finals) ===
The quarter-finals were played on 4 September as the best of nine {{cuegloss|frame|frames}}.<ref name="s.o_M" /><ref>{{cite web |title=What the quarter-finals look like in the 2026 British Open draw |url=https://snookerhq.com/2026/09/04/2026-british-open-quarter-finals-draw/ |website=snookerhq.com |first=David |last=Caulfield |date=4 September 2026 |access-date=4 September 2026}}</ref>

In the afternoon {{cuegloss|session}} Selby whitewashed Lisowski and Highfield beat Moody 5–2.<ref>{{cite web |title=Highfield floors Moody to reach first semi |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/september/04/Highfield-Floors-Moody-To-reach-First-Semi/ |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |date=4 September 2026 |access-date=4 September 2026}}</ref> In the evening session Trump beat Zhao 5–1, and Jones beat Hill 5–4.<ref>{{cite web |title=Determined Trump thrashes Zhao |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/september/04/Determined-Trump-Thrashes-Zhao/ |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |date=4 September 2026 |access-date=5 September 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |title=Trump thrashes Zhao to reach last four of British Open |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/c305qe2zrndo |work=[[BBC Sport]] |first=Steve |last=Sutcliffe |date=4 September 2026 |access-date=5 September 2026}}</ref>

=== Round six (semi-finals) ===
The semi-finals were played on 5 September as the best of eleven frames.<ref name="s.o_M" />

[[File:Mark Selby PHC 2011-1.jpg|left|thumb|[[Mark Selby]] (''pictured in 2011'') beat [[Liam Highfield]] 6–3, in a match that lasted five hours.]]
[[File:Jak Jones PHC 2016-3.jpg|upright=0.85|thumb|[[Jak Jones]] (''pictured in 2016'') beat [[Judd Trump]] 6–3.]]
In the afternoon session Selby beat Highfield 6–3, in a match that lasted five hours, to reach his 40th ranking final. After the match, Selby said: "It was a tough match, Liam {{bracket|Highfield}} didn't make it easy for me at all, his {{cuegloss|safety}} was incredible. I can't remember him playing a poor safety shot all the way through the match. The frames just went scrappy, if no one got in first and won it in one visit, the frames seemed to just follow the same pattern. It was hard to keep your patience and not try and push the boat out because you get frustrated. I was out there fighting. It looked like going 3–1, he probably deserved to be in front. That was probably the difference between me winning and losing the match, it was a key turning point. I won the next frame to go 3–2 up, then the momentum was on my side. Hopefully I get another victory tomorrow, but if not I'll keep on trying and keep playing the way I've played this season. It has been my best ever start to a season, I've played four tournaments and only lost two matches."<ref>{{cite web |title=Selby reaches 40th ranking final |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/september/05/Selby-Reaches-40th-Ranking-Final-/ |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |date=5 September 2026 |access-date=5 September 2026}}</ref>

In the evening session Jones defeated Trump 6–3. After the match, Jones said: "I knew it would be very tough, Judd {{bracket|Trump}} missed a few balls early on and I punished him, I let him back into it at 3–3 but then made a good break in the next frame. I enjoy taking on the top players because I know I have to play well. I practised hard over the summer, it has been my best start to a season and I feel good about my life on and off the table. Mark Selby is one of the all{{nbh}}time greats, he's big favourite but hopefully I can give him a decent game."<ref>{{cite web |title=Jones stuns Trump to reach final |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/september/05/Jones-Stuns-Trump-To-Reach-Final/ |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |date=5 September 2026 |access-date=6 September 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |title=Jones stuns Trump to set up final against Selby |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/czezl839xpdo |work=[[BBC Sport]] |first=Mandeep |last=Sanghera |date=6 September 2026 |access-date=6 September 2026}}</ref>

=== Final ===
The final was played on 6 September as the best of 19 frames, played over two sessions.<ref name="s.o_M" />

Selby faced Jones.<ref>{{cite web |title=2026 British Open final: Mark Selby vs Jak Jones |url=https://snookerhq.com/2026/09/06/2026-british-open-final-mark-selby-jak-jones/ |website=snookerhq.com |first=David |last=Caulfield |date=6 September 2026 |access-date=6 September 2026}}</ref> At the end of the afternoon session the score was tied at 4–4.<ref>{{cite web |title=Selby and Jones tied in Cheltenham final |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/september/06/Selby-And-Jones-Tied-In-Cheltenham-Final/ |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |date=6 September 2026 |access-date=6 September 2026}}</ref> Jones went on to win the match, and the tournament 10–6.<ref name="WST_Selby_Jones">{{cite web |title=Match: Mark Selby v Jak Jones |url=https://www.wst.tv/match-centre/d6474398-b0e5-4d6a-b6aa-ff5d317dfda9 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |date=6 September 2026 |access-date=7 September 2026 |archive-date=7 September 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260907100032/https://www.wst.tv/match-centre/d6474398-b0e5-4d6a-b6aa-ff5d317dfda9 |url-status=live}}</ref> After the match, Jones said: "The [[2026 Championship League (ranking)|Championship League]] felt good, but obviously this is a completely different level, it's a major tournament, I'm over the moon. I felt I dragged Mark {{bracket|Selby}} down a little bit throughout the day. I just kind of battled away and made a few 50 and 60 breaks. But to beat someone like Mark, who's an all{{nbh}}time great and has won everything in the game, is incredible. It's definitely the best day of my career. Winning a ranking event is better than coming runner{{nbh}}up in a [[2024 World Snooker Championship|World Championship]]. To finally win a big tournament like this – I thought it might not happen. It's an unbelievable start to the season for me, so I'm buzzing. It has been tough for 15 years, I have been battling away but haven't really achieved that much, so to start winning titles feels amazing." Selby said: "Jak {{bracket|Jones}} played great from start to finish, he scored well and his safety was good. He was the best player on {{sic|expected=double word from source}} on the day. I felt it was only a matter of time before he started winning titles."<ref name="WST_FinalResult">{{cite web |title=Giant-killer Jones beats Selby in British final |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/september/06/Giant-Killer-Jones-Beats-Selby-In-British-Final/ |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |date=6 September 2026 |access-date=7 September 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |title=Jones shocks Selby to win British Open title |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cvgy87npvkno |work=[[BBC Sport]] |first=Mandeep |last=Sanghera |date=6 September 2026 |access-date=7 September 2026}}</ref>
