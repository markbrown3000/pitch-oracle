The '''2026 PDC World Cup of Darts''' (known for sponsorship reasons as the '''2026 BetVictor World Cup of Darts''') was a professional [[darts]] tournament that took place from 11 to 14 June 2026 at the [[Eissporthalle Frankfurt|Eissporthalle]] in [[Frankfurt]], Germany. It was the 16th edition of the [[PDC World Cup of Darts]] to be staged by the [[Professional Darts Corporation]] (PDC). The total prize fund was £500,000, of which the winning team shared £100,000.

The tournament featured 40 pairs of national teams. The top four nations based on lowest cumulative placement in the [[PDC World Rankings]] entered the tournament in the second round, with the remaining 36 teams starting in the group stage. [[Trinidad and Tobago at the PDC World Cup|Trinidad and Tobago]] and [[Mongolia at the PDC World Cup|Mongolia]] made their World Cup debuts, while [[Slovenia at the PDC World Cup|Slovenia]] participated in the event for the first time since the [[2010 PDC World Cup of Darts|inaugural edition]] in 2010. [[Uganda at the PDC World Cup|Uganda]] were also set to make their debut, but the team withdrew due to visa issues and were replaced by [[Gibraltar at the PDC World Cup|Gibraltar]]. [[Simon Whitlock]], who won the [[2022 PDC World Cup of Darts|2022 edition]] alongside [[Damon Heta]], missed the event for the first time after representing [[Australia at the PDC World Cup|Australia]] at each previous edition.

[[Northern Ireland at the PDC World Cup|Northern Ireland]]'s [[Josh Rock]] and [[Daryl Gurney]] were the defending champions, having defeated [[Wales at the PDC World Cup|Wales]] 10–9 in the [[2025 PDC World Cup of Darts|2025 final]]. However, they lost 8–2 to the [[Netherlands at the PDC World Cup|Netherlands]] in the semi-finals. [[England at the PDC World Cup|England]], represented by [[Luke Littler]] and [[Luke Humphries]], won a record-extending sixth World Cup title by defeating the Netherlands 10–5 in the final.

=== Format ===
The 40-team format that was introduced in [[2023 PDC World Cup of Darts|2023]] remained. The top four [[Seeding (sports)|seeded]] teams received a [[Bye (sports)|bye]] to the second round, while the remaining 36 teams competed in the group stage. The group stage consisted of twelve groups of three teams, with one qualifying from each group.<ref>{{cite web |date=9 March 2023 |title=World Cup of Darts expanded as radical new format announced |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/12829643/world-cup-of-darts-expanded-as-radical-new-format-announced |website=Sky Sports |access-date=7 February 2026}}</ref>

In this format, all rounds featured doubles matches where teams alternated between players for each turn. All matches were in [[Leg (darts)|leg]] play format, with the number of legs required to win increasing as the tournament progressed:<ref name="HowItWorks">{{cite web |last=Colman |first=Jonty |date=10 June 2025 |title=World Cup of Darts 2026: Teams, schedule, prize money, format and how it works |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/darts/articles/cn4qdxenpw2o |website=BBC Sport |access-date=10 June 2026}}</ref>

*'''Group stage:''' Best of seven legs
*'''Second round, quarter and semi-finals:''' Best of fifteen legs
*'''Final:''' Best of nineteen legs

== Teams and seedings ==
[[File:Mickey Mansell.png|thumb|[[Mickey Mansell]] ''(pictured in 2024)'' switched from [[Northern Ireland at the PDC World Cup|Northern Ireland]] to the [[Republic of Ireland at the PDC World Cup|Republic of Ireland]], becoming the first player in the tournament's history to represent two different nations.]]
The tournament consisted of 40 pairs of national teams. The first 33 nations were confirmed on 6 February 2026, with a further seven teams being determined through various qualifiers: three from a PDC Asian Tour qualifier, two from the PDC Nordic and Baltic rankings, one from a Championship Darts Latin America and Caribbean Tour qualifier, and one from an African Darts Group qualifier.<ref name="Nations">{{cite web |last=Simpson |first=Will |date=6 February 2026 |title=Competing nations confirmed for 2026 BetVictor World Cup of Darts |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/february/06/competing-nations-confirmed-for-2026-betvictor-world-cup-of-darts/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=7 February 2026}}</ref>

== Summary ==
=== Groups ===
The group stage was held over three sessions, the first taking place on 11 June with the second and third taking place on 12 June. In each of the three sessions, one match was played from each group. The loser of the first match played the remaining team in the second match, while the winner of the first match played the remaining team in the third match, ensuring there were no [[dead rubber]]s. In each session, the matches were played in the following group order: H, K, L, D, E, F, J, B, A, C, I, G.<ref name="GS_Draw"/><ref>{{cite web |date=11 June 2026 |title=World Cup of Darts 2026: Schedule, dates, group stage draw as Northern Ireland defend crown in Frankfurt |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13550475/world-cup-of-darts-2026-schedule-dates-group-stage-draw-as-northern-ireland-defend-crown-in-frankfurt |website=Sky Sports |access-date=12 June 2026}}</ref>

Van Gerwen gave the Dutch team a 2–1 lead through a 113 checkout, but England entered the first interval 3–2 ahead after Littler found the match's first break of throw.<ref name="PDC_Final"/><ref name="Sky_Final"/> Humphries converted an 87 finish to break again and establish a three-leg lead at 5–2. England went into the second interval with a 7–3 advantage.<ref name="BBC_Final"/> As England led 8–5 in the 14th leg, Van Gerwen [[Glossary of darts#Bust|bust]] his score by hitting double 13 instead of double 4, allowing Humphries to capitalise and put England one leg away from victory.<ref name="DNews_Final">{{cite web |last=Wood |first=Kieran |date=14 June 2026 |title=England win the World Cup! Luke Littler and Luke Humphries live up to the hype with astonishing final display |url=https://dartsnews.com/pdc/world-cup-of-darts-2026-roundup-england-win-the-world-cup-luke-littler-and-luke-humphries-live-up-to-the-hype-with-astonishing-final-display |website=dartsnews.com |access-date=15 June 2026}}</ref> The English duo then secured the 10–5 win in the next leg when Humphries completed a 12-darter by pinning double 8.<ref name="PDC_Final"/><ref name="BBC_Final"/> England hit a combined 15 maximums and finished the match with a three-dart average of 104.77, the highest of the tournament and the highest pairs average in a World Cup final.<ref name="Sky_Humphries">{{cite web |date=14 June 2026 |title=Luke Humphries says ‘huge expectation’ made World Cup of Darts victory for England with Luke Littler more special |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13553960/luke-humphries-says-huge-expectation-made-world-cup-of-darts-victory-for-england-with-luke-littler-more-special |website=Sky Sports |access-date=15 June 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |date=14 June 2026 |title=BetVictor World Cup of Darts: Littler & Humphries holen Englands sechsten Titel |trans-title=BetVictor World Cup of Darts: Littler & Humphries claim England's sixth title |url=https://www.pdc-europe.tv/de/aktuell/betvictor-world-cup-of-darts-littler-humphries-holen-englands-sechsten-titel/ |website=PDC Europe |access-date=15 June 2026 |language=de}}</ref><ref name="PDC_Legacy">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=15 June 2026 |title=Humphries & Littler targeting World Cup of Darts legacy |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/june/15/humphries---littler-targeting-world-cup-of-darts-legacy/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=15 June 2026}}</ref>

[[File:England WCoD 2026.png|thumb|upright=1.2|Top seeds [[England at the PDC World Cup|England]], represented by [[Luke Littler]] and [[Luke Humphries]] ''(pictured at the event)'', won a record-extending sixth World Cup of Darts title.]]
England won a record-extending sixth World Cup title, denying the Netherlands a record-equalling fifth title.<ref name="PDC_Legacy"/> It was Humphries' second World Cup, while Littler won it for the first time.<ref name="Sky_Littler">{{cite web |last=Wellens |first=Megan |date=14 June 2026 |title=Luke Littler targeting trophy clean sweep after World Cup of Darts triumph with Luke Humphries |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13553928/luke-littler-targeting-trophy-clean-sweep-after-world-cup-of-darts-triumph-with-luke-humphries |website=Sky Sports |access-date=15 June 2026}}</ref> Littler and Humphries became the third different English duo to win the World Cup, after four-time champions [[Phil Taylor (darts player)|Phil Taylor]] and [[Adrian Lewis]], and [[2024 PDC World Cup of Darts|2024]] champions Humphries and [[Michael Smith (darts player)|Michael Smith]].<ref name="HowItWorks"/><ref name="BBC_Final"/> England received the top prize of £100,000, while runners-up the Netherlands received £48,000.<ref name="Sky_Final"/> Speaking after the match, Humphries stated that the pressure put on him and Littler to win the title made the victory more special. He also outlined his hopes of the pair winning more World Cup titles than Taylor and Lewis, while also commenting on the dynamic of partnering a great rival: "You put those differences aside. We're here to win as a team, we won as a team and I'm really, really proud."<ref name="Sky_Humphries"/> Winning the World Cup continued Littler's streak of televised titles in 2026, adding to his victories at the [[2026 PDC World Darts Championship|World Championship]], [[2026 PDC World Masters|World Masters]], [[2026 UK Open|UK Open]], and [[2026 Premier League Darts|Premier League]].<ref name="PDC_Legacy"/> "All the talk now is, am I going to lift every major this year? If I keep playing the way I do, then yeah, I've got a brilliant chance," remarked the reigning world champion in response to talk of a 2026 clean sweep, which would include completing his honours list with his final major singles title, the [[European Championship (darts)|European Championship]].<ref name="Sky_Littler"/> On his team's performance, Littler praised Humphries' contributions and confirmed the duo's plans to defend the title the following year.<ref name="PDC_Legacy"/> Speaking in defeat, Van Gerwen said that the English pair played an "absolutely phenomenal final" that was built on confidence from their quarter-final against Wales, where the three-time world champion claimed they "got a bit lucky" during their route.<ref name="Sky_Humphries"/>
