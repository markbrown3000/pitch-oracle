The '''2026 Saudi Arabia Darts Masters''' (known for sponsorship reasons as the '''2026 Riyadh Season Saudi Arabia Darts Masters''')<ref>{{cite web |title=Riyadh Season Saudi Arabia Darts Masters |url=https://www.pdc.tv/tournament/riyadh-season-saudi-arabia-darts-masters |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=7 January 2026}}</ref> was a professional [[darts]] tournament held at the Global Theatre in [[Riyadh]], Saudi Arabia, from 19 to 20 January 2026. It was the second event in the [[2026 World Series of Darts]].<ref>{{cite web |last=Phillips |first=Josh |date=5 October 2025 |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/october/05/2026-world-series-darts-schedule-confirmed |title=2026 World Series of Darts schedule confirmed |publisher=PDC |access-date=7 January 2026}}</ref> The winner received [[Pound sterling|£]]30,000 from a total prize fund of £100,000. Additionally, a [[United States dollar|$]]100,000 (£74,700) bonus was on offer for any player who hit a [[nine-dart finish]] during the tournament, with the opportunity to double their prize if they hit an extra dart at the [[Bullseye (target)|bullseye]].

Organised by the [[Professional Darts Corporation]] (PDC) as part of [[Riyadh Season]], it was the first PDC event to be staged in Saudi Arabia, following interest shown by PDC president [[Barry Hearn]] and [[General Entertainment Authority]] chairman [[Turki Al-Sheikh]] to bring darts to the country.<ref>{{cite web |last=Keogh |first=Frank |date=6 October 2025 |title=No alcohol allowed - darts heads to Saudi Arabia |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/darts/articles/ckgew816k67o |website=BBC Sport |access-date=7 January 2026}}</ref> The tournament featured 16 players (eight PDC representatives and eight Asian representatives).

[[Luke Littler]] won the tournament, his fifth [[World Series of Darts|World Series]] title, by defeating [[Michael van Gerwen]] 8–5 in the final.

==Overview==
===Format===
Eight elite PDC representatives were drawn to play eight Asian representatives in the first round on Monday 19 January; the quarter-finals, semi-finals and final took place on Tuesday 20 January.<ref name="Draw">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=18 January 2026 |title=2026 Riyadh Season Saudi Arabia Darts Masters draw & schedule |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/january/18/2026-riyadh-season-saudi-arabia-darts-masters-draw-schedule |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=18 January 2026}}</ref>

*'''First round and quarter-finals:''' Best of eleven legs
*'''Semi-finals:''' Best of thirteen legs
*'''Final:''' Best of fifteen legs

===Prize money===
The total prize fund for the event was [[Pound sterling|£]]100,000, of which the winner received £30,000. The breakdown of prize money is shown below.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Saudi Arabia Darts Masters 2026 |url=https://mastercaller.com/tournaments/saudi-arabia-darts-masters/2026 |access-date=7 January 2026 |website=Mastercaller}}</ref>

As a special prize for the event, the PDC offered a [[United States dollar|$]]100,000 (£74,700) bonus to any player who hit a [[nine-dart finish]] during the tournament. Additionally, the player would then be given the chance to hit one dart at the [[Bullseye (target)|bullseye]], known as the Riyadh Season Bullseye, to double their prize to $200,000 (£149,400).<ref>{{cite web |date=15 January 2026 |title=Saudi event offers £149K for nine-dart bullseye bonus |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/darts/articles/c3ve3zx32xeo |website=BBC Sport |access-date=15 January 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=15 January 2026 |title=Riyadh Season Bullseye introduced ahead of Saudi Arabia Darts Masters |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/january/15/riyadh-season-bullseye-introduced-ahead-saudi-arabia-darts-masters |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=15 January 2026}}</ref> No nine-dart finishes were hit.

===Broadcasts===
The tournament was broadcast on [[ITV4]] and [[ITVX]] in the United Kingdom. It was also available for subscribers outside of Germany, Austria and Switzerland on the PDC's streaming service, PDCTV. Other broadcasters included [[Viaplay]] in the Netherlands, Iceland and [[Scandinavia]]; [[DAZN]] in Germany, Austria and Switzerland; [[Fox Sports (Australia)|Fox Sports]] in Australia; [[Sky Sport (New Zealand)|Sky Sport]] in New Zealand; [[VTM (TV channel)|VTM]] in Belgium; [[Nova Sport (Czech Republic and Slovakia)|Nova]] in Czechia and Slovakia; Pragosport in Hungary; [[FanDuel TV|FanDuel TV Extra]] in the United States; [[beIN Sports (Middle East TV channel)|BeIN Sports]] in the [[Middle East and North Africa]] and [[Arena Sport]] in Serbia, Bosnia & Herzegovina, Montenegro, North Macedonia & Kosovo; and a number of broadcasters in Saudi Arabia.<ref>{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=19 January 2026 |title=Where to watch the 2026 Riyadh Season Saudi Arabia Darts Masters |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/january/19/where-watch-2026-riyadh-season-saudi-arabia-darts-masters |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=19 January 2026}}</ref>

==Participants==
The 16-player lineup was announced on 7 January 2026. The eight PDC representatives were joined by the top eight players from the [[2025 in PDC#Asian Tour|2025 PDC Asian Tour]] rankings. No players from Saudi Arabia took part in the event.<ref>{{cite web |title=Luke Littler, Gian van Veen and Luke Humphries headline 2026 Bahrain and Saudi Arabia Darts Masters |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12040/13491526/luke-littler-gian-van-veen-and-luke-humphries-headline-2026-bahrain-and-saudi-arabia-darts-masters |website=Sky Sports |access-date=7 January 2026 |date=7 January 2026}}</ref> [[Paolo Nebrida]] was originally invited as an Asian representative but withdrew from the event due to illness, being replaced by [[Nitin Kumar]].<ref>{{cite web |last=Phillips |first=Josh |date=17 January 2026 |title=Kumar to replace Nebrida in Riyadh Season Saudia Arabia Darts Masters |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/january/17/kumar-replace-nebrida-riyadh-season-saudia-arabia-darts-masters |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=17 January 2026}}</ref>

==Summary==
===First round===
[[File:Man Lok Leung.png|thumb|[[Man Lok Leung]] became the third Asian player to win a match in [[World Series of Darts|World Series]] history.]]
The first round took place on 19 January, where seven of the eight PDC representatives won their opening match.<ref name="PDCDay1">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=19 January 2026 |title=Price takes centre stage in Riyadh, as Leung stuns Noppert |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/january/19/price-takes-centre-stage-riyadh-leung-stuns-noppert |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=20 January 2026}}</ref> The sole PDC representative to lose in the first round was [[Danny Noppert]], who was beaten 6–3 by [[Man Lok Leung]] in the tournament opener. Leung became the third Asian player to win a match in [[World Series of Darts|World Series]] history.<ref name="SkyDay1">{{cite web |last=Wellens |first=Megan |date=19 January 2026 |title=Saudi Arabia Darts Masters: Luke Littler beats Paul Lim to set up Gian van Veen showdown as Danny Noppert beaten |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12040/13496159/saudi-arabia-darts-masters-luke-littler-beats-paul-lim-to-set-up-gian-van-veen-showdown-as-danny-noppert-beaten |website=Sky Sports |access-date=20 January 2026}}</ref> "It is my first win in the first match [in Saudi Arabia], so this is a big win for me," said Leung afterwards.<ref name="PDCDay1"/> [[Luke Littler]] registered another 6–1 win against [[Paul Lim]], having already defeated him at the [[2026 Bahrain Darts Masters|Bahrain Darts Masters]] the week prior. Despite not hitting a [[Glossary of darts#Maximum|maximum]] and posting a [[three-dart average]] of 89.33, Littler only allowed Lim to win the fifth [[Leg (darts)|leg]] of the match.<ref name="RTE">{{cite web |date=19 January 2026 |title=Luke Littler to face Gian van Veen in first rematch since World Darts Championship final |url=https://www.rte.ie/sport/darts/2026/0119/1553990-littler-to-face-van-veen-in-first-clash-since-pdc-final/ |website=RTÉ Sport |access-date=20 January 2026 |agency=Press Association}}</ref> "Obviously it is a very weird atmosphere, it is all new to us players," Littler remarked about the event. "It is a very different environment but as long as you play well you are going to win 90 per cent of the time."<ref name="SkyDay1"/> [[Gian van Veen]] defeated Motomu Sakai 6–2 to set up a tie with Littler in the next round.<ref name="RTE"/> [[Michael van Gerwen]], the champion in Bahrain, won his opening match 6–1 against [[Nitin Kumar]].<ref name="SkyDay1"/>

[[Gerwyn Price]] produced a [[Whitewash (sport)|whitewash]] win against Alexis Toylo, hitting a [[Glossary of darts#Maximum check|170 checkout]] en route. Price highlighted the finish as a turning point in the match after a "slow start", adding: "I want to hit the ground running at the beginning of the season and win here for the first time, and I'm definitely after that [[Nine-dart finish|nine-darter]] as well."<ref name="PDCDay1"/> Facing each other in a third consecutive televised event,<ref>{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=18 January 2026 |title=2026 Riyadh Season Saudi Arabia Darts Masters preview |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/january/18/2026-riyadh-season-saudi-arabia-darts-masters-preview |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=20 January 2026}}</ref> [[Nathan Aspinall]] defeated [[Lourence Ilagan]] 6–1. "There is definitely a long way to go. It's certainly different. I'm not stupid. I feel like they weren't all there of their own accord, shall we say, and it was quite a small venue," Aspinall said about the PDC's first staging in Saudi Arabia.<ref name="AspTele">{{cite web |last=Rumsby |first=Ben |date=19 January 2026 |title=Saudi darts crowd 'not here of their own accord', says Nathan Aspinall |url=https://www.telegraph.co.uk/darts/2026/01/19/nathan-aspinall-saudi-arabia-darts-masters-pdc-crowd/ |website=The Telegraph |access-date=20 January 2026}}</ref> In a statement about Aspinall's comments, the PDC said that he was "reflecting on the fact that this was a first-of-its-kind darts event in the region".<ref>{{cite web |last=McKenna |first=Lorraine |date=20 January 2026 |title=Aspinall questions crowd at new Saudi tournament |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/darts/articles/c20dg6n2093o |website=BBC Sport |access-date=20 January 2026}}</ref> [[Luke Humphries]] won four consecutive legs to beat Ryusei Azemoto 6–2, while [[Stephen Bunting]] defeated Tomoya Goto 6–1. Humphries called his opening match a "decent start" and added: "Everybody wants to be the first player to win this title. It's an exciting tournament and hopefully I can be the first player to put my name on it."<ref name="PDCDay1"/>

===Quarter-finals, semi-finals and final===
[[File:2025-04-03 Premier League Darts Berlin 2025 by Sandro Halank–125.jpg|thumb|[[Luke Littler]] won the inaugural Saudi Arabia Darts Masters, his fifth [[World Series of Darts|World Series]] title.]]
The quarter-finals, semi-finals and final took place on 20 January.<ref name="PDCDay2">{{cite web |last1=Gorton |first1=Josh |date=20 January 2026 |title=Terrific Littler wins inaugural Riyadh Season Saudi Arabia Darts Masters |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/january/20/terrific-littler-wins-inaugural-riyadh-season-saudi-arabia-darts-masters |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=20 January 2026}}</ref> Luke Littler and Gian van Veen faced off in the quarter-finals, their first rematch since the [[2026 PDC World Darts Championship|2026 World Championship]] final.<ref name="RTE"/> Van Veen established a 3–0 lead before Littler won the next three legs to level at 3–3. At 4–4, Littler [[Glossary of darts#Hold|held]] throw to take the lead and won the match 6–4 with a 124 [[Glossary of darts#Checkout|checkout]] on the [[Bullseye (target)|bullseye]], ending the match with a three-dart average of 111.58.<ref name="SkyDay2">{{cite web |last=Wellens |first=Megan |date=20 January 2026 |title=Saudi Arabia Darts Masters: Luke Littler crowned inaugural champion with victory over Michael van Gerwen in Riyadh |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13496675/saudia-arabia-darts-masters-luke-littler-crowned-inaugural-champion-with-victory-over-michael-van-gerwen-in-riyadh |website=Sky Sports |access-date=20 January 2026}}</ref> Luke Humphries went 5–4 ahead against Gerwyn Price but Price claimed victory in a [[Glossary of darts#Decider|deciding leg]]. Nathan Aspinall won 6–4 to eliminate Man Lok Leung, while Michael van Gerwen defeated Stephen Bunting by the same scoreline in a match that featured nine consecutive [[Glossary of darts#Break|breaks]] of throw.<ref name="PDCDay2"/> In the semi-finals, Littler and Van Gerwen earned 7–5 wins over Price and Aspinall respectively, setting up a match between the pair in the inaugural Saudi Arabia Darts Masters final. Van Gerwen was seeking a record-extending 18th World Series title, while Littler was aiming to win his first title since becoming a two-time world champion.<ref name="SkyDay2"/><ref name="Ind">{{cite web |last=Blanche |first=Phil |date=20 January 2026 |title=Luke Littler kickstarts birthday celebrations with Saudi Arabia Masters success |url=https://www.independent.ie/sport/other-sports/darts/luke-littler-kickstarts-birthday-celebrations-with-saudi-arabia-masters-success/a654032320.html |website=Irish Independent |access-date=20 January 2026 |agency=Press Association}}</ref>

Littler won the first four legs of the final, converting a 132 finish on the bullseye in the second leg.<ref name="PDCDay2"/> Van Gerwen then claimed the next three legs, reducing his deficit to 4–3 and then 5–4.<ref name="Ind"/><ref name="BBC">{{cite web |date=20 January 2026 |title=Littler beats Van Gerwen to win Saudi Masters |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/darts/articles/cy8y4g6w0lgo |website=BBC Sport |access-date=20 January 2026}}</ref> A run of four consecutive breaks of throw was ended by Littler, who restored a two-leg advantage at 6–4. He went on to win the match 8–5, finishing the match with a 50 checkout.<ref name="SkyDay2"/> Littler averaged 104.84 in the final, hitting six maximums and recording a checkout success rate of 61.5 per cent.<ref name="BBC"/> He became the first winner of the Saudi Arabia Darts Masters a day before his 19th birthday,<ref name="Birthday">{{cite web |last=Wellens |first=Megan |date=20 January 2026 |title=Luke Littler: Teenage sensation 'hungry for more' on 19th birthday after Saudi Arabia Darts Masters win with World Masters looming |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12040/13496798/luke-littler-teenage-sensation-hungry-for-more-on-19th-birthday-after-saudi-arabia-darts-masters-win-with-world-masters-looming |website=Sky Sports |access-date=20 January 2026}}</ref> bringing him to a total of five World Series titles.<ref name="PDCDay2"/> "It didn't work out last week in Bahrain, but I'm really glad to come over to Saudi Arabia for the first time and be the first winner," said Littler, who revealed that he would be celebrating his birthday in [[Dubai]].<ref name="Ind"/> Previewing the upcoming [[2026 PDC World Masters|World Masters]] tournament, he commented: "I think after the holiday that we are going on, when I am back, I will definitely be practising because it's a title that I've not picked up yet and I definitely want to go to [[Milton Keynes]] and I want to become champion."<ref name="Birthday"/> Speaking in defeat, Van Gerwen said that going 4–0 down "didn't really help" him, adding: "Fair play to him [Littler]. He had a fantastic tournament. Sometimes you have to take it on the chin and move on."<ref name="BBC"/>
