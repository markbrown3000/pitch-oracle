The '''2026 Grand Slam of Darts''' (known for sponsorship reasons as the '''2026 Mr Vegas Grand Slam of Darts''') is an upcoming professional [[darts]] tournament that is scheduled to take place from 14 to 22 November 2026 at the WV Active Aldersley in [[Wolverhampton]], England. It will be the 20th staging of the [[Grand Slam of Darts]] by the [[Professional Darts Corporation]] (PDC). The winner will receive £200,000 from a total prize fund of £1,000,000.

The tournament will feature 48 players, expanded from 32. Finalists from televised tournaments, [[2026 PDC Pro Tour|PDC Pro Tour]] title winners, and additional qualifiers will comprise the first 40 places, being joined by eight [[List of players with a 2026 PDC Tour Card|Tour Card holder]] qualifiers.

[[Luke Littler]] will be the defending champion, having defeated [[Luke Humphries]] 16–11 in the [[2025 Grand Slam of Darts|2025 final]].

== Overview ==
=== Background ===
The 2026 [[Grand Slam of Darts]] will be the 20th edition of the tournament to be staged by the [[Professional Darts Corporation]] (PDC) since the [[2007 Grand Slam of Darts|inaugural edition]] in 2007.<ref>{{cite web |title=Grand Slam of Darts |url=https://mastercaller.com/tournaments/grand-slam-of-darts |website=Mastercaller |access-date=12 February 2026}}</ref> First held at the [[Wolverhampton Civic Hall]], the tournament originally involved top [[darts]] players from both the PDC and its rival, the [[British Darts Organisation]] (BDO). PDC chairman [[Barry Hearn]] promoted it as an event "to discover the greatest of them all".<ref>{{cite web |date=21 February 2007 |title=Darts crosses the great divide |url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2007/feb/21/darts |website=The Guardian |access-date=12 February 2026}}</ref> The tournament was mostly dominated by PDC representatives with the exception of the [[2010 Grand Slam of Darts|2010 event]], where [[Scott Waites]] became the first and only BDO player to win the title by overturning an 8–0 deficit to defeat [[James Wade]] 16–12 in the final.<ref>{{cite web |date=22 November 2010 |title=Scott Waites stuns James Wade in Grand Slam final |url=http://news.bbc.co.uk/sport2/hi/other_sports/darts/9215124.stm |website=BBC Sport |access-date=12 February 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |last=Barry |first=Brian |date=17 November 2018 |title=Scott Waites says the Grand Slam of Darts was his most 'prestigious' title |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/11553117/scott-waites-says-the-grand-slam-of-darts-was-his-most-prestigious-title |website=Sky Sports |access-date=12 February 2026}}</ref> Following the dissolution of the BDO in 2020, the [[2021 Grand Slam of Darts|2021 event]] was the first edition to not feature representatives from the organisation, being replaced by additional qualifiers from the PDC's feeder systems.<ref>{{cite web |last=Prenderville |first=Paul |date=20 August 2021 |title=Grand Slam of Darts 2021: PDC Challenge and Development Tours will make up field in absence of BDO |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/12384431/grand-slam-of-darts-2021-pdc-challenge-and-development-tours-will-make-up-field-in-absence-of-bdo |website=Sky Sports |access-date=12 February 2026}}</ref>

The 2026 edition will be held from 14 to 22 November 2026 at the WV Active Aldersley in [[Wolverhampton]], England. Online casino Mr Vegas, owned by Malta-based gambling company [[Videoslots]], continued its sponsorship of the event.<ref>{{cite web |title=Grand Slam of Darts 2026 |url=https://mastercaller.com/tournaments/grand-slam-of-darts/2026 |website=Mastercaller |access-date=12 February 2026}}</ref> It will be the first edition of the tournament to feature a 48-player field, having previously consisted of 32 players.<ref>{{cite web |last=Phillips |first=Josh |date=31 March 2025 |title=Biggest prize money increase in PDC history confirmed |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/03/31/biggest-prize-money-increase-pdc-history-confirmed |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=15 September 2025}}</ref> [[Luke Littler]] will enter the tournament as defending champion, having defeated [[Luke Humphries]] 16–11 in the [[2025 Grand Slam of Darts|2025 final]] to win his second Grand Slam title; the 2025 event also saw Littler overtake Humphries to become the new world number one.<ref>{{cite web |last=Drury |first=Sam |date=16 November 2025 |title=Littler wins Grand Slam and secures world number one ranking |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/darts/articles/crmxy3k3e2eo |website=BBC Sport |access-date=16 November 2025}}</ref>

=== Format ===
All matches will be in [[Leg (darts)|leg]] play format. The 48 players will be drawn into 16 groups of 3, with each group containing one [[Seeding (sports)|seeded]] player. The groups will be contested on a [[Round-robin tournament|round-robin]] basis where every player faces each other once. Players will two points for a win and the top player in each group will qualify for the knockout stage.

=== Prize money ===
The total prize fund for the event increased from £650,000 in 2025 to £1,000,000 in 2026.<ref>{{cite web |title=Grand Slam of Darts 2025 |url=https://mastercaller.com/tournaments/grand-slam-of-darts/2025 |website=Mastercaller |access-date=12 February 2026}}</ref> The winner will receive £200,000 and the [[Eric Bristow]] Trophy, renamed in 2018 in honour of the five-time world champion who died in April the same year.<ref>{{cite web |date=2 May 2018 |title=The late Eric Bristow honoured with renaming of the Grand Slam of Darts trophy |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/11356164/the-late-eric-bristow-honoured-with-renaming-of-the-grand-slam-of-darts-trophy |website=Sky Sports |access-date=12 February 2026}}</ref> The prize money breakdown is:

== Qualification ==
Coinciding with the expansion to 48 players, the qualification criteria for the tournament were announced on 11 February 2026. The first 40 participants will be decided by the following criteria:

* Finalists from [[2026 in PDC#Televised events|PDC televised events]] over the previous twelve months (up to a maximum of 24 players)
* Additional qualifiers (up to a maximum of 12 players)
* [[2026 PDC Pro Tour#European Tour|European Tour]] events winners (to fill up to 40 players)
* [[2026 PDC Players Championship series|Players Championship]] event winners (to fill up to 40 players)

The list of qualifiers will be invited in the following order:<ref>{{cite web |last=Simpson |first=Will |date=11 February 2026 |title=2026 Mr Vegas Grand Slam of Darts qualification criteria confirmed |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/february/11/2026-mr-vegas-grand-slam-of-darts-qualification-criteria-confirmed/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=12 February 2026}}</ref>

==Pools==
The top sixteen qualified players, based on their [[PDC Order of Merit]] ranking, will be [[Seeding (sport)|seeded]] into the group stage, with the other 32 participants being split into pools based on their ranking. Each group will consist of one player from each pool.
