The '''2026 German Masters''' (officially the '''2026 Machineseeker German Masters''')<ref>{{cite web |date=3 September 2025 |title=Machineseeker renews partnership with snooker's German Masters |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/september/03/machineseeker-renews-partnership-with-snooker-s-german-masters--/ |access-date=6 September 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418183946/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/september/03/machineseeker-renews-partnership-with-snooker-s-german-masters--/|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref> was a professional [[snooker]] tournament that took place from 26 January to 1 February 2026 at the [[Tempodrom]] in [[Berlin]], Germany. Qualifying took place from 5 to 8 January at the [[Ponds Forge|Ponds Forge International Sports Centre]] in [[Sheffield]], England. The 16th consecutive edition of the [[German Masters]] since it was revived in [[2011 German Masters|2011]], it was the 12th [[List of snooker ranking tournaments|ranking event]] of the [[2025–26 snooker season]], following the [[2025 Scottish Open (snooker)|2025 Scottish Open]] and preceding the [[2026 World Grand Prix (snooker)|2026 World Grand Prix]]. The tournament was broadcast by [[TNT Sports]] in the United Kingdom and Ireland, by [[Eurosport]] in mainland Europe, by local channels in China and elsewhere in Asia, and by [[WST Play]] in all other territories. The winner received £100,000 from a total prize fund of £550,400.

[[Kyren Wilson]] was the defending champion, having defeated [[Barry Hawkins]] 10–9 in the [[2025 German Masters|2025 final]], but he lost 1–5 to [[Shaun Murphy]] in the quarter-finals. The world number one [[Judd Trump]] defeated Murphy 10–4 in the final to win a record-extending fourth German Masters title and the 31st ranking title of his career. It was Trump's first professional title since winning the [[2024 UK Championship]] 14 months earlier. The tournament produced a total of 109 [[Century break|century breaks]], 58 during the qualifying matches played in Sheffield and 51 at the main stage in Berlin. The highest was a [[maximum break]] by [[Zhang Anda]], the fifth of his career, during his last-32 match against Hawkins. It was the record-extending 22nd maximum of the season and the fourth in the history of the tournament.

==Overview==
The tournament originated as the German Open, a ranking event that was held from 1995 to 1997. The inaugural German Open champion was [[John Higgins]], who defeated [[Ken Doherty]] 9–3 in the [[1995 German Open (snooker)|1995 final]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=German Open (1995)|url=https://www.snooker.org/res/index.asp?event=1674 |access-date=30 December 2025 |website=snooker.org |language=en |archive-date=4 January 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240104234528/https://www.snooker.org/res/index.asp?event=1674 |url-status=live }}</ref> After being staged once in [[1998 German Masters|1998]] as the non-ranking German Masters, the tournament dropped off the calendar; it was restored in 2011 as the ranking [[German Masters]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Poole |first=Joseph |date=27 January 2025 |title=German Masters Snooker: The best moments, tournament history, list of winners |url=https://www.totallysnookered.com/sport/snooker/tournaments/german-masters/german-masters-snooker-the-best-moments-tournament-history-list-of-winners-4962511 |access-date=30 December 2025 |website=Totally Snookered |language=en |archive-date=15 July 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250715173648/https://www.totallysnookered.com/sport/snooker/tournaments/german-masters/german-masters-snooker-the-best-moments-tournament-history-list-of-winners-4962511 |url-status=live }}</ref><ref>{{Cite news |last=Nunns |first=Hector |date=3 February 2014 |title=How Germany fell in love with snooker |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/26019104 |access-date=30 December 2025 |work=[[BBC Sport]] |language=en-GB |archive-date=15 April 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250415183050/https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/26019104 |url-status=live }}</ref> The first winner of the tournament after its revival was [[Mark Williams (snooker player)|Mark Williams]], who defeated [[Mark Selby]] 9–7 in the [[2011 German Masters|2011 final]].<ref>{{Cite news |date=6 February 2011 |title=Mark Williams edges past Selby to win German Masters |url=http://news.bbc.co.uk/sport2/hi/other_sports/snooker/9389058.stm |access-date=2 January 2026 |work=[[BBC Sport]] |language=en-GB |archive-date=7 February 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110207051506/http://news.bbc.co.uk/sport2/hi/other_sports/snooker/9389058.stm |url-status=live }}</ref> In 2021, the tournament trophy was named the Brandon Parker Trophy in memory of the late manager, promoter, and [[World Snooker Tour]] director who first brought the tournament to Berlin's [[Tempodrom]] in 2011. === Format ===
[[File:Berlin - Tempodrom.jpg|thumb|The tournament's main stage took place at the [[Tempodrom]] ''(pictured in 2016)'' in [[Berlin]].]]
A total of 128 players entered the tournament. Qualifying took place over three rounds, with higher ranked players given [[bye (sport)|byes]] to later rounds. In qualifying round one, players [[Seeding (sports)|seeded]] 65–96 faced those seeded 97–128. In qualifying round two, the 32 first-round winners played those seeded 33–64. In qualifying round three, the 32 second-round winners played the top 32 seeds. The third-round qualifying matches featuring players seeded 17–32 were played in Sheffield. The third-round qualifying matches featuring the top 16 seeds were held over and played at the main venue in Berlin.<ref>{{cite web |date=5 April 2024 |title=Tiered format for Home Nations and German Masters in 2024/25 |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2024/april/05/tiered-format-for-home-nations-and-german-masters-in-2024-25/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240405185554/https://www.wst.tv/news/2024/april/05/tiered-format-for-home-nations-and-german-masters-in-2024-25/ |archive-date=5 April 2024 |access-date=5 April 2024 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]]}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |date=16 December 2025 |title=2026 Machineseeker German Masters draw |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/december/16/2026-Machineseeker-German-Masters-Draw/ |access-date=30 December 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |archive-date=6 January 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260106174019/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/december/16/2026-Machineseeker-German-Masters-Draw/ |url-status=live }}</ref>

All matches up to and including the quarter{{nbh}}finals were played as the best of nine {{cuegloss|frame|frames}}. The semi{{nbh}}finals were the best of 11, and the final was a best{{nbh}}of{{nbh}}19 frame match played over two {{cuegloss|session|sessions}}.<ref name="s.o_M" />

It was the record-extending 22nd maximum of the season, the 239th in professional snooker history, and the fourth in the history of the German Masters.<ref name="Max 239">{{cite web |title=Zhang makes Berlin maximum |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/january/28/zhang-makes-berlin-maximum/ |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |date=28 January 2026 |access-date=28 January 2026 |archive-date=28 January 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260128111234/https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/january/28/zhang-makes-berlin-maximum/ |url-status=live}}</ref> Zhang went on to win the match 5–2. "Making a maximum at the German Masters is really special, and doing it in the opening frame makes it even better," Zhang said afterwards. "To be honest, I wasn't really thinking about a maximum in the early part of the break. It only came into my mind later on. I think the crowd already realized what I was trying to do. The atmosphere was fantastic. I really enjoyed that moment." Neil Robertson made three centuries of 108, 100, and 106 as he defeated [[Pang Junxu]], also by a 5–2 scoreline.<ref name="WST_day3">{{Cite web|title=Machineseeker German Masters day three|url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/january/28/machineseeker-ger/|website=[[World Snooker Tour]]|access-date=28 January 2026|language=en|date=28 January 2026|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260420070819/https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/january/28/machineseeker-ger/|archive-date=20 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref> [[Jimmy Robertson (snooker player)|Jimmy Robertson]] eliminated Williams 5–3.<ref>{{cite web |date=28 January 2026 |title=2026 German Masters: Mark Williams v Jimmy Robertson |url=https://www.wst.tv/match-centre/5b1f3c24-e6c9-4d87-80d5-484b006824d8 |access-date=28 January 2026 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260420070738/https://www.wst.tv/match-centre/5b1f3c24-e6c9-4d87-80d5-484b006824d8|archive-date=20 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

Carter made a century of 128 in the opening frame against O'Sullivan and went on to take a 3–0 lead. O'Sullivan tied the scores at 3–3, but Carter won the last two frames for a 5–3 victory.<ref>{{Cite web |date=28 January 2026 |title=O'Sullivan beaten by Carter at German Masters|url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cy4qepv44pgo |access-date=20 April 2026|website=[[BBC Sport]] |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260420071021/https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cy4qepv44pgo|archive-date=20 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref> It was only the second time Carter had beaten O'Sullivan in their 22 professional meetings. Speaking afterwards about his longstanding rivalry with O'Sullivan, Carter said: "We are fine now. There is no animosity at all. It is all heat of the moment stuff between two sportsmen and he is the best snooker player of all time. It is great to be on the table with him and competing. Getting the odd win over him is a feather in my cap." Trump produced three century breaks of 102, 102, and 127 as he beat [[Ryan Day (snooker player)|Ryan Day]] 5–0, his second consecutive whitewash win in the tournament. Day made a highest break of just 14 in the match. Higgins defeated [[Zhou Yuelong]] 5–3, Bingham beat Donaldson 5–2, and [[Xiao Guodong]] advanced with a 5–1 win over Lisowski. ==== Last 16 ====
[[File:Mark Allen 2026 (7).jpg|thumb|[[Mark Allen (snooker player)|Mark Allen]] ''(pictured at the event)'' was [[whitewash (sport)|whitewashed]] in the last 16 by [[Shaun Murphy]].]]
Murphy produced breaks including 125, 74, 75, and 62 as he whitewashed Allen, saying afterwards that: "5–0 wins over Mark Allen don't come along every day. It was a really good match, certainly from my point of view. I thought the conditions caught us both out. It was the quickest table I've ever played on in my life. Controlling the {{cuegloss|cue ball}} isn't always easy. Mark missed a few shots and that gave me the chance to get used to the speed quicker." Murphy also commented on his recent first-round defeat to [[Wu Yize]] at the 2026 Masters, where he had been the defending champion. "I was very disappointed after the Masters, not just because of the loss but how I played," he stated. "It was totally inexplicable. My preparation was second to none and I worked very hard on my game. I've come here this week feeling the same as I did for [the Masters] but thankfully I've strung a few wins together."<ref name="WST_day4">{{Cite web|title=Machineseeker German Masters day four|url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/january/29/machineseeker-german-masters-day-four/|website=[[World Snooker Tour]]|access-date=29 January 2026|language=en|date=29 January 2026|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260420071034/https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/january/29/machineseeker-german-masters-day-four/|archive-date=20 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

The defending champion Wilson lost the first two frames against Zhang but then won five in a row for a 5–2 victory. Wilson, who needed to improve his standing on the one-year [[Snooker world rankings|ranking list]] to defend his title at the [[2026 Players Championship (snooker)|2026 Players Championship]], commented: "I'm definitely chasing the points. That is for sure. There is a little bit of pressure on me this week. I'm coping well so far." Neil Robertson and Carter advanced with whitewash victories over Chadha and Xu respectively. Jimmy Robertson defeated Jak Jones, Bingham beat Ford, and Trump defeated Si, all by 5–2 scorelines. Xiao and Higgins played a lengthy evening match that finished after midnight, with Xiao eventually securing a 5–3 win.<ref name="WST_day4" />

==== Quarter-finals ====
[[File:Kyren Wilson 2026 (4).jpg|thumb|The defending champion [[Kyren Wilson]] ''(pictured at the event)'' lost in the quarter-finals to [[Shaun Murphy]].]]
Facing the defending champion Wilson, Murphy made breaks of 78, 101, 68, and 58 as he won the first four frames. Wilson produced a {{Cuegloss|total clearance}} of 139 to take frame five, but Murphy won frame six on the {{Cuegloss|colours}} to secure a 5–1 victory. "These results have been totally unexpected," Murphy said afterwards. "Whether I win 5–4 on the {{cuegloss|black ball|black}} or 5–0, it doesn't matter to me. I'm just happy to still be in this great tournament. It is one of the few events which I haven't won during my career. It would mean a lot to go on and lift the trophy on Sunday night. I've come here on a bit of a mission." Carter, who had won the tournament in [[2013 German Masters|2013]] and [[2023 German Masters|2023]] and been runner-up in [[2017 German Masters|2017]], made a highest break of 137 as he also secured a 5–1 victory over Bingham.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Murphy and Carter ease to Berlin semis|url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/january/30/murphy-and-carter-ease-to-berlin-semis/|website=[[World Snooker Tour]]|access-date=30 January 2026|language=en|date=30 January 2026|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260420071037/https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/january/30/murphy-and-carter-ease-to-berlin-semis/|archive-date=20 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

Trump made breaks of 65 and 107 as he won the first two frames against Xiao, who responded with breaks of 104 and 62 to tie the scores at 2–2 at the mid-session interval. Xiao won frame five, but Trump then won three consecutive frames with breaks of 105, 53, and 69 to secure a 5–3 victory. "It was a tough game tonight and I didn't feel I did a lot wrong," Trump said afterwards. "We both played to a good standard. I feel a bit tired now as it took a lot of concentration out there to get the job done." In the last quarter-final, Neil Robertson defeated Jimmy Robertson 5–1. Looking ahead to his semi-final match against Murphy, Robertson said: "All of the top players have their own kind of aura around the table. Each one is just as imposing as the other. I like to see that. It spurs me on to play better. When you see the top players strutting round the table, it inspires you to play really well too. We are both very attacking players and great {{cuegloss|long pot|long potters}}. The key is maybe who can pot great balls to get in or the {{cuegloss|safety}}."<ref>{{Cite web|title=Trump and Robertson complete last four lineup|url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/january/30/trump-and-robertson-complete-last-four-line-up/|website=[[World Snooker Tour]]|date=30 January 2026|access-date=31 January 2026|language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260420071041/https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/january/30/trump-and-robertson-complete-last-four-line-up/|archive-date=20 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|title=Trump to face Carter in German Masters semi-final|url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/c62nv8x13gxo|website=[[BBC Sport]]|date=30 January 2026|access-date=18 April 2026|language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418183606/https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/c62nv8x13gxo|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

==== Semi-finals ====
[[File:Shaun Murphy 2026 (10).jpg|thumb|[[Shaun Murphy]] ''(pictured at the event )'' defeated [[Neil Robertson]] to reach his second [[German Masters]] final.]]
Facing Neil Robertson in the first semi-final, Murphy made three centuries in the first four frames—a 122 in the opener, followed by back-to-back centuries of 102 and 106 in frames three and four—as he took a 3–1 lead at the mid-session interval. Murphy won a further three frames after the interval, making breaks including 51 and 56 as he completed a 6–1 victory. He commented afterwards: "I think whenever you go up against one of the game's legends, which for me Neil [Robertson] is one of, it makes things very straightforward. You know you need to play your best. For a lot of that match I was close to my best. I had a bit of luck at the right moments and that always helps. I'm delighted to have won."<ref name="WST_Murphy_infinal">{{Cite web|title=Murphy into second Tempodrom final|url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/january/31/murphy-into-second-tempodrom-final/|website=[[World Snooker Tour]]|access-date=31 January 2026|language=en|date=31 January 2026|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260420071045/https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/january/31/murphy-into-second-tempodrom-final/|archive-date=20 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|title='Emotional' Murphy in German Masters trophy chase|url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cz7yddgqn20o|website=[[BBC Sport]]|date=31 January 2026|access-date=18 April 2026|language=en-GB|last=Armstrong|first=Simon|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418183537/https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cz7yddgqn20o|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

Playing Carter in the second semi-final, Trump made breaks including 52, 67, and 87 as he won all four frames before the mid-session interval. Carter won frames five and six, but Trump took frames seven and eight to secure a 6–2 victory. "[Carter] missed a couple early on and I think in the end missed one too many and I was able to punish him and put him under pressure," said Trump afterwards. "He always puts up a fight and tries to the very end. He made it difficult at 4–2, but I managed to nick the last two." Trump added: "It is an incredible arena so whenever you play in a semi-final or a final here it is a real privilege to be out there. It is always nice to perform well in that environment."<ref name="WST_Trump_infinal">{{Cite web|title=Trump sets up Murphy final in Berlin|url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/january/31/trump-sets-up-murphy-final/|website=[[World Snooker Tour]]|date=31 January 2026|access-date=1 February 2026|language=en|archive-date=7 February 2026|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260207080910/https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/january/31/trump-sets-up-murphy-final/|url-status=live}}</ref>

==== Final ====
[[File:Judd Trump 2026 (4).jpg|thumb|[[Judd Trump]] ''(pictured at the event)'' defeated [[Shaun Murphy]] 10–4 in the final to win his fourth [[German Masters]] title and 31st [[List of snooker ranking tournaments|ranking title]].]]
The final took place on 1 February as the best of 19 frames, played over two {{cuegloss|sessions}}, between the third seed Trump and the eighth seed Murphy. Trump contested his fifth German Masters final, having previously won the title in [[2020 German Masters|2020]], [[2021 German Masters|2021]], and [[2024 German Masters|2024]] and been runner-up in [[2014 German Masters|2014]].<ref name="WST_Trump_infinal" /> Murphy reached the final for a second time, having been runner-up in [[2015 German Masters|2015]].<ref name="WST_Murphy_infinal" /> Trump had won eight of the previous nine professional meetings between the two players.<ref name="WST_Trump_infinal" />

Trump won the first two frames with breaks of 101 and 71, but Murphy responded with breaks of 103 and 58 to tie the scores at 2–2 at the mid-session interval. Trump then made breaks including 62 and 64 as he won three consecutive frames to move 5–2 ahead. Murphy produced a century of 117 to win frame eight, leaving Trump with a 5–3 advantage after the first session.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Trump leads Murphy in Berlin|url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/february/01/trump-leads-murphy-in-berlin-/|website=[[World Snooker Tour]]|access-date=1 February 2026|language=en|date=1 February 2026|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418185003/https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/february/01/trump-leads-murphy-in-berlin-/|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref> When play resumed for the evening session, Trump came from 60 points behind to win frame nine on the last black. He also won frame 10 on the last black, took frame 11 with a 68 break, and won frame 12 on the last {{cuegloss|pink}} to lead 9–3. Murphy recovered from 51 points behind to take frame 13 on the last black, but Trump won the next frame to complete a 10–4 victory.<ref>{{Cite web|title=2026 German Masters: Shaun Murphy v Judd Trump|url=https://www.wst.tv/match-centre/c98c987e-cfe2-476f-8b41-2a3276394ea1|website=[[World Snooker Tour]]|access-date=1 February 2026|language=en|date=1 February 2026|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418185000/https://www.wst.tv/match-centre/c98c987e-cfe2-476f-8b41-2a3276394ea1|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref> It was Trump's record-extending fourth German Masters title, the 31st [[List of snooker ranking tournaments|ranking title]] of his career, and his first professional title since winning the [[2024 UK Championship]] 14 months earlier.<ref name="WST_Trump_infinal" /><ref>{{Cite web|title=Trump ends trophy drought with German Masters win|url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cly17ddznzgo|website=[[BBC Sport]]|date=1 February 2026|access-date=18 April 2026|language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418183509/https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cly17ddznzgo|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref><ref name="WST_Trump_win">{{Cite web|title=Trump masters the Tempodrom again|url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/february/01/trump/|website=[[World Snooker Tour]]|access-date=1 February 2026|language=en|date=1 February 2026|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418184956/https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/february/01/trump/|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

"It is incredible to win such a special event," Trump said after the final. "Every time I come here, I'm very happy to have the chance to play in front of that crowd. There are such special memories walking out here for the final. Today was no different. I played a very solid game." Commenting on his 14-month wait for a title, he stated: "The time without winning tournaments wasn't difficult. It was just about being in the right mental state to win when the next chance came around. It was important not to get too down on myself and take the positives that I was putting myself in position to win." Murphy thanked the audience, calling them "unbelievably supportive." He added: "It has been an incredible week for me here in Berlin... I'll come back next year and try again."<ref name="WST_Trump_win" />
