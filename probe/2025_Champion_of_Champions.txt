The '''2025 Champion of Champions''' (officially the '''2025 Sportsbet.io''' '''Champion of Champions''') was a professional non-ranking [[snooker]] tournament that took place from 10 to 16 November 2025 at the [[Leicester Arena]] in [[Leicester]], England. It featured 16 participants, all of whom had won significant tournaments since the [[2024 Champion of Champions|previous year's event]]. Part of the [[2025–26 snooker season]], it was the 15th edition of the [[Champion of Champions (snooker)|Champion of Champions]] since the tournament was first held in [[1978 Champion of Champions|1978]] and the 13th consecutive edition since it was revived in [[2013 Champion of Champions|2013]]. It was broadcast domestically by [[ITV4]] and internationally by other broadcasters. The winner received £150,000 from a total prize fund of £440,000.

[[Mark Williams (snooker player)|Mark Williams]] was the defending champion, having defeated [[Xiao Guodong]] 10–6 in the 2024 final, but he lost 5–‍6 to [[Mark Selby]] in the quarter-finals. Selby defeated [[Judd Trump]] 10–5 in the final to win the tournament for the first time. Trump lost his fifth Champion of Champions final out of the six he had contested. The tournament produced 23 [[Century break|century breaks]], of which the highest was a 138 by Selby in the final.

== Overview ==
The [[Champion of Champions (snooker)|Champion of Champions]] is a professional non-ranking [[snooker]] tournament that was first held in [[1978 Champion of Champions|1978]], when it was contested by four players at the [[Wembley Conference Centre]] in London.<ref>{{cite news |date=2 November 1978 |title=Snooker – World champion in semi-final faces Fagan |newspaper=The Times |page=13}}</ref><ref name="s.o_1978">{{cite web |date=24 November 2023 |title=Daily Mirror Champion of Champions (1978) |url=https://www.snooker.org/res/index.asp?event=1653 |access-date=16 November 2024 |website=snooker.org |archive-date=25 December 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20241225170221/https://www.snooker.org/res/index.asp?event=1653 |url-status=live }}</ref> The inaugural winner was [[Ray Reardon]], who defeated [[Alex Higgins]] 11–9 in the final.<ref name="s.o_1978" /> After its second staging in [[1980 Champion of Champions|1980]], the tournament was not held again until [[2013 Champion of Champions|2013]], since which time it has been staged annually.<ref>{{Cite web |title=History |url=https://championofchampionssnooker.co.uk/history/ |access-date=2025-11-08 |website=Champion of Champions Snooker |language=en-GB |archive-date=8 November 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20251108010614/https://championofchampionssnooker.co.uk/history/ |url-status=live }}</ref> As of 2025, the most successful player in its history was [[Ronnie O'Sullivan]], who had won the title four times and been runner-up twice.<ref name="TS_info">{{Cite web |last=Day |first=Michael |date=12 April 2024 |title=Champion of Champions Snooker: Information and a Potted History |url=https://www.totallysnookered.com/sport/snooker/tournaments/champion-of-champions/champion-of-champions-snooker-information-and-a-potted-history-4585417 |access-date=8 November 2025 |website=Totally Snookered |language=en}}</ref>

The 2025 edition of the tournament—the 15th staging overall and the 13th consecutively since its revival in 2013—took place from 10&nbsp;to 16&nbsp;November 2025 at the [[Leicester Arena]] in [[Leicester]], England.<ref>{{cite web |title=Tournaments 2025–26 |url=https://www.wst.tv/tournaments |access-date=14 July 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en |archive-date=5 December 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20251205154034/https://www.wst.tv/tournaments/ |url-status=live }}</ref><ref name="s.o_main">{{cite web |title=Sportsbet.io Champion of Champions 2025 |url=https://www.snooker.org/res/index.asp?event=2287 |access-date=14 July 2025 |website=snooker.org |language=en |archive-date=13 August 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250813062126/https://www.snooker.org/res/index.asp?event=2287 |url-status=live }}</ref> It featured 16&nbsp;participants, 14&nbsp;winners of events from the previous 12&nbsp;months on the [[World Snooker Tour]] in addition to the reigning [[2025 World Women's Snooker Championship|World Women's Snooker Champion]] and [[2025 World Seniors Championship|World Seniors Champion]]. Organised by [[Matchroom Sport]], the tournament was sponsored by sports betting company Sportsbet.io.<ref>{{cite web |date=14 January 2025 |title=Sportsbet.io becomes new title partner of snooker's Players Championship, Tour Championship and Champion of Champions |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/january/14/sportsbet-io-becomes-new-title-partner-of-snooker-s-players-championship--tour-championship-and-champion-of-champions/ |access-date=25 July 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en |archive-date=26 July 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250726002616/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/january/14/sportsbet-io-becomes-new-title-partner-of-snooker-s-players-championship--tour-championship-and-champion-of-champions/ |url-status=live }}</ref> [[Mark Williams (snooker player)|Mark Williams]] was the defending champion, having defeated [[Xiao Guodong]] 10–6 in the [[2024 Champion of Champions|2024 final]].<ref>{{cite web |date=17 November 2024 |title=Williams wins first Champion of Champions title |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cz9x0pd4724o |access-date=17 April 2026 |website=[[BBC Sport]] |language=en-GB |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260417072229/https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cz9x0pd4724o|archive-date=17 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

=== Format ===
The 16&nbsp;qualifiers were split into four groups of four players. Each group competed on a different day, with the group finals (event quarter-finals) played on the same day as the corresponding first-round matches. The opening group matches were played as the best of 7 {{cuegloss|frames}}, while the group finals and semi-finals were the best of 11 frames. The final was the best of 19 frames, played over two {{Cuegloss|session|sessions}}.<ref name="TS_info" />

== Summary ==

=== Group stages ===

==== Group 2 ====
[[File:Shaun Murphy PHC 2015-3.jpg|thumb|[[Shaun Murphy]] ''(pictured in 2015)'' complained of "despicable" treatment by tournament organisers after losing to [[Lei Peifan]] in the first match of the event.]]
The tournament began on 10 November with the matches from Group 2, which comprised [[Shaun Murphy]], [[Lei Peifan]], [[Judd Trump]], and [[Bai Yulu]].<ref name="WST_schedule" /> In the first group semi-final, the [[2025 Masters (snooker)|2025 Masters]] champion Murphy faced the [[2024 Scottish Open (snooker)|2024 Scottish Open]] champion Lei, who won the first {{Cuegloss|frame}} with a 72 {{cuegloss|break}} and also took the second. Murphy won frame three, but Lei moved 3–1 ahead with a 61 break in frame four and completed a 4–1 win in frame five.<ref>{{cite web |date=10 November 2025 |title=2025 Champion of Champions: Shaun Murphy v Lei Peifan |url=https://www.wst.tv/match-centre/1317d064-1bd8-4232-bfb6-11c1ad70c7d2 |access-date=10 November 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en-GB |archive-date=11 December 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20251211161050/https://www.wst.tv/match-centre/1317d064-1bd8-4232-bfb6-11c1ad70c7d2 |url-status=live }}</ref> Following the match, Murphy criticised the tournament organisers, [[Matchroom Sport]], for scheduling his match on the first day of the tournament, since he had just returned from the [[2025 International Championship]] in China. "The way I've been treated by Matchroom in this tournament is nothing short of despicable," Murphy said, claiming he had been informed of the match schedule just 48 hours previously, midway through a 36-hour trip from China. Murphy stated that players who had not travelled to the International Championship should have been scheduled to play first in the event. The organisers responded that the match schedule had been based on the players' [[Seeding (sports)|seedings]]. "Scheduling is always a challenge every year, but we take on board the points and always strive to improve for next year and the future," Matchroom Sport said in a statement.<ref name="BBC_Murphy_complaining" /><ref>{{Cite web |last=Livie |first=Alex |date=10 November 2025 |title='Made an embarrassment of me' - Shaun Murphy hits out at 'despicable' treatment by Champion of Champions organisers after early exit to Lei Peifan |url=https://www.tntsports.co.uk/snooker/champion-of-champions/2025-2026/shaun-murphy-says-treatment-nothing-short-of-despicable-after-early-exit-lei-peifan_sto23239893/story.shtml |access-date=10 November 2025 |website=[[TNT Sports]] |language=en-GB}}</ref> In the second group semi-final, the [[2024 UK Championship]] winner Trump faced the [[2025 World Women's Snooker Championship|2025 World Women's Championship]] winner Bai. She took the first frame, but Trump then won four consecutive frames, making breaks including 50, 84, and 71, as he also secured a 4–1 victory.<ref>{{cite web |date=10 November 2025 |title=2025 Champion of Champions: Judd Trump v Bai Yulu |url=https://www.wst.tv/match-centre/34ad8b54-03fb-497f-93ac-dcb0ca590078 |access-date=10 November 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en-GB |archive-date=13 November 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20251113043939/https://www.wst.tv/match-centre/34ad8b54-03fb-497f-93ac-dcb0ca590078 |url-status=live }}</ref><ref name="BBC_Murphy_complaining">{{Cite web |date=10 November 2025 |title=Murphy accuses Matchroom of 'despicable treatment' |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/c620xywyd32o |access-date=10 November 2025 |website=[[BBC Sport]] |language=en-GB |archive-date=13 November 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20251113200651/https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/c620xywyd32o |url-status=live }}</ref><ref name="WST_Trump_to_semis">{{cite web |date=11 November 2025 |title=Trump into semi-finals |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/november/11/trump-into-semi-finals/ |access-date=16 November 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260417071702/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/november/11/trump-into-semi-finals/|archive-date=17 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

=== Final ===
[[File:Mark Selby 2020.jpg|thumb|[[Mark Selby]] ''(pictured in 2020)'' defeated [[Judd Trump]] in the final to win the [[Champion of Champions (snooker)|Champion of Champions]] for the first time.]]
The final was played on 16 November as the best of 19 frames, held over two {{cuegloss|sessions}}, between Trump and Selby. Trump contested his sixth [[Champion of Champions (snooker)|Champion of Champions]] final, having previously won the title at the [[2021 Champion of Champions|2021 event]] and been runner-up on four other occasions.<ref name="TS_Trump_Zhao" /> Selby reached the final of the event for the first time, following three previous defeats at the semi-final stage.<ref name="WST_Selby_Robertson" /> Trump won the first frame with a century of 111, but Selby responded with a century of 102 to take the second. Trump won two frames in a row with breaks of 73 and 114 to lead 3–1 at the mid-session interval, but Selby then won four consecutive frames, making breaks including 70, 99, 65, and 64 as he moved into a 5–3 lead. Trump made a 71 break to win the ninth and last frame of the session, which ended 5–4 in favour of Selby.<ref name="WST_JT_v_MS" /><ref name="WST_Selby_Trump" />

When play resumed for the second session, Trump made a 61 break in frame 10, which he eventually won after a 25-minute {{Cuegloss|safety}} battle over the last red, tying the scores at 5–5. Frame 11 featured 25 safety shots on one red in the {{Cuegloss|pack}} and eventually went to Selby, who also won frame 12 after a safety battle on the last {{Cuegloss|pink ball|pink}}. Selby then made three consecutive centuries of 138, 101, and 132 to secure a 10–5 victory and his first Champion of Champions title. It was his first professional title since winning the 2025 Welsh Open nine months earlier.<ref name="WST_JT_v_MS">{{cite web |date=16 November 2025 |title=2025 Champion of Champions: Judd Trump v Mark Selby |url=https://www.wst.tv/match-centre/4f06005a-3b0f-4ffa-8c2d-4ee1055c28df |access-date=16 November 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en-GB |archive-date=27 November 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20251127052627/https://www.wst.tv/match-centre/4f06005a-3b0f-4ffa-8c2d-4ee1055c28df |url-status=live }}</ref><ref name="WST_Selby_Trump">{{cite web |date=16 November 2025 |title=Selby beats Trump to become Champion of Champions for first time |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/november/16/Selby-Beats-Trump-To-Become-Champion-Of-Champions-For-First-Time/ |access-date=16 November 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260417072049/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/november/16/Selby-Beats-Trump-To-Become-Champion-Of-Champions-For-First-Time/|archive-date=17 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Rostance |first=Tom |date=16 November 2025 |title=Selby wins Champion of Champions for first time|url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cp8yg5j35lgo |access-date=16 November 2025 |website=[[BBC Sport]] |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260417072715/https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cp8yg5j35lgo|archive-date=17 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref> "This event is only for the best players and it was a privilege to play [Trump] in the final," said Selby afterwards. "I played well from start to finish and I knew I had to because [Trump] is the man to beat. I have felt good about my game for the last few days, I have found something and I know I have to stick with it. It's a while since I have felt like that so it was great to play to that standard in a big final." After losing his fifth Champion of Champions final out of the six he had played, Trump commented: "I couldn't win with that [[cue stick|cue]]. I gave it my all today, but I just can't get used to it. I didn't know where to aim. There are no excuses, and congratulations to [Selby] because he was the best player all week and deserved to win. He scored very heavily, his safety was good and he showed why he is one of the all-time greats."<ref name="WST_Selby_Trump" />
