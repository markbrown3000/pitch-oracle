The '''2025 Xi'an Grand Prix''' (officially the '''2025 Du Xiaoman Xi'an Grand Prix''')<ref name="lookahead_wst">{{cite web |title=Leaders Look Ahead To Du Xiaoman 2025 Xi'an Grand Prix |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/september/05/leaders-look-ahead-to-du-xiaoman-2025-xi-an-grand-prix/ |date=5 September 2025 |work=[[World Snooker Tour]]|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418115909/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/september/05/leaders-look-ahead-to-du-xiaoman-2025-xi-an-grand-prix/|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref> was a professional [[snooker]] tournament that took place from 7 to 13 October 2025 at the [[Qujiang New District|Qujiang]] Sports Complex in [[Xi'an]], China. Qualifying took place from 1 to 3 September at the [[Leicester Arena]] in [[Leicester]], England. The second consecutive edition of the tournament since its inaugural staging in [[2024 Xi'an Grand Prix|2024]], it was the sixth [[List of snooker ranking tournaments|ranking event]] of the [[2025–26 snooker season]], following the [[2025 British Open]] and preceding the [[2025 Northern Ireland Open]]. It was broadcast by local channels in China and elsewhere in Asia; by [[TNT Sports]] and [[Discovery+]] in the United Kingdom and Ireland; by [[Eurosport]], Discovery+, and [[HBO Max]] in mainland Europe; and by [[WST Play]] in all other territories. The winner received £177,000 from a total prize fund of £850,000.

[[Kyren Wilson]] was the defending champion, having defeated [[Judd Trump]] 10–8 in the [[2024 Xi'an Grand Prix|2024 final]], but he lost 0–5 to [[Shaun Murphy]] in the last 16. [[Mark Williams (snooker player)|Mark Williams]] defeated Murphy 10–3 in the final to win his 27th ranking title. Aged 50 years and 206 days, he became the oldest winner of a ranking event, surpassing [[Ray Reardon]], who had been 50 years and 14 days old when he won the [[1982 Professional Players Tournament]]. Williams became the first player to win professional titles in his teens, 20s, 30s, 40s, and 50s.

The tournament produced 118 [[Century break|century breaks]], 25 during the qualifying round in Leicester and 93 at the main stage in Xi'an. Three [[Maximum break|maximum breaks]] were made at the event. During the Leicester qualifiers, [[Zhou Yuelong]] made the third maximum of his professional career in his 5–2 win over [[Julien Leclercq (snooker player)|Julien Leclercq]]. During the held-over qualifiers in Xi'an, Trump made the ninth maximum of his career in his 5–2 win over [[Ng On-yee]]. In the second round, [[Aaron Hill (snooker player)|Aaron Hill]] made the second maximum of his career in his 5–2 win over [[Huang Jiahao]]. These maximums were respectively the 8th, 12th, and 13th of the season and the 225th, 229th, and 230th in professional snooker history. Trump and Hill both made their maximums on 7 October, marking the fifth occasion that multiple 147s were made on the same day. [[Ronnie O'Sullivan]] played his 1,000th match at a ranking event during the held-over qualifiers.

==Overview==
The [[Xi'an Grand Prix]] is a professional ranking snooker tournament held in [[Xi'an]], China. The 2025 edition—the second staging of the tournament—took place from 7 to 13 October at the Qujiang Sports Complex in Xi'an.<ref name="lookahead_wst" /> Qualifying took place from 1 to 3 September at the [[Leicester Arena]] in [[Leicester]], England.<ref name="s.o_Q">{{cite web |title=Xi'an Grand Prix Qualifiers 2025|url=https://www.snooker.org/res/index.asp?event=2339 |access-date=13 July 2025 |website=snooker.org|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418115913/https://www.snooker.org/res/index.asp?event=2339|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref> The tournament was the sixth [[List of snooker ranking tournaments|ranking event]] of the [[2025–26 snooker season]], following the [[2025 British Open]] and preceding the [[2025 Northern Ireland Open]].<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |last= |first= |title=Tournaments 2025–26 |url=https://www.wst.tv/tournaments |access-date=2025-07-13 |website=World Snooker Tour |language=en |archive-date=5 December 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20251205154034/https://www.wst.tv/tournaments/ |url-status=live }}</ref> [[Kyren Wilson]] was the defending champion, having defeated [[Judd Trump]] 10–8 in the final of the inaugural [[2024 Xi'an Grand Prix|2024 edition]].<ref name="WST_main">{{cite web |title=Xi'an Grand Prix |url=https://www.wst.tv/xiangrandprix/ |work=[[World Snooker Tour]] |access-date=22 July 2024 |archive-date=20 July 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240720171233/https://www.wst.tv/xiangrandprix/ |url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=2024-08-25 |title=Wilson beats Trump to win Xi'an Grand Prix final |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cd6y7dnxwj9o |access-date=2026-04-18 |website=BBC Sport |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418115246/https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cd6y7dnxwj9o|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref> 

=== Format ===
All matches up to and including the quarter{{nbh}}finals were the best of 9 {{Cuegloss|frame|frames}}. The semi{{nbh}}finals were the best of 11 frames, and the final was the best of 19 frames, held over two {{Cuegloss|session|sessions}}.<ref name="s.o_M">{{Cite web |title=Xi'an Grand Prix 2025|url=https://www.snooker.org/res/index.asp?event=2338 |access-date=2025-08-27 |website=snooker.org |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418115916/https://www.snooker.org/res/index.asp?event=2338|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

[[Marco Fu]] won his qualifying match in Leicester but withdrew from the tournament's main stage after suffering a fractured elbow. His withdrawal meant that the defending champion Kyren Wilson received a [[Bye (sports)|bye]] to the last 32.<ref name="FE_Marco">{{cite web |title=Fu out for two months with fractured elbow |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/september/28/Fu-Out-For-Two-Months-With-Fractured-Elbow/ |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |date=28 September 2025 |access-date=6 October 2025|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418120215/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/september/28/Fu-Out-For-Two-Months-With-Fractured-Elbow/|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref> [[Scott Donaldson]] and [[Jamie Jones (snooker player)|Jamie Jones]] also withdrew from the main stage, and their respective last-64 opponents [[Mark Williams (snooker player)|Mark Williams]] and [[Yuan Sijun]] also received byes to the last 32.<ref name="s.o_M" /><ref name=":4">{{Cite web |last=Haigh |first=Phil |date=2 October 2025 |title=Xi'an Grand Prix draw gets late change after withdrawal |url=https://metro.co.uk/2025/10/02/xian-grand-prix-draw-gets-late-change-withdrawal-24323220/ |access-date=3 October 2025 |website=Metro |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418120110/https://metro.co.uk/2025/10/02/xian-grand-prix-draw-gets-late-change-withdrawal-24323220/|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=2 October 2025 |title=Updated Xi'an Grand Prix draw |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/october/02/updated-xi-an-grand-prix-draw/ |access-date=3 October 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418120115/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/october/02/updated-xi-an-grand-prix-draw/|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

=== Round two (last 64) ===
[[File:Judd Trump at Snooker German Masters (Martin Rulsch) 2014-02-01 18 (cropped).jpg|thumb|The [[List of world number one snooker players|world number one]] and previous year's runner-up [[Judd Trump]] ''(pictured in 2014)'' made a [[maximum break]] in the held-over qualifiers but lost to [[Matthew Stevens]] in the second round.]]
[[Aaron Hill (snooker player)|Aaron Hill]] made the second maximum break of his career during his 5–2 win over [[Huang Jiahao]], having made his first maximum 23 days earlier at the [[2025 English Open (snooker)|2025 English Open]]. It was the 13th maximum break of the season and the 230th in professional snooker history. Trump and Hill both made their maximums on 7 October, marking the fifth time in the sport's history that multiple 147s were made on the same day.<ref name=":5" /><ref name=":6" /> Zhou Yuelong, recently runner-up at the 2025 English Open, made two centuries and three half-centuries as he beat [[Ryan Day (snooker player)|Ryan Day]] 5–2. [[Louis Heathcote]] whitewashed [[Jackson Page]], and [[Elliot Slessor]] made centuries of 132 and 117 to eliminate [[Ricky Walden]] 5–3. [[Jak Jones]] defeated [[Dylan Emery]] 5–1.<ref name=":7" /><ref name=":6" />

Facing [[Matthew Stevens]], the [[List of world number one snooker players|world number one]] and previous year's runner-up Trump took a 3–1 lead, but Stevens then won three frames in a row to move 4–3 ahead. Trump {{Cuegloss|snookers required|required snookers}} in frame eight, as he trailed by 38 points with only the {{Cuegloss|colours}} remaining, but he secured {{Cuegloss|penalty points}} from {{Cuegloss|foul|fouls}} on the {{Cuegloss|blue ball|blue}} and {{Cuegloss|pink ball|pink}} and went on to win the frame on a {{Cuegloss|re-spotted black}} to tie the scores at 4–4. However, Trump failed to score in the decider as Stevens won the frame and match. Trump's loss meant that he had not reached a ranking quarter-final since the 2025 World Championship. O'Sullivan made a century of 128 and three further half-centuries as he defeated [[Yao Pengcheng]] 5–1. "I feel like I'm cueing as well as I have done since 2012," O'Sullivan said afterwards. "I played well between 2016 and 2019, but the last few years were pretty awful. I know I had some good wins but I was struggling with ball striking. At the moment I'm hitting the ball so pure and so clean."<ref>{{Cite web |date=8 October 2025 |title=Trump suffers surprise defeat by Stevens in China |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/c1wgv9nl789o |access-date=18 April 2026 |website=[[BBC Sport]] |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418115323/https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/c1wgv9nl789o|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref><ref name=":8">{{Cite web |last=Day |first=Michael |date=8 October 2025 |title=Zhao and Trump continue results struggle after suffering early Xi'an Grand Prix exits |url=https://www.totallysnookered.com/sport/snooker/tournaments/xian-grand-prix/zhao-xintong-and-judd-trump-continue-results-struggle-after-suffering-early-exits-at-the-xian-grand-prix-5351670 |access-date=8 October 2025 |website=Totally Snookered |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418121148/https://www.totallysnookered.com/sport/snooker/tournaments/xian-grand-prix/zhao-xintong-and-judd-trump-continue-results-struggle-after-suffering-early-exits-at-the-xian-grand-prix-5351670|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref><ref name=":9">{{Cite web |date=8 October 2025 |title=Xi'an Grand Prix day two |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/october/08/xi-an-grand-prix-day-two-1/ |access-date=8 October 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418121253/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/october/08/xi-an-grand-prix-day-two-1/|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

The reigning World Champion Zhao, who also had not reached a ranking quarter-final since the World Championship, lost 2–5 to the world number 56 [[Robert Milkins]] in a match that produced only two half-century breaks. === Round three (last 32) ===
[[File:Neil Robertson 2025 (2).jpg|thumb|The world number three [[Neil Robertson]] ''(pictured in 2025)'' lost to [[He Guoqiang (snooker player)|He Guoqiang]] in a {{Cuegloss|deciding frame}}.]]
Kyren Wilson made two centuries as he continued his title defence with a 5–2 victory over [[Yuan Sijun]]. "It's a great feeling defending the title and I want to make that last as long as possible," Wilson said afterwards. "I really enjoyed being here for the first time last year so I was looking forward to coming back. When you are excited and happy as a player, it brings out the best in you." O'Sullivan made a century of 134 as he whitewashed Maguire, meaning that he reached the last 16 having lost just one frame. Maguire scored just 25 points in the match. "This game has a way of humbling you," O'Sullivan commented afterwards. "So far I'm very happy, but maybe next month I'll be saying this game is really hard. You have to enjoy it when it's good, and when it's not you have to get through those periods." Pullen made breaks of 103, 83, 96, 101, and 77 as he whitewashed [[Noppon Saengkham]] to reach the last 16 of a ranking event for the second time. Afterwards, Pullen called the victory "the best win of my career and the best I have played." 

Lines recovered from 2–4 behind to defeat Slessor with a {{Cuegloss|clearance}} of 58 in the deciding frame. "I kept telling myself I was going to do it, and that calmed me down," said Lines of his match-winning break. Milkins defeated Jimmy Robertson in a decider, making three centuries of 127, 113, and 121 in the match, including his 200th century in professional competition. The world number three Neil Robertson made century breaks of 126 and 103 but lost in a deciding frame to [[He Guoqiang (snooker player)|He Guoqiang]]. Facing Wu, [[Shaun Murphy]] made breaks of 140, 94, 138, and 78 and won the match in a decider. Gary Wilson made two centuries as he beat [[Mateusz Baranowski]], also in a deciding frame. Hawkins defeated [[Thepchaiya Un-Nooh]] 5–2, and [[Mark Williams (snooker player)|Mark Williams]] beat Zhou Yuelong by the same score. Hill defeated Davis 5–1, making breaks of 97, 131, 81, and 102, and [[Stuart Bingham]] advanced with a whitewash victory over Moody.<ref>{{Cite web |date=9 October 2025 |title=Xi'an Grand Prix day three |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/october/09/xi-an-grand-prix-day-three/ |access-date=9 October 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en |archive-date=11 October 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20251011124417/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/october/09/xi-an-grand-prix-day-three/ |url-status=live }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=9 October 2025 |title=O'Sullivan not taking form 'for granted' after whitewash |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cwy80p2676jo |access-date=18 April 2026 |website=[[BBC Sport]] |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418115338/https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cwy80p2676jo|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

=== Round four (last 16) ===
[[File:Kyren Wilson EuM 2022-3.jpg|thumb|The defending champion [[Kyren Wilson]] ''(pictured in 2022)'' did not pot a ball against [[Shaun Murphy]], who scored 533 points without reply and won the match 5–0.]]Murphy made breaks of 74, 141, 133, 84, and 84 as he whitewashed the defending champion and world number two Kyren Wilson. [[Daniel Wells (snooker player)|Daniel Wells]] made four half-century breaks as he beat Milkins 5–2. === Quarter-finals ===
[[File:Daniel Wells at Snooker German Masters (DerHexer) 2013-01-30 05.jpg|thumb|[[Daniel Wells (snooker player)|Daniel Wells]] ''(pictured in 2013)'' defeated [[Liam Pullen]] 5–2 to reach the semi-finals of the tournament for a second consecutive year.]]
Williams and Lyu were tied at 2–2. Williams then made breaks of 68 and 70 as he took a 4–2 lead, but Lyu responded with breaks of 94 and 62 to tie the scores at 4–4. Williams won the 36-minute deciding frame to reach his 69th ranking semi-final. "I didn't play great, but I stuck in there," Williams said afterwards. "He let me off the hook a little bit. He was the better player, but I managed to find a way to scrape over the line." Pullen and Wells were also tied at 2–2, but Wells then took three frames in a row to win 5–2 and reach the semi-finals of the tournament for a second consecutive year. I don't want to go home in the semi-final."<ref>{{Cite web |date=11 October 2025 |title=Welsh wonderland for Williams and Wells |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/october/11/welsh-wonderland-for-williams-and-wells/ |access-date=11 October 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418120627/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/october/11/welsh-wonderland-for-williams-and-wells/|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref><ref name=":10">{{Cite web |last=Edwards |first=Melissa |date=11 October 2025 |title=Superb Wilson stuns O'Sullivan in Xi'an Grand Prix |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cpvlvrnlgvwo |access-date=18 April 2026 |website=[[BBC Sport]] |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418115427/https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cpvlvrnlgvwo|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

"Against [O'Sullivan], you can tell he's trying to look at signs," Wilson commented afterwards. "Trying to tell if you feel good, or you feel bad. Whether you are taking your chances or you aren't. === Semi-finals ===
[[File:Ebdon Murphy Masters 2025.jpg|thumb|[[Shaun Murphy]] ''(right; pictured in 2025)'' made {{Cuegloss|total clearance|total clearances}} of 131, 140, and 141 as he defeated [[Gary Wilson (snooker player)|Gary Wilson]] 6–4 to reach his 28th ranking final.]]
In the first semi-final, Williams faced Wells. The scores were tied at 2–2 at the mid-session interval. Wells won frame five on the last pink, but Williams took the next to tie the scores again at 3–3. After six frames, the highest break of the match was just 42, but Williams then produced breaks of 129 and 53 as he moved 5–3 ahead. Wells required snookers in frame nine, as Williams led by 33 points with just the colours remaining, but Wells secured the penalty points he needed by laying snookers on the {{Cuegloss|yellow ball|yellow}}. However, Williams then laid two snookers on the {{Cuegloss|brown ball|brown}}, and Wells {{Cuegloss|concession|conceded}} the frame after failing to escape from the second. Williams won the match 6–3. Defeat for Wells meant that he had played in four ranking semi-finals without reaching a final. "Most of my games this week have been the same, pretty poor," said Williams afterwards. "I scraped over the line again. I'm glad to win. Apart from the one century break, it was tough out there. I might have looked calm but you wouldn't want to know the names I was calling myself in my head."<ref name=":11">{{Cite web |date=12 October 2025 |title=Williams on track for new record |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/october/11/williams-on-track-for-new-record/ |access-date=12 October 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418120613/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/october/11/williams-on-track-for-new-record/|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref><ref name=":13">{{Cite web |last=Ramsdale |first=Ben |date=12 October 2025 |title=Murphy beats Wilson to set up meeting with Williams |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cvgqg6457z1o |access-date=18 April 2026 |website=[[BBC Sport]] |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418115449/https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cvgqg6457z1o|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

In the second semi-final, Murphy played Gary Wilson. Murphy won the first frame with a 91 break, but Wilson responded with breaks including 81 and a {{Cuegloss|total clearance}} of 139 as he won three frames in a row to lead 3–1 at the mid-session interval. Murphy then made breaks including 81 and total clearances of 131 and 140 as he won four consecutive frames to lead 5–3. Wilson took frame nine with a 74 break, but Murphy made another total clearance of 141 in frame 10 to secure a 6–4 victory, his 13th consecutive win on the professional tour. In all, the match produced four breaks over 130 and four more breaks over 70. "This vein of form won't last forever but I am enjoying it," Murphy said afterwards. "I played well [at the 2025 English Open] in [[Brentwood, Essex|Brentwood]], played well {{bracket|at the [[2025 British Open]]}} in [[Cheltenham]] and I was delighted to lift the trophy. Here this week I've got another chance. But I have got one of the best of all time in my way and he will give me nothing." By reaching the final, Murphy ensured that he would re-enter the top 10 in the world rankings after the event and qualify for the [[2025 Riyadh Season Snooker Championship]] in Saudi Arabia.<ref name=":13" /><ref name=":12">{{Cite web |date=12 October 2025 |title=Marvellous Murphy keeps winning streak going |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/october/11/Marvellous-Murphy-Keeps-Winning-Streak-Going-/ |access-date=12 October 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en |archive-date=3 November 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20251103205205/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/october/11/Marvellous-Murphy-Keeps-Winning-Streak-Going-/ |url-status=live }}</ref>

=== Final ===
[[File:Mark J Williams 2025.jpg|thumb|[[Mark Williams (snooker player)|Mark Williams]] ''(pictured in 2025)'' defeated [[Shaun Murphy]] 10–3 to win his 27th ranking title. Aged 50 years and 206 days, he became the oldest [[List of snooker ranking tournaments|ranking event]] winner, surpassing [[Ray Reardon]].]]
The final was played as the best of 19 frames, held over two {{Cuegloss|session|sessions}}, between the world number 5 Williams and the world number 12 Murphy. Williams contested the 44th ranking final of his career, while Murphy competed in his 28th.<ref name=":11" /><ref name=":12" /> Murphy had won 10 of the previous 17 professional matches between the two players.<ref name=":14">{{Cite web |date=13 October 2025 |title=Williams dominates Xi'an final |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/october/12/Williams-Dominates-Xi-an-Final/ |access-date=13 October 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418120557/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/october/12/Williams-Dominates-Xi-an-Final/|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref> Williams won the opening frame with a 75 break, recovered from 32 points behind to win the second with a break of 73, and made clearances of 56 and 68 in the next two frames to lead 4–0 at the mid-session interval. Murphy won frame five with a 69 break, but Williams took the sixth to restore a four-frame advantage at 5–1. Williams then made breaks of 59 and 55 to win frame seven and a century of 127 to win frame eight as he extended his lead to 7–1 after the first session.<ref name=":14" />

When play resumed for the second session, Williams won the ninth frame with a century of 122. Murphy won the next two frames, making a 93 break in the 11th, but Williams took the 12th frame with a 65 break and won the 13th with breaks of 61 and 22 to complete a 10–3 victory. It was the 27th ranking title of his career. "I played really well from the first frame to the last," said Williams, who advanced from 5th to 4th place in the world rankings after his win. "I don't think I missed more than a couple of easy balls all day. I put pressure on [Murphy] and he missed a couple of times from 50 in front. I made some good clearances and kept the pressure on. I think I made more frame-winning breaks today than I did in the whole tournament." Murphy's loss was his 15th defeat in his 28 ranking finals, although he advanced from 12th to 9th in the world rankings as runner-up. "[Williams] was very tough and the better man on the day," Murphy said afterwards. "I missed a few shots and he punished me. His {{cuegloss|safety}} was good and his tactics were strong."<ref>{{Cite web |date=13 October 2025 |title=Williams crushes Murphy and sets new record |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/october/13/williams-crushes-murphy-and-sets-new-record/ |access-date=13 October 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418120608/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/october/13/williams-crushes-murphy-and-sets-new-record/|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref><ref name=":15" /> 

At the age of 50 years and 206 days, Williams became the oldest winner of a ranking event, breaking a 43-year-old record by [[Ray Reardon]], who had been 50 years and 14 days old when he won the [[1982 Professional Players Tournament]]. Williams also became the first player to win professional titles in his teens, 20s, 30s, 40s, and 50s. "It's unbelievable," he said. "I cannot honestly believe how I'm still winning tournaments. I'm not practicing enough to really compete in the latter stages of tournaments because I just can't do it these days. But here I am at the venue trying to play as much as I can, and you've got to scrape me off the table until that last ball is potted. I just seem to be scraping results and playing well in the final."<ref name=":15">{{Cite web |last=Cartwright |first=Phil |date=13 October 2025 |title=Williams, 50, becomes oldest ranking event winner|url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cze65163222o |access-date=18 April 2026 |website=BBC Sport |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418115518/https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cze65163222o|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Blainey |first=Gareth |date=13 October 2025 |title=Williams seals spot as all-time Welsh sporting great|url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cql93zygg77o |access-date=18 April 2026 |website=[[BBC Sport]] |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260418115623/https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cql93zygg77o|archive-date=18 April 2026|url-status=live }}</ref>
