The '''2025 Grand Slam of Darts''' (known for sponsorship reasons as the '''2025 Mr Vegas Grand Slam of Darts''') was a  professional [[darts]] tournament that was held at the WV Active Aldersley in [[Wolverhampton]], England, from 8 to 16 November 2025. It was the 19th staging of the [[Grand Slam of Darts|Grand Slam]] by the [[Professional Darts Corporation]] (PDC). The total prize fund was £650,000, with the winner receiving £150,000.

The tournament featured 32 players; finalists from televised tournaments, [[2025 PDC Pro Tour|PDC Pro Tour]] title winners and additional qualifiers were joined by eight [[List of players with a 2025 PDC Tour Card|Tour Card holder]] qualifiers. It was the last Grand Slam to feature a 32-player field before the tournament's expansion to 48 players in 2026. [[Gary Anderson (darts player)|Gary Anderson]] was eliminated in the group stage for the first time in 18 appearances, while [[Jurjen van der Velde]], [[Niko Springer]] and [[Lukas Wenig]] all reached the knockout stage in their tournament debuts. 

[[Luke Littler]] was the defending champion, having defeated [[Martin Lukeman]] 16–3 in the [[2024 Grand Slam of Darts|2024 final]]. Following his semi-final victory over [[Danny Noppert]], Littler became the world number one on the [[PDC Order of Merit]] for the first time by overtaking [[Luke Humphries]]. He also became the youngest PDC world number one at age 18, surpassing [[Michael van Gerwen]] who achieved the feat at age 24 in 2014. He successfully retained the title, beating Humphries 16–11 in the final.

Humphries hit a [[nine-dart finish]] to win his second group stage match against [[Michael Smith (darts player)|Michael Smith]]. [[Beau Greaves]] set the record for the highest [[three-dart average]] by a female player at a televised PDC event, averaging 102.46 in her group stage loss to Anderson.

===Format===
All matches were in [[Leg (darts)|leg]] play format. The 32 players were drawn into eight groups of four, contested on a [[round-robin tournament|round-robin]] basis where every player faced each other once. Players received two points for a win. The top two players in each respective group qualified for the knockout stage. Two-player ties were first be decided by leg difference, then by looking at who won the match between the two. Three-player ties were decided in the following order: leg difference, legs won, legs won [[Glossary of darts#Break|against throw]], tournament [[Three-dart average|average]], nine-dart shootout (relevant players accumulate a score with nine darts).

The number of legs required to win increased as the tournament progressed:<ref name="BBCWhat">{{cite web |last=Colman |first=Jonty |date=3 November 2025 |title=What is Grand Slam of Darts and what is prize money? |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/darts/articles/cgkze6gvrg1o |website=BBC Sport |access-date=7 November 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |last=Wellens |first=Megan |date=4 November 2025 |title=Grand Slam of Darts 2025: Dates, schedule, fixtures, results, format, players, prize money as Luke Littler defends title |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13456559/grand-slam-of-darts-2025-dates-format-players-prize-money-schedule-as-luke-littler-defends-title |website=Sky Sports |access-date=7 November 2025}}</ref>
* '''Group stage:''' Best of 9 legs
* '''Second round:''' Best of 19 legs
* '''Quarter-finals, semi-finals and final:''' Best of 31 legs

===Broadcasts===
The tournament was broadcast on Sky Sports in the United Kingdom and Ireland. Other broadcasters included [[DAZN]] in Germany, Austria and Switzerland; [[Viaplay]] in the Netherlands and the [[Nordic countries]]; [[Fox Sports (Australia)|Fox Sports]] in Australia; [[Sky Sport (New Zealand)|Sky Sport]] in New Zealand; [[Peacock (streaming service)|Peacock]] and [[FanDuel Sports Network|FanDuel]] in the United States; [[Nova Sport (Czech Republic and Slovakia)|Nova]] in Czechia and Slovakia; [[L'Équipe (TV channel)|L'Équipe]] in France; [[Sport1 (Eastern Europe)|Sport1]] in Hungary; [[VTM (TV channel)|VTM]] in Belgium; and Zonasport in Croatia. It was also available on the PDC's streaming service, PDCTV, for international subscribers outside of the UK, Germany, Austria and Switzerland.<ref>{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=7 November 2025 |title=Where to watch the 2025 Mr Vegas Grand Slam of Darts |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/11/07/where-watch-2025-mr-vegas-grand-slam-dartsP |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=9 November 2025}}{{dead link|date=February 2026|bot=medic}}{{cbignore|bot=medic}}</ref>

==Qualification==
The first 24 participants were decided by the following criteria:<ref name="Cri">{{cite web |last=Phillips |first=Josh |date=29 April 2025 |title=2025 Mr Vegas Grand Slam of Darts qualification criteria |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/april/29/2025-mr-vegas-grand-slam-darts-qualification-criteria |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=25 July 2025}}</ref>

* Finalists from PDC televised events over the previous twelve months (up to a maximum of 16 players)
* Additional qualifiers (up to a maximum of 8 players)
* European Tour events winners (to fill up to 24 players)
* Players Championship event winners (to fill up to 24 players)

==Summary==
===Groups===
The group stage began on 8 November. The first set of group stage matches was played on 8 November, the second set was played on 9 November, and the third set was played across 10 and 11 November.<ref name="PDC8">{{cite web |last=Wood-Thompson |first=Lewis |date=8 November 2025 |title=Littler begins title defence in style as Van Gerwen & Smith edge to victory |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/november/08/littler-begins-title-defence-style-van-gerwen-smith-edge-victory |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=9 November 2025}}</ref><ref name="PDC10">{{cite web |last=Simpson |first=Will |date=10 November 2025 |title=Humphries, Price & Smith progress to Grand Slam knockout stages |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/november/10/humphries-price-smith-progress-grand-slam-knockout-stages |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=11 November 2025}}</ref>

====Groups A–D====
[[File:Alexis Toylo 2024.jpg|thumb|Alexis Toylo ''(pictured in 2024)'' became the first Asian player to win a match at the Grand Slam of Darts by defeating [[Stephen Bunting]] in his opening game.]]
Group A featured [[2023 Grand Slam of Darts|2023]] champion [[Luke Humphries]], [[Nathan Aspinall]], [[2022 Grand Slam of Darts|2022]] champion [[Michael Smith (darts player)|Michael Smith]] and [[Alex Spellman]]. Smith was competing in his first televised tournament since the [[2025 UK Open|UK Open]] in March after coming through the Tour Card holder qualifier.<ref>{{cite web |last=Parsons |first=Mike |date=1 November 2025 |title=Michael Smith qualifies for Mr Vegas Grand Slam of Darts |url=https://www.sthelensstar.co.uk/sport/25588246.michael-smith-qualifies-mr-vegas-grand-slam-darts/ |website=St Helens Star |access-date=9 November 2025}}</ref> Humphries, the world number one, [[Whitewash (sport)|whitewashed]] Spellman in his opening match, while Smith defeated Aspinall 5–3 in a repeat of the 2022 final.<ref name="BBC8">{{cite web |last=Kearns |first=Sean |date=8 November 2025 |title=Van Gerwen survives Greaves scare as Littler wins |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/darts/articles/cd67l82q00xo |website=BBC Sport |access-date=9 November 2025}}</ref><ref name="Sky8">{{cite web |last=Stafford |first=Ali |date=8 November 2025 |title=Grand Slam of Darts: Michael van Gerwen pips Beau Greaves in final-leg thriller as Luke Littler, Luke Humphries make winning starts |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12040/13466563/grand-slam-of-darts-michael-van-gerwen-pips-beau-greaves-in-final-leg-thriller-as-luke-littler-luke-humphries-make-winning-starts |website=Sky Sports |access-date=9 November 2025}}</ref> In a post-match interview with [[Viaplay]], Smith declared that he was "a fighter" and expressed his frustrations with his lack of televised appearances: "I just want to be competing. I'm sick of sitting at home watching people win tournaments and I'm sat at home."<ref>{{cite web |last=Allen |first=Dave |date=8 November 2025 |title=Battling Smith vows: 'I'm a fighter - I'll be back!' |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/november/08/battling-smith-vows-im-fighter-ill-be-back |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=9 November 2025}}</ref> On day two, Humphries hit a [[nine-dart finish]]—his second [[Nine-dart finish#List of televised nine-dart finishes|televised nine-darter]]—to clinch a 5–3 victory against Smith for his second win in Group A. Humphries described the contest as "the weirdest game of darts [he'd] been involved in"<ref name="PDC9">{{cite web |last=Simpson |first=Will |date=9 November 2025 |title=Humphries lands Grand Slam nine-darter as six stars progress to last 16 |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/november/09/humphries-lands-grand-slam-nine-darter-six-stars-progress-last-16 |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=10 November 2025}}</ref> due to a poor start on the [[Glossary of darts#Double|doubles]] and the subsequent perfect leg.<ref name="BBC9">{{cite web |date=9 November 2025 |title=Humphries hits nine-darter in Grand Slam win |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/darts/articles/cy4p200p9m9o |website=BBC Sport |access-date=10 November 2025}}</ref> "To then go on and hit a nine-darter, that's why darts is one of the best sports in the world," he proclaimed.<ref name="PDC9"/> Aspinall kept himself in the Group A qualification race with a 5–2 win over Spellman.<ref name="Sky9">{{cite web |last=Wellens |first=Megan |date=9 November 2025 |title=Grand Slam of Darts: Luke Humphries hits sensational nine-darter as Luke Littler progresses and Beau Greaves' hopes come to an end |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13467211/grand-slam-of-darts-luke-humphries-hits-sensational-nine-darter-as-luke-littler-progresses-and-beau-greaves-hopes-come-to-an-end |website=Sky Sports |access-date=10 November 2025}}</ref> Humphries finished top of Group A after defeating Aspinall 5–3 in a match where both players had [[three-dart average]]s over 100, as Smith secured qualification by defeating Spellman 5–2 to finish in second place.<ref name="BBC10">{{cite web |last=Drury |first=Sam |date=11 November 2025 |title=Humphries win eliminates Aspinall at Grand Slam |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/darts/articles/cjekpeq30vzo |website=BBC Sport |access-date=11 November 2025}}</ref>

Group B featured [[Chris Dobey]], [[Damon Heta]], [[Martin Lukeman]] and [[Jurjen van der Velde]]. Dobey opened his campaign with a 5–1 win against Van der Velde, while [[2024 Grand Slam of Darts|2024]] runner-up Lukeman lost to Heta by the same scoreline.<ref name="PDC8" /> Lukeman then lost 5–4 to Van der Velde in a result that eliminated him from the qualification race, as Dobey beat Heta 5–1 to progress to the knockout stage, setting up a shoot-out between Van der Velde and Heta for the second spot.<ref name="PDC9" /> Dobey earned his third win by defeating Lukeman 5–1 to top the group.<ref name="Sky10">{{cite web |last=Wellens |first=Megan |date=10 November 2025 |title=Grand Slam of Darts 2025: Michael Smith, Luke Humphries, Gerwyn Price through to last 16 as Nathan Aspinall misses out |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13467969/grand-slam-of-darts-2025-michael-smith-luke-humphries-gerwyn-price-through-to-last-16-as-nathan-aspinall-misses-out |website=Sky Sports |access-date=11 November 2025}}</ref> In the group's deciding match, world number 147 Van der Velde claimed the second qualifying place by beating Heta 5–3, advancing to the last 16 in his tournament debut.<ref>{{cite web |date=10 November 2025 |title=Debutant Van der Velde (22) stunt op Grand Slam of Darts: 'Leef mijn droom' |trans-title=Debutant Van der Velde (22) stuns at Grand Slam of Darts: 'Living my dream' |url=https://www.nu.nl/darts/6375526/debutant-van-der-velde-22-stunt-op-grand-slam-of-darts-leef-mijn-droom.html |website=NU.nl |access-date=12 November 2025 |language=Dutch}}</ref>

Group C featured [[Stephen Bunting]], [[Martin Schindler]], [[Luke Woodhouse]] and Alexis Toylo. Toylo of the Philippines earned an upset victory over Bunting in a [[Glossary of darts#Decider|deciding leg]] in Group C, becoming the first Asian player to win a match in the event's history.<ref name="Sky8Blog">{{cite web |last=Stafford |first=Ali |date=8 November 2025 |title=Grand Slam of Darts 2025: Live updates, video, highlights, commentary, schedule, and results from Wolverhampton |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/live-blog/12288/13463201/grand-slam-of-darts-2025-live-updates-video-highlights-commentary-schedule-and-results-from-wolverhampton |website=Sky Sports |access-date=9 November 2025}}</ref> Woodhouse defeated Schindler 5–2 to end the opening day at the top of the group.<ref name="PDC8" /> Bunting suffered another deciding loss to Schindler that eliminated him from the tournament, as Woodhouse secured qualification by beating Toylo 5–2.<ref name="Sky9" /> Bunting's third consecutive 5–4 defeat came against group winner Woodhouse, while Schindler won his shoot-out match with Toylo 5–2 to finish in second place and reach the knockout stage for the first time.<ref name="Sky10" />

Group D featured [[James Wade]], three-time champion [[Gerwyn Price]], [[Ricky Evans (darts player)|Ricky Evans]] and [[Stefan Bellmont]]. Price hit a 167 [[Glossary of darts#Checkout|checkout]] but was beaten 5–4 by Evans in his opening match, while Wade missed four [[Glossary of darts#Match dart|match darts]] as he lost to Bellmont by the same scoreline.<ref name="Sky8" /> Price recovered with a whitewash win against Wade in his second match, eliminating Wade from contention. Evans converted a [[Glossary of darts#Maximum check|170 checkout]] on his way to beating Bellmont in a deciding leg to advance, which set up a match between Price and Bellmont for the second qualification spot in Group D.<ref name="PDC9" /> Despite losing his first match, Price finished top of the group with a 5–1 win over Bellmont, as Evans fell to second place due to a 5–2 loss to Wade.<ref name="BBC10" />

====Groups E–H====
[[File:Beau Greaves 2024.png|thumb|[[Beau Greaves]] ''(pictured in 2024)'' recorded the highest [[three-dart average]] by a female player at a televised PDC event, averaging 102.46 in her 5–4 loss to [[Gary Anderson (darts player)|Gary Anderson]].]]
Group E featured defending champion [[Luke Littler]], [[Daryl Gurney]], [[Connor Scutt]] and [[Karel Sedláček]]. Littler began his Group E campaign with a 5–1 win over Sedláček, later admitting that he was "obviously looking for the whitewash".<ref name="BBC8" /> Gurney missed a match dart whilst 4–3 up against Scutt, with Scutt taking advantage to claim victory in a deciding leg.<ref name="Sky8" /> Scutt hit a 170 checkout to level his meeting with Littler at 1–1 but would ultimately lose 5–3, as the defending champion secured qualification to the knockout stage.<ref>{{cite web |date=9 November 2025 |title=Grand Slam of Darts 2025: Luke Littler works past Connor Scutt |url=https://www.espn.co.uk/darts/story/_/id/46906790/grand-slam-darts-2025-luke-littler-beats-connor-scutt |website=ESPN |access-date=10 November 2025}}</ref> Gurney was eliminated following a 5–3 loss to Sedláček, who won five legs in a row and secured victory with his 11th match dart.<ref name="Sky9" /> Littler defeated Gurney 5–2 to win Group E and was joined by Scutt, who earned a 5–3 victory over Sedláček to qualify.<ref name="PDC11">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=11 November 2025 |title=Van Gerwen, Rock and Nijman advance to Grand Slam knockout stages |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/november/11/van-gerwen-rock-and-nijman-advance-grand-slam-knockout-stages |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=12 November 2025}}</ref>

Group F featured [[Gian van Veen]], [[Josh Rock]], [[Wessel Nijman]] and [[Lisa Ashton]]. Newly-crowned [[2025 European Championship (darts)|European]] champion Van Veen almost suffered an upset loss to Ashton as the [[2025 Women's World Matchplay|Women's World Matchplay]] champion led 4–3, but Van Veen took the next two legs to avoid defeat.<ref name="Sky8Blog" /> Despite averaging 109, Rock lost to Nijman in a deciding leg.<ref name="PDC8" /> In their second match-ups, Van Veen averaged 107 in his 5–3 over Nijman and Rock whitewashed Ashton, which meant that Van Veen, Nijman and Rock were all still in contention to qualify heading into the final day of group stage action.<ref name="PDC9" /> Rock and Nijman would progress from Group F as Rock eliminated Van Veen through a 5–2 win to top the group while Nijman confirmed his qualification by defeating Ashton.<ref name="RTE11">{{cite web |date=12 November 2025 |title=Josh Rock and Michael van Gerwen reach Grand Slam of Darts knockouts |url=https://www.rte.ie/sport/darts/2025/1111/1543465-rock-and-van-gerwen-reach-grand-slam-of-darts-knockouts/ |website=RTÉ Sport |access-date=12 November 2025}}</ref>

Group G featured three-time champion [[Michael van Gerwen]], [[Gary Anderson (darts player)|Gary Anderson]], [[Niko Springer]] and [[Beau Greaves]]. In his opening match, Van Gerwen held a 4–2 lead against Greaves but was taken to a deciding leg, where Greaves was unable to convert a 52 checkout for the victory and Van Gerwen ultimately won 5–4.<ref name="BBC8" /> Tournament debutant Springer landed a 152 checkout on his way to beating Anderson to lead Group G ahead of Van Gerwen.<ref name="Sky8" /> Greaves was beaten in another deciding leg by Anderson, with Van Gerwen's 5–4 loss to Springer ensuring her elimination from Group G. Despite the defeat, Greaves set the record for the highest three-dart average by a female player in a televised PDC event, averaging 102.46 to beat [[Fallon Sherrock]]'s 101.55 that she registered at the [[2021 Grand Slam of Darts|2021 Grand Slam]].<ref name="BBC9" /><ref name="Sky9" /> Speaking about Greaves, Anderson said: "She's going to cause so much trouble next year in the [[PDC Pro Tour|Pro Tour]]."<ref name="BBC9" /> She earned a consolation win against Springer in her final game after going 3–0 down, which allowed Van Gerwen to top the group following his 5–2 victory over Anderson in which he averaged 108.45.<ref name="BBC11">{{cite web |date=12 November 2025 |title=Van Gerwen beats Anderson to progress at Grand Slam |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/darts/articles/c993278k880o |website=BBC Sport |access-date=12 November 2025}}</ref> Anderson finished bottom of Group G, marking the first time he had been eliminated in the group stage in 18 Grand Slam appearances.<ref name="Sky11">{{cite web |last=Wellens |first=Megan |date=11 November 2025 |title=Grand Slam of Darts: Michael van Gerwen ends Gary Anderson's 17-year record as Beau Greaves registers victory |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13468536/grand-slam-of-darts-michael-van-gerwen-ends-gary-andersons-17-year-record-as-beau-greaves-registers-victory |website=Sky Sports |access-date=12 November 2025}}</ref>

Group H featured [[Jonny Clayton]], [[Danny Noppert]], [[Lukas Wenig]] and [[Cam Crabtree]]. Noppert survived seven match darts from tournament debutant Wenig to win his opening match, with Cam Crabtree's 5–1 victory over Jonny Clayton placing Noppert in second place.<ref name="PDC8" /> Clayton's elimination was confirmed following a 5–3 defeat to Wenig, while Noppert secured qualification with his second 5–4 win.<ref name="Sky9" /> Clayton managed to pick up a 5–4 victory over Noppert in his closing group stage match, allowing Wenig to win the group after he defeated Crabtree 5–1 to qualify.<ref name="PDC11" />

===Semi-finals===
[[File:2025-04-03 Premier League Darts Berlin 2025 by Sandro Halank–103.jpg|thumb|[[Luke Humphries]] ''(pictured)'', who hit a [[nine-dart finish]] in the group stage, defeated [[Gerwyn Price]] 16–13 to advance to the final.]]
The semi-finals were played in the afternoon session on 16 November.<ref name="PDC16">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=16 November 2025 |title=Littler topples Humphries to retain Grand Slam of Darts title |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/november/16/littler-topples-humphries-retain-grand-slam-darts-title |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=16 November 2025}}</ref> Luke Humphries faced Gerwyn Price and Luke Littler faced Danny Noppert. Humphries was competing in his third Grand Slam semi-final in four years and Price was competing in his fourth overall; Price went on to win the title in the three previous years he had reached the last four.<ref name="PDC14" /><ref name="Sky14" /> Defending champion Littler reached a second successive Grand Slam semi-final, putting him one win away from overtaking Humphries on the [[PDC Order of Merit]] and becoming world number one.<ref name="BBC15" /> Noppert reached the semi-finals of the tournament for the first time, marking his fifth televised semi-final of 2025 after the [[2025 PDC World Masters|World Masters]], [[2025 PDC World Cup of Darts|World Cup]] (with Gian van Veen), [[2025 World Grand Prix (darts)|World Grand Prix]] and European Championship.<ref name="PDC15" />

Humphries landed a 142 checkout on his way to going 5–1 ahead against Price. The three-time champion was able to find breaks of throw as he attempted to reduce the deficit, but Humphries stayed in front and found a break of his own in the 24th leg for 14–11. Humphries then pinned double 5 for a 16–13 victory.<ref name="Sky16">{{cite web |last=Wellens |first=Megan |date=16 November 2025 |title=Grand Slam of Darts: Luke Littler defends his crown with 16-11 victory over Luke Humphries |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13471044/grand-slam-of-darts-luke-littler-defends-his-crown-with-16-11-victory-over-luke-humphries |website=Sky Sports |access-date=17 November 2025}}</ref> The 2023 champion defied four ton-plus checkouts from Price by averaging 106.25 and hitting 16 maximums.<ref name="LittlerSecureOne" /><ref name="PDC16" /> [[Sky Sports]] pundit [[Mark Webster (darts player)|Mark Webster]] called Humphries's performance a "brilliant display" and said that it was "what we associate with Luke Humphries". Commenting on the world number one race, he stated: "In the scale of things, does it matter? You're going to keep winning events and put yourself back in the equation."<ref name="Littler1Sky">{{cite web |date=16 November 2025 |title=Littler crowned world No 1 after reaching Grand Slam of Darts final |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12040/13471187/littler-crowned-world-no-1-after-reaching-grand-slam-of-darts-final |website=Sky Sports |access-date=17 November 2025}}</ref> Humphries discussed the possibility of it being his last day as world number one in his post-match interview. He claimed that he was focusing on winning the title, but also said he would be fighting to take his spot back: "I'll be fighting hard, it's a war now."<ref name="Ind16">{{cite web |last=Veal |first=Jonathan |date=16 November 2025 |title=Luke Littler makes more darts history by becoming youngest World No 1 |url=https://www.independent.co.uk/sport/darts/luke-littler-world-no-1-humphries-b2866254.html |website=The Independent |access-date=17 November 2025}}</ref>

Noppert took an early 3–2 lead against Littler and broke throw to extend his advantage to 6–4. However, at 8–6 down, Littler took control and claimed 10 of the next 11 legs as he raced to a 16–9 win.<ref name="LittlerSecureOne" /><ref name="Sky16" /> This victory guaranteed that Littler would overtake Humphries on the PDC Order of Merit regardless of the result of the final, cementing Littler as the new world number one.<ref name="Ind16" /> "It's not even been two years on the tour and I'm already world number one. ===Final===
[[File:2025-04-03 Premier League Darts Berlin 2025 by Sandro Halank–123.jpg|thumb|[[Luke Littler]] ''(pictured)'', the new world number one, retained the title by beating [[Luke Humphries]] 16–11 in the final.]]
The final between Luke Humphries and Luke Littler was played in the evening session on 16 November.<ref name="PDC16" /> It was the sixth major final contested between the pair, adding to their meetings at the [[2024 PDC World Darts Championship|2024 World Championship]], two [[Premier League Darts|Premier League]] finals, the [[2024 Players Championship Finals]] and the 2025 World Grand Prix.<ref name="Sky16" /> Humphries was competing in his second Grand Slam final, having defeated [[Rob Cross]] 16–8 in the 2023 final to win the tournament for the first time.<ref>{{cite web |date=19 November 2025 |title=Grand Slam of Darts: Luke Humphries beats Rob Cross 16-8 to win final |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/darts/67470522 |website=BBC Sport |access-date=17 November 2025}}</ref> The Grand Slam final was also his third consecutive ranking major final after finishing runner-up at the World Grand Prix and the European Championship.<ref name="PDC16" /> Littler reached a second straight final, having defeated Martin Lukeman to triumph in 2024.<ref name="24Slam" /> Humphries was looking to win his third major title of 2025, having already won the World Masters and the [[2025 Premier League Darts|Premier League]]. Littler was seeking his fifth major title of the year, having won the [[2025 PDC World Darts Championship|World Championship]], UK Open, [[2025 World Matchplay|World Matchplay]] and World Grand Prix.<ref>{{cite web |date=16 November 2025 |title=Luke Litter vs Luke Humphries: Head-to-head record, past darts matches in TV finals and who has the edge in darts' battle for world No 1? |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12028/13448555/luke-litter-vs-luke-humphries-head-to-head-record-past-darts-matches-in-tv-finals-and-who-has-the-edge-in-darts-battle-for-world-no-1 |website=Sky Sports |access-date=17 November 2025}}</ref>

Littler quickly established a 2–0 lead in the final, but Humphries responded by winning the next four legs to go 4–2 ahead.<ref name="LittlerSecureOne" /> The two players' efficiency on the doubles declined in the second session, although Humphries would take out a 112 finish to level at 5–5 after Littler regained his lead.<ref name="PDC16" /><ref name="Sky16" /> Humphries landed a 79 checkout and completed successive 12-dart legs on his way to 8–6, just for Littler to restore parity at 8–8 with a 167 finish. The defending champion then went seven darts into a nine-dart finish in the 19th leg, missing the eighth.<ref name="PDC16" /> The tie shifted in Littler's favour in the 20th leg, as Humphries opted to go for a 170 checkout while having a firm lead. An unsuccessful finish and subsequent set-up mishaps allowed Littler to hit a 160 checkout to go 11–9 up.<ref name="LittlerSecureOne" /> Littler proceeded to win four of the next five legs to go one away from the title. Humphries would claim leg 26 but was unable to stop Littler, who converted a 96 checkout on double 9 to win 16–11.<ref name="PDC16" /><ref name="Sky16" /> Littler finished the match with an average of 100.61 and 15 maximums.<ref name="PDC16" />

Littler retained the title—the first time he had retained a PDC major title—for his second Grand Slam triumph, extending his unbeaten record at the tournament to 14 matches.<ref name="PDC16" /> Littler became the fourth player to defend the Grand Slam, after [[Phil Taylor (darts player)|Phil Taylor]], Michael van Gerwen and Gerwyn Price.<ref name="Sky16" /> It was his fifth major title of 2025, becoming the third player to win five majors in a single year, after Taylor and Van Gerwen.<ref name="PDC16" /> It was his eighth major title overall, as well as the 16th title won by either Littler or Humphries in the last 23 major tournaments.<ref name="SL16">{{cite web |last=Hammer |first=Chris |date=16 November 2025 |title=Darts results: New world number one Luke Littler beats Luke Humphries to win the Grand Slam of Darts |url=https://www.sportinglife.com/darts/news/darts-results-new-world-number-one-luke-littler-beats-luke-humphries-to-win-the-grand-slam-of-darts/228749 |website=Sporting Life |access-date=17 November 2025}}</ref> In addition to securing the title, Littler finished the tournament as the new world number one on the PDC Order of Merit, making him the 14th different player in PDC history to top the ranking. The tournament marked the end of Humphries's 682-day reign as world number one, having taken the top spot at the 2024 World Championship.<ref>{{cite web |last=Hammer |first=Chris |date=16 November 2025 |title=Luke Littler becomes the youngest world number one in history but who else has topped the rankings and which legends have never made it? |url=https://www.sportinglife.com/darts/news/luke-littler-becomes-the-youngest-world-number-one-in-history-but-who-else-has-topped-the-rankings-and-which-legends-have-never-made-it/228736 |website=Sporting Life |access-date=17 November 2025}}</ref> 18-year-old Littler became the youngest player to achieve the feat, surpassing Van Gerwen's record from when he became world number one at age 24 in 2014.<ref name="Ind16" /> The £150,000 top prize saw Littler's total amount of prize money on his ranking rise to £1,850,000.<ref name="LittlerSecureOne" /><ref name="PDC16" /> "Now I'm number one, I'm hungry to stay there. I want to be there for the next few years," Littler said after the final, mentioning that there was going to be a "target on [his] back from Luke [Humphries] and all the other players". Despite the defeat, Humphries remained motivated and declared that he was "going to go to war" with Littler at the upcoming World Championship. The two also shared the belief that they were in a league of their own, with Littler noting: "The finals show it, the stats show it and like Luke [Humphries] said, if everyone is getting bored of me and him then someone needs to stop us otherwise we're going to keep winning."<ref name="LittlerSecureOne" />
