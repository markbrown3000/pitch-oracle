The '''2026 International Championship''' was a professional [[snooker]] tournament that took place from 31 October to 7 November 2026 at the South New City National Fitness Center (SNCNFC) in [[Nanjing]], China. Qualifiers took place from 17 to 19 September at the [[Leicester Arena]] in [[Leicester]], England. The 12th edition of the [[International Championship]] since it was first staged in [[2012 International Championship|2012]], it was the eighth [[List of snooker ranking tournaments|ranking event]] of the [[2026–27 snooker season]], following the [[2026 Northern Ireland Open]] and preceding the [[2026 UK Championship]].

[[Wu Yize]] was the defending champion, having defeated [[John Higgins]] 10–6 in the [[2025 International Championship|2025 final]] but he was beaten by [[Stephen Maguire]] in the last 32.

[[Kyren Wilson]] beat [[John Higgins]] 10–8 in the final to win the tournament.

== Overview ==
The [[International Championship]] is a professional ranking snooker tournament held in China. It first took place in [[2012 International Championship|2012]] and the inaugural champion was [[Judd Trump]], who defeated [[Neil Robertson]] 10–8 in the final.<ref>{{Cite news |date=4 November 2012 |title=Judd Trump beats Robertson in International Championship final |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/20199610 |access-date=4 September 2025 |work=[[BBC Sport]] |language=en-GB |archive-date=16 May 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230516180219/https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/20199610 |url-status=live }}</ref> Staged from 2012 to 2014 in [[Chengdu]] and from 2015 to 2019 in [[Daqing]], the tournament was not held from 2020 to 2022 due to the impact of the [[COVID-19 pandemic]]. Following its return to the calendar, the tournament was staged in [[Tianjin]] in 2023 and [[Nanjing]] in 2024 and 2025.<ref name="WST_main">{{cite web |title=International Championship |url=https://www.wst.tv/internationalchampionship |access-date=6 October 2026 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |archive-date=6 October 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20261006084647/https://www.wst.tv/internationalchampionship/ |url-status=live}}</ref>

The 2026 edition of the tournament—the 12th staging of the event—took place from 31 October to 7 November in Nanjing.<ref name="s.o_M">{{cite web| url=https://www.snooker.org/res/index.asp?event=2762 |title=International Championship 2026 |website=snooker.org |date=7 November 2026 |access-date=7 November 2026 |archive-date=7 November 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20261107163803/https://www.snooker.org/res/index.asp?event=2762 |url-status=live}}</ref> Qualifiers took place from 17 to 19 September at the [[Leicester Arena]] in [[Leicester]], England.<ref name="s.o_Q">{{cite web|url=https://www.snooker.org/res/index.asp?event=2771|title=International Championship Qualifiers 2026|website=snooker.org|access-date=27 May 2026|language=en}}</ref> it was the eighth [[List of snooker ranking tournaments|ranking event]] of the [[2026–27 snooker season]], following the [[2026 Northern Ireland Open]] and preceding the [[2026 UK Championship]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.snooker.org/res/index.asp?season=2026&template=2&tour=all|title=Calendar 2026/2027|website=snooker.org|access-date=26 May 2026|language=en}}</ref> [[Wu Yize]] was the defending champion, having defeated [[John Higgins]] 10–6 in the [[2025 International Championship|2025 final]],<ref>{{cite web |date=9 November 2025 |title=Wonderful Wu captures maiden glory |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/november/09/wonderful-wu-captures-maiden-glory/ |access-date=9 November 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260420141850/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/november/09/wonderful-wu-captures-maiden-glory/|archive-date=20 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref> but he was beaten 5–6 by [[Stephen Maguire]] in the last 32.<ref name="WST_day03" />

[[Kyren Wilson]] won the tournament, beating [[John Higgins]] 10–8 in the final.<ref name="WST_FinalResult">{{cite web |title=Warrior downs the Wizard in Nanjing |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/november/07/warrior-downs-the-wizard-in-nanjing/ |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |date=7 November 2026 |access-date=7 November 2026}}</ref><ref name="WST_Wilson_Higgins">{{cite web |title=Match: Kyren Wilson v John Higgins |url=https://www.wst.tv/match-centre/70c47be8-cd9c-4b1c-a94c-3643dd63cb33 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |date=7 November 2026 |access-date=8 November 2026 |archive-date=8 November 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20261108075846/https://www.wst.tv/match-centre/70c47be8-cd9c-4b1c-a94c-3643dd63cb33 |url-status=live}}</ref> There were 145 [[century break]]s made in the event, with 45 made during qualifying,<ref name="si_cent_Q" /> and 100 made at the main stage.<ref name="si_cent_M" /> The highest {{cuegloss|break}} was a [[Maximum break|147]] made by [[Thepchaiya Un-Nooh]].<ref name="Max 247" />

=== Format ===
All matches were played as the [[Playoff format#Best-of formats|best of]] 11 {{cuegloss|frame|frames}} up to the semi‑finals, which were played as the best of 17 frames, held over two {{cuegloss|session|sessions}}. The final was the best of 19 frames, also held over two sessions.<ref name="s.o_M" />

=== Last 64 ===
Murphy, Hawkins, and Brecel withdrew and so [[Mitchell Mann]], [[Ricky Walden]] and Un-Nooh were given [[walkover]]s to the last 32, respectively.<ref name="WST_withdrawals">{{cite web |title=Murphy, Hawkins and Brecel withdraw from International Championship |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/october/29/murphy--hawkins-and-brecel-withdraw-from-international-championship/ |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |date=29 October 2026 |access-date=29 October 2026}}</ref>

Chang became the 94th player to reach 100 [[century break]]s in the seventh frame of his 6–2 win over [[Ross Muir]].<ref name="s.o_M" /><ref>{{cite web |title=SnookerInfo: Centuries & Stats |url=https://snookerinfo.co.uk/ |website=snookerinfo.co.uk |access-date=1 November 2026 |archive-date=1 November 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20261101194456/https://snookerinfo.co.uk/ |url-status=live}}</ref>

=== Last 32 ===
Un-Nooh made the eighth [[maximum break]] of his professional career in his 3–6 loss to Williams. It was the sixth 147 of the [[2026–27 snooker season|season]], and the 247th official maximum.<ref name="Max 247">{{cite web |title=Un-Nooh makes Nanjing maximum |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/november/02/un-nooh-makes-nanjing-maximum/ |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |date=2 November 2026 |access-date=2 November 2026 |archive-date=2 November 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20261102054710/https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/november/02/un-nooh-makes-nanjing-maximum/ |url-status=live}}</ref>

Maguire defeated defending champion [[Wu Yize]] 6–5. [[Sam Craigie]] potted a {{cuegloss|re-spotted black}} in the {{cuegloss|deciding frame}}, but the {{cuegloss|cue ball}} went {{cuegloss|in-off}} to hand [[Zhao Xintong]] a 6–5 win.<ref name="WST_day03">{{cite web |title=Craigie re-spot foul hands Zhao victory |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/november/02/international-championship--day-three/ |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |date=2 November 2026 |access-date=4 November 2026}}</ref>

=== Final ===
The final was played on 7 November as the [[Playoff format#Best-of formats|best of]] 19 {{cuegloss|frame|frames}}, played over two {{cuegloss|session|sessions}}. [[Kyren Wilson]] faced [[John Higgins]].<ref name="s.o_M" />

At the end of the afternoon session Higgins led Wilson 5–4.<ref name="WST_matches_M" /><ref>{{cite web |title=Higgins secures narrow lead over Wilson |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2026/november/07/higgins-secures-narrow-lead-over-wilson/ |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |date=7 November 2026 |access-date=7 November 2026}}</ref> Wilson went on to win the match, and the tournament 10–8.<ref name="WST_FinalResult" /><ref>{{cite news |title=Wilson beats Higgins to win International Championship |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/c54g1n39g30zo |work=[[BBC Sport]] |first=Mandeep |last=Sanghera |date=7 November 2026 |access-date=7 November 2026}}</ref><ref name="WST_Wilson_Higgins" />
