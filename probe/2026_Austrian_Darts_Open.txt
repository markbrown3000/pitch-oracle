The '''2026 Austrian Darts Open''' (known for sponsorship reasons as the '''2026 Elten Safety Shoes Austrian Darts Open''') was a professional [[darts]] tournament that took place at the [[Stadthalle Graz]] in [[Graz]], Austria, from 8 to 10 May 2026.<ref>{{cite web |title=Elten Safety Shoes Austrian Darts Open 2026 |url=https://www.pdc-europe.tv/tournaments/et-2026-en/austrian-darts-open-2026/ |access-date=1 March 2026 |website=PDC Europe}}</ref> It was the sixth of fifteen [[PDC European Tour]] events on the [[2026 PDC Pro Tour]]. It featured a field of 48 players and £230,000 in prize money, with £35,000 going to the winner.

[[Martin Schindler]] was the defending champion, having defeated [[Ross Smith (darts player)|Ross Smith]] 8–4 in the [[2025 Austrian Darts Open|2025 final]].<ref>{{cite web |date=28 April 2025 |title=Austrian Darts Open: Martin Schindler claims third European Tour title in Graz with win over Ross Smith |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13357338/austrian-darts-open-martin-schindler-claims-third-european-tour-title-in-graz-with-win-over-ross-smith |website=Sky Sports |access-date=29 April 2026}}</ref> However, he lost 6–2 to [[Daryl Gurney]] in the quarter-finals. [[Cristo Reyes]] hit a [[nine-dart finish]] in his second-round win against [[Damon Heta]].

[[Josh Rock]] won his second European Tour title by defeating [[Kevin Doets]] 8–6 in the final, having survived three missed [[Glossary of darts#Match dart|match darts]] from [[Niko Springer]] in the third round.

== Qualification and format ==
The top 16 players on the two-year [[PDC Order of Merit]] were [[Seeding (sports)|seeded]] and entered the tournament in the second round, while the next 16 highest-ranked players from the one-year [[PDC Pro Tour Order of Merit]] automatically qualified for the first round. The seedings were confirmed on 27 March.<ref>{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=27 March 2026 |title=2026 ET5-6 Seeds & Tour Card Holder qualifier entries confirmed |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/march/27/2026-et5-6-seeds---tour-card-holder-qualifier-entries-confirmed/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=20 April 2026}}</ref> The remaining 16 places went to players from four qualifying events – 10 from the Tour Card Holder Qualifier (held on 1 April),<ref>{{cite web |date=1 April 2026 |title=PDC ET06 Tour Card Holder Qualifier|url= https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdceutcq26et06/matches|website=Dart Connect TV |access-date=16 April 2026}}</ref> four from the Host Nation Qualifier (held on 7 May),<ref>{{cite web |date=7 May 2026 |title=PDC ET06 Host Nation Qualifier|url= https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdc26et06hnq/matches|website=Dart Connect TV |access-date=7 May 2026}}</ref> one from the Nordic & Baltic Associate Member Qualifier (held on 30 March),<ref>{{cite web |date=30 March 2026 |title=PDCNB 2026 Tour - Latvia 2 - Weekend 02 |url=https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdcnbpro26e02/matches|website=Dart Connect TV |access-date=16 April 2026}}</ref> and one from the East European Associate Member Qualifier (held on 11 April).<ref>{{cite web |date=11 April 2026 |title=PDC ET06 E. Europe Qualifier |url= https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdceeu26et06q/matches|website=Dart Connect TV |access-date=16 April 2026}}</ref>

[[Jonny Clayton]] withdrew and was replaced by [[Madars Razma]]. In Clayton's place, [[Daryl Gurney]] moved up to become the 16th seed.<ref name="Draw">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=7 May 2026 |title=2026 Elten Safety Shoes Austrian Darts Open draw & schedule |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/may/07/2026-elten-safety-shoes-austrian-darts-open-draw---schedule/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=7 May 2026}}</ref>

== Summary ==
=== First round ===
[[File:Mensur Suljovic and Rowby-John Rodriguez (cropped).png|thumb|[[Mensur Suljović]] ''(pictured in 2024)'' was the only victorious Austrian player in the first round. He defeated [[Cameron Menzies]] 6–2.]]
The first round (best of 11 [[Leg (darts)|legs]]) was played on 8 May.<ref name="PDC_D1">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=8 May 2026 |title=Austrian icon Suljovic stars on Day One in Graz |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/may/08/austrian-icon-suljovic-stars-day-one-graz/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=8 May 2026}}</ref> Austria's [[Mensur Suljović]], one of four host nation qualifiers, won his opening match 6–2 against [[Cameron Menzies]], hitting 75 per cent of his attempts at [[Glossary of darts#Double|double]]. Speaking after the match, he thanked his home crowd and called them "my family".<ref name="PDC_D1"/> The three other host nation qualifiers were eliminated: [[Zoran Lerchbacher]] was defeated 6–2 by two-time world champion [[Peter Wright (darts player)|Peter Wright]], who claimed his first European Tour win of the season; debutant Nick Zwittnigg lost 6–4 to [[Andrew Gilding]]; and Aaron Hardy made his first European Tour appearance in 11 years, losing 6–3 to [[Krzysztof Ratajski]].<ref name="EU_D1">{{cite web |date=8 May 2026 |title=ELTEN Safety Shoes Austrian Darts Open: Mensur Suljović sorgt für Ekstase in Graz |trans-title=ELTEN Safety Shoes Austrian Darts Open: Mensur Suljović causes ecstasy in Graz |url=https://www.pdc-europe.tv/de/aktuell/elten-safety-shoes-austrian-darts-open-mensur-suljovic-sorgt-fuer-ekstase-in-graz/ |website=PDC Europe |access-date=8 May 2026 |language=de}}</ref> [[Alan Soutar]] beat five-time world champion [[Raymond van Barneveld]] 6–2, while [[Ian White (darts player)|Ian White]] defeated the [[2023 PDC World Darts Championship|2023]] world champion [[Michael Smith (darts player)|Michael Smith]] 6–4. After winning his first ranking title at [[2026 PDC Players Championship series#Players Championship 13|Players Championship 13]] earlier in the week, [[Kevin Doets]] posted a [[three-dart average]] of 99.35 in his 6–3 victory over [[Chris Landman]].<ref name="Sky_D1">{{cite web |date=8 May 2026 |title=Austrian Darts Open: Mensur Suljovic into second round as Michael Smith and Raymond van Barneveld suffer early exits |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13541758/austrian-darts-open-mensur-suljovic-into-second-round-as-michael-smith-and-raymond-van-barneveld-suffer-early-exits |website=Sky Sports |access-date=8 May 2026}}</ref>

Despite an average of 100.19, the only [[Glossary of darts#Ton|ton]]-plus average of the first round, [[Dirk van Duijvenbode]] lost 6–4 to reserve player [[Madars Razma]].<ref name="EU_D1"/> [[Niko Springer]] landed a 167 [[Glossary of darts#Checkout|checkout]], one of his three ton-plus finishes, during his 6–3 victory against [[Nick Kenny (darts player)|Nick Kenny]]. [[Kim Huybrechts]] helped his chances of qualifying for the [[2026 World Matchplay|World Matchplay]] by defeating [[Karel Sedláček]] 6–3. [[Niels Zonneveld]] triumphed in a [[Glossary of darts#Decider|deciding leg]] against [[James Hurrell]], sealing victory with a 135 checkout. [[Robert Owen (darts player)|Robert Owen]] also won a deciding leg, marking his first European Tour appearance of 2026 with a victory over [[Joe Cullen (darts player)|Joe Cullen]]. Hungarian qualifier [[Patrik Kovács (darts player)|Patrik Kovács]] defeated [[Ricardo Pietreczko]] 6–4, while Spain's [[Cristo Reyes]] earned a 6–3 win against [[Dave Chisnall]]. [[Ryan Joyce]] and [[William O'Connor (darts player)|William O'Connor]] were 6–3 winners against [[Gabriel Clemens]] and Anton Östlund, respectively.<ref name="PDC_D1"/><ref name="Sky_D1"/>

=== Second round ===
[[File:2026-03-26 Premier League Darts – Night 8 – Berlin 2026 by Sandro Halank–099.jpg|thumb|Top seed [[Gian van Veen]] ''(pictured in 2026)'' suffered a third consecutive opening-match defeat on the European Tour, losing 6–5 to [[Madars Razma]].]]
The second round (best of 11 legs) was played on 9 May.<ref name="PDC_D2">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=8 May 2026 |title=Nine-dart Reyes raises the roof at Austrian Darts Open |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/may/09/nine-dart-reyes-raises-roof-austrian-darts-open/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=9 May 2026}}</ref> [[Cristo Reyes]] hit his first [[nine-dart finish]] on stage in the fourth leg of his match against twelfth seed [[Damon Heta]]. Reyes went on to win the match 6–4, finishing with an average of 101.89 and six [[Glossary of darts#Maximum|maximums]]. "When I hit the nine-darter I got more nervous, but this is amazing. I want to say thank you to this fantastic crowd," commented Reyes afterwards.<ref name="Sky_D2">{{cite web |date=9 May 2026 |title=Austrian Darts Open: Gian van Veen suffers shock exit as Michael van Gerwen progresses and Cristo Reyes fires nine-darter |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13542155/austrian-darts-open-gian-van-veen-suffers-shock-exit-as-michael-van-gerwen-progresses-and-cristo-reyes-fires-nine-darter |website=Sky Sports |access-date=9 May 2026}}</ref><ref name="DNews_D2">{{cite web |last=Thomson |first=Milly |date=9 May 2026 |title=Kim Huybrechts hits remarkable 132 to down Dobey, Ross Smith impresses as Cristo Reyes hits perfection |url=https://dartsnews.com/pdc/austrian-darts-open-saturday-afternoon-round-up-kim-huybrechts-hits-remarkable-132-to-down-dobey-ross-smith-impresses-as-cristo-reyes-hits-perfection |website=dartsnews.com |access-date=9 May 2026}}</ref> Top seed [[Gian van Veen]] was eliminated in his opening match for the third straight European Tour event, losing a deciding leg to Madars Razma.<ref name="DNews_D2"/> Four-time Austrian Darts Open champion [[Michael van Gerwen]] averaged 103.41 in a 6–3 win over Krzysztof Ratajski; Van Gerwen progressed to a third-round meeting with the [[2018 PDC World Darts Championship|2018]] world champion [[Rob Cross]], who defeated William O'Connor by the same scoreline. Defending champion [[Martin Schindler]] won five legs in a row to beat Peter Wright 6–2, while his German compatriot Niko Springer [[Whitewash (sport)|whitewashed]] thirteenth seed [[Wessel Nijman]] 6–0.<ref name="Sky_D2"/>

Alan Soutar missed three match darts to beat fifth seed [[Danny Noppert]], who prevailed in a deciding leg. Kim Huybrechts was also a 6–5 winner, landing a 132 checkout on the [[Bullseye (target)|bullseye]] to defeat seventh seed [[Chris Dobey]]. [[James Wade]] eliminated the final Austrian player left as he averaged 103.20 to beat Mensur Suljović 6–2, while Ryan Joyce inflicted a sixth consecutive European Tour defeat on eleventh seed [[Mike De Decker]], winning 6–3. Northern Ireland's [[Josh Rock]] and [[Daryl Gurney]] earned 6–3 victories against Patrik Kovács and Ian White, respectively. Kevin Doets triumphed 6–2 to achieve his first career win against sixth seed [[Ryan Searle (darts player)|Ryan Searle]]. [[Ross Smith (darts player)|Ross Smith]], [[Luke Woodhouse]], and Andrew Gilding recorded ton-plus averages in their respective victories over Niels Zonneveld, Robert Owen, and tenth seed [[Jermaine Wattimena]].<ref name="PDC_D2"/><ref name="Sky_D2"/><ref name="DNews_D2"/>

=== Final day ===
[[File:Team NI Darts WC 2025.png|thumb|Northern Irish [[PDC World Cup of Darts|World Cup]] teammates [[Daryl Gurney]] ''(left)'' and [[Josh Rock]] ''(right)'' faced each other in the semi-finals. Rock won 7–3, later defeating [[Kevin Doets]] 8–6 in the final to win his second European Tour title.]]
The third round, quarter-finals, semi-finals and final were played on 10 May 2026. The third round and quarter-finals were contested over the best of 11 legs, the semi-finals over the best of 13 legs, and the final over the best of 15 legs.<ref name="PDC_D3">{{cite web |last=Simpson |first=Will |date=10 May 2026 |title=Rampant Rock downs Doets to scoop Austrian Darts Open |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/may/10/Rampant-Rock-downs-Doets-to-scoop-Austrian-Darts-Open/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=10 May 2026}}</ref> The final day saw Josh Rock and Kevin Doets reach the final. In the third round, Rock's match with Niko Springer went to a deciding leg, where both players [[Glossary of darts#Bust|bust]] their score by hitting the wrong double. Rock finally hit double 8 to prevail 6–5, having survived three missed match darts from Springer.<ref>{{cite web |last=Vehren |first=Johannes |date=10 May 2026 |title=Matchdart-Drama um Springer |trans-title=Match dart drama for Springer |url=https://www.sport1.de/news/darts-sport/2026/05/darts-matchdart-drama-um-springer-schindlers-traum-lebt |website=Sport1 |access-date=10 May 2026 |language=de}}</ref> Rock won his quarter-final 6–4 against Cristo Reyes, who hit a [[Glossary of darts#Maximum check|170 checkout]], then defeated his [[PDC World Cup of Darts|World Cup]] teammate Daryl Gurney 7–3 in the semi-finals.<ref name="PDC_D3"/> Following a 6–2 victory over Ryan Joyce, Doets averaged 112.31 and hit a 170 checkout on his way to beating Luke Woodhouse 6–3. He sealed a 7–3 semi-final win against Rob Cross with a 124 finish.<ref name="Sky_D3">{{cite web |date=10 May 2026 |title=Austrian Darts Open: Josh Rock beats Kevin Doets 8-6 to claim second PDC European Tour title and traditional green jacket |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12040/13542533/austrian-darts-open-josh-rock-beats-kevin-doets-8-6-to-claim-second-pdc-european-tour-title-and-traditional-green-jacket |website=Sky Sports |access-date=10 May 2026}}</ref> Woodhouse also landed a 170 checkout during his victory against James Wade in the third round.<ref>{{cite web |last=Gill |first=Samuel |date=10 May 2026 |title=Van Gerwen's poor run continues as Rock, Schindler, Reyes reach final session |url=https://dartsnews.com/pdc/austrian-darts-open-sunday-afternoon-round-up-van-gerwens-poor-run-continues-as-rock-schindler-reyes-reach-final-session |website=dartsnews.com |access-date=11 May 2026}}</ref> Rock was looking to win the Austrian Darts Open for the first time after finishing as runner-up to [[Jonny Clayton]] at the [[2023 Austrian Darts Open|2023 edition]], while Doets contested his maiden European Tour final.<ref name="EU_D3">{{cite web |date=10 May 2026 |title=ELTEN Safety Shoes Austrian Darts Open: Josh Rock erstmals Champion in Österreich |trans-title=ELTEN Safety Shoes Austrian Darts Open: Josh Rock first-time champion in Austria |url=https://www.pdc-europe.tv/de/aktuell/elten-safety-shoes-austrian-darts-open-josh-rock-erstmals-champion-in-oesterreich/ |website=PDC Europe |access-date=11 May 2026 |language=de}}</ref>

The final began with four consecutive [[Glossary of darts#Break|breaks of throw]], halted by Doets achieving the match's first [[Glossary of darts#Hold|hold of throw]] in 11 darts to lead 3–2.<ref name="PDC_D3"/> Rock landed the tournament's fourth 170 checkout for another break to go 4–3 ahead.<ref name="Sky_D3"/> After Rock missed a dart at single 20 to leave a shot at double 20, Doets converted a 90 finish on double 5 to level the match at 6–6.<ref name="DNews_D3Final">{{cite web |last=Michael |first=Lucas |date=10 May 2026 |title=Josh Rock claims second Euro Tour title after defeating in-form Kevin Doets in Graz |url=https://dartsnews.com/pdc/austrian-darts-open-sunday-evening-round-up-josh-rock-claims-second-euro-tour-title-after-defeating-in-form-kevin-doets-in-graz |website=dartsnews.com |access-date=11 May 2026}}</ref> Rock then capitalised on Doets missing an 80 checkout to lead as he found another break of throw, before a 60 finish completed an 8–6 win for the Northern Irishman.<ref name="Sky_D3"/> Both players finished the match with an average over 101.<ref name="PDC_D3"/>

Rock won his second European Tour title after his first triumph at the [[2024 Dutch Darts Championship]], becoming the sixth different winner in the first six European Tour events of the year.<ref name="EU_D3"/> He was awarded the customary green jacket, traditionally given to the tournament's champion.<ref name="Sky_D3"/> The title win also meant that Rock rose to the top of the 2026 European Tour rankings.<ref name="DNews_D3Final"/> "I made my first final against Jonny [Clayton] here and when he got the jacket I was kind of jealous. Martin Schindler beat me last year in the semi and won it, so I was determined to win it this year. I'm happy I've finally got it," said Rock in his post-match interview. Calling back to golfer [[Rory McIlroy]]'s win at the [[2026 Masters Tournament]], he added: "I'm just happy because two Northern Irishmen got a green jacket this year."<ref name="Sky_D3"/> Doets, who rose to number 33 in the [[PDC World Rankings]], believed that Rock was the deserved winner. He explained his future ambitions by stating: "I want to be a regular in the [[Premier League Darts|Premier League]] and become a top eight, top four player in the world, or world champion one day—that's everyone's goal. We keep fighting for it."<ref name="PDC_D3"/>
