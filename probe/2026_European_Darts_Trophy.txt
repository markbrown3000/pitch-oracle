The '''2026 European Darts Trophy''' (known for sponsorship reasons as the '''2026 Elten Safety Shoes European Darts Trophy''') was a professional [[darts]] tournament that took place at the {{Interlanguage link|Lokhalle|de|Lokhalle Göttingen}} in [[Göttingen]], Germany, from 13 to 15 March 2026.<ref>{{cite web |title=Elten Safety Shoes European Darts Trophy 2026 |url=https://www.pdc-europe.tv/tournaments/et-2026-en/european-darts-trophy-2026/ |website=PDC Europe |access-date=6 February 2026}}</ref> It was the second of fifteen [[PDC European Tour]] events on the [[2026 PDC Pro Tour]].<ref>{{cite web |date=12 October 2025 |title=Darts 2026 schedule and dates: Premier League Darts, World Matchplay and more with Luke Littler in action |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12040/13428768/darts-2026-schedule-and-dates-premier-league-darts-world-matchplay-and-more-with-luke-littler-in-action |website=Sky Sports |access-date=6 February 2026}}</ref> It featured a field of 48 players and £230,000 in prize money, with £35,000 going to the winner.

[[Nathan Aspinall]] won the [[2025 European Darts Trophy|2025 edition]], having defeated [[Ryan Joyce]] 8–4 in the final.<ref>{{cite web |date=23 March 2025 |title=Aspinall wins first European Tour title |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/darts/articles/c7780k0rgngo |website=BBC Sport |access-date=12 May 2025}}</ref> However, Aspinall did not enter the tournament, and therefore did not defend his title.

[[Wessel Nijman]] won his first European Tour title, defeating [[Gerwyn Price]] 8–3 in final.

==Prize money==
As part of a mass boost in prize money for [[Professional Darts Corporation]] (PDC) events in 2026, the prize fund for all [[2026 PDC Pro Tour#European Tour#European Tour|2026 European Tour]] events rose to £230,000, of which the winner received £35,000.<ref>{{cite web |last=Phillips |first=Josh |date=31 March 2025 |title=Biggest prize money increase in PDC history confirmed |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/march/31/biggest-prize-money-increase-pdc-history-confirmed |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=6 February 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=European Darts Trophy 2026 |url=https://mastercaller.com/tournaments/european-darts-trophy/2026 |website=Mastercaller |access-date=6 February 2026}}</ref>

==Qualification and format==
The top 16 players on the two-year [[PDC Order of Merit]] were [[Seeding (sports)|seeded]] and entered the tournament in the second round, while the next 16 highest-ranked players from the one-year [[PDC Pro Tour Order of Merit]] automatically qualified for the first round. World number one and back-to-back world champion [[Luke Littler]] did not participate, having not entered a European Tour event in Germany since the [[2025 German Darts Grand Prix|German Darts Grand Prix]] in April 2025. The seedings were confirmed on 5 February.<ref name="Entries">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=5 February 2026 |title=2026 ET1-2 Seeds & Tour Card Holder qualifier entries confirmed |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/february/05/2026-et1-2-seeds---tour-card-holder-qualifier-entries-confirmed |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=6 February 2026}}</ref><ref name="Race">{{cite web |title=Race to ET1 and ET2 2026 |url=https://www.dartsrankings.com/et2 |website=dartsrankings.com |access-date=6 February 2026}}</ref> The remaining 16 places went to players from four qualifying events – 10 from the Tour Card Holder Qualifier (held on 11 February),<ref>{{cite web |date=11 February 2026 |title=PDC ET02 Tour Card Holder Qualifier |url=https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdceutcq26et02/matches |website=DartConnect |access-date=6 February 2026}}</ref> four from the Host Nation Qualifier (held on 28 February),<ref name="HNQ">{{cite web |date=28 February 2026 |title=PDC ET02 Host Nation Qualifier |url=https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdc26et02hnq/matches |website=DartConnect |access-date=6 February 2026}}</ref> one from the Nordic & Baltic Associate Member Qualifier (held on 6 February),<ref>{{cite web |date=6 February 2026 |title=PDCNB 2026 Tour - Latvia - Weekend 01 |url=https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdcnbpro26e01/matches |website=DartConnect |access-date=6 February 2026}}</ref> and one from the East European Associate Member Qualifier (held on 24 January).<ref>{{cite web |date=24 January 2026 |title=PDC ET02 E. Europe Qualifier |url=https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdceeu26et02q/matches |website=DartConnect |access-date=6 February 2026}}</ref>

[[Gary Anderson (darts player)|Gary Anderson]], [[James Wade]] and [[Nathan Aspinall]] withdrew and were replaced by [[Mensur Suljović]], [[Keane Barry]] and [[Lukas Wenig]]. [[Jermaine Wattimena]], [[Mike De Decker]] and [[Rob Cross]] moved up to become the 14th, 15th and 16th seeds respectively.<ref name="Draw">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=12 March 2026 |title=2026 Elten Safety Shoes European Darts Trophy draw & schedule |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/march/12/2026-elten-safety-shoes-european-darts-trophy-draw---schedule/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=12 March 2026}}</ref> After the draw was made, [[Luke Humphries]] and [[Jonny Clayton]] withdrew from the tournament. [[Karel Sedláček]] and [[Cam Crabtree]] replaced them from the reserve list, going straight in at the second-round stage.<ref>{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=12 March 2026 |title=Sedlacek and Crabtree replace Humphries & Clayton in Goettingen |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/march/12/sedlacek-replaces-humphries-in-elten-safety-shoes-european-darts-trophy/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=13 March 2026}}</ref>

==Summary==
===First round===
[[File:Kevin Doets Darts Actueel 2024.jpg|thumb|[[Kevin Doets]] ''(pictured in 2024)'' defeated Jonas Masalin 6–0 with a [[three-dart average]] of 117.12, the highest in tournament history and the joint-fifth highest in European Tour history.]]
The first round (best of 11 [[Leg (darts)|legs]]) was played on 13 March.<ref name="PDCDay1">{{cite web |last=Simpson |first=Will |date=13 March 2026 |title=Doets shatters record average at European Darts Trophy |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/march/13/Doets-shatters-record-average-at-European-Darts-Trophy/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=14 March 2026}}</ref> [[Kevin Doets]] recorded a career-high [[three-dart average]] of 117.12 in a [[Whitewash (sport)|whitewash]] win over Finnish debutant Jonas Masalin. Surpassing [[Michael van Gerwen]]'s 115.13 mark in [[2016 European Darts Trophy|2016]] as the highest average in the tournament's history, it was also the joint-fifth highest average in European Tour history.<ref name="PDCEurope">{{cite web |date=13 March 2026 |title=European Darts Trophy: Kevin Doets bricht MvG-Rekord |url=https://www.pdc-europe.tv/de/aktuell/european-darts-trophy-kevin-doets-bricht-mvg-rekord/ |website=PDC Europe |access-date=14 March 2026 |language=German}}</ref> Speaking after the match, Doets stated that he "started to feel comfortable" after Masalin missed darts to level the match at 1–1. "I’m in a really good place right now, I’m very confident, and I think I’m going to win tomorrow as well," he added.<ref name="PDCDay1"/> Eight-time European Tour winner [[Dave Chisnall]] trailed [[Jeffrey de Zwaan]] 5–0 but won the next six legs to complete a 6–5 comeback victory, surviving three missed [[Glossary of darts#Match dart|match darts]] from De Zwaan. "It was a weird game," said Chisnall afterwards. "That last leg was a bit weird, we both didn’t score well. But I took the finish, and I’m happy."<ref name="PDCDay1"/> Host nation qualifier [[Paul Krohne]] earned his first win on the European Tour by defeating [[Cameron Menzies]] 6–4. The other host nation qualifiers were unsuccessful: [[Dragutin Horvat]] and Florian Preis were whitewashed by [[Luke Woodhouse]] and [[Ricky Evans (darts player)|Ricky Evans]], respectively, while [[Florian Hempel]] lost 6–3 to [[Richard Veenstra]].<ref name="PDCEurope"/> [[Ryan Joyce]], the runner-up [[2025 European Darts Trophy|the previous year]], lost 6–3 to [[Lukas Wenig]].<ref>{{cite web |last=Michael |first=Lucas |date=13 March 2026 |title=European Darts Trophy Friday Afternoon Round-up - Gilding, Menzies and Joyce suffer early exits, Ratajski and Evans secure safe passageway while Doets averages over 117 |url=https://dartsnews.com/pdc/live-european-darts-trophy-friday-afternoon-gilding-joyce-menzies-headline-first-round-action-in-gottingen |website=dartsnews.com |access-date=14 March 2026}}</ref>

[[Wessel Nijman]] overturned a 3–0 deficit to defeat [[Cristo Reyes]] in a [[Glossary of darts#Decider|deciding leg]].<ref>{{cite web |last=Thomson |first=Milly |date=13 March 2026 |title=European Darts Trophy Friday Evening Round-up - Chisnall completes stunning comeback against De Zwaan; Nijman edges past Reyes despite poor finishing |url=https://dartsnews.com/pdc/live-european-darts-trophy-friday-evening-chisnall-cullen-gurney-nijman-reyes-among-the-names-in-action |website=dartsnews.com |access-date=14 March 2026}}</ref> In another last-leg decider, [[Niko Springer]] landed [[Glossary of darts#Checkout|checkouts]] of 142 and 124 as he beat German compatriot [[Kai Gotthardt]], avenging his loss to Gotthardt in the third round of the [[2026 UK Open|UK Open]].<ref name="PDCEurope"/> [[Joe Cullen (darts player)|Joe Cullen]] earned a 6–3 victory against Polish debutant Wojciech Bruliński after initially going 2–0 behind. [[Shane McGuirk]], another European Tour debutant, lost 6–4 to [[Krzysztof Ratajski]]. [[Dirk van Duijvenbode]] and [[Mensur Suljović]] were 6–2 winners over [[Mario Vandenbogaerde]] and [[William O'Connor (darts player)|William O'Connor]], respectively. [[Niels Zonneveld]] set up a meeting with three-time European Darts Trophy champion Van Gerwen in the second round by defeating [[Keane Barry]] 6–1.<ref name="PDCDay1"/><ref name="PDCEurope"/>

===Second round===
[[File:Niels Zonneveld.png|thumb|upright=0.8|[[Niels Zonneveld]] ''(pictured in 2021)'' eliminated three-time champion [[Michael van Gerwen]] in the second round.]]
The second round (best of 11 legs) was played on 14 March.<ref name="PDCDay2">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=14 March 2026 |title=Zonneveld stuns Van Gerwen at European Darts Trophy |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/march/14/zonneveld-stuns-van-gerwen-european-darts-trophy/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=15 March 2026}}</ref> Despite third seed Michael van Gerwen averaging 107, Niels Zonneveld eliminated the three-time champion in a 6–2 victory, averaging 104.46 himself. [[Gerwyn Price]] began his campaign by defeating Joe Cullen 6–2 with an average of 106.70. "If I can build on that and get better, I think this tournament is mine," said Price afterwards.<ref name="SkyDay2">{{cite web |date=14 March 2026 |title=European Darts Trophy: Michael van Gerwen suffers shock exit Niels Zonneveld despite the three-time world champion averaging 107 |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13519813/european-darts-trophy-michael-van-gerwen-suffers-shock-exit-niels-zonneveld-despite-the-three-time-world-champion-averaging-107 |website=Sky Sports |access-date=15 March 2026}}</ref> Niko Springer achieved another deciding-leg win, beating fifth seed [[Stephen Bunting]] 6–5 in a match marred by whistling from the [[Göttingen]] crowd, which resulted in referee [[Huw Ware]] warning the crowd about their behaviour.<ref>{{cite web |last=Rivers |first=David |date=15 March 2026 |title=Stephen Bunting left 'really hurt' as fan whistling 'spoils' match with Niko Springer at European Darts Trophy in Germany |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13520156/stephen-bunting-left-really-hurt-as-fan-whistling-spoils-match-with-niko-springer-at-european-darts-trophy-in-germany |website=Sky Sports |access-date=15 March 2026}}</ref> Springer was the only German player to reach the third round, following the eliminations of Paul Krohne, Lukas Wenig, and eleventh seed [[Martin Schindler]].<ref>{{cite web |last=Lüers |first=Jakob |date=14 March 2026 |title=Springer rettet deutsche Ehre |trans-title=Springer saves German honour |url=https://www.sport1.de/news/darts-sport/2026/03/european-darts-trophy-schindler-kassiert-bittere-niederlage |website=Sport1 |access-date=15 March 2026 |language=de}}</ref> [[Karel Sedláček]] and [[Cam Crabtree]] entered the second round as replacements for [[Luke Humphries]] and [[Jonny Clayton]]. Crabtree, who flew from [[Washington D.C.]] to participate, was beaten 6–4 by Kevin Doets, while Sedláček lost to Mensur Suljović by the same scoreline.<ref name="PDCDay2"/>

Fifteenth seed [[Mike De Decker]] and sixteenth seed [[Rob Cross]] were beaten by Wessel Nijman and Dirk van Duijvenbode, respectively. The [[2025 European Championship (darts)|2025 European Championship]] winner [[Gian van Veen]] defeated Krzysztof Ratajski 6–1, while Dutch compatriot [[Danny Noppert]] beat Ricky Evans by the same scoreline. [[Damon Heta]] averaged 101.42 as he defeated [[Darius Labanauskas]] 6–3. [[Ross Smith (darts player)|Ross Smith]] beat Dave Chisnall 6–4, [[Chris Dobey]] defeated Luke Woodhouse 6–1, and [[Ryan Searle (darts player)|Ryan Searle]] earned a 6–3 victory over [[Daryl Gurney]].<ref name="SkyDay2"/> A total of eight Dutch players reached the last 16.<ref name="PDCDay2"/>

===Final day===
[[File:Wessel Nijman 2025 World Matchplay.png|thumb|upright=0.8|[[Wessel Nijman]] ''(pictured in 2025)'' won his maiden European Tour title in his first final.]]
The third round, quarter-finals, semi-finals and final were played on 15 March. The third round and quarter-finals were contested over the best of 11 legs, the semi-finals over the best of 13 legs, and the final over the best of 15 legs.<ref name="PDCFinal">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=15 March 2026 |title=Inspired Nijman powers past Price to win European Darts Trophy |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/march/15/inspired-Nijman-powers-Price-European-Darts-Trophy/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=16 March 2026}}</ref> The final day saw Gerwyn Price and Wessel Nijman reach the final. Danny Noppert missed a match dart to eliminate Price in the third round, allowing the Welshman to take a 6–5 victory. He then defeated Dirk van Duijvenbode 6–3 and Damon Heta 7–5 to secure his place in the final. Nijman achieved his highest three-dart average on stage in his third-round meeting with Gian van Veen, averaging 112.87 on his way to a 6–1 win. He followed that with a 106.05 average to defeat Josh Rock 6–2, missing [[Glossary of darts#Double|double]] 12 for a nine-dart finish in the third leg of the match, and proceeded to average 110.41 in his 7–1 victory over Niels Zonneveld in the semi-finals. Elsewhere, Richard Veenstra reached his first European Tour quarter-final, losing 6–2 to Zonneveld.<ref name="PDCFinal"/><ref>{{cite web |last=Michael |first=Lucas |date=15 March 2026 |title=European Darts Trophy Sunday Afternoon Round-up - Price, Rock, Springer advance in last leg deciders while Nijman averages over 112 enroute to defeating van Veen |url=https://dartsnews.com/pdc/live-european-darts-trophy-sunday-afternoon-van-veen-price-noppert-rock-dobey-battling-for-quarterfinal-spots |website=dartsnews.com |access-date=16 March 2026}}</ref><ref name="DNFinal">{{cite web |last=Wood |first=Kieran |date=15 March 2026 |title=European Darts Trophy 2026 Roundup - Wessel Nijman storms past Gerwyn Price to seal first Euro Tour title in Göttingen |url=https://dartsnews.com/pdc/european-darts-trophy-2026-roundup-wessel-nijman-storms-past-gerwyn-price-to-seal-first-euro-tour-title-in-gottingen |website=dartsnews.com |access-date=16 March 2026}}</ref> In the final, Price looked to become the second player to win ten European Tour titles, after Michael van Gerwen, while Nijman was pursuing his maiden European Tour title in his first final.<ref name="SkyFinal">{{cite web |last=Rivers |first=David |date=15 March 2026 |title=European Darts Trophy: Wessel Nijman beats Gerwyn Price with brilliant 8-3 win in Germany and says he has 'no words' |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12040/13520127/european-darts-trophy-wessel-nijman-beats-gerwyn-price-with-brilliant-8-3-win-in-germany-and-says-he-has-no-words |website=Sky Sports |access-date=16 March 2026}}</ref><ref name="Telegraaf">{{cite web |last=Veltman |first=Pascal |date=15 March 2026 |title=Ontketende Wessel Nijman vernedert Gerwyn Price en wint eerste Euro Tour in zijn carrière: 'Geen woorden voor' |trans-title=Unleashed Wessel Nijman humiliates Gerwyn Price and wins first Euro Tour of his career: 'No words for it' |url=https://www.telegraaf.nl/sport/darts/ontketende-wessel-nijman-vernedert-gerwyn-price-en-wint-eerste-euro-tour-in-zijn-carriere-geen-woorden-voor/142133568.html |website=De Telegraaf |access-date=16 March 2026 |language=nl}}</ref> The pair had met in the [[2026 PDC Players Championship series#Players Championship 2|second Players Championship final]] of 2026, where Nijman won 8–7.<ref>{{cite web |date=18 February 2026 |title=Darts results: Wessel Nijman wins second Players Championship event of the season |url=https://www.sportinglife.com/darts/news/darts-results-wessel-nijman-wins-second-players-championship-event-of-the-season/230455 |website=Sporting Life |access-date=16 March 2026}}</ref>

Nijman opened the final with a 116 checkout to win the first leg, soon taking a 3–0 lead. At 3–1, Price missed double 18 for a 156 checkout to find a [[Glossary of darts#Break|break of throw]], which Nijman capitalised on to extend his advantage to 4–1.<ref name="DNFinal"/> He then converted checkouts of 97 and 145 to lead 6–1. Following Price's second leg of the match, Nijman completed the next leg in 11 darts to move one away from victory. He secured an 8–3 win with a 150 finish on double 18, finishing the match with a three-dart average of 100.80, hitting 73 per cent of his attempts at double.<ref name="PDCFinal"/>

Nijman won the European Darts Trophy, the first European Tour title of his career.<ref name="Telegraaf"/> His 100.80 average in the final completed a run of five consecutive averages above the 100 mark. Winning the £35,000 top prize, he entered the world's top 20 on the [[PDC Order of Merit]].<ref name="SkyFinal"/> "I think there are no words to describe how I'm feeling right now," commented Nijman after the victory. He added that he "needed to prove to other people that [he] can play like this on the big stage", and said that winning the title was a "big step" towards his goal of reaching the world's top 16, ensuring his seeding status at major tournaments.<ref name="SkyFinal"/> Speaking in defeat, Price believed he "ran out of gas in the end". He concluded: "Every time I play against Wessel, he plays brilliantly against me. He's been brilliant all year, he played fantastic darts today, but I will get my own back one day."<ref name="PDCFinal"/>
