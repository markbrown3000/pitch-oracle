The '''2025 English Open''' (officially the '''2025 BetVictor English Open''')<ref name="snookerorg"/> was a professional [[snooker]] tournament that took place from 11 to 21 September 2025 at the Brentwood Centre in [[Brentwood, Essex|Brentwood]], England. The 10th consecutive edition of the tournament since it was first staged in [[2016 English Open (snooker)|2016]], it was the fourth [[List of snooker ranking tournaments|ranking event]] of the [[2025–26 snooker season]], following the [[2025 Wuhan Open (snooker)|2025 Wuhan Open]] and preceding the [[2025 British Open]]. It was the first of four tournaments in the season's [[Home Nations Series]], preceding the [[2025 Northern Ireland Open]], the [[2025 Scottish Open]], and the [[2026 Welsh Open (snooker)|2026 Welsh Open]]. The tournament was broadcast by [[TNT Sports]] in the United Kingdom and Ireland, by [[Eurosport]] in mainland Europe, by local channels in China and elsewhere in Asia, and by [[WST Play]] in all other territories. The winner received £100,000 from a total prize fund of £550,400.

[[Neil Robertson]] was the defending champion, having defeated [[Wu Yize]] 9–7 in the [[2024 English Open (snooker)|2024 final]], but he lost 1–4 to [[Jackson Page]] in the last 32. The final was contested between [[Mark Allen (snooker player)|Mark Allen]] and [[Zhou Yuelong]]. Allen led 6–2 after the first session, but Zhou recovered to lead 8–7 in the second session. Allen won the last two frames of the match for a 9–8 victory, securing his first English Open title, the 12th ranking title of his career, and his first ranking win since the [[2024 Players Championship (snooker)|2024 Players Championship]] in February 2024. He won five consecutive matches at the event in {{Cuegloss|deciding frame|deciding frames}}. Following the [[2025 Saudi Arabia Snooker Masters]] and the 2025 Wuhan Open, it was the first time that three consecutive ranking finals had gone to deciding frames.

The event produced 82 [[Century break|century breaks]], including two [[maximum break]]s. [[Aaron Hill (snooker player)|Aaron Hill]] made the first maximum of his career during his second-round match against [[Yao Pengcheng]], and [[Ali Carter]] made the fourth maximum of his career in his fourth-round match against Hill. These were respectively the ninth and tenth maximums of the season and the 226th and 227th official maximums in professional snooker history. [[Judd Trump]] made the 1,100th [[century break]] of his career during his third-round match against [[Michael Holt (snooker player)|Michael Holt]]. [[Ng On-yee]] made a 137 break in her first-round match against [[Liam Highfield]], then a record for the highest break by a woman in professional competition.

===Format===
The tournament used a tiered format first implemented for the [[Home Nations Series]] in the [[2024–25 snooker season]]. In round one, players seeded 65–96 faced those [[seeding (sports)|seeded]] 97 and below, including selected amateurs. In round two, the winners from round one faced players seeded 33–64. In round three, the winners from round two faced the top 32 seeds.<ref>{{cite web |date=5 April 2024 |title=Tiered format for Home Nations and German Masters in 2024/25 |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2024/april/05/tiered-format-for-home-nations-and-german-masters-in-2024-25/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240405185554/https://www.wst.tv/news/2024/april/05/tiered-format-for-home-nations-and-german-masters-in-2024-25/ |archive-date=5 April 2024 |access-date=5 April 2024 |work=[[World Snooker Tour]]}}</ref> All matches were played as the best of 7 {{cuegloss|frame|frames}} until the quarter{{nbh}}finals, which were the best of 9. The semi{{nbh}}finals were the best of 11, and the final was a best{{nbh}}of{{nbh}}17-frame match played over two {{cuegloss|session|sessions}}.<ref name="snookerorg" />

In the second semi-final, Allen faced Jones, who made breaks of 92, 84, and 61 as he took a 3–1 lead. After the mid-session interval, Allen produced breaks of 102 and 62 to tie the scores at 3–3, but Jones won the next two frames to move 5–3 ahead. However, Allen made breaks of 68, 67, and 82 to secure a 6–5 victory.<ref name=":17" /><ref>{{Cite web |date=20 September 2025 |title=2025 English Open: Mark Allen v Jak Jones |url=https://www.wst.tv/match-centre/57edc32b-9d95-4568-9bba-defb0b477de6 |access-date=20 September 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260421082950/https://www.wst.tv/match-centre/57edc32b-9d95-4568-9bba-defb0b477de6|archive-date=21 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref> It was Allen's third consecutive comeback win at the event, having beaten Ding from 0–3 behind in the fifth round and Slessor from 0–4 behind in the quarter-finals. It was also his second consecutive match that ended after midnight. "There's a few events in my career I still want to win and the English Open is one of them," Allen said afterwards. "The [[Welsh Open (snooker)|Welsh Open]] is another and obviously the [[World Snooker Championship|World Championship]]. I'd love to win tomorrow and make it three out of the four [[Home Nations Series|Home Nations]] events."<ref name=":18">{{Cite web |date=20 September 2025 |title=Pistol fights back to make Brentwood final |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/september/20/pistol-fights-back-to-make-brentwood-final/ |access-date=21 September 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260421081518/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/september/20/pistol-fights-back-to-make-brentwood-final/|archive-date=21 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

=== Final ===
[[File:Mark Allen with English Open trophy 2025.jpg|thumb|upright=0.8|[[Mark Allen (snooker player)|Mark Allen]] ''(pictured at the event)'' defeated [[Zhou Yuelong]] to win his first [[English Open (snooker)|English Open]] title and 12th ranking title. He received the [[Steve Davis]] Trophy ''(also pictured)''.]]
The final was played on 21 September as the best of 17 frames, held over two {{cuegloss|session|sessions}}, between the world number 10 Allen and the world number 32 Zhou.<ref name=":4" /><ref name=":0" /><ref name="Allencharge">{{Cite web |date=21 September 2025 |title=Allen in charge of final |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/september/21/allen-in-charge-of-final/ |access-date=21 September 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260421081505/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/september/21/allen-in-charge-of-final/|archive-date=21 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref><ref name="Zhoudowns"/> Allen was playing in the 20th ranking final of his career, while Zhou was competing in his fourth. The two players had previously faced each other in a Home Nations Series final at the [[2022 Northern Ireland Open]], which Allen won 9–4.<ref name=":18" /> The match was refereed by Kevin Dabrowski.<ref name="snookerorg" /><ref>{{cite web |title=Polski sędzia Kevin Dabrowski cichym bohaterem finału English Open 2025. Został pochwalony przez Marka Allena |trans-title=Polish referee Kevin Dabrowski is the unsung hero of the 2025 English Open final. He was praised by Mark Allen. |url=https://eurosport.tvn24.pl/snooker/betvictor-english-open/2025-2026/polski-sedzia-kevin-dabrowski-cichym-bohaterem-finalu-english-open-2025.-zostal-pochwalony-przez-marka-allena_sto23224235/story.shtml |date=22 September 2025 |last=Przybyłowicz |first=Łukasz |language=Polish |website=[[Eurosport]]}}</ref> Allen won the opening frame with a 132 break. Zhou won the second, but Allen then made breaks including 72 and 59 as he won four consecutive frames to move 5–1 ahead. Zhou took the seventh, but Allen won frame eight to end the session with a 6–2 lead.<ref name="Allencharge"/>

When play resumed for the second session, Zhou won the 45-minute ninth frame, but Allen took the 10th to lead 7–3. Zhou won the next two frames to trail by two at 7–5 at the mid-session interval. Allen made a break of 60 in frame 13 but missed a red, and Zhou made a 72 clearance to win the frame on the last black. Zhou then made a break of 60 to tie the scores at 7–7 and a 53 break to win a fifth consecutive frame and move 8–7 in front. In the 16th frame, Allen made a 71 break, leaving Zhou {{Cuegloss|snookers required|requiring snookers}}, but went {{Cuegloss|in-off}}. Zhou got the required {{Cuegloss|penalty points}}, but Allen won the frame on the colours to tie the scores at 8–8. Allen then won the decider with a 61 break to secure a 9–8 victory, securing his first English Open title, the 12th ranking title of his professional career, and his first ranking win since February 2024.<ref name="Allen12th">{{Cite web |date=21 September 2025 |title=Allen captures 12th ranking crown |url=https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/september/21/allen-captures-12th-ranking-crown-1/ |access-date=22 September 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260421081551/https://www.wst.tv/news/2025/september/21/allen-captures-12th-ranking-crown-1/|archive-date=21 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref><ref name="AllenvZhou">{{Cite web |date=21 September 2025 |title=2025 English Open: Mark Allen v Zhou Yuelong |url=https://www.wst.tv/match-centre/b97b60fe-7bb9-42a3-8c9d-74893939fd97 |access-date=21 September 2025 |website=[[World Snooker Tour]] |language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260421081519/https://www.wst.tv/match-centre/b97b60fe-7bb9-42a3-8c9d-74893939fd97|archive-date=21 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref><ref name="Allensurvives">{{Cite web |date=2025-09-21 |title=Allen survives Zhou comeback to win English Open|url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cp8ww42jkpxo |access-date=21 April 2026|website=BBC Sport |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260421074704/https://www.bbc.com/sport/snooker/articles/cp8ww42jkpxo|archive-date=21 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

Allen won five consecutive matches at the event in deciding frames. Following the [[2025 Saudi Arabia Snooker Masters]] and the [[2025 Wuhan Open (snooker)|2025 Wuhan Open]], it was the first time that three consecutive ranking finals had gone to deciding frames. "I thought Zhou battled really hard in that second session. He played some good stuff," said Allen afterwards. "He put me under pressure. I missed a few balls and I was nervous. I missed a few that I shouldn't have but at 8–7 I suddenly switched on a bit. It's a good trait to have. I don't want to keep relying on it but I felt as good as any part of the match in those last two frames." Allen, who had not won a ranking title since the [[2024 Players Championship (snooker)|2024 Players Championship]] held the previous February, advanced from 10th to 7th in the world rankings after the tournament. Zhou's defeat meant that he had lost all four of the ranking finals he had contested, two of them to Allen. "Of course it will give me confidence," he said of his performance in the final. "[Allen] was very good in the first session. He was 6–2 up and I think I just wanted to give him some trouble this evening. I'm very happy I managed to get 8–7 up. It is a good experience and I will learn from it."<ref name="Allen12th"/><ref name="AllenvZhou"/><ref name="Allensurvives"/><ref>{{Cite news |last=Calladine |first=Rob |date=2024-02-26 |title=Mark Allen battles past Zhang Anda in tense final to win Players Championship |url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2024/feb/26/mark-allen-battles-past-zhang-anda-in-tense-final-to-win-players-championship |access-date=2025-09-18 |work=The Guardian |language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260421080724/https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2024/feb/26/mark-allen-battles-past-zhang-anda-in-tense-final-to-win-players-championship|archive-date=21 April 2026|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.totallysnookered.com/sport/snooker/tournaments/english-open/brentwood-comeback-king-mark-allen-wins-the-2025-english-open-in-historic-deciding-frame-5327495|title=Brentwood comeback king Mark Allen wins the 2025 English Open in historic deciding frame|first=Michael|last=Day|date=22 September 2025|work=Totally Snookered|access-date=19 June 2026|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260619053157/https://www.totallysnookered.com/sport/snooker/tournaments/english-open/brentwood-comeback-king-mark-allen-wins-the-2025-english-open-in-historic-deciding-frame-5327495|archive-date=19 June 2026|url-status=live}}</ref>
