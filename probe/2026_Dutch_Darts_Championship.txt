The '''2026 Dutch Darts Championship''' was a professional [[darts]] tournament that took place at the [[MECC Maastricht]] in [[Maastricht]], from 16 to 18 October 2026. It was the first time Maastricht has hosted the Dutch Darts Championship since 2018, and was the fifteenth and final [[PDC European Tour]] event on the [[2026 PDC Pro Tour]]. It featured a field of 48 players and £230,000 in prize money, with £35,000 going to the winner.

[[Jonny Clayton]] was the defending champion, having defeated [[Niko Springer]] 8–6 in the [[2025 Dutch Darts Championship|2025 final]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wellens |first=Megan |date=25 May 2025 |title=Dutch Darts Championship: Jonny Clayton beats Luke Humphries on his way to third Euro Tour title. |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13374440/dutch-darts-championship-jonny-clayton-beats-luke-humphries-on-way-to-third-euro-tour-title |url-status=live |website=Sky Sports}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-05-25 |title=Jonny Clayton wins in Netherlands to earn first European title in two years |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/darts/articles/cy5e0ed711ro |access-date=20 September 2026 |website=BBC Sport |language=en-GB}}</ref> However, he lost 6–1 to [[Damon Heta]] in the second round.

During the event, [[Gian van Veen]] broke the record for the highest ever [[three-dart average]] on the European Tour, previously held by [[Dave Chisnall]] at the [[2018 German Darts Championship]], averaging 120.24 in his 6–0 win over [[Sebastian Białecki]] in the second round.

[[Niels Zonneveld]] won his first European Tour title, defeating [[Danny Noppert]] 8–5 in the final.

== Qualification and format ==
The top 16 players on the two-year [[PDC Order of Merit]] were [[Seeding (sports)|seeded]] and entered the tournament in the second round, while the next 16 highest-ranked players from the one-year [[PDC Pro Tour Order of Merit]] automatically qualified for the first round. The seedings were confirmed on 17 September.<ref>{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=21 September 2026 |title=2026 ET15 Seeds & Tour Card Holder qualifier entries confirmed |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/september/21/2026-et15-seeds-tour-card-holder-qualifier-entries-confirmed/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=21 September 2026}}</ref> The remaining 16 places went to players from four qualifying events – 10 from the Tour Card Holder Qualifier (held on 24 September),<ref>{{cite web |date=24 September 2026 |title=PDC ET15 Tour Card Holder Qualifier |url=https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdceutcq26et15/matches |website=DartConnect |access-date=24 September 2026}}</ref> four from the Host Nation Qualifier (held on 15 October),<ref>{{cite web |date=15 October 2026 |title=PDC ET15 Host Nation Qualifier |url=https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdc26et15hnq/matches |website=DartConnect |access-date=21 September 2026}}</ref> one from the Nordic & Baltic Associate Member Qualifier (held on 8 August),<ref>{{cite web |date=8 August 2026 |title=PDCNB 2026 Tour - Norway - Weekend 05 |url=https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdcnbpro26e05/matches |website=DartConnect |access-date=8 August 2026}}</ref> and one from the East European Associate Member Qualifier (held on 9 August).<ref>{{cite web |date=9 August 2026 |title=PDC ET15 E. Europe Qualifier |url=https://tv.dartconnect.com/event/pdceeu26et15q/matches |website=DartConnect |access-date=21 September 2026}}</ref>

[[Luke Littler]], [[Josh Rock]] and [[Gary Anderson (darts player)|Gary Anderson]] didn't enter the event.

In addition, before the Tour Card Holder Qualifier, [[James Wade]] and [[Kevin Doets]] withdrew and were replaced by [[Karel Sedláček]] and [[Jeffrey de Graaf]]. [[Martin Schindler]] and [[Krzysztof Ratajski]]  moved up to become the 14th and 15th seeds respectively.

[[Luke Humphries]] and [[Joe Cullen (darts player)|Joe Cullen]] withdrew and were replaced by [[Mike De Decker]] and [[Daryl Gurney]]. After the draw was made, [[Rob Cross]] withdrew from the tournament. [[Brendan Dolan]] replaced him from the reserve list, going straight in at the second-round stage.
