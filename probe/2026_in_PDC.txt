The year '''2026''' is the 35th year in the history of the [[Professional Darts Corporation]] (PDC), a [[darts]] organisation based in the United Kingdom.

==PDC in 2026==
===Increases to prize money and tournament sizes===
On 31 March 2025, the PDC announced a mass increase in prize money for tournaments, starting from the [[2026 PDC World Darts Championship]], where the champion receives a doubled top prize of £1,000,000 from a total prize fund of £5,000,000. The total amount of prize money on offer in PDC events in 2026 went up £7,000,000 from the 2025 season, along with increases for the PDC's [[PDC secondary tours|secondary tours]] and Global Affiliate Tours, with only the World Series events and the World Masters not receiving increases in prize money.<ref>{{cite web |date=31 March 2025 |title=The million-pound dart: PDC doubles prize money for world championship |url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2025/mar/31/world-darts-championship-pdc-doubles-prize-money-million-pounds |website=The Guardian |access-date=3 July 2025 |agency=PA Media}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |date=31 March 2025 |title=World Darts Championship winner's prize doubled to one million pounds |url=https://www.reuters.com/sports/world-darts-championship-winners-prize-doubled-one-million-pounds-2025-03-31/ |website=Reuters |access-date=12 October 2025}}</ref><ref name="pdc_money_up">{{cite web |last1=Phillips |first1=Josh |title=Biggest prize money increase in PDC history confirmed |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/march/31/biggest-prize-money-increase-pdc-history-confirmed |website=PDC |access-date=4 January 2026 |language=en |date=31 March 2025}}</ref> The field for the World Championship also expanded from 96 to 128 players, while the [[Grand Slam of Darts]] will expand from 32 to 48 players.<ref name=GS25>{{cite web |last1=Gorton |first1=Josh |title=Five more qualifiers confirmed for 2025 Mr Vegas Grand Slam of Darts |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/october/13/five-more-qualifiers-confirmed-2025-mr-vegas-grand-slam-darts |website=PDC |access-date=2 January 2026 |language=en |date=13 October 2025}}</ref>

=== Changes to Q-School ===
On each day of the Final Stage of Q-School, a Tour Card is awarded to both finalists, rather than just to the day's tournament winner as in previous years. As before, remaining Tour Card places are awarded through the Q-School Order of Merit.<ref name="DNNewRules">{{Cite web |last=Gill |first=Samuel |date=2 January 2026 |title=PDC updates regulations: Q School and European Tour undergo significant changes |url=https://dartsnews.com/pdc/pdc-updates-regulations-q-school-and-european-tour-undergo-significant-changes |access-date=2 January 2026 |website=Darts News |language=en}}</ref> As before, players who qualify on the first three days of the Final Stage do not need to play the remaining days. However, each day of the Final Stage will be topped up to 128 players with the best available players who failed to qualify from the First Stage, rather than byes being awarded.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=31 December 2025 |title=2026 PDC Qualifying School entries close on Friday |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/december/31/2026-pdc-qualifying-school-entries-close-friday |access-date=2 January 2026 |website=PDC |language=en}}</ref>

=== Changes to qualification criteria for events ===
Several events had tweaks to their qualification criteria. European Tour seeding changed so that reserve players no longer take a seeded player's position in the draw. It was originally announced that seeding in first round matches would be based on the full Order of Merit rather than the Pro Tour Order of Merit, but this rule change was reverted before the first event.<ref name="DNNewRules" /><ref>{{cite web |last1=Wood |first1=Kieran |title=PDC amends controversial rule change: Pro Tour Top 16 seeded on European Tour |url=https://dartsnews.com/pdc/pdc-amends-controversial-rule-change-pro-tour-top-16-seeded-on-european-tour |website=Darts News |access-date=24 January 2026 |language=en |date=18 January 2026}}</ref> Additionally, it was announced that players would be chosen for World Cup teams based on their Challenge Tour Ranking if a nation has no players ranked on the Order of Merit and no local Affiliate Tour.<ref name="DNNewRules" /> Tour Card holders ranked inside the top 64 who failed to qualify for the World Championship by the main or Pro Tour Orders of Merit have been re-permitted to enter regional qualifying tournaments, while World Youth Championship finalists will qualify for the newly-expanded [[Grand Slam of Darts]] in the same year, rather than in the next.<ref name="DNNewRules" />

===New event locations===
The [[2026 Premier League Darts]] schedule was announced in September 2025 and included a night in Belgium for the first time, as [[Antwerp]] replaced [[Exeter]] in the lineup.<ref>{{cite web |date=11 September 2025 |title=Premier League Darts 2026 dates and schedule confirmed: Antwerp replaces Exeter |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13428630/premier-league-darts-2026-dates-and-schedule-confirmed-antwerp-replaces-exeter |website=Sky Sports |access-date=12 October 2025}}</ref> The [[2026 PDC Pro Tour#European Tour|2026 European Tour]] saw the introduction of two new events: the [[2026 Poland Darts Open|Poland Darts Open]] in [[Kraków]] and the [[2026 Slovak Darts Open|Slovak Darts Open]] in [[Bratislava]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Poland Darts Open 2026 |url=https://www.pdc-europe.tv/tournaments/et-2026-en/poland-darts-open-2026/ |website=PDC Europe |access-date=12 October 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=Slovak Darts Open 2026 |url=https://www.pdc-europe.tv/tournaments/et-2026-en/slovak-darts-open-2026/ |website=PDC Europe |access-date=12 October 2025}}</ref> The Poland Darts Open replaced the Poland Darts Masters, a [[World Series of Darts|World Series]] event that was on the PDC calendar since 2023, while the Slovak Darts Open marked the PDC's first visit to Slovakia.<ref name="Calendar">{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=11 September 2025 |title=European Tour set for further expansion in 2026 with PDC calendar released |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/september/11/european-tour-set-further-expansion-2026-pdc-calendar-released |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=12 October 2025}}</ref>

As part of the [[2026 World Series of Darts]], it was announced that the PDC would hold its first event in Saudi Arabia, the Saudi Arabia Darts Masters, in January.<ref name="WorldSeries">{{cite web |last=Phillips |first=Josh |date=5 October 2025 |title=2026 World Series of Darts schedule confirmed |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/october/05/2026-world-series-darts-schedule-confirmed |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=12 October 2025}}</ref> The announcement followed interest shown by PDC president [[Barry Hearn]] and [[General Entertainment Authority]] chairman [[Turki Al-Sheikh]] to take darts to the country.<ref>{{cite web |last=Keogh |first=Frank |date=6 October 2025 |title=No alcohol allowed - darts heads to Saudi Arabia |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/darts/articles/ckgew816k67o |website=BBC Sport |access-date=12 October 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |date=2 October 2025 |title=Barry Hearn teases Saudi future with a twist for darts |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/video/30998/13442196/barry-hearn-teases-saudi-future-with-a-twist-for-darts |website=Sky Sports |access-date=12 October 2025}}</ref>

In December 2025, it was confirmed that the 2026 PDC World Championship would be the last edition of the tournament to be held in the West Hall of [[Alexandra Palace]]. The event will be held in the venue's Great Hall for the first time at the [[2027 PDC World Darts Championship|2027 PDC World Championship]].<ref>{{cite web |last=Byrne |first=Sean |date=4 December 2025 |title=Worlds to stay at Alexandra Palace until 2031 |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/darts/articles/c2lvky9qrx8o |website=BBC Sport |access-date=4 December 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |last=Gent |first=Oli |date=4 December 2025 |title=World Darts Championship to continue at Alexandra Palace until 2031 |url=https://www.standard.co.uk/sport/pdc-world-darts-championship-alexandra-palace-b1261143.html |website=The London Standard |access-date=4 December 2025}}</ref>

===Television deals===
In February 2025, [[Sky Sports]] announced a five-year extension of its partnership with the PDC, continuing coverage of the [[PDC World Darts Championship]], [[Premier League Darts]], [[World Matchplay (darts)|World Matchplay]], [[Grand Slam of Darts]], [[World Grand Prix (darts)|World Grand Prix]], and the [[World Cup of Darts]] on the broadcaster until 2030.<ref>{{cite web |date=4 February 2025 |title=Darts to continue on Sky Sports after new five-year broadcast deal |url=https://www.irishtimes.com/sport/2025/02/04/darts-to-continue-on-sky-sports-after-new-five-year-broadcast-deal/ |website=The Irish Times |access-date=26 June 2026}}</ref>

In October 2025, the PDC agreed to a new three-year deal with [[ITV Sport]] to broadcast events on [[ITV4]] and [[ITVX]] until 2028; these events included the [[PDC World Masters|World Masters]], [[UK Open]], [[European Championship (darts)|European Championship]], [[Players Championship Finals]], [[World Series of Darts Finals|World Series Finals]], and all global World Series events. The announcement also stated that the PDC would take control of its own event production, which was previously managed by ITV.<ref>{{cite web |date=29 October 2025 |title=ITV & Professional Darts Corporation agree new multi-year broadcast extension |url=https://www.itv.com/presscentre/media-releases/itv-professional-darts-corporation-agree-new-multi-year-broadcast-extension |website=ITV Press Centre |access-date=29 October 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |last1=Gabbitass |first1=Anna-Rose |date=29 October 2025 |title=PDC extends free-to-air ITV deal until 2028 |url=https://www.sportindustry.biz/news-categories/news/the-professional-darts-corporation-agree-broadcast-extension-itv/ |website=Sport Industry Group |access-date=29 October 2025}}</ref>

In June 2026, the PDC announced a deal with [[ESPN]] to broadcast five events on [[ESPN2]] and [[ESPN+]] in the United States. These events were the [[2026 US Darts Masters|US Darts Masters]], [[2026 World Matchplay|World Matchplay]], [[2026 World Grand Prix (darts)|World Grand Prix]], Grand Slam of Darts, and the [[2027 PDC World Darts Championship|2027 World Championship]].<ref>{{cite web |date=25 June 2026 |title=ESPN to broadcast PDC events in USA |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/june/25/espn-to-broadcast-/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=25 June 2026}}</ref>

===Affiliate Tour expansion===
In December 2025, the PDC announced a three-year funding cycle for PDC Global Affiliate Tours that involved an investment of over £3,000,000.<ref name="GlobalBoost">{{cite web |last=Allen |first=Dave |date=24 December 2025 |title=Global boost with PDC's new £3 million-plus investment in Affiliate Tours |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2025/december/24/global-boost-pdcs-new-ps3-million-plus-investment-affiliate-tours |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=26 December 2025}}</ref> There will be increased prize money for events on the Nordic and Baltic Pro Tour and the DartPlayers New Zealand Tour, while the China Pro Tour and Australian Darts Association Tour will have more events. Additionally, the African Continental Tour will expand to six events in each of the North and South regions, with an increase in the number of African qualifiers for the World Championship from one to two in 2027.<ref name="GlobalBoost" /><ref name="DNAffTourExpand">{{cite web |last1=Gill |first1=Samuel |title=PDC invests over three million in global darts growth after unprecedented international success at the World Darts Championship |url=https://dartsnews.com/pdc/pdc-invests-over-three-million-in-global-darts-growth-after-unprecedented-international-success-at-the-world-darts-championship |website=Darts News |access-date=27 December 2025 |language=en |date=25 December 2025}}</ref>

==Pro Tour==
{{Main|2026 PDC Pro Tour}}
The 2026 [[PDC Pro Tour]] is a series of darts tournaments organised by the PDC, consisting of 34 [[PDC Pro Tour#Players Championships|Players Championship]] events and 15 [[PDC European Tour|European Tour]] events.<ref name="Calendar" />

===Tour cards===
{{main|List of players with a 2026 PDC Tour Card}}

The '''2026 PDC Tour Cards''' were awarded to:
* (64) The top 64 players from the [[PDC Order of Merit]] after the [[2025 PDC World Darts Championship|2025 World Championship]].
* (28) 28 qualifiers from [[2024 PDC Pro Tour#Q-School|2024 Q-School]] not ranked in the top 64 of the [[PDC Order of Merit]] following the World Championship.
* (1) The highest qualifier from the [[2024 PDC Development Tour series|2024 Development Tour]] ([[Sebastian Białecki]])
** [[Niko Springer]], who also earned a Tour Card through the 2024 Development Tour, entered the top 64 in his first year on tour. Therefore, one extra Tour Card was awarded to a 2026 Q-School qualifier.
* (2) Two highest qualifiers from the [[2024 PDC Challenge Tour series|2024 Challenge Tour]] ([[Wesley Plaisier]] and [[Christian Kist]])
* (2) Highest qualifiers from the [[2025 PDC Development Tour series|2025 Development Tour]] ([[Beau Greaves]] and [[Owen Bates]]).
* (2) Highest qualifiers from the [[2025 PDC Challenge Tour series|2025 Challenge Tour]] ([[Stefan Bellmont]] and [[Darius Labanauskas]]).
* (16) The daily top two players from the 2026 Q-Schools.

Afterwards, the playing field was complemented by the highest qualified players from the Q-School Order of Merit until the maximum number of 128 Pro Tour Card players was reached.<ref>{{citenews|url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/01/04/2026-pdc-qualifying-school-entries-confirmed|title=2026 PDC Qualifying School entries confirmed|publisher=PDC|date=4 January 2026}}</ref>

== Secondary tours ==
{{main|2026 PDC Challenge Tour|2026 PDC Development Tour|2026 PDC Women's Series}}
The 2026 [[PDC secondary tours]] are series of darts tournaments organised by the PDC that operate outside of the main PDC Pro Tour and predominantly feature players without a Tour Card. There are three secondary tours: the [[PDC Challenge Tour|Challenge Tour]] (for players who unsuccessfully participated in the 2026 Q-School), [[PDC Development Tour|Development Tour]] (for players aged 16 to 24) and [[PDC Women's Series|Women's Series]] (for female players).

==== Asian Championship ====
Following the final four Asian Tour events, the PDC Asian Championship was contested on 14 September in [[Changjiang Li Autonomous County|Changjiang]], Hainan, China. The tournament featured 64 players, and the top 16 players in the 2026 Asian Tour rankings were [[Seeding (sport)|seeded]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Asian Tour 25–28 and Asian Championship |url=https://www.pdc-asia.tv/index.php?a=Index&c=Index&m=category&mid=1&cid=367 |website=www.pdc-asia.tv |access-date=10 August 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |last=Simpson |first=Will |title=Nebrida wins maiden Asian Championship as Chen makes history |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/september/15/nebrida-wins-maiden-asian-championship-as-chen-makes-history/ |website=Professional Darts Corporation |access-date=15 September 2026}}</ref> The winner [[Paolo Nebrida]] qualified for the [[2026 Grand Slam of Darts]] and together with Finalist Kam Weng Cheng for the [[2027 PDC World Darts Championship]].

===Next Gen===
The PDC Europe Next Gen is the PDC Global Affiliate Tour for players from Germany, Switzerland, Austria, Luxembourg and Liechtenstein. The 2026 season consists of 16 events organised over eight weekends using five different event formats. In 2026, two new formats were introduced: 170, where each leg is played from 170 rather than 501; and Swiss, where the preliminary round will use a [[Swiss-style tournament|Swiss-style]] league phase.<ref>{{cite web |title=Für Spieler – Informationen zu Qualifiern und Next Gen |url=https://www.pdc-europe.tv/de/fuer-spieler/ |website=PDC Europe |access-date=10 January 2026 |language=de-DE}}</ref> Orders of Merit are maintained for all players, players aged 16–24 and women, with top-ranked players qualifying for the PDC Europe Super League and/or winning free entry to Q-School, Development Tour and Women's Series events.<ref>{{cite web |title=PDC Europe Next Gen Regelweek |url=https://www.pdc-europe.tv/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Regelwerk-NEXT-GEN-2026.pdf |website=PDC Europe |access-date=10 January 2026 |language=de}}</ref>
