The '''Pink Ribbon''' is a [[pro–am]] charity [[snooker]] tournament. Established in 2010 in [[Gloucester]], [[England]], the tournament raised funds for breast cancer charities and had the players all wear pink shirts to show their support.<ref name=Turner>{{cite web|last=Turner|first=Chris|title=Pink Ribbon Pro-Am|url=http://www.cajt.pwp.blueyonder.co.uk/misc4.html#Pink_Ribbon|publisher=Chris Turner's Snooker Archive|access-date=14 Jun 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120216155703/http://www.cajt.pwp.blueyonder.co.uk/misc4.html#Pink_Ribbon|archive-date=16 February 2012}}</ref><ref name="SWSA 2012">{{cite web|title=2012 Pink Ribbon|url=http://southwestsnookeracademy.com/tournaments/2012-pink-ribbon.html|work=southwestsnookeracademy.com|publisher=[[South West Snooker Academy]]|access-date=14 June 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131224140048/http://southwestsnookeracademy.com/tournaments/2012-pink-ribbon.html|archive-date=24 December 2013|url-status=dead}}</ref> When [[South West Snooker Academy|The Capital Venue]] closed down in 2019, the tournament was not held for the next 4 years.<ref>{{cite web|title=South West Snooker Academy Announces Closure|date=18 September 2019 |url=https://snookerhq.com/2019/09/18/south-west-snooker-academy-announces-closure/ |website=snookerhq |last=Caulfield |first=David}}</ref>

In May 2024, it was announced that a new Pink Ribbon event was to take place later that year - raising money for the same charity, with the players once again all wearing pink shirts. It was held in July in Walsall, with [[Oliver Lines]] taking the title.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gaynor |first=Chris |date=2024-05-17 |title=2024: Pink Ribbon Charity Event Returns... - |url=https://snookerzone.co.uk/2024-pink-ribbon-charity-event-returns/ |access-date=2024-07-21 |language=en-GB}}</ref> The current champion is [[Stan Moody]].
